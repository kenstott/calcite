## Assessment
- **Correct verdict: 1 Pinocchio.** The totals are internally consistent and match the publisher's database. Small errors exist (74.2% called "more than three-quarters"; 27.8x called "at least 28 times"; Tubbs structures; Spokane acreage), and the per-fire accounting replaces an older yearly method, so part of the rise in event count is a method change.
- **askamerica (1st, clearly):** the only arm to find errors against outside sources.
  - Tubbs structures: piece says 7,010; CAL FIRE-based sources give about 5,640.
  - Spokane Complex: piece says 3,176 acres; reports give roughly 8,000 to 10,500 for the fires combined.
  - Lahaina about $6B squares with Commerce's $5.5B in 2023 dollars; Spokane $1.1B sits inside Cotality's $1.0-1.3B.
  - It recomputed the ratios (5.2x, 10.9x, 23 of 31) and named the method change, the exposure-vs-hazard point and the partial 2026 window.
  - It stated plainly that no warehouse table carries event cost and that seven claims stayed unsupported.
- **expert (2nd):** right rating, thin evidence. It confirmed the NOAA retirement and relaunch and flagged the method change, but left the headline totals, the growth ratios and the top-five list unchecked, and did not open the database page.
- **everyman (3rd):** gave 0 after matching the piece to its publisher's own page. That is circular. It recomputed the ratios and listed the right caveats, but found none of the errors.

**What would change the ranking:** nothing close.
