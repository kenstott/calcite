Misleading:
1. The article says the order returns money to the 330,000 importers "whether or not they filed an individual lawsuit." The certified class excludes any entity that separately sued; it covers only the non-suing portion (tens of thousands of importers).
2. The headline and lede frame all $166B as pending. $166B and roughly 330,000 importers are CBP's March 4 estimates of total duties; about $117B was refunded by August (about $136B through September per MTS).
