- "At risk of closing" is a modeled classification by one advocacy group (a payment-reform center), disputed by the hospital association. The piece offers one line of rebuttal and no hospital-side detail.
- The Oklahoma numbers are not in the PDF it links, so a reader cannot verify them from the cited source.
- It is ambiguous whether 17 is a subset of 44. The 61% arithmetic implies it is.
