**The Fortune article's claims about the Fed, October CPI and the inflation 'forever war' are mostly accurate.**

Rating: **1 Pinocchio**.

Article: Jason Ma, Fortune, 2026-11-14. The connector fetch succeeded and no fallback outlet was needed.

| Claim | Verdict | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| October CPI hotter than expected | True | Warehouse econ.fred_indicators: core CPI +0.296% m/m, headline +0.35%. CNBC: headline 0.4% vs 0.3% expected, core 0.3% vs 0.2%. |
| Policy rate 3.75-4.00% | True | FEDFUNDS monthly average Aug 3.63, Sep 3.75, Oct 3.88. CNBC: September hike, October hold by a 10-2 vote. |
| September SEP moves 2% inflation to 2029 from 2028 | True | Fed SEP PDF read directly. Sept median PCE is 2.1 in 2028 and 2.0 in 2029. June had 2.0 in 2028. |
| Six years above 2% in a few months | True | CPI y/y first above 2% in March 2021 (2.6%). Minimum y/y since then is 2.31%. |
| "Eight straight years, or as long as Operation Iraqi Freedom" | Mostly true | The Fed span is about 8 years. OIF ran Mar 2003 to Aug 2010, about 7.4 years, from my general knowledge (not fetched). The comparison is rhetorical and depends on the end date. |
| Goolsbee quote "above 2% for almost six years..." | True | Verbatim in CNBC, Nov 13. |
| Waller quote "Hiking only 25 basis points would put our credibility at risk" | Mostly true | Verbatim in CNBC. His trigger was core CPI "around 0.3", not Fortune's "0.3% or more". |
| Logan "may be possible to move more rapidly" | True | CNBC quotes "this winter..." and her call for two more hikes. |
| Goolsbee "borderline disturbing" | True | Secondary reports only. It was said in a separate interview from the CNBC one the article links. |
| Warsh "inflation is a choice" | True | Widely reported signature line. Not graded as a claim in the report. |
| Trump "We're just keeping it the status quo" | Unsupported | Not found in search results or on the White House briefings page. Fortune is the only source. |
| GDP "running hot" on AI capex | Mostly true | Real GDP Q3 about +3.4% annualized, Q2 +2.2% (GDPC1). The capex dollar figure was not verified. |

**Misleading or omitted:**
- BLS published no y/y CPI for October because of last year's shutdown gap. The article does not mention it.
- "Forever war" and the Iraq analogy are framing, not fact.
- The statement that the Fed "has also provided monetary stimulus" is an unsourced causal attribution.
- Not verified: oil and diesel claims, the Hormuz closure, the Swonk LinkedIn post, and the AI capex dollar amount.

**Method:**
- I compared the claims to FRED series in the warehouse (CPILFESL, CPIAUCSL, CPIAUCNS, PCEPI, FEDFUNDS, GDPC1).
- I read the Fed SEP PDF and three CNBC pages directly.
- I used web searches for the Warsh and "borderline disturbing" quotes, and for the Trump quote and the Logan wording, which I could not source beyond Fortune and CNBC.
