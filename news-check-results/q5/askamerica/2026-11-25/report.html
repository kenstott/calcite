Summary

1 Pinocchio. The piece is a faithful roundup of five outlets and its hard numbers check out: Thailand bought $1.34B of US farm goods in 2025 (USDA FAS), US goods exports to Thailand were $19.6B and imports $91.3B, up 45% (warehouse: $19.59B, $91.26B, +45.0%). The weakness is framing. The headline presents Trump's statements (all tariffs eliminated, pork opened, $2.6B in ag purchases) as the deal's terms. Other reporting shows the signed document was a joint statement, Thailand's commerce minister said tariffs were not abolished on all US goods, and the $2.6B is called 'additional' by Trump/Bloomberg but 'a year' by the Nation. The section title 'Nearly Double' conflicts with 'additional $2.6 billion' (1.34 + 2.6 is about triple; DTN reads $2.6B as the new total). The piece does not reconcile this or carry Thailand's pushback.
