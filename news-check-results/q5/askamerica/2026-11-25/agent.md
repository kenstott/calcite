The Farm Policy News article's claims about the US-Thailand trade deal, the $2.6 billion in ag purchases and US-Thailand trade totals are mostly accurate.

**Rating: 1 Pinocchio.**

Article: https://farmpolicynews.illinois.edu/2026/11/thailand-trade-deal-will-include-2-6-billion-in-us-ag-purchases-pork-market-access-trump-says/
Report page: http://127.0.0.1:52345/a/45d8702990ffee4016433012bd2a6961.html (report_id 20261125-083918-the-farm-policy-news-article-s-claims-ab)

**Summary.** The piece is a roundup of Bloomberg, AgWeb, Farms.com, DTN/Progressive Farmer and AgroLatam. Its hard numbers check out. The weakness is framing.

**Checked against the warehouse (Census tables, econ.trade_exports and econ.trade_imports)**
- US goods exports to Thailand in 2025: $19.59B. The article says $19.6B. True.
- US imports from Thailand in 2025: $91.26B, against $62.96B in 2024. That is +45.0%. The article says $91.3B and +45%. True.
- Census HS 01-24 ag exports to Thailand in 2025: $1.29B. This is a narrower definition than FAS. The warehouse has no 2026 trade data and no table for the FAS agricultural series.

**Checked against independent sources**
- Thailand's 2025 purchases of US ag products were $1.34B, rank #23 (USDA FAS Thailand page, read directly). True. FAS also lists $1.7B on a broader "agricultural and related" measure for 2025 and $1.1B through nine months of 2026, so "keeping pace" is roughly right.
- The $1.69B peak in 2021 is stated only by DTN. I did not read the FAS annual series, and the Census HS 01-24 series in the warehouse peaks in 2018 ($1.76B) on a different definition. Graded mostly true on one source.
- The October 2025 framework and its 99% tariff figure are confirmed by DTN and Investing.com. True.
- The Rollins quote matches DTN.
- Trump's $2.6B figure is accurately relayed (DTN, Investing.com, AgroLatam). No schedule has been published. The Nation (Nov 19) describes it as an estimated $2.6B a year in commercial purchases.

**Materially misleading framing**
1. The headline says the deal "will include" $2.6B in purchases and pork access. The signed document was a joint statement toward an agreement, with terms unpublished and legal reviews still pending (DTN, Investing.com, Nation, AgroLatam).
2. Trump's claim that Thailand will eliminate tariffs on US exports is contested. Thai Commerce Minister Suphajee said Thailand had not abolished tariffs on all US goods. The 99% proposal remains under negotiation, with quotas for sensitive goods. The piece carries none of this. Graded partially false for Trump as a speaker.
3. The "Nearly Double" section heading conflicts with "$2.6 billion in additional purchases". $1.34B plus $2.6B is about triple, while DTN reads $2.6B as the new total. The piece does not reconcile the two.
4. Pork access is unresolved. The Nation reports that Thailand has long opposed US pork from pigs given lean-growth substances, and its Agriculture Ministry was reviewing safeguards on Nov 19. The piece does note that the key now is for the agreement to be signed and followed through.

**Not verified.** The Bloomberg and AgWeb pages returned HTTP 403, and Farms.com returned an empty page. I did not re-verify the Flammini, LeGrand or Morgan quotes at their primaries. The Bloomberg claims were checked through the Investing.com copy. The report lists this as an unanswered clause.
