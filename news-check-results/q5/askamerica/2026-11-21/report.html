The Fortune article's claims about U.S. Chamber's Neil Bradley, 20 years of 2% growth and business applications are accurate.

Article under reviewhttps://fortune.com/2026/11/16/us-chamber-neil-bradley-midterms-growth-easy-button-price-controls/

Summary

Bradley's 2% vs 3% growth claim is true (3.46%/yr 1950-2005, 2.04%/yr 2005-2025). His 5.68M 'new business starts' record is partly misleading: the figure matches Census business applications (record 5.67-5.70M), but applications are not businesses started, and high-propensity applications fell to about 1.67M in 2025, two years running. Fortune itself flags this. The reporter's own claims hold up.
