Summary

Rating: 2 Pinocchios. The core story holds: 2026 set records for Oregon acreage and firefighting cost. But the acreage headline (2.1M acres, 13% above 2024) conflicts with OPB's own Sept. 1 report (2.5M by Aug. 20) and with the warehouse (2.54M in 2026 vs 2.01M in 2024, about +26%); 2.1 vs 1.9 is also only +10.5%. And 'farmers in 18 counties' misstates the USDA release (9 primary plus 15 contiguous counties; drought-criteria designation with storm/flood trigger, not wildfire).
