# The OPB article's claims about Oregon's 2026 wildfire acreage, costs and USDA loan eligibility are balanced and partly false.

Article: https://opb.org/article/2026/10/24/oregon-2026-wildfire-season-by-the-numbers (fetched successfully). Rating: 2 Pinocchios.

Local report: http://127.0.0.1:57728/a/c5f523acd6eb3aa4a0309b9fecba803b.html (report_id 20261108-143339-the-opb-article-s-claims-about-oregon-s)

## Findings
- Record acreage and record cost: true in direction. Warehouse (disasters.wildfire_perimeters, Oregon WF, gis_acres falling back to incident size): 2026 = 2.54M acres, 2024 = 2.01M.
- Headline acreage inconsistent: article says 2.1M, "about 13%" above 1.9M. 2.1/1.9 is +10.5%, OPB's own Sept. 1 report said 2.5M by Aug. 20, and the warehouse gives +26%. Partially false.
- Five fires above half of acres: true (top five perimeters 1.44M of 2.54M, about 57%).
- Costs: $236.2M as of Aug. 20, +$35.3M vs 2024 final net (OPB Sept. 1). Mostly true, but a mid-season figure.
- Snowpack nearly 30% below 2015: true (OPB Feb. 2026).
- FSA loans: article says 18 counties. The USDA release lists 9 primary plus 15 contiguous (24), and the trigger is storms/flooding with drought criteria, not wildfire. Mostly false and misleading. Deadline Sept. 30, 2027 is correct.
- Flags lowered "for both men": KVAL confirms only the Gutierrez Toral order. Partially false.
- O'Neill drought-2027 statement: true.
- Not checkable here: evacuation counts, 1,813 fires and the 2,100 average, $350M projection, $123M/$108M/$15M Emergency Board funding. The ODF/ODEM pages returned no usable data; only $150M HB 3940 borrowing was corroborated. The Wallace quote was not graded.

## Misleading flags
The acreage vintage is unclear to readers. The USDA item implies wildfire-based eligibility. The cost figure is partial-season.
