🤥🤥○○ Claims Accuracy: 2 of 4 Pinocchios

Two of the claim's three factual clusters check out precisely against primary sources: the DCMP letter details and the $177M/35,000-household IHP figure are both exactly right. But the $2.5B+ 'FEMA public-assistance projects pending' figure is a real number wearing a misleading label. No source anywhere ties $2.5 billion to FEMA Public Assistance for these fires; the best-documented genuine FEMA PA backlog (from California's own Governor's Office, May 2026) is $732 million -- and even that is 3.4x smaller than claimed and four months older than the claimed September 2026 date. The $2.5B figure that does circulate in wildfire coverage refers to Governor Newsom's separate state relief fund, an entirely different funding stream from FEMA Public Assistance. This is exactly the kind of magnitude-and-category error -- a true-sounding number attached to the wrong program -- that keeps this out of the 0-1 range despite the other two clusters being flawlessly accurate.
