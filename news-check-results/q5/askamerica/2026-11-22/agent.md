The Christian Science Monitor article's claims about farm income, harvest size, government aid and food prices are accurate.

Article: https://www.csmonitor.com/Business/2026/1110/farmers-harvest-subsidies-inflation-crops (fetched directly).

## Claims checked (warehouse first)
- **Illinois 93% corn / 96% soy harvested: TRUE.** ag.nass_crop_progress, week ending Nov 8, 2026; matches USDA Crop Progress.
- **Corn second-largest crop ever; soybeans a record (central claim): TRUE.** ag.nass_crop_estimates_current: corn 15.89B bu (2025: 16.96B; 2023: 15.34B); soybeans 4.574B bu vs prior high 4.464B (2021). Estimates.
- **Net farm income down 5.5% real: TRUE.** ERS Sept 3, 2026 forecast: -$9.1B, 5.5% real. A forecast.
- **Payments could double and set a record: MOSTLY TRUE.** ag.ers_farm_income: 2025 $27.9B, 2026F $47.4B (above 2020's $45.6B). That is 1.7x before the $8B and about 2x with it, only if paid within 2026.
- **$8B Harvest Fuel Relief announced last month, two weeks before the midterms: TRUE.** Announced Oct 19; midterms Nov 3.
- **Grocery prices +1% in 2027, smallest since 2019: TRUE.** food-at-home CPI: 2019 +0.9%, 2024 +1.2%, 2025 +2.2%.
- **Food costs up more than 30% since pre-pandemic: TRUE.** +32.6% (2019 to 2026 YTD).
- **Ackerman diesel $2.75 to double; $1,500 fill-up: TRUE as a local anecdote.** The warehouse has no local farm price; EIA national retail diesel rose about 71% ($3.48 to $5.94).
- **Iran war raised fertilizer prices via Hormuz and Middle East nitrogen: TRUE** (AP/PBS).
- **Tariffs raised equipment prices: MOSTLY TRUE.** Cited Farm Progress page returned 403; Washington Post supports higher machinery costs; the Supreme Court struck down the emergency-based tariffs.
- **Texas A&M AFPC feed-grain 1 of 25 good, 19 at over 50% net-worth risk: UNSUPPORTED at the primary source.** AFPC site failed TLS; a search summary reports 1 of 25 good and 19 poor, but the 50% risk figure was not seen.

## Caveats
1. Income and payment figures are the early-September ERS forecast, predating the $8B program.
2. The 1% grocery forecast has a very wide interval (-5.2% to +7.5%).
3. Harvest easing inflation is the authors' inference; 2026 food-at-home CPI still exceeds 2025.

No material distortion found.
