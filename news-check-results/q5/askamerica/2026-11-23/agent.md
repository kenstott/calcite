The FoodNavigator article's claims about US dairy exports topping $10bn in 2026, the $1bn September and Middle East shipments are somewhat biased and mostly accurate; 2 claims could not be confirmed.

**Rating: 1 Pinocchio.**

Source article: https://www.foodnavigator.com/Article/2026/11/16/us-dairy-exports-set-to-top-10bn-in-2026-as-global-demand-drives-growth/ (fetched in full). It relays a U.S. Dairy Export Council (USDEC) blog post of 12 Nov 2026 (also fetched). Every figure matches that post.

**Warehouse check.** econ.trade_exports (Census) ends in 2025, so none of the 2026 figures can be tested there. I found no table with butterfat or milk-solids export data. Census HS chapter 04 exports, for scale only, were $7.26bn in 2022, $5.92bn in 2023, $6.06bn in 2024 and $7.01bn in 2025. That is narrower than USDEC's dairy definition and cannot test the thresholds.

**Claims**
- Central: exports expected to exceed $10bn in 2026. TRUE as an estimate. YTD is $8.18bn (USDEC, and Ag Proud reports +14% y/y), so only about $1.8bn is needed in Q4.
- "First time above $1 billion in a single month" (September). PARTIALLY FALSE. USDA GATS, as reported by DairyHerd on 19 Nov, puts September at $992.3 million. The claim says over $1bn; the government series says below. USDEC probably uses a broader product definition, and the article never says so.
- September value up 29%. TRUE. GATS shows +28.9%.
- MSE volume +10% in September, YTD about +7%, best Q3 ever. UNSUPPORTED. The figures match USDEC, but USDEC is the only source and I found no independent series.
- Middle East butterfat (+381%, +330%, +46%) and cheese (-26%, +19%, +5%) figures, the Saudi rerouting (+91%, +222%) and Southeast Asia whey (+47%). UNSUPPORTED. Same reason: USDEC is the sole source, and no 2026 Census or GATS country data was found.
- About 70% of US milk powder goes to export. TRUE as USDEC states it. The article drops the word "U.S." from the sentence.

**Misleading by omission.** The piece presents a trade lobby's figures as plain fact. It never notes that USDA's own GATS figure falls just short of the $1bn milestone it headlines, and there is no independent source or caveat anywhere in it.

Limits: I did not query the USDA GATS database directly. The $992.3m comes from DairyHerd's report on it.
