The White House ONDCP release's claims about US overdose deaths, the 41.5% decline and policy credit are accurate.

Rating: 0 Pinocchios (engine rating from claim scores). Framing flagged as materially misleading context below.

Misleading framing: 
- The 12-month-ending count peaked in Aug 2023 and fell from 112,418 to 81,313 (-27.7%) by Dec 2024, before January 2025. That is 67% of the total fall to June 2026 (46,694). Dec 2024 to Jun 2026 added -19.2%.
- The "18th consecutive month" streak is anchored to inauguration, though the decline started 17 months earlier. "Peak under the previous administration" is true but omits that the decline was already well advanced when the administration began.
- Counts are CDC predicted provisional estimates, subject to revision.
- The Carter causal attribution is an official's opinion presented as explanation; CDC data does not test it.

Bottom line: statistics are accurate; the causal credit is unsupported and the streak framing omits that the downturn pre-dates the administration.
