Method: WebFetch (returned a summary, not verbatim text, so quote wording is as relayed), plus WebSearch. I did not reach the Fed's own tables, BLS pages or transcripts directly, only secondary coverage and search snippets. Verdicts are limited by that.

## Overall rating: 1 Pinocchio (mostly accurate), with a low-confidence caveat

The macro facts (rate level, SEP path, October core CPI, Waller) check out. Unconfirmed items are mostly quotes I could not locate, not items contradicted.

## Misleading or missing context
- Waller's 50 bp idea was explicitly conditional on hot core data; the piece reads as a firmer stance.
- "Trump rejected negotiations" conflicts with contemporaneous reporting of ongoing talks and a phased-deal discussion; what he rejected was Iran's specific proposal.
- October CPI has no year-over-year figure because of the shutdown data gap, which complicates "inflation has been above target" comparisons.
- Headline CPI (+0.4%) was the bigger miss than core; framing around core alone understates the energy effect.
- "Spreading beyond energy" rests on a single 0.3% core print.
