# Fact-check: Boston Globe, "Amid battle against historic N.H. wildfire, concern about other fires" (2026-11-12)

Limitation: WebFetch returned only a paraphrased summary of the Globe piece (no verbatim text), so claims below are as summarized. Sources reached: NHPR (11/9, 11/10), InciWeb 11/12 via search, WMUR drought report (11/5), WCAX, Forest Service releases, WMUR/Forest Society on 1941 fire. New Hampshire Bulletin returned 403.

## Claims
| # | Claim | Verdict | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Wild River Fire burned "nearly 1,000 acres" | Outdated / misleading | Peak mapped was 921 acres; Forest Service remapped down to 856 (11/5), 821 (11/10), 817 (InciWeb 11/12). The 11/12 figure is ~817. |
| 2 | Crews at it since Nov. 1 | True | NHPR, Forest Service: started Sunday Nov. 1. |
| 3 | Nearly 60% contained | True | InciWeb 11/12: 59%. |
| 4 | Drought, access, terrain, wind aided spread | Largely true | NHPR/Berthiaume: steep, roadless terrain, dry conditions, warm nights. Wind is less documented (later reports say winds under 10 mph helped slow it). |
| 5 | Largest fire in White Mountain NF since founding in 1918 | True | Forest Service. |
| 6 | 1941 Marlow-Stoddard fire larger, 24,000 acres, four days | Mostly true | Most sources say about 24,000 acres (some 27,000); duration reported as 3 days in one source, so "four days" is unverified. |
| 7 | More than 90% of NH abnormally dry; about 40% moderate drought | True | WMUR on Drought Monitor (11/5): over 93% abnormally dry, just over 40% moderate drought. |
| 8 | 87 people working on the fire as of Wednesday | Partly verified | InciWeb 11/12 lists both 87 and 58 personnel in different sections. |
| 9 | Cause under investigation | True | Forest Service: human-caused, under investigation. |
| 10 | Nearby roads and trails closed | True | NHPR: Wild River Road and trails closed. |
| 11 | Matson: "I'm definitely concerned about that"; leaf fall adds fuel | Not checkable here | Matson is real and quoted elsewhere (smoldering "until winter conditions come"); exact quote not found. |
| 12 | 127 other fires this year burned 180 acres; typical year ~250 acres; "four times" | Not checkable here | No public source located. Arithmetic is odd: 180 acres is below the typical 250, so "four times" only works if the Wild River Fire is included. Needs the original wording. |
| 13 | Shelburne reports business as usual | Not checkable here | No source found. |
| 14 | Guiterman: kills small trees, larger survive, habitat benefits, fewer ticks | Not checkable here | Expert quote, no independent source found. |

## Rating: 1 Pinocchio (minor problems)
Most checkable facts are accurate. Main error: the headline-level acreage of "nearly 1,000" was superseded by remapping to about 817 acres more than a week before publication, overstating the fire by about 20%.

## Materially misleading / caveats
- Stale acreage figure alongside a current containment figure.
- The "180 acres vs 250 typical" comparison needs clarification of whether the Wild River Fire is included.
- Personnel count conflicts within InciWeb itself (58 vs 87).
- Four claims could not be verified; rating rests on the remainder and on the summary, not the full text.
