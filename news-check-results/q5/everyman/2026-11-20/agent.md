# Fact-check: Epoch Times, "US Rents Post First Annual Increase in More Than Three Years" (Mary Prenon, 11/19/2026)

Method: WebFetch of the article, then web searches for the primary reports. I did not open the Realtor.com, NAR, Freddie Mac or Neighbors Bank primary documents directly. Verification below rests on search-result summaries and secondary coverage.

## Claims
| Claim | Verdict |
|---|---|
| Realtor.com (Nov. 18): national median asking rent up 0.1% YoY in October to $1,692 (a $1 gain) | Supported (Realtor.com release via finviz, MPA, CRE Daily) |
| "First annual increase since June 2023" | Partly accurate. Realtor.com ties June 2023 to one-bedrooms. Two-bedrooms last rose in May 2023. The overall median's last gain date is not shown in what I saw. |
| 2BR +0.3% to $1,889; 1BR +0.1% to $1,566; studio -1.2% to $1,411 | Supported |
| Q3 multifamily completions -26% YoY to a 330,000 annual rate; no rebound expected for 12 months | Supported |
| Units under construction -2.7% to 674,000; starts -9.2% to 403,000 | Not checked (not seen in the snippets) |
| Rent 4.6% ($82) below summer 2022 peak, 15% ($221) above pre-pandemic | Not checked. The arithmetic is plausible. |
| Pending Home Sales Index 63, -4.7% MoM, -16.7% YoY, lowest since 2001 (NAR, Nov. 18) | Supported (NAR headline, TheStreet, RISMedia). It is the second straight record low. |
| Freddie Mac 30-year rate 7.28% on Nov. 19, up from 7.11% | Partly supported. The 7.11% figure for Nov. 12 matches. I could not find the Nov. 19 figure, so 7.28% is unconfirmed. |
| Neighbors Bank (Nov. 4): buying cheaper than renting in 23 of 50 metros; Birmingham $625, Pittsburgh $609, Memphis $597, Washington $11; survey of 1,166 buyers (40%, 24%, 17%, 9%) | Not checkable here. I did not locate the study. |

## Misleading context
- The headline is technically true but the increase is tiny: $1, or 0.1%. That is effectively flat. Studio rents fell for a 39th straight month.
- The data cover only the 50 largest metros and studio, one- and two-bedroom units. This is asking rent, not rents paid.
- Other indexes differ: Yardi Matrix shows +1.4% and Apartment List +0.1%. Rent "direction" depends on the source.
- The mortgage and pending-sales items are largely unrelated to the rent story.
- The renting-versus-buying study uses July data and a 5% down payment, and "starter home" is not defined in the article. The article also sets a 7%+ mortgage rate beside a claim that buying is cheaper than renting in 23 of 50 metros, without noting the tension.

## Rating: 1 Pinocchio (mostly accurate)
The core numbers match the primary release. The weaknesses are a framing that overstates a $1 change and one unconfirmed rate figure.
