## Overall grade: 1 Pinocchio (mostly accurate)

The checkable figures hold up, and the article adds its own context, including the Chetty caveat and the business-applications caveat. Two things keep this from a clean 0:

- Bradley's framing implies that more growth would restore mobility. Chetty's own paper says distribution matters more.
- The record application count is a weak proxy for new employer firms. The headline number overstates real business formation, as the article itself acknowledges.

The 5.68M figure and the verbatim quotes are unverified.
