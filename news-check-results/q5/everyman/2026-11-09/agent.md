# Fact-check: CBS "Face the Nation" transcript, Nov. 8, 2026

Limits: the fetch tool would not return the transcript verbatim (it returned a summary). Claims below come from that summary, so I could not check exact quotes word for word. I made a limited number of searches.

## Verified (checked against outside reporting)
- Democrats won Congress in the Nov. 3 midterms. Early counts showed Democrats winning both chambers (Senate about 54-44, House about 228-139 early on). Not yet certified. The summary I got said the House majority was "not veto-proof"; I did not check that.
- Trump announced Nov. 6 that Susie Wiles is out as chief of staff and Stephen Miller replaces her. Confirmed by ABC, Inquirer, Deseret, and others.
- A bipartisan housing bill became law without Trump's signature (July 11, 2026), after he withheld it in protest over the SAVE America Act. Confirmed.
- Nancy Lacore was removed as Chief of Navy Reserve on Aug. 22, 2025, with no reason given. She is running for Congress in South Carolina. Confirmed (USNI, Fox, Axios).
- Calley Means left the White House/HHS adviser role in October 2026 (Oct. 22 reports). The "former adviser" description holds.

## Not checkable here
- Means's allegations against Wiles, Richard Walters, Walker Barrett, James Blair, and Tony Fabrizio, and his claim about vape policy and FDA nominee Heidi Overton. These are his accusations. I found no outside coverage, and the transcript's own summary says the named people disputed them.
- His statement that he wanted Wiles removed, and that Trump did not fully know what was happening. These are his opinions and intent.
- Walters's reported resignation. I found no independent confirmation.
- Warren's and Hill's statements: Warren calling the Iran war illegal, Hill on Hegseth impeachment and the Scalise-Jordan race, and Lacore's stated priorities. These are positions, not facts. The Iran war's status was not independently checked.
- Overton's FDA nomination status. Not found.
- The preview of Douthat's 60 Minutes interview with Trump. Not checked.

## Not found to be false
Nothing I checked was contradicted.

## Rating
1 Pinocchio (mostly accurate). This is an interview transcript, so the main question is whether the guests' claims hold up. The ones I could check are accurate. The most serious allegations (Means on the White House staff) are one-sided and unverified, though the transcript shows the targets' denials. Provisional: I did not read the full transcript text.

## Potentially misleading
- Means's claims are allegations from a departed insider with an obvious stake. A viewer could take them as established fact.
- The election tallies were still preliminary when the show aired.
- Hill's and Warren's framings of the election and the Iran war are partisan and were not independently tested.
