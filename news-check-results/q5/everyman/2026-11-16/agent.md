## Overall rating: 1 Pinocchio (leaning 2)

Most checkable headline figures (WICHE, Hanover, NACUBO, Whitman, BLS 2024) hold up. Two numbers are wrong or unsupported: the Common App institution count (mixed from two years) and the Georgia State 22% to 8% result. The yield-rate trend is doubtful. About half the claims could not be reached.

## Misleading or missing context

- Summer melt and yield are different measures. The piece uses falling yield and rising melt as one story; falling yield mostly reflects more applications per student, not necessarily less interest.
- The Georgia State result is a university-reported figure, the best case for the intervention, and it is not independently evaluated. Rising melt at one school (Whittier) is an anecdote, not a national trend.
- Hanover's 66% is "interest" in a vendor survey of 1,000, not enrollment behaviour. Enrollment rose 0.6% in the same article, which cuts against the "falling interest" framing.
- Survey and institutional sources are vendors selling enrollment services (Human Capital Education, Hanover), a conflict of interest the piece does not note.
