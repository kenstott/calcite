# Fact-check: "U.S. unemployment remained at a one-year low of 4.1% through August 2026, with nonfarm payrolls up 162,000 in August 2026"

## Verdict: TRUE

## Evidence

Per the BLS Employment Situation Summary for August 2026 (released 2026-09-04):

- **Unemployment rate:** 4.1% in August 2026, unchanged from July 2026. ([BLS Employment Situation Summary](https://www.bls.gov/news.release/empsit.nr0.htm), [full PDF](https://www.bls.gov/news.release/pdf/empsit.pdf))
- **Nonfarm payrolls:** +162,000 in August 2026, well above the prior-12-month average monthly gain of 31,000 and well above the ~53,000 consensus estimate. ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/04/jobs-report-august-2026.html))

### "One-year low" check

Trailing 12-month unemployment-rate trend (BLS monthly releases):
- Aug 2025: 4.3%
- Feb–May 2026: 4.3–4.4%
- Jun 2026: 4.2%
- Jul 2026: 4.1% ([BLS archive, July 2026 release](https://www.bls.gov/news.release/archives/empsit_08072026.htm))
- Aug 2026: 4.1% (unchanged)

4.1% is the lowest monthly rate in the trailing 12 months, first reached in July 2026 and held steady into August 2026 — confirming the "remained at a one-year low ... through August 2026" framing.

## Conclusion

Both parts of the claim check out against the official BLS release: unemployment held at 4.1% (a genuine one-year low first reached in July 2026 and sustained through August), and nonfarm payrolls rose 162,000 in August 2026.
