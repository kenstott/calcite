# Fact-check: FoodNavigator, "US dairy exports set to top $10bn in 2026 as global demand drives growth" (2026-11-16)

Method: WebFetch of the article (summary only, no verbatim text), then WebSearch/WebFetch of AgProud, Cheese Reporter and USDEC blog snippets. I could not open the USDEC release itself, so some checks rest on secondary reports of USDEC data. No charts were produced.

## Rating: 1 Pinocchio (out of 4)
Most numbers match USDEC and USDA reporting. The weakest claim is the "$1bn month". It is headline-grade and depends on which data source is used.

## Misleading or missing context
- The $1bn-month claim is stated flatly. USDA FAS data shows $992m, so it is a USDEC-dataset milestone, not a settled fact.
- The $10bn headline is a trade-group forecast, not an outcome. Value growth of 14% YTD against volume growth of about 7% means prices explain half the gain. Volume growth is the more meaningful measure of "global demand".
- "Rerouted via Saudi Arabia" is a causal story from USDEC. The data I found are mixed: Saudi volume was down in Q3 and direct Bahrain shipments resumed.
- The article leans on a single source (USDEC, an industry promotion body) with no independent or critical voice.
