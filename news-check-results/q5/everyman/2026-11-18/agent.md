# Fact-check: Public Radio Tulsa, "National report's findings show 61% of Oklahoma's rural hospitals at risk of closing" (2026-11-16, Angel Ford)

Limits: I could not open the CHQPR report itself. I relied on secondary coverage (Becker's state table, Oklahoma Voice / States Newsroom, Nov 12 2026, via search summaries). Search summaries, not full pages, so sourcing is thinner than a full fact-check.

## Claims
1. A CHQPR national report finds 61% of Oklahoma rural hospitals at risk. SUPPORTED (Becker's table: 44 at risk = 61%; 17 = 24% immediate risk).
2. Nearly one-third of rural hospitals nationwide at risk. PLAUSIBLE, not independently verified (Becker's lists roughly 700 at risk; I did not confirm the national denominator).
3. Oklahoma has 44 at risk, 17 on the brink. SUPPORTED by Oklahoma Voice (44 "at risk", 17 "immediate risk"). Older editions gave 45/18; the article uses the current one. Wording issue: the 61% is "at risk" and 17 are a subset at "immediate" risk; the article's "44 of them, and 17 of those" reads correctly only if 44 is the whole at-risk group.
4. Three causes (costs exceed payments, insufficient outside funding, low reserves). Consistent with CHQPR's methodology as I understand it; not verified against the report.
5. OHA head says the assessment ignores supplemental Medicaid and tax payments. SUPPORTED. Oklahoma Voice quotes OHA CEO Rich Rasmussen saying this (the SHOPP program is the cited example).
6. Slow, below-cost health plan payments and Medicaid cuts contribute. SUPPORTED as attributed context (Rasmussen cites Medicare Advantage denials/delays, low Medicaid rates, federal cuts).
7. Seven rural Oklahoma hospitals closed since 2015. NOT CHECKABLE HERE exactly. Regional reporting repeats 7; Sheps Center shows 8 closures/downsizings since 2005 and 8 for 2010-2019, so definitions matter (closure vs conversion).

## Misleading-context flags
- "At risk" is a model-based financial-vulnerability measure, not a prediction of closure; 61% does not mean 61% will close. The article does include the OHA rebuttal, which helps.
- The headline's 61% applies to hospitals CHQPR deems at risk; only 24% are at immediate risk, and the headline omits that.
- CHQPR is an advocacy group with a payment-reform agenda; the article does not say so.
- The article is attributed secondhand to Oklahoma Voice.

## Grade
1 Pinocchio (mostly accurate; figures check out, minor omissions of context on "at risk" meaning and the 7-closures count).
