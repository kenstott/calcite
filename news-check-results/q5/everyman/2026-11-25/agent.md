# Fact-check: Farm Policy News, "Thailand trade deal will include $2.6 billion in US ag purchases, pork market access, Trump says" (Nov 2026)

Limits: WebFetch returned only a summary of the article, not verbatim text. AgWeb returned 403. I could not check exact quote wording. The USDA/FAS 2025 and 2021 farm export figures were not independently retrieved.

| # | Claim | Verdict | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Trump says the deal includes about $2.6B in US ag purchases | Accurate as attribution; the figure is not new | The USTR Oct 2025 framework fact sheet gives "an estimated $2.6 billion per year" in feed corn, soybean meal and DDGS. The 2026 claim repeats it. |

## Rating: 1 Pinocchio (mostly accurate, minor context omissions)

The article attributes its claims accurately and flags that the deal is unsigned.

## Potentially misleading
- The $2.6B figure is presented as news, but it dates to Oct 2025. The sources do not say whether the 2026 package replaces it or repeats it.
- The pledge is a Trump claim about a deal with no signed text, no timeline and no enforcement. "Will include" in the headline overstates certainty. The article body hedges.
- The $2.6B is an annual estimate. Headlines can read it as one-time.
- The pledge is roughly double current purchases, but nothing guarantees volumes or prices (as AgroLatam cautions).
