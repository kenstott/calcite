## Overall rating

1 Pinocchio (mostly accurate), provisional. Nothing found is false. The weakness is that I could not confirm the DOJ quote, the Dec 9 deadline, or the Nov 13 deadline, and the $115B figure is not directly matched to a primary source.

## Misleading-risk flags

- The $115B "disbursed" figure may be mixed with certified or accepted amounts. The $166B figure is before interest, and the refunds include interest, so the percent repaid depends on the measure used.
- "Closer to receiving $166B" is a framing. The relief motion is unresolved, the government has appealed, and a CASA-type objection to non-party relief could block it.
- A class certification is not a refund order.
