# Fact-check: OPB, "Fewer fires, record damage: Oregon's 2026 wildfire season by the numbers" (Oct 24, 2026)

## Verdict: 1 Pinocchio (provisional, "some shading"), with two items that could move it to 2

I fetched the OPB piece (Casey Frizzell, Oct 24, updated Oct 26, 2026). The fetch tool returned a summary plus a few exact quotes, not the full text. I could not open the primary agency data (ODF, NWCC, ODEM, NIFC) directly. Everything below is checked against OPB's own earlier coverage and other outlets' reporting, not agency datasets. Confidence is therefore moderate at best.

### Material issues

1. **Acreage totals are not reconciled.** ODF's "more than 2.1M" sits below NWCC's roughly 2.34M, which was reported in August. The 2024 baseline also varies between 1.8M and 2.08M depending on the source. The headline ("record") holds under every set of numbers I found, but the size of the margin ("13%") depends on which dataset is used, and the piece does not say.
2. **The cost figure is stale.** A mid-August number ($235M) appears in an October article, while the more current $350M projection is only an unconfirmed projection.
3. **The "Legislature approved $123M" wording is slightly loose.** The approval came from the Joint Emergency Board, not the full legislature.

### What would change the grade

If ODF's actual 2024 and 2026 totals do not yield about 13%, or if the firefighter deaths or home counts fail verification, the grade moves to 2 Pinocchios. If the ODF and NWCC data confirm the numbers, it drops to 0–1.
