# Fact-check: CCF "Millions of low-income children are losing both Medicaid/CHIP coverage and SNAP benefits" (2026-11-09)

**Rating: 1 Pinocchio.** Core numbers consistent and corroborated in direction; headline overreaches.

## Claims
- **2.56M fewer child Medicaid/CHIP, all states, Jan 2025-Aug 2026.** Answer: not checkable here (interactive tracker, no text figures); KFF confirms child enrollment down in 49 states + DC (Jul 2025-Jul 2026). Sources: CCF tracker, KFF. Confidence: low-medium.
- **26 states: child SNAP -1.6M (-15.7%), Medicaid/CHIP -1.5M (-6.3%), Jan 2025-May 2026; 2.5x.** Arithmetic checks (15.7/6.3 = 2.49; implied bases 10.2M and 23.8M children). CBPP source returned HTTP 403. CBPP via PBS: 25 states, ~1.2M fewer children since Jul 2025. Confidence: medium.
- **Children about 42% of SNAP losses.** PBS/CBPP: 43% (25 states); state range 28-77%. Roughly supported. Confidence: medium.
- **Arizona lost half its SNAP children.** CBPP via Newsweek: 49%, 182,058. Supported. Confidence: medium.
- **H.R.1 timing (SNAP admin 75% Oct 2026, cost share 5-15% Oct 2027, Medicaid work reporting Jan 2027, immigrant limits Oct 2026, 12-month continuous eligibility since Jan 2024).** Consistent with law and CMS SHO 23-004; not re-fetched. Confidence: medium.
- **"Largest cuts in history."** Characterization, not checkable.

## Misleading despite true facts
- Headline implies the same children lose both; footnote says state totals, not matched children.
- Window begins Jan 2025, before H.R.1 (July 2025); causal link to the law is asserted, not shown.
- 26-state subset, not national; 2.56M (all states) and 6.3% (26 states) are different bases.

## Sources
CCF article and tracker; CBPP tracker (403); PBS NewsHour; KFF tracker; Newsweek; CMS SHO 23-004.
