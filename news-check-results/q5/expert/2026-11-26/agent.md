# Fact-check: ONDCP "overdose deaths decrease for 18th consecutive month" (16 Nov 2026)

**Answer**: Every figure is accurate; framing is misleading. Rating: 2 Pinocchios.

**Key values** (CDC VSRR predicted, US, 12-month-ending): Jun 2026 65,724 (reported 64,433); peak Aug 2023 112,418; -41.5%. Dec 2024 81,313 (-27.7% from peak before inauguration); Dec 2024 to Jun 2026 -19.2%. About 67% of the peak-to-now drop came before Jan 2025. Streak is 34 consecutive monthly declines (since Sep 2023), not 18. Monthly pace slowing (-2,637 Sep 2024 vs -642 to -1,124 recent months).
Drug classes recomputed Jun 2025 to Jun 2026: synthetic opioids -18.7, natural/semi-synthetic -5.4, heroin -34.8, psychostimulants -14.1, cocaine -6.3 (all match). Lowest since Dec 2016: true (Dec 2016 64,557; Jan 2017 66,194).
Quote by Sara Carter is verbatim on the page; she was confirmed ONDCP Director 6 Jan 2026 (52-48). Her causal claims (border, cartels, prevention) are opinion and untestable here; "thousands saved each year" is counterfactual, not checkable.
