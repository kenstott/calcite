Fact-check: US rents post first annual increase

Findings: 1 Pinocchio, facts hold but the headline is flat

Almost every figure checks out against primary sources, but the rent "increase" is $1 (+0.1%) on a $1,692 median.

  • Rent, unit-size, peak and construction figures: verified (Realtor.com October 2026 Rent Report).
  • NAR pending sales -4.7% m/m, -16.7% y/y verified; index level 63 and record low from secondary sources.
  • Freddie Mac 7.28% on Nov 19: not checkable here (Nov 12 7.11% confirmed).
  • Neighbors Bank 23 of 50 metros, Birmingham $625, Pittsburgh $609, Memphis $597: verified; remaining details not checked.
  • Misleading: flat change framed as a turn; studios still falling; Realtor.com finds renting a starter home cheaper in every listed metro; lender-sourced buy case; 50 largest metros asking rents only.
