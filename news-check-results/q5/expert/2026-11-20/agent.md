# Fact-check: Epoch Times, "US rents post first annual increase in more than three years"

## Summary
Grade: 1 Pinocchio. Nearly every checkable figure matches primary sources. The main weakness is framing: the "first annual increase" is +$1 (+0.1%) on a $1,692 median, which is flat within noise.

## Claims
- Median rent +0.1% YoY to $1,692, first rise since June 2023: VERIFIED (Realtor.com Oct 2026 Rent Report, Nov 18; the +$1 change is confirmed).
- 2BR +0.3% to $1,889, 1BR +0.1% to $1,566, studio -1.2% to $1,411: VERIFIED (Realtor.com via search summary).
- 4.6% ($82) below peak, 15% ($221) above pre-pandemic: VERIFIED. Report dates peak to August 2022 and baseline to Oct 2019; the article says "summer 2022".
- Completions -26% to 330k, under construction -2.7% to 674k, starts -9.2% to 403k; "unwinds" quote; no rebound expected in 12 months; elevated borrowing costs may keep households renting: VERIFIED (Realtor.com release).
- Freddie Mac 30-year 7.28% on Nov 19, up from 7.11%: PARTIAL. 7.11% for Nov 12 confirmed; the Nov 19 value could not be accessed. Not checkable here.
- NAR PHSI 63, -4.7% m/m, -16.7% y/y, lowest since 2001: VERIFIED for the changes (NAR, Nov 18); index level 63.0 and record low from secondary sources (TheStreet, HousingWire).
- Neighbors Bank: buying cheaper in 23 of 50 metros; Birmingham $625, Pittsburgh $609, Memphis $597: VERIFIED (Neighbors Bank page, July 2026 data). Other details (Chicago/Cleveland/St. Louis >$300, Washington $11, survey 40/24/17/9%) not individually checked.

## Misleading context
- A $1 rise is statistically flat; the headline implies a turn. Studios still fell, 39th straight monthly decline.
- Realtor.com's own separate analysis finds renting a starter home cheaper than buying in every metro on its list, which conflicts with the Neighbors Bank result. The article omits this.
- Neighbors Bank is a mortgage lender with an interest in the buy case; its assumption was a 5% down payment.
- Mixing in sales and mortgage items is tangential to rents.
- Realtor.com covers the 50 largest metros, not the whole US, and tracks asking rents.

## Confidence
High on rent and construction; medium on PHSI; low on the Nov 19 mortgage rate.
