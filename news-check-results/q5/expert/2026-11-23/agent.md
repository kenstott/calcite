# Fact-check: FoodNavigator, US dairy exports to top $10bn (2026-11-16)
**Rating: 1 Pinocchio.** 13 claims: 10 verified against USDEC, 2 verified with nuance, 1 conflicting.
- Verified: Sept volume +10%, YTD +7%, YTD value $8.18bn, Saudi cheese +91%, SE Asia low-protein whey +47% in Q3, Bahrain direct shipments resumed in Q3, milk powder fall May-Aug.
- Conflict: first $1bn month. USDEC says yes; DairyHerd citing USDA GATS says $992.3M. Unresolved.
- $10bn is a forecast; YTD is $8.18bn.
- Misleading by omission: value +14% vs volume +7%, so about half of value growth is price.
- Not checkable: high-protein whey at historic price highs; China as largest buyer rank.
- Limitation: all evidence derives from USDEC; GATS not reached; article read via summary.
Sources: blog.usdec.org (Nov 5 and Nov 12 2026 posts), agproud.com/articles/64408, dairyherd.com. Confidence: high on USDEC-matched figures, medium on the rest.
