Fact-check: FoodNavigator's US dairy export piece earns 1 Pinocchio

Summary of findings: accurate, with one unresolved dollar threshold

Of 13 claims, 10 match USDEC's own releases, 2 verify with nuance and 1 conflicts between sources. Rating: 1 Pinocchio on the 0-4 scale, because the piece relays USDEC faithfully but states the $1bn month more firmly than the data supports.

  • $1bn month: USDEC says September exceeded $1bn. DairyHerd, citing USDA GATS, reports $992.3M. I could not reach GATS directly, so this is unresolved.
  • $10bn headline: It is a forecast. YTD value is $8.18bn (9 months), which implies about $1.8bn needed over three months, in line with the $1bn September pace.
  • Misleading by omission: Value is up about 14% YTD but volume only about 7%, so roughly half of the dollar growth is price, not demand. The headline's "global demand drives growth" does not say so.
  • Not checkable: Whether high-protein whey prices are at historic highs (no price series fetched), and China's rank as largest buyer (USDEC calls it an anchor).
  • All figures trace to USDEC, so every claim shares one source. No independent primary dataset (USDA GATS, Census) was reachable.
