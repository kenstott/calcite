# Fact-check: PRT "61% of Oklahoma's rural hospitals at risk of closing" (2026-11-16)

**Grade: 1 Pinocchio.** Figures match the primary source; causal framing is shaded.

## Step 1: Headline statistics
**Answer:** All verified against CHQPR "Rural Hospitals at Risk of Closing" (Oct 2026).
**Key Values:** OK 72 open rural inpatient hospitals; 40 losing money (56%); 44 at risk (61%); 17 immediate risk (24%); 7 closures since 2015; 7 REH conversions. US: 2,246 open; 698 at risk (31%); 260 immediate (12%). Cross-check: CHQPR hospital list shows 79 open OK rural hospitals incl. 7 REH; 79-7 = 72.
**Sources:** https://chqpr.org/downloads/Rural_Hospitals_at_Risk_of_Closing.pdf ; https://ruralhospitals.chqpr.org/Data1.html
**Confidence:** High (primary source + denominator reconstruction). The risk model itself is not reproducible.

## Step 2: Closures
**Answer:** "Seven since 2015" is correct (Epic 2016, Frederick 2016, Latimer 2018, Sayre 2018, El Reno 2019, Claremore Indian 2025, Valley Community 2025) but omits 7 REH conversions that ended inpatient care.
**Sources:** CHQPR table and hospital list; Sheps Center closures list.
**Confidence:** High on count.

## Step 3: Quote and causes
**Answer:** The OHA head's statement via Oklahoma Voice is not checkable here (403). KOKH 2026-08-11 has OHA CEO Rasmussen on supplemental payments and Medicaid cuts (2027), consistent in gist. CHQPR methodology says supplemental payments are inconsistently reported (Medicaid margin vs non-patient revenue) and total margin counts tax/grants, so "fails to consider" is partly contestable. CHQPR names private-plan underpayment as primary cause and does not cite Medicaid cuts; those cuts are prospective, so "alongside Medicaid cuts" is unsupported for closures to date. "On the brink" overstates the "immediate risk" modeled tier.
**Sources:** KOKH https://okcfox.com/news/local/report-shows-45-rural-hospitals-at-risk-of-closing-in-oklahoma ; CHQPR methodology.
**Confidence:** Medium on substance, low on quote wording.

## Caveats
July 2026 CHQPR edition gave 45/18 (KOKH); Oct edition 44/17 matches the article. Risk is modeled, not a forecast; CHQPR is an advocacy group. Not a causal test.
