Fact-check: the numbers are right; the causal story is shaded (1 Pinocchio)

Summary of findings: every headline figure matches the source, but the cause framing is loose

Overall grade: 1 Pinocchio. Public Radio Tulsa's figures reproduce the Center for Healthcare Quality and Payment Reform (CHQPR) October 2026 table exactly: Oklahoma has 44 of 72 open rural inpatient hospitals (61.1%) at risk of closing, 17 (23.6%, shown as 24%) at immediate risk, 7 closures since 2015, and the nation has 698 of 2,246 (31%) at risk. The problems are framing, not arithmetic.

  • Cause shading. The last paragraph says slow, below-cost plan payments contribute to closures "alongside Medicaid cuts." CHQPR names private-plan underpayment as the primary cause and does not cite Medicaid cuts. The Medicaid cuts the Oklahoma Hospital Association (OHA) chief describes take effect from 2027 (per KOKH, Aug 2026), so they cannot explain the 7 closures since 2015.
  • "On the brink of shutting down" restates CHQPR's "immediate risk" tier, a modeled score from Medicare cost-report margins and reserves, as near-certainty. It is not a forecast, and CHQPR is a payment-reform advocacy group; the piece does not say either.
  • Omission. Oklahoma also had 7 Rural Emergency Hospital (REH) conversions that ended inpatient care; the piece's "seven closures" leaves that out, so access loss is understated.
  • Not checkable here: the OHA head's exact statement as reported by Oklahoma Voice. Oklahoma Voice and its republisher returned HTTP 403 to automated fetches. The OHA CEO is on record saying supplemental Medicaid payments are how 90+ Oklahoma hospitals offset losses (KOKH, 11 Aug 2026), which fits the gist. CHQPR's methodology says these payments are inconsistently reported on cost reports, sometimes inside Medicaid margin, sometimes inside non-patient revenue, and its total margin counts tax and grant revenue. So "fails to consider" is partly contestable.
  • What would change the grade: if Oklahoma Voice shows the OHA head's criticism was materially different from the paraphrase, the grade could move. Also, CHQPR's July 2026 edition gave 45 at risk and 18 immediate (KOKH), so the figures are version-specific; the Oct 2026 edition (44/17) is the one the article matches.
