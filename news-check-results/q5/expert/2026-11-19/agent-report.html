Fact-check: CRFB "Trump CEA Projections Tracker" (Nov 11, 2026)

Rating: 1 Pinocchio. The headline numbers check out; the framing is advocacy and the CEA baseline is hand-reconstructed.

FY2026 actuals match published data: deficit about $1.99T (6.2% of GDP) per Treasury's final statement, receipts $5.40T, debt held by the public 100% of GDP per CBO. Each is worse than the CEA path CRFB reconstructs ($1.7T, 5.2%, 97%). The statement "none of CEA's projections have materialized" is fair for GDP, deficits and primary deficits. It is a stretch for revenue (about $0.1T below) and debt (3 points above).

  • Materially misleading risk: the CEA figures are CRFB's hand measurements of chart-book graphics (CRFB footnote 1). The chart book shows several scenarios (conventional, 2.5% low growth, 2.8% average model-implied), and the piece does not say which one it reads. 4% growth through 2028 then 2% is consistent with the 2.8% average line ((4x4+2x6)/10), which I could infer but not confirm.
  • CRFB itself notes outcomes may differ because of tariff rulings and non-OBBBA factors; the SCOTUS tariff ruling plus ~$145B refunds depressed FY2026 revenue. So the gap is not a clean test of the CEA's OBBBA estimates.
  • Typos: "5.2% in 2026 and 2026"; footnote 2 cites the "FY 2017 Budget" where FY 2027 is meant.
  • Not checkable here: CEA's underlying data (never released), the CRFB Google Sheet values for individual years, OMB FY2027 Budget 2034 values, the 70% tariff replacement estimate, and the other dynamic scores ($3.5-4.0T).
