# Fact-check: Inside Climate News, Maine storms and power bills (2026-11-25)

**Overall: 1 Pinocchio (provisional).** Mostly accurate. Several headline numbers could not be verified.

## Confirmed
- **Answer:** CMP asked for about $18/month more (distribution, average household). PUC unanimously denied the temporary increase (about $7/month) on 2026-10-06. Full $189M case decision expected spring 2027, consistent with the article's April 2027.
- **Sources:** Maine Public, Bangor Daily News, Press Herald (2026-10-06/07).
- **Confidence:** high.

## Price level
- **Key values:** Maine residential 31.29 c/kWh vs US 18.17, Sep 2026. A year earlier: Maine 27.88, US 18.05.
- **Source:** EIA Electric Power Monthly Table 5.6.A (2026 preliminary).
- **Confidence:** high for level, none for the 64% vs 38% 2019-2025 claim.

## Not checkable here
- 64% vs 38% growth, since the EIA annual table fetch returned empty.
- CMP $600M+ storm costs 2021-2025, $179 vs $68 per customer, the $3M annual cap, and the outage ranking.
- Quotes from Berry, Sanborn and the ROE expert, because the article was read only via a summary and not verbatim.
- ROE dispute is real (requested 9.8% vs 9.35% current).
- **Confidence:** low.

## Misleading flags
- The CMP vs Versant per-customer figures are a single-year (2021) comparison, and storm severity differs between territories.
- The $18 distribution request should not be conflated with the $7 temporary request.
