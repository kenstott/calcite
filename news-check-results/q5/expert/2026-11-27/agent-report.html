Fact check: Patently-O, "Issue Fee to Grant: Record Weekly Patent Issues Are a USPTO Catch-Up" (Nov 24, 2026)

The piece holds up: 0 Pinocchios, with one count discrepancy and one claim that cannot be checked

Official Gazette lists show 9,913 utility patents on Nov 3, 2026 versus the article's 9,828 (0.9% apart), the highest week of 2026, and Nov 10, 17 and 24 (8,678, 8,840, 7,948) are the next three. The prior-record figure also checks out approximately (7,946 by number range vs 7,837 claimed).

  • The article is paywalled; only the opening paragraphs were readable. Claims in the hidden portion are not assessed.
  • Counts are my own tally of contiguous utility numbers on each OG list, which may include numbers the author's source excludes; the consistent ~1% overshoot in both 2023 and 2026 suggests a counting-basis difference, not an error.
  • Not checkable: the 49-day fee-to-grant wait (needs per-application payment dates) and the 1976 record (I tested only 2023-2026).
  • Not misleading: the "catch-up" framing matches the author's own October data (paid-but-unissued queue ~51k vs ~28k norm). The record week is a one-off backlog effect, not higher examination output, and the article says so.
