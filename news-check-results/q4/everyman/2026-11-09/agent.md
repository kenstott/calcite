## Grade: 1 Pinocchio (minor issues)
The facts about the election, the pledge, the governor's limited powers and SB 6 hold up. The main weakness is the headline "about 40%" price rise, given as an expert's figure without an independent number. Comparable data suggest 20-30%.

## Potentially misleading framing
- The headline implies the governor can lower bills. The article itself admits the limits, but the freeze is only a campaign pledge. Freezing residential rates while delivery costs rise could shift costs elsewhere or create deferred recovery.
- Abbott had already directed the PUC to make data centers pay for their own infrastructure, so the candidates differ more in method than in goal. Hinojosa also voted for data center tax incentives as a House member. Neither point appears in the summary of the piece.
- The piece draws mainly on a single expert (Krishnamoorti).
