# Fact-check: CBS/AP, "Fewer foreign students studying at U.S. universities due to Trump administration policies, study shows" (Nov 16, 2026)

Method: WebFetch returned only a paraphrase of the article (AP copyright), so quotes are checked as summarized. The IIE Fall 2026 Snapshot itself and the NAFSA/JBI PDF were not opened. Most checks rest on search-result summaries and secondary outlets, not primary documents.

## Overall rating: 1 Pinocchio (mostly accurate)
The headline statistics match the IIE snapshot.

Issues:
- The causal headline ("due to Trump administration policies") overstates what a survey of institutions shows. The same survey cites global competition and cost, and the article itself notes this.
- The $70,000 OPT fee is simplified: it applies to a student's first OPT recommendation and is charged to schools.
- The "$2.4 billion" figure does not match NAFSA/JBI's published estimate of about $3.4B. Treat it as probably misattributed until checked.
- The 3% undergraduate figure was not found in any source.
- Outlets differ on the headline: WGBH reports a 3% fall in the separate Open Doors total for 2025/26. A reader could confuse the two measures; the snapshot compares fall 2026 with fall 2025.
- IIE Snapshot figures come from a self-selected survey of institutions, not full counts.
