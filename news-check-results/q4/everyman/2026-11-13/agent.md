# Fact-check: "US power demand to beat record highs in 2026 and 2027 as AI use surges, EIA says" (Reuters via BOE Report, 2026-11-10)

Caveat: WebFetch returned only a summary of the article, not verbatim text, so the claim list below comes from that summary. Wording-level issues (exact quotes, framing) could not be checked.

Primary source: EIA Short-Term Energy Outlook (STEO), released 2026-11-10. I read Tables 7a (electricity) and 5a (natural gas) from the EIA PDFs and the STEO web pages.

## Claims

| # | Claim | Verdict | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | EIA says US electricity consumption will hit record highs in 2026 and 2027 | True | STEO Table 7a total consumption 4,216 (2025), 4,306 (2026), 4,371 (2027) billion kWh, so each year is higher. |
| 2 | 2025 record 4,216 bn kWh; 2026 4,306; 2027 4,370 | True | Table 7a. 2027 is 4,371 in the table, a 1 bn kWh rounding gap. |
| 3 | 2026 would be the third straight record year | Mostly checkable, consistent | 2026 exceeds 2025. Earlier Reuters coverage called 2025 the second straight record, so 2026 would be the third. I did not pull the 2023-24 history. |
| 4 | 2026 sales: residential 1,546; commercial 1,557; industrial 1,061 bn kWh | True | Table 7a, exact match. |
| 5 | Coal share 17% (2025) to 15% (2026 and 2027) | Partly verified | EIA text gives 17% in 2025 and 15% in 2027. The 2026 figure was not stated in the page text I could read. |
| 6 | Natural gas about 40% in 2026, 39% in 2027 | Partly verified | EIA text says "close to 40%" for both years. The 39% vs 40% split could not be confirmed. |
| 7 | Renewables 24% to 25% (2026) and 28% (2027); nuclear 18% | Not checkable here | EIA page gives only solar and wind shares. Nuclear about 18% is confirmed. Totals for renewables were not accessible. |
| 8 | 2026 gas use: residential 12.2, commercial 9.4, industrial 23.8, power 37.4 bcfd | True | Table 5a, exact match for all four. |
| 9 | Driver is AI data centers and electrification | Plausible, not directly confirmed | EIA text mentions commercial and industrial demand growth. No numbers attributing growth to AI were accessible. |

Attribution: the piece is credited to Reuters (Scott DiSavino) and republished by BOE Report. The article's figures match the STEO release.

## Materially misleading or confusing points

- The STEO web page is internally inconsistent: it says 2026 power-sector generation is about 4,390 bn kWh, while the generation table shows 4,539 (total utility-scale supply 4,548). Generation and consumption are different measures (4,306 consumption vs about 4,387 power-sector generation in Table 7a). Readers who mix them up may see conflicting "record" figures.
- Headline "AI use surges" implies AI is the main cause. EIA's own sector numbers show residential sales are flat in 2027 (1,539 vs 1,546) and growth comes from commercial and industrial. That is consistent with data centers but does not isolate AI.
- These are forecasts. Only 2025 is a measured figure, and the headline is stated with more certainty than a forecast warrants.
- Growth is modest: 2026 is about 2.1% above 2025 and 2027 is about 1.5% above 2026.

## Rating

Pinocchio scale 0-4: **0 Pinocchios (The Geppetto Checkmark), with a minor caveat.** Every numeric claim I could access matches EIA's STEO exactly or within rounding. Remaining items are unchecked share percentages and causal framing, not errors.
