Method: WebFetch returned only a condensed summary of the article, not verbatim text. Verification used web search (limited depth). No primary DOE file was opened.

## Rating

2 Pinocchios provisional is too harsh. Given what could be verified, the headline figures and projection are supported and the piece looks sound. Rating: **1 Pinocchio** (mostly accurate), with the caveat that coverage of verification was thin.

## Misleading-context flags

- The 766,900 and 863,100 totals mix scopes (K-12 vs. including preschool; district vs. charter). Other published figures (about 884,000 district) differ, so readers can be confused.
- The 130,000 projection is baseline-dependent (128,000 per Comptroller; 153,000 from 2024-25).
- Class-size gains are attributed to state law, with enrollment decline as a helper. The article states this fairly.

## Limits

Primary sources (DOE enrollment release, SCA report, PEP records) were not opened. Most items are "not checkable here" or only secondary-supported.
