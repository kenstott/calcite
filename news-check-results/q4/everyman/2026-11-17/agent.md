# Fact-check: Dorchester Reporter, "BPS enrollment continues to decline, nearly 2,000 fewer students this year" (Nov 12, 2026, Seth Daniel)

Method note: WebFetch returned only a summary of the article (not verbatim), so claims below are as summarized; exact quotes could not be checked word for word. Tools: web search/fetch only.

## Overall rating: 1 Pinocchio (mostly accurate)
Everything I could check matches independent reporting (baseline, arithmetic, school counts, causes). The core 44,846 count and the grade-band and kindergarten numbers could not be reached, so they are unverified, not wrong.

## Possibly misleading / context
- "Birth rates main driver" understates immigration: the district itself said the earlier drop was "mainly driven by a decrease in international immigration," and the 2025-26 loss was tied heavily to fewer newcomers. The article's own account (immigration offset then reversed) is partly in tension with its headline cause.
- Counts differ by definition (with or without in-district charters, BPS vs. DESE Oct 1); readers can see apparent contradictions (44,416 vs 46,514 vs 46,824). The article should state the definition.
- Long-run context: enrollment is about 9,000 below a decade ago (WBUR), larger than the one-year drop suggests.
- Closure framing: the closures target specific small or underused schools (Charlestown High, Greater Egleston with 76 students, East Boston Early Education Center); the decline is one factor, not the only one.
