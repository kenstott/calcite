## Materially misleading or notable

- The sourcing date and size of the reserve action (claim 13) appear wrong by about two weeks and 15,000 barrels, and it omits that this is an exchange.
- Prices are shown as a year-on-year spike, but the latest EIA week is a decline of about 3.6% nationally from the week before. The headline "spike" framing is true over a year but omits current easing.
- Household spending: the 21% is a seasonal national estimate, driven by a 34% price rise partly offset by lower use. It is not Maine-specific, where the price rise is larger.
- The EIA residential price series excludes taxes; the article's dollars-per-gallon framing is fine but the 100-gallon "about $600" is rounded up.

## Rating

2 Pinocchios (Mixed). The core numbers (prices, EIA expenditures) check out against EIA, but a government action is misdated and mis-sized, and several quotes and the percentage year-on-year figures could not be verified. If the 300,000-barrel/Nov 4 claim reflects the article's real text, that is the main error.
