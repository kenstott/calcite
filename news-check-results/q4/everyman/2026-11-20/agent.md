## Misleading framing
- "Ranks last" is a headline simplification. The three states are the bottom three of 52 jurisdictions. "Gulf South" here means AL, LA and MS only; Texas and Florida are not covered.
- Mississippi and Louisiana improved in 2025, though they remain worst. Alabama's rise is the only worsening.
- The Medicaid-cuts link is a forecast. The report documents Medicaid's share of births; it does not measure the effects of the cuts.

## Rating
1 Pinocchio (Mostly True, minor framing issues). Every checkable number matched. A few details (Mississippi and Alabama Medicaid shares, Louisiana and Mississippi policy counts, the quote) could not be verified here.
