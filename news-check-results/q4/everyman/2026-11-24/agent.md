## Rating: 1 Pinocchio (minor shortcomings)

The checkable statistics match their sources and the legislation is described correctly.

## Potentially misleading

- The ~40% (2019 onward) and 10% (2021-2024) figures use different sources, baselines and windows. Side by side they can read as contradictory or confusing.
- National nonrenewal and premium figures are regional averages, not Montana-specific.
- The nonrenewal and premium rises have several drivers (reinsurance, rebuilding costs, hail). The headline framing leans on wildfire.
- HB 136 does not require discounts.
