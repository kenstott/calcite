# Fact-check: TheWrap, "Elon Musk Regrets Spending Nearly $500 Million Backing Republicans: 'I Feel Pretty Foolish'" (A.J. Katz)

Method: WebFetch returned only a summary of the article, not verbatim text, so claims are taken from that summary. I checked them against Al Jazeera (Nov 19), Adweek, and a web search on the Texas donations. No primary documents (FEC, OpenSecrets, DealBook transcript) were opened directly.

## Claims

| # | Claim | Verdict | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Musk said at the NYT DealBook Summit, with Andrew Ross Sorkin, that he feels "pretty foolish" | Supported | Al Jazeera quotes "I feel pretty foolish about having spent so much money, and not seeing much for it." Adweek confirms the venue and Sorkin. |
| 2 | Date: TheWrap dated Nov 18 | Supported | Al Jazeera puts the event on Wednesday Nov 18 and its own article on Nov 19. Musk dialed in from China. |
| 3 | At least $445M supporting Republicans per OpenSecrets | Supported (secondary) | Adweek attributes the $445M to OpenSecrets. I did not open OpenSecrets. |
| 4 | Headline "nearly $500 million" | Partly supported | Reuters, via Al Jazeera, says "nearly half a billion". It is a rounding up from the $445M figure, so the headline figure is looser than the body. |
| 5 | He would have spent less if he could rewind | Supported | Adweek: "Probably, if I could rewind it back, I would have spent less money." |
| 6 | Goal was a 51-seat GOP Senate majority; Republicans "routed" | Unverified in detail | Not found in the Al Jazeera or Adweek pages I read. Adweek gives a different motive: avoiding a Democratic supermajority. Search results confirm Democrats won Congress. |

| 8 | $158M to a GOP Senate super PAC, which sent matching sums to a pro-Paxton group | Supported, with a caveat | Reporting on FEC filings: $75M on Sept 9 and about $83M later to the Senate Leadership Fund, with matching transfers to Texas PAC. ## Rating

Overall: 1 Pinocchio (a few shortcomings).

The core story is accurate. Musk said he felt foolish, would spend less, and the Republicans lost. The figures are sourced. Three issues reduce the rating:

- The headline rounds $445M up to "nearly $500 million".
- The 51-seat goal and the Talarico quote could not be verified.
- The piece may present the $158M as flowing to Paxton when its own sourcing admits the money may not be traceable.

## Possibly misleading

- "Regrets" is stronger than his words. He said "foolish" and "I'd probably have spent less". He did not say he wished he had not backed Republicans. His stated aim was checks and balances.
- "Nearly $500M" versus $445M: the $445M is the OpenSecrets minimum for federal races, so the true total may be higher or lower depending on what is counted.
- Musk's lack of a commitment on future spending is context a reader would want.
