# Fact-check: "US LNG Flows Hit Record High as Global Gas Supply Crisis Deepens" (Oilprice.com, Tsvetana Paraskova, 16 Nov 2026)

Method: WebFetch of the article and its AMP version; web searches for primary or near-primary sources. I could not open BloombergNEF (paywalled), EIA or GIE AGSI+ directly. Some checks rely on secondary reports of those sources.

## Claims

| # | Claim | Verdict | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | BNEF: pipeline flows to US LNG plants hit a record 21.8 "billion cubic feet" on Saturday, up 12% y/y | Not checkable here (BNEF paywalled) | No other source gives 21.8. Reuters/LSEG reported a record 20.4 bcfd on Nov 9, and Industrial Info reported a weekend record about Nov 11. Different datasets give different numbers, so 21.8 is plausible but unconfirmed. The article gives no "per day" unit, which is a sloppy omission. The 12% is also unverified. |
| 2 | Flows to Golden Pass hit a record | Supported (secondary) | RBN Energy and the BNEF-cited reports say Golden Pass feedgas reached levels consistent with full Train 1 operation. LSEG put it at about 0.8 bcfd. |
| 3 | Sabine Pass flows highest since April 2026 | Supported (secondary) | Same BNEF-cited reporting. |
| 4 | EIA: US LNG exports averaged 17.4 Bcf/d in H1 2026, up 23% y/y | Verified | EIA Today in Energy, 1 Sep 2026, and EIA Natural Gas Monthly, carried by OGJ, JPT and Hydrocarbon Processing. |
| 5 | Iran war cut Qatar's LNG exports, which must transit Hormuz | Verified | Widely reported: Qatar force majeure since spring, Hormuz disruption, about 20% of global LNG. |
| 6 | QatarEnergy force majeure runs to end-December 2026 "at the very least" | Verified, with a caveat | Reuters (27 Oct) reported the Edison extension to end-December, and a Petrobangla source said South Asian deliveries were extended to year-end. Oilprice and gasworld ran the story. I found no single announcement covering all buyers, and the force majeure is applied buyer by buyer. |
| 7 | EU storage 70.5% full on Nov 14 per AGSI, "well below the five-year average" | Substantially verified | A fact-check of AGSI+ data (oilpriceapi) gives 71.24% on Nov 11, the lowest for that date since 2011. The 5-year average for Nov 11 (2021-25) is about 89%, so "well below" is an understatement. 70.5% on Nov 14 is consistent with the start of withdrawals. I could not see Nov 14 data directly, and AGSI marks recent values as estimates. The article gives no average figure. |
| 8 | Russian LNG import ban takes effect 1 Jan 2027 | Not independently checked in this session | It matches the EU regulation timeline as I understand it, but I did not re-verify it against the Official Journal. Treat it as plausible but unchecked. |
| 9 | Europe heads into winter with the lowest storage level | Supported | Media reports (Il Foglio, DH, 3 Nov) say the lowest since 2013. The AGSI-based fact-check says a record low for the date. |
| 10 | Withdrawals have begun in most EU countries; Hormuz LNG flows "way below pre-war" | Plausible and consistent with the above | No primary data checked. |
| 11 | Europe and Asia compete for spot cargoes avoiding Hormuz | Interpretive, reasonable | EIA notes H1 exports to Asia up 108% and to Europe up only 1%. |

No direct quotes from named people appear in the piece. The only quoted items are data phrases.

## Grade: 1 Pinocchio (a few minor shortcomings)

The facts that could be checked hold up. The weaknesses are as follows.

- The headline "record high" rests on a single paywalled BNEF figure. The unit is missing "per day", and it differs from LSEG's 20.4 bcfd record.
- The five-year storage average is never quantified.
- The Russian ban date was not verified.

## Potentially misleading context

- The headline implies US exports are relieving Europe. EIA data show the H1 export surge went almost entirely to Asia (+108%), while Europe gained about 1%.
- Feedgas flow is not export volume. A pipeline record signals demand for gas at the plants, not cargoes delivered.
- The article's "force majeure to end-December" reads like one announcement, but it is a set of buyer-specific extensions.
