The Oilprice.com article's claims about U.S. LNG feedgas, EIA export growth and EU gas storage are accurate.

**Rating: 0 Pinocchios.**

Article: "U.S. LNG Flows Hit Record High as Global Gas Supply Crisis Deepens" (Tsvetana Paraskova, Oilprice.com, Nov 16, 2026).

**Warehouse check.** search_catalog and list_tables (energy) found no LNG feedgas, LNG export or EU storage series. The warehouse has only U.S. weekly storage, Henry Hub prices and production. I verified against primary sources instead.

| Claim | Verdict | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Record U.S. LNG "flows" this weekend (central claim) | Mostly true | 21.8 Bcf on Saturday, per BNEF via Marcellus Drilling News. Bloomberg returned HTTP 403. The record is single-day pipeline feedgas, not exports. |
| 21.8 Bcf record, up 12% from a year ago | True | The copy says "more than 12%". The article omits "per day". |
| Golden Pass record; Sabine Pass highest since April 2026 | True | Same BNEF copy. |
| War reduced Qatar's LNG exports through Hormuz | True | EIA (read directly): the Hormuz disruption in March cut off about 20% of global LNG, mostly Qatari. |
| 17.4 Bcf/d in 1H26, up 23% | True | EIA Today in Energy 68064, read directly. |
| Higher U.S. exports "could be some relief" for Europe | Mostly true | EIA forecasts 17.3 Bcf/d for 2H26, flat against 1H26. Oilprice's March piece said U.S. plants were already at capacity. |
| QatarEnergy force majeure extended to end of December 2026 | True | Oilprice, Oct 28, relaying an Edison notice. I did not fetch Edison's notice itself. |
| Ban on Russian LNG imports from Jan 1, 2027 | True | Web searches (S&P Global and others): the EU regulation entered into force Feb 2, 2026 and bans long-term-contract Russian LNG from Jan 1, 2027. Short-term contracts are already banned. |
| EU storage 70.5% full on Nov 14, below the five-year average | True | The GIE AGSI page returned no data. A blog citing AGSI gives 71.24% on Nov 11, a record low for the date since 2011. I did not confirm 70.5% to the decimal. |
| Lowest storage level ahead of winter | True | Same record-low-for-the-date reading, from the blog only. |

**Framing, which does not change the facts.**
- The headline "LNG flows" is one day of feedgas, not exports.
- The one-day record suggests a trend, but EIA sees flat 2H26 exports.
- "Crisis deepens" is editorial. Oilprice's own same-week items report European gas easing on mild weather and record U.S. flows.

**Gaps.** Bloomberg and BNEF were read through a secondary copy. The other linked Oilprice pages loaded, but I read only their first characters, so I checked no claim against them.
