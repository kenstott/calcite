Warehouse check

fec.individual_contributions (2026 file) shows Musk gifts of $100k or more totaling about $358M through Sept 30 (SLF PAC $168M, America PAC $138.9M, CLF $30M, others $27.5M). Press totals of $366M (Oct) and $445M (AP, Nov 5) are consistent with later giving. The 2024 file shows $213M, in line with the widely reported ~$290M for the 2024 cycle including 2025 gifts.

Misleading framing

Omitting 'or maybe it would have been much worse without that' tilts the quote toward pure regret. The headline's figure exceeds the body's. Sister TheWrap pieces give $224M and $440M for related totals, inconsistent with each other.
