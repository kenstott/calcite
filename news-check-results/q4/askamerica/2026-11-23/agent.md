**The TheWrap article's claims about Elon Musk's regret over his 2026 midterm spending, his donations and the Texas Senate result are mostly accurate.**

**Rating: 1 Pinocchio.**

Article: https://thewrap.com/media-platforms/politics/elon-musk-regrets-spending-republicans-2026-midterms (fetched in full; no block).

## Verdicts
- Central quote ("I feel pretty foolish about having spent so much money and not seeing much for it"), Nov 18 NYT DealBook with Sorkin: confirmed by Al Jazeera and Daily Wire. Mostly true: Musk's sentence continued "or maybe it would have been much worse without that." TheWrap cut that, which tilts the quote toward pure regret.
- "Nearly half a billion dollars" (headline and lede): mostly true. The article's own body figure is "at least $445 million" (AP, Nov 5). Warehouse (fec.individual_contributions, gifts of $100k or more, filings through Sept 30, 2026): about $358M. These are consistent with later giving. Other counts: $366M (Oct), $458M (Forbes via Daily Wire).
- "$445 million ... according to OpenSecrets": mostly true. I found it attributed to AP, not OpenSecrets, and could not confirm OpenSecrets as the source.
- "Musk donated $158 million to a Republican Senate super PAC": mostly true. Warehouse shows $168M to SLF PAC across four gifts by Sept 23. The $158M was the NYT/FEC figure for two September gifts. TheWrap's own earlier piece says it is unclear that the money sent to the Texas group was Musk's directly.
- "Preserve a 51-seat Republican Senate majority heading into 2029": unsupported. It appears in no other account of the interview I read (Al Jazeera, Daily Wire, AP). Republicans held 53 seats before the election.
- Talarico beat Paxton, and Democrats won both chambers: true (AP, Al Jazeera).
- Talarico "Texas is not for sale" on The View: true (TheWrap's own View piece).
- May 2025 "a lot less" and "done enough" after more than $290M in 2024: true. The 2024 FEC file alone shows $213M, consistent with the roughly $290M cycle total.
- "Dramatically increased contributions in 2026": mostly true. Gifts began in June 2025, and the 2026-cycle total exceeds the 2024 cycle only moderately.
- "I haven't committed any crimes" and "I'm not MAGA": true. The "first crime, then prison" wording differs from Daily Wire's "prison first, crime later".

## Misleading flags
- The headline figure ("nearly $500M") is higher than the article's own sourced $445M.
- The truncated quote drops Musk's "maybe it would have been much worse" hedge.
- TheWrap's linked pieces disagree with each other: $224M (The View piece), $440M+ (SNL piece), $158M plus $80M (Senate GOP piece).
- Nothing in the sources read supports the 51-seat goal.
