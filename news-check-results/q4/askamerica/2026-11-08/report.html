Exclusions disclosed

Queries excluded rows where month IS NULL (fiscal-year total rows). Rows with null persons_participating were excluded: American Samoa, Puerto Rico and the Commonwealth of Northern Mariana Islands in May 2026, and every June 2026 row. The national total excluded PR, GU, VI, AS, MP. Warehouse May 2026: 36.50M without territories, 36.56M with them; June 2025: 42.02M without, 42.08M with. The exclusion changes the headline by under 0.2%.
