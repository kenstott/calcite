## Claim grades
- Headline "nearly $12B per OpenSecrets" (fidelity): partially false. The magnitude matches AdImpact's ad-spending projection ($11.6B; $11.21B booked as of Oct 26), not an OpenSecrets total. opensecrets.org returned HTTP 403, so the attribution is unconfirmed.
- Congressional spending surpassed any election including presidential: mostly true (AdImpact: $11.6B vs $11.2B for 2024, ad spending).
- More than $500M on the Texas Senate race: true (Al Jazeera: more than $534M).
- Republican groups outspending Democratic groups by nearly $400M: unsupported. Sources say Republicans out-spent Democrats but give no such gap.
- Musk $445M to Republican PACs: mostly true (Outlook India $445M, Al Jazeera $446M; the Reuters/NYT tally is $366M on a different scope and date).
- Soros about $100M to Democratic-aligned groups: mostly true (Al Jazeera: about $102M).
- Musk more than $150M for Paxton: mostly true ($158M via Senate Leadership Fund per Outlook India, which says it was transferred to a Texas PAC backing Paxton; Reuters shows only $10M directly to Lone Star Liberty PAC).
- Talarico $145M individual vs Paxton $22M: mostly true. Warehouse (fec.candidate_summaries, through 2026-10-14): Talarico $146.3M total, $139.8M individual; Paxton $22.3M total, $21.0M individual.
- David Miller's small-dollar vs big-donor remark: mostly true. Talarico's unitemized share is 54% of individual money, Paxton's is 29%.

Not graded: Braseth's quote (opinion).
