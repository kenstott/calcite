Warehouse caveats and exclusions

energy.eia_electricity_prices mixes rollups: sector_code ALL contains RES+COM+IND, and state_abbr holds regional codes (ENC, MAT, PACC etc.) plus US. My first query summed all of them (about 3x double counting, 12,174 BkWh for 2025), so I discarded it and used only state_abbr='US' rows. One inspection query used the predicate state_abbr NOT IN (SELECT state_abbr FROM geo.state_ref), which excluded all 50 states and DC and left only the regional rollups and US, to list the rollup rows. No headline figure depends on it: the US rows give 2025 RES 1,515, COM 1,493, IND 1,042 BkWh with or without that predicate. In eia_electricity_generation the energy_source_code rollup ALL is used only as the denominator for COW, NG, NUC and REN shares, so the rollup_contamination warning on that query (which summed ALL with its parts) does not affect the percentages. The warehouse ends at 2025 actuals, so 2026 and 2027 forecasts were graded on the primary EIA documents.
