The Gulf News article's claims about US crude oil production, inventories and EIA price forecasts are mostly false; 1 claim could not be confirmed.

Rating: 3 Pinocchios.

Central claim: "US crude oil production has hit a record 14.3 million barrels per day" is mostly false. EIA's Nov 10, 2026 STEO says production will "reach a record 14.3 million b/d in 2027", up 3% from a record 13.9 in 2026. The warehouse (energy.eia_crude_oil_production, national NUS) shows a peak monthly 13.995 mb/d (Oct 2025) and 2026 Jan-Aug average 13.79. The headline turns a forecast into an achieved record.

Other claims, graded true against the STEO: inventories rising in 2027 on higher net imports; lowest October crude inventories since 2014 (410 million barrels); about 70 Bcf more gas in storage (EIA: 67 Bcf); Henry Hub $2.87 Nov-Mar, 16% lower; Brent $86 in 2027; LNG exports 15 to 19 Bcf/d; diesel $5.03 vs $4.49 and gasoline $3.73 vs $3.56.

Unsupported: Brent $104 in November and $99 in December. The STEO narrative says only "near $100"; the monthly figures were not found in the text and the tables were not parsed. The warehouse holds no Brent forecast.

Misleading flag: all other figures are accurate, but the headline and lead present a 2027 projection as a current achievement. The article also never says the record is a forecast.
