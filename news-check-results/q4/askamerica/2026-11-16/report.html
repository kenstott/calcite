Summary

Rating: 1 Pinocchio (mostly accurate, minor issues). The core IIE figures (9% overall, 15% new students, 15% graduate, 95% would look elsewhere if OPT is limited) match NYT and GBH coverage of the survey. Weak points: Cleveland State figures are internally inconsistent and conflict with local reporting, the $70,000 OPT fee is simplified ($70,000 first, $30,000 later), the NAFSA $2.4 billion, the 6% OPT drop and the 3% undergraduate drop could not be found, and the headline's causal 'due to Trump policies' rests on survey respondents' stated reasons.
