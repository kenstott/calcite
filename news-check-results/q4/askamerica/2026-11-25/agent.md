**Framing flags (none material):**
- The headline credits Gov. Shapiro. The 3.5% record was first set in summer 2023, after he took office in January 2023. This is consistent, but a state unemployment rate reflects national and regional forces and is not attributable to one governor.
- The article says "gains from last September", but its table compares with October 2025. This is a wording slip.
- The rate comes from a BLS model-based, smoothed series. The October 2025 survey month was disrupted by the federal shutdown, so the "change from October 2025" comparison rests on estimated data. The article does not mention this.
- The "record low" covers a series that begins in 1976. The warehouse holds 2009 onward, and the L&I release is the source for the longer record.
