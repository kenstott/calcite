**Possible misleading framing.**
- The 40% figure spans 2019 on. The Montana figures I found cover shorter windows (2021-24, 2023-25) and run lower.
- The mixed windows and methods give very different Montana averages, so a single "40% since 2019" figure needs a named, Montana-specific APCIA source.
- Insurify puts Montana's 2025 average premium ($2,399) $549 below the national average. Whether the article says Montana is mid-range nationally is unknown.
