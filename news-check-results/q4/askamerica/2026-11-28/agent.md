## Misleading framing
1. **The cause is misattributed.** Fox says higher home prices and mortgage rates "increased the time needed to save for a down payment." Mortgage rates play no role in down-payment time.
2. **Prices barely moved after 2019.** Median sales price over median income was 4.66 in 2019 and 4.75 in 2025. The warehouse's personal saving rate (PSAVERT) fell from 7.3% in 2019 to 4.5% in 2026.
3. **The warehouse reproduces Realtor.com's figure, driven by the saving rate.** 10% of the 2025 price is $41.5k. Divided by income of $87.5k at a 4.5% saving rate, that is about 10.5 years. The same calculation for 2019 gives 6.4 years. For the 1980s, with a saving rate near 9.6%, it gives about 4 years.
4. **So the "more than triple the 1980s" headline is real, but it comes from price-to-income rising about 26% since the 1980s plus a roughly halved saving rate.** The piece frames it as a price and rate story, and omits the saving-rate assumption.
5. **The CPI figure is the September reading**, labelled "most recent."

## Provenance and limits
- Warehouse figures come from econ.fred_indicators (MORTGAGE30US, MSPUS, CPIAUCSL, PSAVERT) and census.household_income_history_national.
- The $150k, 43-of-50 and metro-share claims rest only on Realtor.com's own page. That confirms the article relayed the source faithfully, not that the source is right.
- All pages Fox links to were fetched (related stories, a video page, an app page). None carries claims used in the piece.
