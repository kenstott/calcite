census.acs_congressional_district (ACS 5-year, summed over districts): renter cost burden 45.9% (2019) to 47.4% (2024); severe burden 22.9% to 24.0%. Consistent with a rising, near-half burden, but no income-bracket split exists, so the $45,000-$74,999 band is not testable. Harvard's all-renter 49% (ACS 1-year) differs from 47.4% (5-year) by design.

Misleading-framing flags

  • The headline leans on Harvard; the 'new' BofA data gives no middle-tercile figure (the article admits it), so BofA does not support the middle-class claim.
  • Harvard's own data show burden is still highest at the bottom; the middle is rising fastest.
  • The 'big bite' quote was not found in the BofA PDF or HTML page.
  • The listings share (49% to 23%) is NAR/Realtor.com data relayed by Harvard.
