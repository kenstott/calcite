# Fact-check: Gulf News, "US crude oil production hits a record 14.3 million barrels per day: EIA" (Nov 11, 2026)

**Rating: 3 Pinocchios.** Nearly every number is accurate to EIA's November 2026 STEO (released Nov 10), but the headline says production "hits" 14.3 million b/d. That figure is EIA's forecast annual average for 2027, not output reached.

## Claims
| # | Claim | Verdict | Primary source |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Crude output "hits a record 14.3 million b/d" | MISLEADING: 14.3 = 2027 forecast average. 2026 est. 13.9 (13.86 from monthly series). Highest published monthly 13.995 (Oct 2025); highest weekly 14.003 (week ending Oct 30 2026); STEO estimates Sep 2026 at 14.01 | STEO Nov 2026 pp.2, 8; PSM MCRFPUS2; WPSR WCRFPUS2 |
| 2 | Inventories to rise in 2027 as net imports increase | TRUE: 416 mb (Jan) to 444 mb (Dec 2027); also higher production per EIA | STEO p.8 |

## Misleading elements
- Headline/lede present a forecast as an achieved record. EIA's own wording: "reaches a record 14.3 million b/d in 2027."
- Piece omits that Brent hit $124 in October (highest since July 2022) and that Brent Nov/Dec forecasts are far below actual October.
- "Raised its forecasts" for diesel/gasoline are 2027 forecasts; undated in the piece.
- Observed records are lower: weekly 14.003 (Oct 30 2026); annual 2025 13.7 was record until 2026 (13.9).

Confidence: high (all figures from EIA STEO PDF/XLSX and EIA data tables). Gap: Gulf News text was only retrievable as a summary via WebFetch, not verbatim; the extracted claim list may omit minor sentences.
