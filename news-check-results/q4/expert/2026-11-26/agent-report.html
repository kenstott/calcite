Fact-check: KEV additions 298, up 35 percent - 0 Pinocchios

Verdict: every headline number reproduces from CISA's catalog (Pinocchio 0 of 4)

The piece's counts are accurate. CISA's KEV JSON (version 2026.11.25) shows 298 additions Jan 1-Nov 17 2026 and 221 for the same 2025 window, a 34.8% rise. The catalog had 1,782 entries with 361 ransomware-Known (20.3%) at the Nov 17 cutoff; today it is 1,805 and 362.

  • The piece itself says the ransomware share is lifetime, not about 2026 additions. That is right: 27 of 298 (9.1%) 2026 additions are Known.
  • Caveat: the 78% short-deadline figure is window-dependent (73-78%) and the piece rightly labels it unverified.
  • Caveat: Beazley's 38% is quarter-over-quarter, not year-over-year; one trade outlet reports 37%.
  • Not checkable: ImmunitySec as originator of the count. The rise reflects CISA listing choices, which the piece says. Nothing materially misleading found; the BOD 26-04 effect on listing volume is not tested.
