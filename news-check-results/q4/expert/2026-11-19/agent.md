# Fact-check: "Americans increasingly see crime declining, but fears of scams persist" (2026-11-16)

**Rating: 1 Pinocchio.** Nearly all claims match primary sources.

## Claims
- **Answer:** Gallup figures (46% national, 77% in 2023, 49% last year, 33% local, 70% identity theft, 55% scams, 77% vs 52% by age, 40% vs 13% sexual assault, 32% vs 13% murder worry by income, Republicans -15 pts) confirmed. **Sources:** Gallup 2026-11-16 (n=1,200, Oct 1-19). **Confidence:** high.
- **Answer:** FBI violent crime -9.3%, murder rate 4.1/100k confirmed; murder decline is 18.1% not the piece's 18.5% (18.5% is robbery). **Sources:** HSToday, Fox News summaries of FBI release (fbi.gov not retrieved). **Confidence:** medium.
- **Answer:** BJS violent excl. simple assault -25% (9.9 to 7.4 per 1,000) confirmed. **Sources:** BJS preliminary NCVS-R 2025. **Confidence:** high.

## Misleading context
BJS data are preliminary and NCVS-R is not comparable to legacy NCVS; total violent victimization change reportedly not significant. "Largest since 1936/1937" start year varies. FBI data voluntary. The 22% murder-worry figure was not in the fetched Gallup text and is unconfirmed (not contradicted). Stateline page returned 403.
