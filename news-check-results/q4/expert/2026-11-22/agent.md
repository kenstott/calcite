# Fact-check: Chalkbeat, "NYC public school enrollment drops by 23,000 students" (2026-11-16)

## Verdict: 1 Pinocchio (essentially accurate; minor rounding/attribution gaps, nothing misleading)

## Step 1 - Enrollment figures
**Answer:** Consistent with each other and with independent coverage; the primary DOE release was not retrievable.
**Key Values:** K-12 ~766,900 (Oct 31, 2026), down ~23,000 (~3%); with preschool 863,100, down ~2.6%. Back-solving: 863.1/0.974 = 886.1k, a 23.0k drop; since preschool rose slightly, K-12 should fall slightly more than total, so "about 23,000" for both is rounding-tight, not wrong. 766,900 found only in Chalkbeat/The 74 copy. Figures are preliminary.
**Sources:** Chalkbeat article; The 74 syndication; Chalkbeat 2026-11-19 school-level data; Comptroller report (2025-26 drop 21,793, 3%).
**Confidence:** Medium - secondary only; trend corroborated.

## Step 2 - Decade loss (~200,000, ~20%)
**Answer:** Plausible. Comptroller: K-12 down ~187,000 (19%) from SY2013-14 to 2025-26 (12 years); adding 2026-27 loss approaches 200k. Exact "past decade" baseline not verified.
**Confidence:** Medium-low.

## Step 3 - Hold-harmless spending (~$2B since 2020)
**Answer:** Supported. Chalkbeat June 2026: ~$1.9B; Feb 2026: $1.6B. "Nearly $2B" is fair. Policy began 2020 under de Blasio. Mamdani's first budget kept budgets whole (corroborated by Chalkbeat/amNY). Adams rollback claim not independently checked.
**Confidence:** Medium-high.

## Step 4 - SCA projection (130,000 by 2034-35)
**Answer:** Comptroller reports 128,000 from SY2025-26 to 2034-35; article's 130,000 is rounding. Other outlets cite larger totals (150k+) from different baselines/exclusions (District 75). Not misleading but baseline unstated in article.
**Confidence:** Medium-high.

## Step 5 - "Thirty states" declined since 2014
**Answer:** Slightly off. NCES Digest 2025 table 203.20 (fall 2014 to fall 2024): 31 of 50 states+DC lower (Georgia rounds to 0.0%, DC up 16.4%). With Georgia counted down it is 32; "30" is an undercount of 1-2 or uses a different vintage. NYS -7.8%. Other cited (not verified) cities LA, Chicago, Philadelphia, Houston decline: widely reported.
**Confidence:** High on NCES count.

## Step 6 - Closures/mergers and quotes
**Answer:** Samuels' view on closures/mergers for small schools corroborated by secondary reports. Specific proposals (2 Harlem, 1 Brooklyn closures; 2 mergers) and the "going pretty well" quote not checkable here: the PEP Nov 18, 2026 page snippet did not list schools. Class-size law claim and 2-K 10,000 seats not checked. The "Mamdani has not articulated a vision" line is characterization, not fact.
**Confidence:** Low for these specific items.

## Misleading-context flags
- Causes (birth rates, emigration, immigration slowdown) are attributed loosely; Comptroller says decline reflects fewer school-age children, not shifts to other school types.
- Per-pupil inequity claim is attributed to experts, fine.
