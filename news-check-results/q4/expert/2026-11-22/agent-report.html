Chalkbeat NYC enrollment story: essentially accurate, 1 Pinocchio

Summary: the numbers hold; two small rounding gaps

Every checkable figure in the 2026-11-16 Chalkbeat piece matched independent sources within rounding. Overall grade: 1 Pinocchio (accurate; minor imprecision).

  • Enrollment: ~23,000 K-12 loss (~3%), 863,100 total (-2.6%). Internally consistent; the primary DOE release was not retrieved, so confidence is medium.
  • "Thirty states" lost enrollment since 2014: NCES Digest 2025 table 203.20 shows 31 of 50 states + DC lower in fall 2024 than 2014 (Georgia ~0.0%).
  • SCA projection "130,000": Comptroller reports 128,000 (SY2025-26 to 2034-35); other outlets give larger totals on different baselines.
  • Hold-harmless "nearly $2 billion": Chalkbeat June 2026 says ~$1.9B.
  • Not checkable here: the specific PEP closure/merger proposals and the Samuels "going pretty well" quote.
  • Would change the grade: DOE data showing a different K-12 base, or a PEP agenda contradicting the proposals listed.
