Grade: 1 Pinocchio (minor shading). Of 14 claims, 10 check out, 3 are partly supported and 1 could not be checked here. The core facts hold: Musk said he felt "pretty foolish" on Nov 18, Republicans lost, and Paxton lost to Talarico.
