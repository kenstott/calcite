# Fact-check: ICN Montana wildfire insurance costs (2026-11-24)
**Rating: 1 Pinocchio (mostly accurate, minor framing issues).** Article text obtained via WebFetch summary only; direct quotes not verbatim.

- NAIC 2018-2024 report: premiums up 18.3% (NE), 24.7%, 26.5%, 43.3% (West) inflation-adjusted; nonrenewals up 96-216%. Verified. Note: article's "18.3 to 43.3 across regions" fits. Confidence high. Source: content.naic.org MCAS homeowners report.
- HB 533 (2025): signed May 8, law May 13, 2025; insurers must disclose wildfire risk scores on request (MCA 33-16-117). Verified, high.
- Montana ~40% since 2019 (APCIA) vs 10% 2021-2024 (CFA via EQC, March 2026): both found; different base years, likely nominal vs real; juxtaposition could confuse. Rural 11% not found. Medium.
- Auditor Brown complaints quote and advisory re cancelation: consistent with csimt.gov; exact quote not verified. Medium.
- HB 136: could not find. Not checkable here. Also Plumb/Amica story, 70% acreage since 2000, two-thirds in WUI, $67k/22% home value, grant program 2027: not checked.
- Misleading flag: GAO Feb 2026 found US avg premium up only 3% real 2019-24; regional West figure is a regional not Montana figure. Montana is described by Realtor.com as mid-range.
