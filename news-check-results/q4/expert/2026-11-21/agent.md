# Fact-check: Fortune 2026-11-16 housing squeeze (Harvard JCHS + BofA)
**Rating: 0 Pinocchios.**

## Claims
- **Answer:** 49% of $45-75k renters burdened 2024, +9.5 pts since 2019 - verified. **Sources:** JCHS America's Rental Housing 2026 pp.6,26. **Confidence:** high.
- 83% under $30k; 14% at $75k+ (+4.1); $120k for median home - verified, JCHS 2026. High.
- BofA 1 in 4 lower-income renters and higher-income mortgage holders >50% of income, Jan-Sep 2026 - verified, BofA Institute 4 Nov 2026 note. High.
- 1969 25% cap / 1981 30% - supported (Brooke Amendment, HUD, NLIHC). Medium-high.
- Listings affordable at $75k: half (2019) to under a quarter - supported via JCHS official/coverage; JCHS blog's own stat is a 60% fall in count. Medium.
- McMillan $872.96/$275 - NPR 2011. Medium.
- Rents +38% vs incomes +28%: only secondary support, not in JCHS 2026 text I searched. Low-medium.
- Homeowners $45-75k 31%, +1.1: not found. "Didn't have much room to grow" quote and "week's wages" rule: not checkable here.

## Misleading flags
BofA terciles differ from Harvard dollar bands; BofA has no middle-income figure; BofA shows renter burden easing in 2026; 30% vs 50% thresholds differ. Article text came from a fetch summary, so wording is paraphrased.
