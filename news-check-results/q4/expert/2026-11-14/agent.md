# Fact-check: NPR/KUOW "Democrats won big. Can they lower your grocery bill?" (9 Nov 2026)

**Overall: 1 Pinocchio.** Nearly all verifiable facts hold; one numeric discrepancy and one unverified poll.

## Claims
- Democrats won both chambers of Congress. **Verified.** Key values: ~54 Senate seats, 228 House (early reports, 4 Nov 2026). Sources: OPB/NPR https://www.opb.org/article/2026/11/04/2026-midterms-democrats-win-the-senate/. Confidence: high.
- Andrews defeated Sen. Darline Graham (SC). **Verified.** Sources: Fox News, WSOC. Confidence: medium-high (projection, not certified).
- At least 28 Democratic governors; ten legislative chambers flipped (NCSL). **28 verified; ten is a discrepancy.** NCSL 6 Nov 2026 says 12 chambers flipped (AZ both, GA H, MN H, MI H, NH both, NC H, PA S, TX H, WI both). Source: https://www.ncsl.org/state-legislatures-news/details/2026-state-legislative-elections-the-results-and-what-they-mean. Confidence: medium (unofficial, shifting counts).
- Hinojosa, Bottoms, Haaland elected; Bottoms local grocery sales tax plan; Haaland permit-fee waiver modelled on Albuquerque. **Verified.** Sources: AJC, Haaland campaign, Albuquerque Journal. Confidence: high.
- Hinojosa pledge on "predatory surveillance pricing"; Talarico WIC/SNAP; Hamilton dynamic-pricing ban. **Not found** (Hamilton did cite SNAP cuts; Hinojosa's surveillance focus found was Flock cameras). Not checkable here. Confidence: low.
- Cooper vows to block food mergers. **Verified** (campaign platform). Confidence: high.
- SNAP overhaul, precipitous participation drop since last summer. **Verified**: ~5.3M below June 2025 (USDA via press, CBPP). Confidence: medium-high.
- Hormuz closure cut significant share of fertilizer trade. **Verified**: roughly 34-50% of traded urea (CSIS, ICC, FAO). Confidence: medium-high.
- Kitchen Table Project / GSG September poll, nearly half want lower bills within six months. **Not checkable here**; only a May poll found (n=1,100, 65% "need to lower costs now"). Confidence: low.
- Quotes from Jeffries, Brainard, Borick. **Not checkable here** (interviews), consistent with known roles.

## Misleading flags
Tariffs and the Iran war are called "two of the main causes" of high food prices without quantification. Not false, but unsourced causal attribution.

## Caveats
Web search only; results days after election and unofficial. Board: agent-dashboard.png; report: agent-report.html.
