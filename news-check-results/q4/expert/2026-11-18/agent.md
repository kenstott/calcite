# Fact-check: Oilprice "U.S. LNG Flows Hit Record High" (16 Nov 2026) - 1 Pinocchio

Answer: Numbers are accurate; two framing issues.

- Feedgas record 21.8 Bcf/d (BNEF, 14 Nov, +12% y/y; Golden Pass record, Sabine highest since April): confirmed via secondary reports (Marcellus Drilling News, NGI). Other trackers: NGI 21.2 (13 Nov), LSEG 20.4 (9 Nov). Confidence medium-high.
- EIA H1 2026 exports 17.4 Bcf/d, +23%: confirmed, eia.gov/todayinenergy/detail.php?id=68064. High.
- Qatar exports cut by Hormuz: confirmed. High.
- QatarEnergy force majeure to end-December: only Oilprice reports it; no second source. Low-medium.
- Russian LNG ban 1 Jan 2027: partly; long-term contracts only; short-term banned since 25 Apr 2026 (Reg. EU 2026/261). High.
- EU storage 70.5% on 14 Nov: confirmed (70.55%, 15-18 pp below 5-yr avg depending on source). Medium-high.
- Misleading: "flows" are feedgas, not exports; EIA says H1 exports to Europe rose 1% vs Asia 108%.
- Limits: fetch returned only a summary, so attributed quotes were not checked. Raw AGSI/BNEF not pulled.
