BPS enrollment story: 2 of 4 Pinocchios

Headline numbers hold; decade-decline and Seattle claims do not

The 44,846 count and 1,978 drop are consistent with the January 2026 baseline of 46,824. But the "21 percent / roughly 5,000 students" decade claim is internally inconsistent and overstated (Globe: 53,263 in 2017 to 44,416, about 16%; WBUR: about 9,000 fewer). Seattle is cited at about 51,000; the district's own data show 48,957 in 2025-26.

  • Could not reach DESE or the Nov 4 deck; grade-level and K2 figures and the Skipper quotes are not checkable here.
  • Counting basis (with or without in-district charters) differs across sources and could shift baselines.
  • The 1,978 drop exceeds the 1,300 projected in December, not flagged by the article.
