# Fact-check: Dorchester Reporter, "BPS enrollment continues to decline" (2026-11-12)
Rating: 2 Pinocchios (mostly accurate headline numbers; two claims wrong or internally inconsistent).

## Claims
1. 44,846 students, down 1,978 (first time under 45,000). Answer: consistent with the Jan 2026 dotnews figure of 46,824 (46,824-1,978=44,846). Could not reach DESE directly or a primary Nov 4 deck; Globe/WBUR coverage corroborates decline. Counting basis (with/without in-district charters) differs across sources (44,416 vs 46,547 vs 46,514). Confidence: medium.
2. Last year's decline 1,769. Jan 2026 article says ~1,700; Globe "just over 1,600". Medium.
3. Decade decline ~21%, "roughly 5,000 students". Internally inconsistent (5,000 is ~9%; 21% of ~53-56k is ~11-12k). Globe: 53,263 (2017) to 44,416 = 16%; WBUR: ~9,000 fewer than a decade ago. Computed from 53,263: -15.8%. Overstated/inconsistent. Confidence: medium-high.
4. Grades 6-8 -420 (5%), 9-12 -1,000+ (7%), K2 just over 3,000. Not verifiable without the committee deck; plausible. Not checkable here.
5. Seattle "~51,000". SPS district page: 48,957 (2025-26); 50,187 in 2021-22. 51,000 is not current. No official 2026-27 count found. Wrong by ~2,000. Medium-high.
6. 95 facilities by 2030, 97 with proposals. WBUR 2026-11-03 confirms. High.
7. Charters ~11,000; hundreds of teaching positions cut (Boston.com Jul 2026: 560+ positions cut). Roughly supported. Medium.
8. Immigration/birth-rate causes: Globe Jul 2026 supports. Medium-high.
9. Nov 4 School Committee meeting, Skipper quotes: attributed to Skipper; not independently confirmed on video. Not checkable here.

## Misleading
The decade figure is the headline context; 21% vs ~16% overstates the trend, and mixing 5,000 with 21% is incoherent. The Seattle comparison uses a stale number. Decline (1,978) exceeds the 1,300 projected in Dec 2025, not noted.
