Every headline figure I could check in the piece matches EIA's November 2026 Short-Term Energy Outlook (STEO): total consumption 4,216 / 4,306 / 4,371 BkWh (2025/26/27; article says 4,370, a 1 BkWh rounding difference), 2026 sales of 1,546 residential, 1,557 commercial, 1,061 industrial, 2025 records of 1,519 and 1,503, generation shares, and gas use by sector (12.2, 9.4, 23.8, 37.4 bcfd in 2026).

  • Material context: STEO table 7a has industrial sales at 1,086 BkWh in 2027, above the 1,064 BkWh 2000 level the piece cites, so "industrial falls short of its 2000 record" holds for 2026 only. I could not confirm 1,064 as the all-time record from an EIA source (NREL/EIA table lists 1,064 for 2000).
  • Attribution: the STEO narrative pages I read credit commercial-sector demand and did not mention data centers or AI (page-reader tool, not full text); the AI framing may come from EIA's press release or Reuters reporting. Not verified.
  • Not checked: "third straight record year" (2024 record) and the pre-2025 sector records; no quotes were in the piece as I could read it.
  • Limits: the article was read through a summarizing fetch tool, not verbatim, so wording-level claims may be missed.
