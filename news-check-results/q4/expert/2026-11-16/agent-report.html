Core enrollment numbers hold up; grade 1 Pinocchio, with two unverified figures

Summary of findings

The article's headline statistics match IIE's Fall 2026 Snapshot: total international enrollment -9%, new students -15%, graduate -15%, undergraduate -3%, OPT -6%, 809 institutions responding, 88% citing visa processing and 70% travel restrictions. The OPT fee and four-year-cap claims also check out against the Federal Register and court reporting.

  • Unverified: the $2.4 billion NAFSA/JBI loss (the only published fall 2026 figure I found is a $3.4B projection from August 2026) and Cleveland State's 46% (found 34% and 41% for fall 2025 only).
  • Not checkable: the three attributed quotes.
  • Potentially misleading: the headline attributes the drop to Trump policies, but the snapshot is a voluntary institutional survey recording reasons institutions cite, not causal evidence; competition and costs are also cited. Also, the text was read via an automated summary of the CBS page, so wording was not confirmed verbatim.
  • Grade: 1 Pinocchio. Would change to 2 if the $2.4B proves wrong or misattributed.
