The Newsweek article's claims about Rollins's ground-beef price remark, record beef prices, and Trump's ranching orders are mostly accurate.

BLS Average Price series, EIA diesel and USDA NASS cattle inventory (econ, energy, ag schemas); DTN, CPA, Agri-Pulse for other claims. Checked 2026-11-11.

Article under reviewhttps://www.newsweek.com/rollins-americans-good-with-surging-beef-prices-12468996

Summary

The article's own numbers check out: BLS all-uncooked ground beef was $7.158/lb in August 2026 (up from $7.116 in July and $6.631 a year earlier), the highest in the series since 1995. The weak points are (1) Secretary Rollins's claim that ground beef 'was $6.50' and 'most Americans say I'm good with that': the price is about 65 cents stale and no survey supports the sentiment, yet the article does not flag the gap; (2) the article says Trump signed two ranching executive orders 'earlier in August' when DTN reports Sept 4; (3) the USDA spokesperson's 'demand grew almost 10% in a decade' is not supported by the USDA projections found; (4) Rollins's claim that herd expansion stopped 'under the Democrats' is contradicted by NASS data showing the herd peaked in Jan 2019 and has fallen every year since. Overall: 1 Pinocchio for the article, 3 Pinocchios for Rollins's central claim.
