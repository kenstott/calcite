## Possibly misleading

- The headline says Americans are saying "I'm good" with high prices. The only evidence is a secretary's hypothetical, and no consumer survey is cited. Other coverage notes that demand is holding up, but that does not show that people are content.
- Rollins also reportedly told people to substitute chicken, pork, salmon or eggs. Raw Story says her claim that those prices are falling is inaccurate. If the piece omits this, it leaves out her fuller message.
- Fox Business reports beef and veal up 14.7% year over year in September, while the piece's 7.9% rise for August ground beef is a different, narrower series. This is not an error, but the piece's price framing could look milder than the broader beef figure.
- Calling the tariff action an executive order or waiver may blur the record.

## Rating: 1 Pinocchio

This is an overall assessment of how accurate the piece's claims appear, and several are unchecked. The checkable figures hold up, and the main weakness is the headline framing. The rating is provisional because I read a summary, not the full text.
