# Fact-check: "Traffic fatalities drop significantly across Florida, nation" (WFTV/WDBO, Sam Martello, Nov 25, 2026)

Note: WebFetch returned a summary, not verbatim text, so I could not extract direct quotes. The summary showed no attributed quotes except the framing "good news on road safety" and NHTSA statements.

## Claims and verdicts
1. National deaths fell 2.5% in H1 2026, to about 16,750 from 17,175. TRUE. NHTSA early estimate (DOT HS 813 875). 425/17,175 = 2.47%. Caveat: NHTSA says figures will be revised.
2. Vehicle miles traveled rose 0.8% while the fatality rate hit near-record lows. TRUE. VMT up about 12.9B miles, rate 1.02 per 100M VMT vs 1.05 (second-lowest H1 rate; 2014 was 1.01).
3. Federal officials are targeting reckless and impaired driving. Plausible and consistent with NHTSA priorities. Not independently verified as an attributed statement.
4. Florida: 2,155 deaths so far this year vs nearly 3,000 at this point last year, 834 fewer. NOT VERIFIED, and doubtful. The arithmetic is consistent (about 2,989 minus 2,155 = 834). But Florida's full-year 2025 total was about 3,047 per NHTSA/DOT (2,930 on the FLHSMV dashboard). Nearly 3,000 "so far" by mid-November would imply almost no deaths in the last six weeks of 2025. A 28% Florida drop is also far out of line with the national 2.5% H1 decline. Most likely explanation: 2026 counts are incomplete because of reporting lag, compared with a completed 2025 figure. I could not access the live NHTSA/FLHSMV 2026 year-to-date figures to confirm.
5. Central Florida (Orange, Osceola, Lake, Seminole, Brevard, Volusia): 550 in 2025 to 375 in 2026, 175 fewer, as of Nov 14. Arithmetic correct. NOT CHECKABLE HERE (no 2026 county data found). The same lag concern applies, and a 32% drop is implausible for a real change.

## Overall: 3 Pinocchios
The national numbers are accurate. The headline and Florida/regional numbers imply a dramatic "significant" drop that is out of line with the 2.5% national decline and with Florida's roughly 3% decline for all of 2025. It likely compares incomplete 2026 data with complete 2025 data. The article also mixes time frames: H1 2026 nationally against a mid-November year-to-date count for Florida.

## Materially misleading even where facts check out
- Mismatched periods (H1 national vs YTD state/regional) make the "nation" and "Florida" drops look like one trend.
- National decline slowed: Q1 down about 5%, Q2 down only 0.2%. The article does not mention this.
- "Significantly" overstates a 2.5% national decline, and NHTSA calls the estimates preliminary.

Sources: NHTSA early estimate DOT HS 813 875 (repairerdrivennews.com, thetruthaboutcars.com coverage); NSC H1 2026 analysis (about 2% decline, not comparable to NHTSA); NHTSA/DOT Florida 2025 preliminary data (3,047 deaths vs 3,138 in 2024); FLHSMV crash dashboard.
