## Overall: 2 Pinocchios (provisional)

Most figures are real and consistent with primary sources, but the headline trust-in-government figure appears to be last year's number, and the $1B downtown figure is unsourced.

## Materially misleading context
- The piece is a Baltimore Sun story republished by Fox Baltimore, and it concerns criticism of both outlets. Neither disclosed that conflict in what I could see.
- Homicide framing: a "decline" of 102 vs 108 is far smaller than the 2026 mid-year drop (about 26%), and nonfatal shootings are up. Readers could take "falling homicides" as a large trend; it has nearly flattened.
- Presenting poll results from different years (2025 and 2026) side by side can blur which is current.

Sources: CBS Baltimore UMBC poll story; politics.umbc.edu April 2026 poll PDFs; baltimorecity.gov vacants and mayor statements; JNS; WCBM; Maryland Matters.
