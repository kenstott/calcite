# Fact-check: WOWT / AP "Flu season is off to a very early start" (Nov 15, 2026)

Method: WebFetch of the article (the fetch tool returned a summary, not verbatim text, so quotes are checked as summarized) and CDC FluView reports (weeks 19, 39, 42, 44). No numeric charts produced.

## Verdict: 1 Pinocchio equivalent (a "Geppetto checkmark" on nearly all claims; one minor unverified item). Overall: Mostly accurate.
Scale: 0 = true, 4 = whopper. Score: **0.5 to 1 (rounded: 1 Pinocchio only for imprecision of one figure and omissions)**.

## Misleading or missing context
- CDC rates severity as **low** across all age groups so far; the headline emphasis on surge omits that.
- Early-season "highest since 2010-11" refers to a very low base rate in absolute terms (1.9 to 2.5 per 100,000 weekly); 2.6M illnesses is far below last season's 32M.
- Five pediatric deaths so far this season, not mentioned; last season had about 203, a relevant comparison.
- Article lists the 7 states as "high"; one is technically "very high."
- Article date: published Nov 14 late evening (CST), dated Nov 15 in URL; data is CDC week 44 (ending Nov 7), a week stale.

## Bottom line
Numbers track CDC data closely. Only the hospitalization figure for last season (380k vs 390k) and the state list/quotes are unconfirmed. Grade: **1 Pinocchio (one-ish minor imprecision, otherwise accurate)**.
