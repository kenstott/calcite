Method: WebFetch returned only a summary of the Newsweek piece, not verbatim text, so claims below are as summarized. Several primary sources (Federal Register, USDA FNS rule page) were blocked (403 / redirect), so rule details rest on law-firm summaries (Hogan Lovells via JD Supra) and AM New York.

## Overall grade: 1 Pinocchio (mostly accurate, minor issues)

The substance matches the primary and secondary record. Problems are framing and sourcing, not fabricated facts.

## Materially misleading or weak points
- The URL slug says "benefits cuts", which implies benefit reductions. The rule cuts no benefits; it threatens retailer eligibility.
- "Deal with Trump" and "personally negotiated" rest almost entirely on Mamdani's account. I found no USDA or White House confirmation of terms, and no formal notice.
- "Nationwide" is asserted without an official notice.
- Compliance is checked when stores apply for or renew SNAP authorization (AM NY), not immediately, so "risk of losing SNAP on Nov 4" is overstated. The delay may matter less than the framing suggests.
- Fox reports "new rules on Sept 4"; the rule was in fact published May 8. Sept 4 was implementation guidance. Newsweek's date handling could not be checked.
- The Florida ban sits under a waiver whose legal status is contested.

## Not checkable here
- 250,000 retailer count
- Exact Mamdani quote wording against a transcript
- Any USDA confirmation of the delay's terms
- Verbatim Newsweek text (only a summary was available)
