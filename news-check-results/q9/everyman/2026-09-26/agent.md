# Fact check: EIA 2026 crude oil and natural gas production records

**Verdict: True.** Both figures match the EIA's own published forecasts exactly.

## Crude oil

EIA's "United States on track for record crude oil production in 2026" (Today in Energy, Sept. 10, 2026) states: U.S. crude oil production will average 13.8 million barrels per day (b/d) in 2026, surpassing the previous record of 13.7 million b/d set in 2025. First-half 2026 production already averaged 13.7 million b/d, up 2% (0.3 million b/d) from the same period in 2025. Growth is concentrated in the Permian Basin (Texas/New Mexico) and the Federal Gulf of America, with Permian output forecast at 6.8 million b/d in 2026 (3% above 2025).

Source: https://www.eia.gov/todayinenergy/detail.php?id=68125

## Marketed natural gas

EIA's "United States on track for record natural gas production in 2026" (Today in Energy, based on the August 2026 Short-Term Energy Outlook) states: U.S. marketed natural gas production will average 122.5 Bcf/d in 2026, about 3.4% above the previous record of 118.5 Bcf/d set in 2025. January-June 2026 output already averaged 121.3 Bcf/d, up 4% (4.6 Bcf/d) from the same period in 2025. Growth is concentrated in the Permian (TX/NM) and Haynesville (LA/TX) regions.

Source: https://www.eia.gov/todayinenergy/detail.php?id=67944

## Conclusion

All four figures in the claim (13.8 million b/d crude for 2026, 13.7 million b/d crude record for 2025, 122.5 Bcf/d gas for 2026, 118.5 Bcf/d gas record for 2025) are accurate and directly sourced from EIA's own Today in Energy releases summarizing its Short-Term Energy Outlook. The claim is true as stated.
