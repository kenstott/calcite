# Fact-check: Brownfield Ag News, "NOPA reports record October soybean crush" (Nov. 25, 2026, Jared White)

Method: WebFetch gave only a summary of the article, not verbatim text, so quotes are paraphrased. I did not reach the NOPA release itself. The figures come from secondary reports.

| # | Claim | Verdict | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Oct 2026 NOPA crush was 234 million bushels, an all-time record | Supported (secondary source) | Ukragroconsult reports 234.19 mbu (about 6.37 million tons), the highest monthly volume on record. It was up about 19% from September's 196.3 mbu and above the analyst average of about 225.4 mbu. AgWeb has a headline "NOPA Crush Sets All-Time Record in October", but it returned 403 and I could not confirm its year. |
| 2 | 27% higher than four years ago | Supported | NOPA Oct 2022 was 184.464 mbu. 234.19 / 184.464 = +26.9%. |
| 3 | A slow western Corn Belt harvest hurt September, then processors ramped up in October | Plausible, partly supported | September was 196.3 mbu and October was up 19% from it. The harvest-delay cause is Mogler's attribution, and I did not verify it. |
| 4 | Industry can meet the expanded EPA Renewable Volume Obligations set earlier this year | Supported on the premise, opinion on the outcome | EPA finalized the 2026-27 RVOs on March 27, 2026. Biomass-based diesel is 8.86 billion gallons in 2026 and 8.95 billion in 2027. With reallocation of small refinery exemptions it is 9.07 and 9.20 billion. EPA says production would need to rise more than 60% from 2025. Whether the industry can meet this is a forward-looking claim and cannot be checked. |
| 5 | Bio-based diesel is 10% of the U.S. distillate pool | Not checkable here | I did not find an EIA figure. The number is plausible but unverified. |
| 6 | Benefits for row crop farmers, livestock producers (more soybean meal) and energy security | Opinion | These are advocacy claims from an industry CEO, not checkable facts. |

## Rating: 1 Pinocchio (mostly accurate, with caveats)

The checkable numbers hold up.

## Materially misleading or missing context
- Mogler is the CEO of NOPA, the trade group for the crushers. The piece is a one-source interview with an interested party and has no independent voice or critic.
- The 27% comparison is to 2022. The year-over-year gain is only about 2.9%, against 227.65 mbu in October 2025, which was itself a record. The four-year framing makes growth look larger than the recent pace.
- NOPA members are not all U.S. crushers. USDA NASS totals are higher, for example 197 mbu for Oct 2022 against NOPA's 184.5 mbu. "Industry" and "record" refer to NOPA members only.
- The record is for one month. The article does not mention that soybean oil stocks rose to about 1.17 billion pounds, or that futures fell despite the record crush (Ukragroconsult headline).
- The piece links the record crush to the biofuel mandate without noting what drives demand. It does not say that soybean oil, not meal, is the profit driver, so the meal benefit for livestock producers is stated without evidence.
