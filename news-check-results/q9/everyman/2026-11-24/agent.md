## Pinocchio rating
Provisional: 1 Pinocchio (mostly accurate). The two headline sales claims are confirmed. The rate-jump claim is built on a daily index peak, which overstates the move relative to Freddie Mac's series (roughly 7.3-7.4% in October). Most remaining claims are unchecked, so this rating is low-confidence.

## Misleading-framing flags
- The rate figure mixes sources. The 6.60% July number may come from a different series than the 7.61% October number, so the "one point in three months" jump may be overstated.
- The sales declines come in part from the rate surge, but the existing-home median price rose only about 0.3-0.4% y/y per the sources found. The "frozen market" story is about volume, not price falls.
- The two sources I found gave different median prices ($416,700 vs $365,100). I did not resolve which is right.
