# Fact-check: "US Fatal Crashes Near Sunset Rise 28.3% in Autumn Compared with Winter" (Travel And Tour World, Nov 22, 2026)

Method: WebFetch returned a summary of the article, not verbatim text. Two web searches found no copy of the Lerner & Rowe study. Primary FARS microdata could not be queried here.

## Misleading points
- The headline compares counts without exposure. The article gives no traffic volume (VMT) or dusk-hour traffic data, and it admits causation is unestablished.
- "Autumn vs winter" is a fixed 4-hour window around sunset. Sunset moves from about 4:30 pm to about 8 pm across seasons, so the window covers different clock hours, commuting patterns and darkness shares by season. The comparison therefore mixes season, time of day and darkness.
- The source is a personal-injury law firm's marketing analysis, not peer-reviewed research. It is relayed by a travel trade site with a tourism-planning angle.
- The travel-planning advice is a stretch from national aggregates.

## Rating
2 Pinocchios (Mixture or mostly accurate numbers, weak framing). The arithmetic is internally consistent and the caveat is disclosed, but the headline invites a causal reading and the core figures could not be verified against primary FARS data. The piece is secondhand, from a law-firm source.
