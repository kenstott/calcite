## Context the article's framing omits
- This vote is a different vehicle from the Ratepayer Protection Act, which failed cloture 57-43 on Sept 30 2026. Not an error, but useful context.
- Environmental groups oppose the bill (limits on NEPA/court challenges, fossil-fuel entrenchment). Some Democrats want to delay until the new Congress in January; the incoming House majority complicates passage.

## Misleading / framing issues
1. Headline: "making data centers pay their own power bills." Data centers already pay power bills. The bill assigns them incremental grid-infrastructure costs. The headline overstates and mislabels.
2. A procedural 73-25 vote is headline-led though the body correctly says nothing passed. Body corrects headline.
3. Cost/savings numbers (Brattle, Rhodium) are unsourced as to links and unreconciled with other reported estimates ($55B, $19B). Presented with precision that readers cannot verify.
4. Market-move details (ETF prices) add false implication of causation, though the article disclaims it.

## Rating: 1 Pinocchio (on 0-4 scale; "some shading/minor inaccuracies")
Core facts (vote count, date, bill, Trump's "BIG WIN," Jeffries) check out and the article honestly states the vote is procedural. Deductions: loose headline and unverified/conflicting analytic figures. Four claims (6, 7, 9, 10) are not checkable here; two (11, 12) are unverified and one conflicts with other reporting. If the Brattle/Rhodium numbers prove fabricated or misattributed, the grade would rise to 2.
