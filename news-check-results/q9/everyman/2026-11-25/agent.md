## Rating

Transcript fidelity: the event, the Newsom attack and the directive announcement are confirmed, so the transcript itself looks reliable. Trump's own factual claims in the speech are mixed.

Overall: 2 Pinocchios, applied to the speech's claims rather than the transcript. The crime declines are real. "Zero crossings for 18 months" and "commuted 548" as a done deal are overstated. Several numbers are unverified.

## Materially misleading context

- The speech's crime-decline claims are presented as proof of the administration's record. Independent researchers say the decline predates and cannot be attributed to any one policy.
- Newsom's commutations convert death sentences to life without parole. He said "every person on California's former death row will die in prison." The speech frames this as sparing killers.
- The piece is a transcript with no fact-checking, so every claim in it passes through unchallenged.
