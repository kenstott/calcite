## Possible issues to check once the text is available
- The wording "largest in decades" depends on the baseline and on whether the count includes uncalled races. Counts shift as the AP calls races.
- Mixing House-only and House-plus-Senate counts (104 vs 109 vs 120) can mislead.
- Comparisons against 2010 (96 House freshmen) and the 1992 class (110 House freshmen, from my memory and not checked here) need primary data from the Clerk of the House or the Congressional Research Service.

## Primary sources a checker should use
