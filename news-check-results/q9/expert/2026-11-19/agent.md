# Fact-check: WTKR "Hampton Roads bridges show improvement, air travel surges in 2026 transportation report"
Primary source: HRTPO, The State of Transportation in Hampton Roads 2025 (T26-01, Jan 2026), https://www.hrtpo.org/DocumentCenter/View/15070/T26-01-State-of-Transportation-in-Hampton-Roads-2025-

Rating: 1 Pinocchio (mostly accurate; one unsupported figure and material omissions).

| Claim | Verdict |
|---|---|
| 2.7% of ~1,300 bridges poor | True: 34 of 1,279, Aug 2025 |
| Down from >5% in 2017 | True: 69 bridges, 5.5% in 2017 |
| Passenger air travel +46% 2015-2024 | True: boardings at ORF+PHF, 2.5M in 2024; +14% vs 2019; U.S. +23% |
| Norfolk added international service (Cancun) | Partly: weekly Breeze flight starts Jan 2026, not yet in the 2024 data period |
| New arrivals terminal being built | True: international arrivals facility, due early 2026 |
| Norfolk Amtrak added third daily train | True but misleading: report says the third train will be replaced by a bus due to Long Bridge construction until ~2030 |
| 17 hours lost to congestion in 2023 | Wrong year: report (TomTom) says 17 hours in 2024 |
| Traffic ~4% below pre-pandemic (Nichols) | Not supported: VDOT daily VMT has returned to/exceeded pre-pandemic; per-capita VMT -0.4% vs 2019; travel time index is below pre-pandemic (possible source of a congestion-based statement). Quote not verifiable |
| Fatalities higher than decade ago, down recently | True: 153 in 2024, +26% vs 2015, down from 179 in 2021 |
| Nichols quotes on funding/"decades old" | Not checkable here (no transcript beyond article paraphrase) |

Misleading: headline says 2026 surge but data end 2024; report is the 2025 edition. Bridges "poor" does not mean unsafe (report says so). Remote work claim consistent with report (more working from home).
Confidence: high on report-matched figures; no independent second source (FAA/BTS) fetched.
