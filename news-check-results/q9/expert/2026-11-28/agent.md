# Fact-check: Brownfield "NOPA reports record October soybean crush"
**Answer:** Substantially accurate, 1 Pinocchio.
**Key Values:** Oct 2026 NOPA crush 234.191M bu (record); Oct 2022 184.464M; +26.96% (claim 27% verified). YoY about +3% vs Oct 2025. EPA final RVO Apr 1, 2026 (BBD 9.07B/9.20B RINs).
**Misleading:** 27% framing vs 4 years ago while YoY is about 3%; RVO-compliance claim is advocacy (needs BBD output +60%; 2025 BBD fell 15.8%); 10% distillate share is an industry figure (EIA 7% 2023).
**Not checkable:** NOPA release itself, Sept 2026 figure (about 196.3M, sources conflict), quotes beyond the article summary.
**Sources:** brownfieldagnews.com article; MarketScreener; soyproteincn (FP Group); Federal Register 2026-06275; dieselnet; EIA.
**Confidence:** High on crush and 27%; low on 10% claim.
