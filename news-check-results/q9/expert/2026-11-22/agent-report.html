Fact-check: Jersey City "25% crime decline"

Figures are accurate; the 25% headline is generous (2 of 4 Pinocchios)

All nine category figures in the Hudson County View piece match the city's JC IMPACT dashboard (data through Nov 14, 2026). But "about 25% overall" is an unweighted average of nine chosen percentages (23.7%). Including aggravated assault, which the dashboard shows down only 3% (790 to 765) but the article omits, the mean is 21.6%; weighted by incidents across those 10 categories the drop is 20.1% (5,720 to 4,569). Fatal crashes rose 33% (3 to 4).

  • Data are preliminary and come from the department announcing them; no independent 2026 source (NJ UCR/FBI) exists yet.
  • The city's own audit found at least 3,251 cases not properly reported to NIBRS in 2021-2024, so historical comparability deserves care.
  • Leadership succession, traffic unit expansion (10 to 19) and quotes: not checkable here.
