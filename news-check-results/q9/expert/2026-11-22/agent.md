# Fact-check: Hudson County View, "Jersey City crime data shows nearly 25% average decline, officials say" (Nov 19, 2026)

Rating: 2 Pinocchios. Every figure matches the city's own dashboard, but the "25% overall crime decline" headline rests on an unweighted average of selected categories.

## Per-claim results
- All 9 category counts and percents (shots fired 47->28 -40%, thefts from vehicles 832->452 -46%, burglaries 597->391 -35%, stolen vehicles 766->624 -19%, homicides 11->9 -18%, robberies 320->274 -14%, thefts 2,242->1,926 -14%, shooting hits 29->25 -14%, sexual assaults 86->75 -13%): VERIFIED against https://impact.jcnj.org/ (data through Nov 14, 2026, "preliminary"). Recomputed percents all round correctly. Confidence: high that article matches source; the source is the city's own, not independent.
- "Average ~24%, supporting ~25% overall": arithmetic mean of the 9 = 23.7%. Misleading: unweighted mean of rates is not an overall crime rate. Same dashboard shows aggravated assault 790->765 (-3%), gun recoveries -4%, fatal crashes +33% (3->4), shoplifting (308, no comparison); aggravated assault omitted. Mean including it = 21.6%; incident-weighted over 10 categories = 20.1% (5,720 -> 4,569); weighted over the 9 cited = 22.8%. Thefts (2,242) and vehicle break-ins swing the total.
- Audit of 3,251 unreported cases 2021-2024: VERIFIED (city press release June 9, 2026, "at least 3,251 criminal cases" not properly entered into NIBRS). Article's sentence order ("announcement came days after") is chronologically confusing: the campaign pledge was 2025, the audit June 2026. Past undercounting also raises baseline-comparability questions for the 2025 comparison.
- JC IMPACT launched earlier this year, public 24/7, weekly: VERIFIED (News12 June 11, 2026; dashboard says weekly, preliminary).
- Ambrose "past 10 months": consistent with appointment as acting director Jan 2026 (InsiderNJ/HCV).
- Kearns still chief; Ambrose/Kearns leaving at year end, Roldan and Ragland succeeding; Traffic Enforcement Division 10->19; Domestic Violence Unit; Motorcycle Unit restored; quotes of Solomon, Ambrose, Kearns: NOT CHECKABLE HERE (not found in searchable primary sources; quotes attributed to a joint city statement I did not locate).
- Independent confirmation (NJ State Police UCR/FBI) for 2026 YTD: not available; data are not yet published.

## Caveats
Preliminary data, self-reported by the department whose leadership announces it; reported-incident counts are sensitive to reporting practice. No independent source for 2026. Dashboard fetched as static HTML; interactive layer not rendered.
