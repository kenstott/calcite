The Register's 100K-CVE story: headline numbers hold, 1 Pinocchio for peripheral errors and causal overreach

Summary of findings

The central facts are accurate: 2026 CVE publication passed 100,000 on 13 Nov 2026 (NVD count 100,416 for 1 Jan-13 Nov, 2.01x the 49,972 published in 2025), and CrowdStrike's 88%-within-48-hours, VulnCheck's 120-to-80-day median, curl's 26 Oct policy and BOD 26-04 all check against primary sources. Rating: 1 Pinocchio (some shading), close to 2 because six specific statements are wrong or overstated and the AI-causation framing is stated as fact.

  • Wrong figure: Microsoft's October count of 1,474 is not supported. Patch Tuesday coverage gives 1,407-1,416 (BleepingComputer 1,411; about 1,759 if 348 earlier out-of-band fixes are added). November 1,094 is within one of Tenable's 1,093 (BleepingComputer says 1,235).
  • Wrong attribution: Open VSX managed-registry customers named by Eclipse are AWS, Google and Cursor. IBM and Windsurf are only listed as platforms that use Open VSX.
  • Overstated Copeland detail: not all 14 flaws are "make-me-root" (VulnCheck describes one direct unauthenticated root plus several root-via-chain paths), and the first honeypot hits came 1h40m and 7h23m after publication, not "minutes". The two-exploited claim is right. The devices are end-of-life and unpatched, which the piece omits.
  • Mischaracterised directive: BOD 26-04 (10 Jun 2026, revokes BOD 19-02 and 22-01) replaced KEV-driven flat deadlines, not CVSS-only ones; CVSS does not set its timelines either. Phase III remediation deadlines start within 180 days, and it binds federal agencies, not "organizations".
  • Materially misleading framing: (1) "The reason can be summed up in two letters: AI" and "also thanks to AI" are the interviewee's opinion presented as fact. VulnCheck's own report counts 1,061 explicitly AI-attributed vulnerabilities in 1H 2026, and CrowdStrike says the exploitation-speed pattern "predates the emergence of frontier AI models". The timing evidence is suggestive (NVD Sep-Oct 2026: 32,555 vs 8,926 a year earlier) but is correlation. (2) The 120-to-80 day median is measured only among CVEs already seen exploited, and the 2026 cohort is right-censored, so some of the drop can be an artefact. (3) The "steady 1.4%" is specific to VulnCheck's catalog; CISA's KEV added 297 CVEs in 2026, about 0.30% of the NVD count.
  • Not checkable here (4): VulnCheck's live 100,222 count, the exact 1,363 KEV count (VulnCheck's full KEV needs a token, not used), the claim the exploitation window "closed further" in 2H, and the Bressers interview quotes.
  • What would change the grade: a source for 1,474 would remove one error; evidence that 2H 2026 exploitation timing was measured would settle the "closed further" claim.

Step 1: Volume and growth

Answer: Verified. Key Values: NVD published-date counts: 2026 to 13 Nov = 100,416 (9,951; 12,189; 14,986; 22,638; 32,555 for Jan-Feb to Sep-Oct, plus 8,097 for 1-13 Nov); 2025 = 49,972. NVD counts include a few CVE-2025 IDs published in 2026. Sources: NVD API. Confidence: high, direct count within 0.2% of VulnCheck's.

Step 2: Exploitation statistics

Answer: CrowdStrike 88%/48h and Panda 24h verified; VulnCheck 120 to 80 days verified; 1.4% verified as VulnCheck's ratio. Key Values: CISA KEV 2026 additions 297 of 1,781 total (dateAdded 2026). VulnCheck 1H KEV-to-CVE ratio: 1.4% vs 1.9% (1H 2025). Sources: CrowdStrike, VulnCheck 1H-2026, CISA KEV. Confidence: high on reports, medium on interpretation (censoring, catalog choice).

Step 3: Microsoft counts

Answer: November roughly right, October wrong. Key Values: Oct 1,407-1,416 vs 1,474; Nov 1,093-1,094 (1,235 BleepingComputer). The MSRC CVRF feed (Nov 1,766 entries, includes third-party and Mariner items) was not usable for a clean own-product count. Confidence: medium-high, vendor counts vary by method but none reach 1,474.

Step 4: Copeland and Iran

Answer: 14 IDs and two exploited are correct; "all root" and "minutes" are not. France attributes the October supermarket refrigeration attack (about 700 sites, fires reported) to IRGC unit Shahid Kaveh; VulnCheck says its canary hits are not evidence of that campaign. Confidence: high on VulnCheck timing, medium on the Europe-wide scope.

Step 5: Policy and open source

Answer: BOD 26-04, Eclipse launch (21 Apr 2026), 99.95% SLA (MSA: a monthly target on commercially reasonable efforts), curl bounty end (31 Jan 2026) and 26 Oct policy are verified; the CVSS framing and the customer list are not. Confidence: high.
