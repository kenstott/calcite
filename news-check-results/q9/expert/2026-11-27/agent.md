# Fact-check: WDBO/WFTV "Traffic fatalities drop significantly across Florida, nation" (2026-11-25)

**Rating: 2 Pinocchios.** National figures check out; the Florida headline figure does not and is likely wrong or misleading.

## Claims
1. **US H1 2026 deaths 16,750 vs 17,175, -2.5%.** Verified. NHTSA DOT HS 813 875 Table 1. Confidence high. It is a statistical projection, not a count.
2. **VMT +0.8%; rate near-record low.** Verified: VMT +0.8% (12.9B miles); rate 1.02 vs 1.05, second lowest first-half rate after 1.01 in 2014. Confidence high.
3. **Florida 2,155 deaths YTD vs "nearly 3,000" same point last year (-834).** Not verified; implausible. NHTSA's Florida H1 estimate is 1,474 (2025) to 1,439 (2026), -2.4% (Table 3). Florida full-year 2025 was 3,047 (DOT), so ~3,000 by mid-Nov 2025 is high and a -28% drop contradicts NHTSA. The 2,155 source (NHTSA FARS data pull) was not accessible; likely incomplete/lagged FARS reporting for 2026 against complete 2025. Confidence medium. (Also 3,000-2,155 = 845, not 834.)
2b. **Central Florida six counties 550 -> 375 as of Nov 14.** Not checkable here (no accessible county-level FARS data); same lag concern.
4. **Federal focus on reckless/impaired driving.** Attributed statement; not located in the NHTSA report. Not checkable here.

## Misleading
- "Drop significantly" for the nation: only -2.5%, and slowing (Q1 -5.0%, Q2 -0.2%).
- Comparing partial-year 2026 FARS to full 2025 data can manufacture a drop.
- National figures are H1 estimates but sit beside Florida year-to-date November figures with no flag.

## Sources
NHTSA DOT HS 813 875 (crashstats.nhtsa.dot.gov/Api/Public/ViewPublication/813875); WDBO article; Florida 2025 totals via DOT as reported in search results; NHTSA first-half summaries (repairerdrivennews.com, thetruthaboutcars.com).
