# Fact-check: Axios "Congress gets a historic overhaul" (2026-11-20)

## Summary: headline holds; Senate figures conflict across sources (Pinocchio rating: 1, provisional)
I could not read the Axios article (HTTP 403, Cloudflare challenge; no archive snapshot). Axios claims below come from a search summary of the piece and Political Wire's write-up, so this is a partial check.

Claims and verdicts:
- Largest freshman class in decades, both chambers: supported. Quorum (Nov 12): 120 new voting members (16 Senate, 104 House). CBS (Nov 19): 19 new senators. Cronkite (Nov 17): 105 House freshmen. Confidence medium-high.
- House biggest since 1992: supported in direction. Cronkite says 1992 had 108 and this class is 105, so "since 1992" is consistent, though 105 < 108 is odd and it is probably in flux (Raw Story/AURN say 109-113). Counts provisional.
- Senate biggest since 1946: NOT verified. CBS says "largest in almost five decades"; conflicts. Axios Senate count (21, per search summary) does not match CBS 19 or Quorum 16. Low confidence either way.
- Gallup 14% approval, lowest pre-midterm since 1974: confirmed (Gallup Oct 1-19, 2026; 81% disapprove). Gallup does not state whether sample is adults or registered voters; Political Wire/Axios say registered voters, unverified. Not an all-time low (9%, Nov 2013).
- Quote "Americans want change, and they're about to get it": not checkable here (article inaccessible).
- Causes (retirements, primary upsets, gerrymandering, flips, deaths): NPR/Brookings report record departures; not individually verified.

Misleading risk: counts are provisional (uncalled races, Louisiana runoffs Dec 12); the Senate "since 1946" comparison is shaky.

Sources: Political Wire 2026/11/20; Quorum 120th Congress; CBS News meet-the-new-senators-120th-congress; Cronkite News 2026/11/17; PBS NewsHour; Gallup news.gallup.com/poll/716204.
Not checked: the full set of Axios claims, any other quotes.
