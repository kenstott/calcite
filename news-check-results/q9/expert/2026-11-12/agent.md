# Fact check: RISMedia 2026-11-09 pent-up demand piece

**Grade: 1 Pinocchio.** All claims match NAR sources except population: 350M claimed vs 343.1M (FRED POPTHM, Sep 2026).

Misleading context: $35T housing wealth is household real-estate equity (Z.1 $35.8T 2026Q2), not market value ($49.8T); nominal; sales-vs-2000 ignores population growth.

Months supply highest since 2015: secondary sources only (medium). No claims uncheckable.

## Claims
- **Population 281M (2000) to 350M**: Partly wrong. FRED POPTHM: 281.1M Jan 2000; 343.1M Sep 2026. 350M overstates by ~2%. Sources: FRED POPTHM.
- **Payroll jobs 132M to 159M**: Verified. PAYEMS: 131.0-131.9M early 2000; 159.3M Oct 2026. Sources: FRED PAYEMS.
- **Housing wealth $7T to $35T**: Verified, definition unstated. Matches household real-estate equity: $7.1T (2000Q1), $35.8T (2026Q2). Market value is $11.6T to $49.8T. Sources: FRED OEHRENWBSHNO, HOOREVLMHMV (Fed Z.1).
- **Existing sales ~4M vs 5.2M in 2000**: Verified. 3.99M SAAR Sep 2026; FRED EXHOSLUSM495S 2026 monthly 3.93-4.19M. 2000 ~5.2M per NAR. Sources: NAR, FRED.
- **Months' supply highest since 2015**: Verified (medium). NAR Sep 2026: 4.8 months, up from 4.5 a year earlier; secondary coverage says highest since Nov 2015; NAR release itself has no such sentence. Sources: NAR Sep 2026 release; Inman.
- **33 days listing to contract, Sept**: Verified. NAR: 33 days, 31 in Aug, 33 in Sep 2025. Sources: NAR.
- **Sentiment about half 2000 level, worse than Great Recession**: Verified. UMich 48.1 (Sep 2026) vs 107.6 avg-ish 2000 (45%); Nov 2008 low 55.3. Sources: FRED UMCSENT.
- **Yun quotes (affordability, longer on market, buyers reject, boost confidence)**: Verified as attributed. Match NAR press release wording. Sources: NAR release 2026-11.
- **First-time buyers 22%, near historic low; median age 40 record**: Verified. NAR 2026 Profile: 22%, up from record-low 21%; age 40 equals record. Sources: NAR Profile.
- **Sellers median 12 years, longest on record**: Verified. NAR Profile. Sources: NAR.
- **90% buyers used agent, 92% sellers record; highest in four decades**: Verified. NAR Profile. Sources: NAR.
- **Lautz: rent, student debt, childcare**: Verified (childcare via NAR takeaways). Profile release cites rent, student debt; childcare in NAR top-10 takeaways page. Sources: NAR.

Confidence: high except months' supply (medium).
