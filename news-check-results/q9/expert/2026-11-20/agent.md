# Fact-check: Political Wire, "Federal Employees Look to Democrats for a Lifeline" (2026-11-15)

**Rating: 2 Pinocchios (confidence medium).**

## Claims
1. Trump administration sheared 17 percent of federal employees (attributed to NYT). Answer: overstated as net figure. OPM net change since 2025-01-20 is -258,108 (data as of Sep 2026, partly incomplete); against ~2.31M base (OPM Sep 2024 via Fox Business) = ~11.2%. GAO: -256,000 / >11% across 22 agencies Dec 2024-Jan 2026. Pew: ~10% in first year. Gross exits 389,000 (cited by Boston Globe version, "government data"; not independently verified) = ~16.8% of base, which matches 17%. So 17% is plausibly gross separations, not net shrinkage. Confidence medium.
2. Embattled current/former workers look to Democrats. Supported by NYT/Boston Globe/Federal News Network same-day reporting. Medium-high.
3. Democrats have competing priorities (affordability, health care, Iran war); reversal may wait. Characterization/analysis; the Globe version carries it. Not checkable against primary data. The Iran war reference was not independently verified.
4. Democrats newly empowered after midterm gains. Supported by Federal News Network (Democrats won majority in both chambers) - reported, not checked against official results.
Also: the Political Wire post is a short summary of an NYT article; the 17% has no stated base or method.

## Misleading
"Shearing 17 percent" reads as net workforce reduction; official net is ~11%. Not stated what denominator.
