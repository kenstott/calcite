# Fact-check: Fox45/Baltimore Sun, "Mayor Scott blames media, surrounding counties" (Oct 29, 2026)

**Rating: 1 Pinocchio.** One clear error: the article gives 65% of city residents trusting city government "never/only sometimes" in the April 2026 UMBC poll; that 65% is the April 2025 value. April 2026 is 62% (n=666, MOE 3.8%) and trust rose slightly. Source: https://politics.umbc.edu/wp-content/uploads/sites/778/2026/04/Part-1-UMBC-Poll-Apr28.pdf. Confidence: high.

## Claims
- Scott blamed media/counties at Chamber event, quote "forces that don't want to acknowledge that process": not checkable here (no transcript found). Low confidence.
- 61% trust local news; 41% right direction: true (UMBC Part 1). High.
- 27% say neighborhood crime dropped: true as perception (44% same, 21% more per Fishbowl/Fox45 via search; not in Part 1 PDF). Medium.
- Homicides 102 vs 108, nonfatal shootings 259 vs 254 (Oct 28): not found in primary dashboard. Consistent with city statements (Oct 1: 83 vs 98; shootings 226 vs 236) and JNS (~Oct 20: 101 vs 105; 253 vs 247). Medium.
- Vacants 16,000 to ~11,000: supported (11,813 Apr 2026 per WCBM/Banner). Medium-high.
- Downtown lost ~$1B: $1.07B assessed loss since 2020 (secondary: Pulse); article's "last year" timing unconfirmed. Medium.
- Harborplace PILOT pending at Board of Estimates; West Lexington PILOT approved, $160M: consistent with Banner/Brew; project cost quoted variously $160M/$263M/~$300M. Medium.

## Misleading context
Homicide lead narrowed from -26% (Aug 3: 60 vs 81) to about -6% (Oct 28); shootings flipped from down to up. Poll items are perceptions, not crime measures. The stale 65% inflates the distrust backdrop.

Sources: UMBC poll PDF; baltimorecity.gov Oct 1 and Aug 3 statements; JNS; Baltimore Banner; Baltimore Brew; WCBM; Pulse. Board: agent-dashboard.png; report: agent-report.html.
