# Fact-check: sunset-window fatal crashes (Travel and Tour World, 2026-11-22)

**Answer:** Accurate facts, 0 Pinocchios on accuracy. Recomputed from FARS 2024 final data (+/-2h of computed sunset): 8,348 crashes / 8,934 deaths (article 8,334 / 8,921); autumn 26.0/day vs winter 20.1 (+29.4%, article +28.3%); Nov 27.7 vs Jan 18.9 per day (article 27.3 / 19.0); after vs before sunset +58.5% (article +57.8%); Jan after-sunset share 72.4% (72.2%); Nov 554 after / 276 before (543/276). Phoenix and Las Vegas sunset times match the NOAA formula within 2 minutes.

**Flags:** raw counts with no exposure (VMT) adjustment, so "rise" is not a risk increase; autumn sunset coincides with the evening commute and the clock change. The article itself disclaims causation. Minor: 27.3/19.0 = +43.7%, not 43.4% (rounding).

**Not checkable here:** the Lerner & Rowe report itself (not located); the pedestrian-concentration claim was not tested.

**Key values:** FARS n=36,297 crashes, 35,894 with valid time and location; 50 states + DC. Seasons are meteorological (article ~91-day autumn).

**Confidence:** high on the match, medium on decimals (own timezone and sunset approximations).

**Sources:** FARS 2024 National CSV (static.nhtsa.gov); the article URL. Board: agent-dashboard.png; report: agent-report.html.
