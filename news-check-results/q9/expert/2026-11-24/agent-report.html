CNBC housing piece: mostly accurate, 1 Pinocchio

Summary of findings: mostly accurate, with several unverified figures

Of 12 checkable claims in Diana Olick's Nov 23, 2026 CNBC piece, 6 verified cleanly, 1 is approximately verified, and 5 could not be confirmed. The headline facts (16-year-low existing sales, record-low pending sales, rates from about 6.6% to about 7.6%, permits -12.9%, builder sentiment lowest since 2012, 43x wealth gap) match primary sources. Grade: 1 Pinocchio.

  • Possible error: mobility rate of 8.3%; JCHS reports 11.2% for 2024 (definitions may differ).
  • Mortgage-jobs -38% resembles a 2025 nonbank-lender figure sourced to BLS, not clearly the MBA.
  • BLS job-loss counts, 3.8% turnover and Redfin six-month buyer streak were not found. They are unconfirmed here, not shown false.
  • Context: mortgage rates eased to 7.46% (Nov 18), and NAR's Yun said October could be the low point for contracts; I could not confirm whether the piece says so.
  • Limit: I could not read the article verbatim (the fetch tool only returned a summary), so claim extraction and quotes (Wacksman, Zhao, Frost, Bull's "shock to the system") are unchecked against the original text. Quotes are not checkable here.
