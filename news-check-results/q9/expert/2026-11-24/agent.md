# Fact check: CNBC 'Stuck' housing piece (2026-11-23)

**Grade: 1 Pinocchio.**

- Existing-home sales fell to 16-yr low in Oct: Verified. NAR: 3.75M SAAR, -5.5% m/m, -8.8% y/y, lowest since Aug 2010
- Pending sales record low on 25-yr-old index: Verified. NAR: PHSI 63.0, second straight record low, index base 2001
- 30-yr rate ~6.60% July to 7.61% early Oct (MND): Verified (approx.). MND 6.60% Jul 2; 7.59% Oct 7; ~7.6% Sep 30. Exact 7.61% daily not located
- Prices up well over 50% vs pre-pandemic: Plausible, not independently computed. NAR Oct 2026 median $416,700; no pre-2020 base checked here
- Active buyers down six straight months, lowest since post-crisis (Redfin): Not confirmed. Redfin August 2026 'record low' buyers found; six-month phrasing not found
- Turnover 3.8% vs 5% post-crisis: Not confirmed. No source found
- Mobility 8.3%, from 11.1% a decade ago (JCHS): Not confirmed / possible discrepancy. JCHS 2026 reports 11.2% household mobility for 2024; 8.3% not found
- SF permits -13% y/y in Oct (Census): Verified. Census/NAHB: -12.9% y/y, 772k rate
- Builder sentiment lowest since 2012: Verified. NAHB HMI 25 in Nov, lowest since Apr 2012
- Lost ~2,000 RE/leasing and 4,400 resi construction jobs (BLS): Not confirmed. BLS tables not retrieved; NAHB cites other resi-construction figures (-43,600 over 12 mo to mid-2026), which would conflict if same measure
- Mortgage industry jobs -38% from Q2 2021 peak (MBA): Partly. 38% appears for nonbank lenders (Wolf Street/BLS, 2025); MBA attribution unverified
- Homeowner median net wealth ~43x renters (Fed SCF): Verified. 2025 SCF: $458,900 vs $10,600 (secondary reports; Fed release Oct 9, 2026)
