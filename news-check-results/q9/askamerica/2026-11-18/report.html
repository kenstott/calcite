The Benzinga article's claims about Social Security's record FY2026 trust fund deficit, reserves and depletion dates are mostly accurate.

Article under reviewhttps://www.benzinga.com/news/financing/26/11/63279024/social-security-deteriorating-rapidly-says-market-commentator-as-fund-deficit-hit-record-221-billion-in-fiscal-year-2026

Summary

Rating: 1 Pinocchio. The $221 billion deficit and $2.18 trillion reserves are consistent with CRS and SSA OASI data (calendar-year deficits of $200B in 2025 and $243B projected for 2026; OASI reserves $2.02T at end-2007 and $2.20T at end-2008), and the Trustees and AARP figures match. Treasury and SSA fiscal-year tables blocked access, so the FY2026 figures are consistent but not directly confirmed. The framing uses an OASI-only figure to describe all of Social Security and relays an X post as breaking news.
