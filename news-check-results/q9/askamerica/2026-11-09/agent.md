**The Fox45/Baltimore Sun article's claims about Mayor Scott, media, and Baltimore polling and crime are somewhat biased and mostly accurate.**

Rating: 1 Pinocchio.

Source: https://foxbaltimore.com/news/local/sun-mayor-scott-blames-media-surrounding-counties-for-baltimores-negative-image (fetched successfully).

**Warehouse check.** The crime schema lists Baltimore PD (ORI MDBPD0000) but carries only annual agency counts. No table holds year-to-date city homicides, polls, or downtown property values. I used primary sources instead.

**Claims**
- Poll of 666 city residents, April: TRUE (UMBC, n=666, Apr 14-19, 2026).
- 65% distrust city government: PARTIALLY FALSE. April 2026 is 62%; 65% is the April 2025 figure.
- 41% right direction, 45% wrong track, 61% optimistic: TRUE.
- 61% trust local news, 38% little or none: TRUE.
- 2025 county poll (n=628): 42% say better public safety would draw them to the city, 19% say nothing: TRUE.
- 42% Sun, 53% Fox45 (2025): MOSTLY TRUE. The poll measures whether respondents have used each outlet, not their primary source.
- 102 vs 108 homicides, 259 vs 254 nonfatal shootings as of Oct 28: MOSTLY TRUE. The city's Oct 1 release says 83 vs 98 and 226 vs 236. JNS in late October says 101 vs 105 and 253 vs 247. The Oct 28 figures were not directly confirmed, but they are consistent with both.
- Vacants from above 16,000 to just above 11,000: MOSTLY TRUE. The official count is 11,558, and Scott's 16,000 baseline is his own statement.
- 27/44/21% crime perception: NOT CHECKABLE. These figures are not in the April 2026 UMBC Part 1 or Part 2 releases, and the originating poll was not identified.
- Downtown "lost $1 billion in value" due to vacancies: NOT CHECKABLE. No source was found, and the off-site page I read has no such figure.
- West Lexington PILOT, $160 million deal: NOT CHECKABLE. No page on it surfaced and no table carries it.
- Scott quote that Baltimore, Howard and Anne Arundel County residents are the biggest "detractors": NOT CHECKABLE. It is a perception claim with no data behind it.

**Misleading framing.** The headline says Scott blames the media, and his quotes support that. But the same UMBC poll shows 61% of city residents trust local news, which cuts against his thesis. The piece reports that figure without drawing the contrast. The 65% error also overstates distrust.
