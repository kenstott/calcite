The WTKR article's claims about Hampton Roads bridges, air travel and congestion are somewhat biased and mostly accurate.

Summary

Rating: 1 Pinocchio. The core numbers are accurate: poor-condition bridges 2.7% (my NBI calc 2.68%, 34 of 1,271) versus 5.0-5.5% in 2017, and regional air passengers +46% 2015-2024 (my BTS T-100 calc +46.6%). Flaws: the 17 hours of congestion delay is a 2024 figure labeled 2023; an engineer quote that traffic is still 4% below pre-pandemic conflicts with the report, which says travel has returned to pre-pandemic levels; the third Amtrak train is to be replaced by a bus during construction.

Misleading framing

The headline says air travel 'surges in 2026' but all data end in 2024 (the report is the 2025 edition released in 2026). The 46% growth is entirely Norfolk: PHF departures fell from 202,509 to 69,342 (T-100), while ORF grew 61%. The report puts fatalities 26% above 2015 and the fatality rate up 20%; 'cautious optimism' is fair given the decline from the 2021 peak. Cancun service started January 2026, after the data period.
