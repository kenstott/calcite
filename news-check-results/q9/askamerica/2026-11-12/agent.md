Claims checked (11):
- Payroll jobs 132M to 159M: TRUE. PAYEMS was 132.1M in mid-2000 and 159.3M in Oct 2026 (warehouse, econ.fred_indicators).
- Existing-home sales near 4M this year: TRUE. EXHOSLUSM495S ran 3.93M to 4.19M in 2026, 3.99M in Sept (warehouse).
- Sentiment roughly half its 2000 level and worse than the Great Recession: MOSTLY TRUE. UMCSENT was 48.1 in Sept 2026, about 45% of the 2000 average (~107.8). The Great Recession low was 55.3 (Nov 2008), so it is below that low (warehouse). "Half" is loose.
- Population 281M to 350M: MOSTLY TRUE. The Census July 2025 estimate sums to 341.8M (census.pep_components_of_change), so 350M overstates by about 2%. The 281M for 2000 is right.
- "5.2 million homes sold in 2000": MOSTLY TRUE. Contemporary reports give 5.03M (first print) and 5.11M (later). 5.20M was 1999. Current sales are about 20% below either figure, so the point stands.
- Months' supply highest since 2015, and 33 days on market in September: TRUE. NAR reports 4.8 months and 33 days. NAHB calls 4.8 the highest since Nov 2015.
- Housing wealth $7T to $35T: TRUE. It matches owners' equity in real estate ($35.8T in Q2 2026, Fed Z.1 via NAHB). Gross owner-occupied real estate value is $49.8T, so "wealth" here means net equity. I did not independently check the $7T figure for 2000.
- First-time buyers 22% and median age 40; sellers' median tenure 12 years; 90% of buyers and 92% of sellers used an agent: TRUE. All confirmed against NAR's 2026 Profile release. I could not find the "highest in four decades" phrasing verbatim on the release page, but the release says agent use is at its highest.
