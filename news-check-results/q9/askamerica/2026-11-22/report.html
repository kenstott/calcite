The Hudson County View article's claims about Jersey City's 25% crime decline, the JC IMPACT dashboard and the police leadership change are mostly accurate.

Sources: JCPD JC IMPACT dashboard (fetched 2026-11-22), Hudson County View companion articles, FBI CDE agency list in warehouse. Rating: 1 Pinocchio.

Article under reviewhttps://hudsoncountyview.com/jersey-city-crime-data-shows-nearly-25-average-decline-officials-say/

Summary

1 Pinocchio. Every figure I could check matches the city's own dashboard and the percentages are computed correctly. But the headline 'nearly 25% decline in overall crime' is an unweighted average of nine officially chosen categories (23.7%). It is not a measure of overall crime: the nine categories' pooled incident count fell 22.8% (4,930 to 3,804), and the list omits aggravated assaults (765 incidents, the largest violent category on the dashboard) and shoplifting. The 'thefts' figure of 1,926 appears to double-count bike and package thefts that the dashboard says are already inside the Thefts card (1,044).
