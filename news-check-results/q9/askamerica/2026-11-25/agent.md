# The Tommy Christopher Substack transcript of Trump's Nov. 23, 2026 DOJ speech: claims about DC crime, murders, officer deaths and California death-row commutations

Article: https://tommyxtopher.substack.com/p/trump-doj-speech-11-23-2026-full (a transcript with a video, posted Nov 24, 2026; the connector fetch worked but truncates at 20,000 characters, so the tail was searched only for specific figures).

**Rating: 3 Pinocchios.** The headline crime claims are false. The California facts are mostly right but overstate finality.

## Key findings
- **"Crime rate's gone down 84%" (DC), also "more than 80%" and "88%": false.** FBI Return A in the warehouse (crime.cde_reta, DC MPD, ori DCMPD00, Jan-Sep): murder 204 (2023) to 72 (2026) is -65%; robbery 2,783 to 1,491 is -46%; burglary -24%. Versus 2025, murder is -25% but aggravated assault is up 28% (2,007 to 2,560), and it is also above 2023. No category approaches 84%.
- **"Three murders a week, 150 murders": partially false.** DC MPD had 267 murders in 2023 and 181 in 2024. The understated baseline inflates the apparent fall.
- **"Responsible for that" (credit to the room): unsupported.** The decline began in 2024, before the 2025 federal deployment.
- **"3,000 fewer murders last year": true.** Warehouse agency reports: 17,152 (2024) vs 14,008 (2025), a difference of 3,144, consistent with the record national drop reported in 2025.
- **Officer deaths "lowest in the history of our country": partially false.** NLEOMF: 111 line-of-duty deaths in 2025 vs 148, the lowest in about 80 years, comparable to 1943 (94). FBI Return A felonious killings were 64 in 2025 vs 58 in 2024, a different measure that did not fall.
- **Newsom "granted clemency" to 548: mostly true.** 548 commuted Nov. 5, but about 400 multi-felony cases need approval from four of seven California Supreme Court justices (CalMatters). Named killers (Ng, Kraft, Turner, Davis) are reported as covered; the "110 child killers / 100 rapist-murderers" tallies were not found anywhere.
- **Day-one executive order on killing officers: mostly true.** The order tells the Attorney General to seek the death penalty (Sec. 3(b)); it cannot ensure a sentence.
- **$5,000 Trump Account limit: true.** DOJ "nearly $150 million to hire over 1,000 officers": true (a DOJ release with that title exists; its date was not read).
- **"1.2 million arrests, violent crime arrests up 90%": unsupported.** Only administration-sourced FBI figures were found (arrests doubled in 17 field offices); no 90% figure.
- Guests' testimony: Jason Davis on voters (mostly true: CalMatters describes one 2016 vote, not two), Sandy Friend on erased sentence and non-notification (mostly true: not final, families reportedly not notified), Gloria Vega on the Oct. 8, 2016 killing (true).

## Misleading even where facts check out
Percentages are given with no category or base year; the murder baseline is understated; "granted clemency" omits pending court review; the "responsible for" credit is unevidenced.

## Limits
Governor's clemency list PDF not parsed (Rhoades, Bracamontes not confirmed on it); Memphis and New Orleans claims not tested; Return A is revised by the FBI and 2021 DC data shows a reporting gap; Pinocchio rating is my own synthesis (the report engine scored the speaker as dishonest, honesty 25, bias 90, for this article only).
