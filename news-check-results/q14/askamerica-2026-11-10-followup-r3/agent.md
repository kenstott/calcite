The Investing.com (Reuters) article's claims about first-half 2026 US traffic deaths, rate and trend are balanced and mostly accurate.

Rating: 1 Pinocchio (mostly accurate). Honesty 82, bias 0 (engine scores, this article only).

Method: Investing.com returned 403. I read the Reuters text on a syndicated copy (wiky.com). The NHTSA press release also returned 403, so NHTSA's own figures were read only through search summaries and Carscoops (which quotes NHTSA). The warehouse (transport.fatal_crashes, FARS final counts) covers 2008-2024 only.

Claims:
1. Central: "lowest rate since 2014" (H1 2026 rate 1.02 per 100M VMT). Mostly true. It is an NHTSA early estimate, relayed by Reuters; not recomputed.
2. 16,750 deaths, -2.5% vs 17,175, lowest since 2015. True. The arithmetic gives -2.47%, and the figures match the search summaries of NHTSA's release.
3. "On pace for fifth straight yearly decline". Mostly true. Warehouse FARS shows 2022 42,721, 2023 41,025 and 2024 39,254, each below the prior year. 2025 and 2026 rest on NHTSA estimates, and "on pace" is a projection.
4. 2021 deaths +10.8% to 43,230, the most since 2005. True. Warehouse: 43,230 vs 39,007 in 2020 is +10.8%. 2005 is outside the warehouse window.
5. 2025 deaths lowest since 2019, rate 1.10, second-lowest ever. Mostly true. Carscoops quoting NHTSA says the same. The warehouse stops at 2024, which is consistent.
6. Deaths -5% in Q1 2026 and -0.2% in Q2. Mostly true. NHTSA's July Q1 estimate was -4.3%; -5% appears to be a later revision.
7. Bicyclist deaths +4% to 1,148 in 2025. Mostly true. Carscoops quoting NHTSA matches. The warehouse has no bicyclist column.
(The $340B/$1.37T cost study and the expert explanation for pandemic-era deaths were not graded separately.)

Misleading-context flags:
- The headline claims the lowest rate since 2014, while the body's death count is only the lowest since 2015. These are different measures.
- All 2025-26 numbers are early estimates subject to revision.
- NSC's H1 estimate (18,460 deaths, rate 1.12) uses a different death window than NHTSA and is not comparable.
