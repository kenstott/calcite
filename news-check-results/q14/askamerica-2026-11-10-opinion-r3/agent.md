# The Investing.com (Reuters) article's claims about first-half 2026 US traffic deaths, the fatality rate and the pandemic spike

**Verdict: 1 Pinocchio (minor issues).**

Article: https://investing.com/news/stock-market-news/us-traffic-deaths-fall-in-first-half-of-2026-lowest-rate-since-2014-5016323 (direct fetch returned HTTP 403). The text was taken from identical Reuters copies at SRN News and WIKY.

## Claims checked
- **2021 deaths:** the piece says they "jumped 10.8% in 2021 to 43,230, the most since 2005". FARS in the warehouse (transport.fatal_crashes) gives 39,007 in 2020 and 43,230 in 2021, which is +10.83%. TRUE.
- **Fifth straight yearly decline:** FARS shows declines in 2022 (42,721), 2023 (41,025) and 2024 (39,254). NHTSA reports 2025 lower still. 2026 is a projection, so the fifth straight decline is a forecast. MOSTLY TRUE.
- **H1 2026 deaths:** 16,750, down 2.5% from 17,175, lowest since 2015. The arithmetic checks (-2.47%). FARS annual totals are consistent (2015 was 35,484). The figure is an NHTSA early estimate, confirmed only through Reuters copies. NHTSA's earlier H1 2025 estimate was 17,140, so 17,175 is a revision. MOSTLY TRUE.
- **Headline:** the fatality rate is the lowest since 2014, at 1.02 per 100 million vehicle miles. This rests on the NHTSA early estimate as relayed by Reuters. MOSTLY TRUE.
- **2025 full year:** lowest deaths since 2019, rate 1.10, second-lowest ever. The warehouse stops at 2024; coverage by Roads & Bridges is consistent. MOSTLY TRUE.
- **Q1 and Q2 2026:** the piece says -5% in Q1 and -0.2% in Q2. NHTSA's Q1 early estimate was -4.3%, so the 5% is plausibly a revision. MOSTLY TRUE.
- **Pandemic causes** (less crowded roads, fewer perceived tickets, impaired driving at home): attributed to unnamed experts and hedged. Plausible but not causally established. MOSTLY TRUE.
- **US pandemic rate worse than other developed nations:** consistent with published comparisons, not independently queried. MOSTLY TRUE.
- **NHTSA 2023 cost study ($30B to taxpayers, $340B to society, $1.37T with quality of life):** matches NHTSA's published study, not re-opened. MOSTLY TRUE.
- **Bicyclist deaths up 4% to 1,148 in 2025:** NOT CHECKABLE HERE. The warehouse has no bicyclist measure, and the 4% and 1,148 appear only in the Reuters article itself. NHTSA crashstats publication 813833 (the Q1 2026 report) has no match for either.

## Flags
- The H1 2026 figures are early estimates, and "fifth straight year" is a projection.
- An NHTSA summary reportedly calls 1.02 the second-lowest half-year rate on record. "Lowest since 2014" is accurate but understates the record context.
- Q2 deaths fell only 0.2%, so the improvement is slowing. The article reports this, but the headline does not.
- NSC's separate H1 2026 count (18,460, -2%, rate 1.12) uses a different definition and is not comparable to NHTSA's.
- I did not open the primary NHTSA H1 2026 release. NHTSA and NSC pages returned 403 or could not be found.
