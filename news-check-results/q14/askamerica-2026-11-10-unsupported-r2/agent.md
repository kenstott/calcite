**Verdict (provisional, headline only): 1 Pinocchio.**

- **"US traffic deaths fall in first half of 2026": mostly true.**
  - The National Safety Council (NSC) preliminary figure is 18,460 deaths for the first half, down 2%. Its rate is 1.12 per 100 million vehicle miles, down 3.4%.
  - NHTSA's first-quarter 2026 projection is 7,770 deaths, down 4.3% from 8,120. This is the 16th straight quarterly decline (NHTSA DOT HS 813 833).
  - All 2025 and 2026 figures are projections, not final counts.
  - I did not find NHTSA's own first-half 2026 release.
- **"Lowest rate since 2014": partially false / imprecise.**
  - NHTSA's first-quarter 2026 rate is 0.99 per 100 million vehicle miles.
  - That ties the first quarter of 2014 (0.99), and 2010 and 2011 were lower (0.98). NHTSA calls it the second-lowest first-quarter rate on record.
  - NSC's rate uses a different method (100-day window, private roads included), so it cannot be compared with NHTSA's series.

The warehouse table transport.fatal_crashes (FARS) covers 2008 to 2024 only. It matches NHTSA's 2014 total of 32,744, but it has no 2025 or 2026 data, so I could not test H1 2026 against it. Everything on 2026 comes from NHTSA and NSC publications.
