**Materially misleading context (facts are right, framing is thin):**
- The 2026 and 2025 figures are early estimates, not final counts.
- The decline nearly stalled: -0.2% in Q2 versus -5% in Q1.
- "Lowest rate since 2014" is a first-half-only rate comparison. The full-year 2025 rate of 1.10 is second-lowest on record, behind 1.08 in 2014.
- The headline mixes a count record (lowest since 2015) with a rate record (lowest since 2014). The body text separates them.
- The cost figures describe 2019 crashes.
- Warehouse FARS coverage is 2008-2024, and 2008 is only partly loaded. The warehouse cannot confirm any 2025 or 2026 figure.
