The Investing.com (Reuters) article's claims about US traffic deaths, fatality rate and trend in first half 2026 are too few to rate.

Provisional rating: 1 Pinocchio.

Note: the connector fetch returned 403. The article text was read only via a WebFetch summary (Reuters, David Shepardson, Nov 10, 2026), so claims are paraphrased.

Claims
1. (Central) H1 2026 deaths fell 2.5% to 16,750 from 17,175. NOT CHECKABLE. The warehouse FARS table ends 2024, and no NHTSA H1 2026 release was found. NSC reports 18,460 (down 2%) but says it is not comparable to NHTSA. NHTSA's earlier H1 2025 estimate was 17,140, and NHTSA's Q1 2026 estimate was 7,770, down 4.3%. These are early estimates.
2. Rate 1.02 per 100M VMT, lowest since 2014. NOT CHECKABLE. NHTSA's Q1 2026 rate was 0.99. Sources describe it variously as the second lowest Q1 on record or the lowest since 2014. This is an early estimate.
3. On pace for a fifth straight yearly decline. MOSTLY TRUE. FARS shows declines in 2022 (42,721), 2023 (41,025) and 2024 (39,254). 2025 and 2026 are not in the warehouse and are projections.
4. 2021 deaths up 10.8% to 43,230, most since 2005. MOSTLY TRUE. FARS gives 39,007 for 2020 and 43,230 for 2021, which is +10.8%. "Since 2005" was not tested.
5. Q1 down 5%, Q2 down only 0.2%. NOT CHECKABLE. NHTSA's Q1 estimate was -4.3%. Q2 was not found.
6. Not checked: 2025 totals, bicyclist deaths up 4% to 1,148, the 2023 NHTSA crash-cost study, and expert causes of the 2021 rise.

Misleading flag: the headline presents an early projection as settled. The 5% then 0.2% quarterly pattern suggests the decline is stalling. Absolute deaths remain far above the 2019 level of 36,355.
