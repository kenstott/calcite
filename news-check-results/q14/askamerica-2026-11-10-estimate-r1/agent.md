The Investing.com article's claims about US traffic deaths in the first half of 2026, the fatality rate and bicyclist deaths are balanced and mostly accurate; 2 claims could not be confirmed.

**Rating: 1 Pinocchio.**

Method: Investing.com returned 403. I read the Fox 5 copy of the Reuters story (https://www.fox5ny.com/news/traffic-deaths-national-highway-traffic-safety-administration). Warehouse: transport.fatal_crashes (FARS 2008-2024). Primary: NHTSA Crash Stats DOT HS 813 833 (Q1 2026 early estimate). I did not locate NHTSA's own H1 2026 release.

Claims:
- H1 2026 deaths 16,750, down 2.5% from 17,175: TRUE (estimate). 17,175 equals NHTSA's Q1+Q2 2025 projections (8,120+9,055). 16,750/17,175 = -2.47%.
- Lowest first half since 2015: TRUE. H1 2015 = 7,370+8,823 = 16,193, below 16,750.
- Rate 1.02 per 100M VMT, lowest since 2014: MOSTLY TRUE. Q1 2026 rate was 0.99; H1 2014 about 1.01. NHTSA notes 2010-11 Q1 rates were lower, so this is not a record.
- Q1 -5%, Q2 -0.2%: MOSTLY TRUE. NHTSA's July Q1 report said -4.3%; the H1 release evidently revised it. The split reconciles to 16,750.
- 2021: +10.8% to 43,230, highest since 2005: TRUE (warehouse FARS 39,007 to 43,230). 2005 not loaded.
- Fifth straight annual decline "on track": MOSTLY TRUE. FARS 2022-2024 declines (42,721; 41,025; 39,254), 2025 projected 36,640, H1 2026 down 2.5%. Forward-looking.
- 2025 rate 1.10, second-lowest ever: MOSTLY TRUE (2014 was 1.08 in NHTSA's table). Full-history ranking not verified.
- Bicyclist deaths +4% to 1,148 in 2025: UNSUPPORTED. Only secondary snippets; NHTSA report 813800 has no bicyclist table; the warehouse has no bicyclist measure.
- Expert attribution (empty roads, less enforcement): UNSUPPORTED. Commonly cited explanation, untested here.
- NHTSA 2023 cost study ($340B, $30B taxpayers, $1.37T): UNSUPPORTED on a primary page (nhtsa.gov and rosap returned 403, codot 404). Search summaries corroborate it as the 2019-data report published February 2023.

Misleading-framing flags: all H1 figures are projections NHTSA says will be revised. Improvement is flattening (Q2 -0.2%). NSC's separate H1 estimate (18,460, -2%, rate 1.12) uses a different definition (100-day, includes private roads) and is not comparable. No false claims found.
