Summary

Rating: 1 Pinocchio. Warehouse FARS confirms 2021 deaths of 43,230 (+10.8% from 39,007) and declines in 2022-2024. H1 2026 NHTSA figures could not be read from NHTSA (403) but are consistent with NHTSA's Q1 2026 estimate (7,770, -4.3%, rate 0.99). Misleading element: the headline compares a half-year rate with annual rates, and first-half rates run seasonally lower. The Q1 revision from -4.3% to -5% and the 17,175 baseline (earlier NHTSA H1 2025 estimate: 17,140) are plausible revisions.
