**Misleading or omitted context**
- All 2025 and 2026 figures are NHTSA statistical projections subject to revision. The piece does not say so.
- NHTSA calls the H1 rate the second-lowest half-year rate on record, so "lowest since 2014" is not a record.
- The National Safety Council's separate estimate (18,460 H1 deaths, -2%) uses a different definition and is not mentioned.
- The headline cites a rate "since 2014" while the body cites a death count "since 2015". Both are correct, but they are different measures.
