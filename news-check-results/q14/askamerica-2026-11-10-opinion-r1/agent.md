# The Investing.com/Reuters article's claims about first-half 2026 US traffic deaths, the 2025 fatality rate and 2021 pandemic deaths are balanced and mostly accurate.

Grade: 0 Pinocchios (Washington Post scale). Tool score: honesty 89 (mostly honest), bias 0.

Source note: the Investing.com page returned HTTP 403 to the connector, so I read the full Reuters (David Shepardson) text syndicated on wiky.com. The NHTSA H1 release page also returned 403; I read NHTSA's Q1 2026 report (DOT HS 813 833) instead.

Claims checked
- True: H1 2026 deaths of 16,750, down 2.5% from 17,175. NHTSA's table gives 8,120 + 9,055 = 17,175 for H1 2025, and 16,750/17,175 is -2.47%. These are projections, not final counts.
- True: the rate of 1.02 and "lowest rate since 2014". The H1 2014 rate is about 1.01 (Q1 0.99, Q2 1.03), and every year from 2015 to 2025 is higher.
- True: "lowest number since 2015". H1 2015 was 16,193, H1 2016 was 17,717, and H1 2019 was 17,025.
- True: "on pace for fifth straight yearly decline". FARS in the warehouse has 43,230 (2021), 42,721, 41,025 and 39,254 (2024); 2025 is 36,640 (NHTSA projection). The article hedges this correctly.
- True: 2021 deaths of 43,230, up 10.8% from 39,007 in 2020. This matches the warehouse (transport.fatal_crashes). "Most since 2005" matches FARS history (2005 was about 43,510), which is outside the warehouse window.
- Mostly true: 2025 was the lowest since 2019 (36,640 vs 36,355). The rate of 1.10 matches the NHTSA table. "Second-lowest in history" is NHTSA's wording and I did not recompute it.
- Mostly true: Q1 -5% and Q2 -0.2%. NHTSA's July report said Q1 was -4.3%, so the -5% looks like a revision. The implied Q2 change is consistent with the H1 total.
- Mostly true: bicyclist deaths up 4% to 1,148 in 2025. I found this only in secondary coverage, and the "highest in four decades in 2023" part is unverified.
- Not graded: the experts' pandemic explanations, the claim about the US rate versus other developed nations, and the 2023 NHTSA cost study ($30B / $340B / $1.37T).

Misleading check: not materially misleading. All 2025-26 figures are NHTSA early projections that will be revised, and the warehouse FARS data ends at 2024. The warehouse can therefore confirm only the historic baseline, not the 2025-26 numbers.
