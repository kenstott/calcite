**The Investing.com (Reuters) article's claims about first-half 2026 US traffic deaths, fatality rate and trend are balanced and mostly accurate.**

Grade: 0 to 1 Pinocchio.

Sourcing: investing.com returned HTTP 403. I read the same Reuters story (David Shepardson, Nov 10, 2026) on AOL. nhtsa.gov and nsc.org also returned 403, so the NHTSA first-half figures were not parsed from NHTSA itself. They come from Reuters and from search-result copies of the NHTSA release.

Warehouse results (transport.fatal_crashes_by_state_year, FARS annual fatalities):
- 2020: 39,007. 2021: 43,230. 2022: 42,721. 2023: 41,025. 2024: 39,254. 2019: 36,355. 2014: 32,744. 2015: 35,484.
- "Jumped 10.8% in 2021 to 43,230": TRUE. 43,230 / 39,007 = +10.83%. "Most since 2005" cannot be tested because the warehouse starts in 2008.
- Annual declines 2022, 2023 and 2024 are verified. 2025 is not in the warehouse; the article says it also fell. "On pace for fifth straight year of declines" counts 2022 to 2026, so it is a forecast. Mostly true.

Claims not testable in the warehouse (NHTSA early estimates, i.e. projections):
- 16,750 first-half deaths, down 2.5% from 17,175. The arithmetic checks (-2.47%).
- A rate of 1.02 per 100 million vehicle miles, "lowest since 2014". Secondary copies of the NHTSA release report this.
- Deaths fell 5% in Q1 and 0.2% in Q2. NHTSA's earlier Q1 release said -4.3%.
- 2025 deaths were lowest since 2019, with a rate of 1.10.

Not verified and left ungraded: bicyclist deaths (+4% to 1,148), the 2023 NHTSA cost study, and the unnamed experts' pandemic explanations.

Materially misleading context:
1. The headline rate benchmark (2014) differs from the count benchmark (2015), and both are early model-based estimates that NHTSA will revise.
2. The improvement is front-loaded: Q2 fell only 0.2%.
3. The National Safety Council's separate first-half estimate (18,460 deaths, rate 1.12) uses a different definition (100-day window, private roads), so it is not comparable. The article does not mix the two.
4. Data defect: the warehouse's 2008 FARS total is 21,917, far below the roughly 37,000 expected, so 2008 and 2009 look incomplete. I did not use them.
