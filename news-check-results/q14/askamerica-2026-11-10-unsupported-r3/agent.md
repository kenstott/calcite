# Fact-check: "US traffic deaths fall in first half of 2026, lowest rate since 2014" (Investing.com / Reuters, Nov 10 2026)

**Rating: 0 to 1 Pinocchio (essentially accurate, with caveats).**

Investing.com returned HTTP 403. I graded the identical Reuters story (David Shepardson) from its AOL copy. NHTSA and NSC pages also returned 403, so the 2025 and 2026 figures rest on copies quoting NHTSA, not a parsed NHTSA table.

## Claims
| # | Claim | Verdict | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 (central) | H1 2026 deaths at lowest rate since 2014 | Mostly true | NHTSA early estimate (projection): 1.02 per 100M VMT vs 1.05 a year earlier. "Since 2014" could not be recomputed. Another outlet calls it the second-lowest half-year rate on record. |
| 2 | Deaths -2.5% to 16,750 from 17,175 | True | 16,750/17,175 = -2.47%. Matches search coverage of NHTSA DOT HS 813875. |
| 3 | Lowest number since 2015 | Mostly true | Warehouse FARS annual 2014 = 32,744 and 2015 = 35,484 are consistent. The half-year series is not loaded. |
| 4 | On pace for fifth straight annual decline | True | Warehouse FARS: 2021 43,230, 2022 42,721, 2023 41,025, 2024 39,254. NHTSA 2025 estimate 36,640. That is four declines through 2025, so 2026 would be the fifth (projection). |
| 5 | 2025 lowest since 2019; rate 1.10, second-lowest ever | True | FARS 2019 = 36,355, below the 36,640 estimate for 2025. 2020-2024 are all higher. NHTSA's rate of 1.10 is confirmed in search coverage. |
| 6 | Q1 -5%, Q2 -0.2% | Mostly true | NHTSA's Q1 report said -4.3%, so -5% is probably a revised figure. Q2 comes only from secondary copies. |
| 7 | Bicyclist deaths +4% to 1,148 in 2025 | Mostly true, unconfirmed | Repeated by other outlets, but no NHTSA table found. |
| 8 | 2021 deaths +10.8% to 43,230, most since 2005 | True | Warehouse 43,230 vs 39,007 = +10.83%. Exact match. |
| 9 | Experts: perceived lax enforcement and impaired driving | Mostly true | Attributed causal claim, consistent with NHTSA/NSC commentary. Not testable in the warehouse. |
| 10 | 2023 NHTSA study: $30B to taxpayers, $340B to society | Mostly true | Matches the published study as I know it. I did not re-fetch the study. |

## Misleading-context flags
- All 2025 and 2026 numbers are early estimates that get revised. The H1 2025 base moved from 17,140 to 17,175.
- The headline says "lowest rate since 2014", while the body says "lowest number since 2015". These are different measures, so the phrasing is loose but not wrong.
- Q2 fell only 0.2%, so the fifth straight decline is not assured. The article hedges with "on pace".

The warehouse (transport.fatal_crashes_by_state_year, FARS 2008-2024) covers only the historical comparisons. The 2025 and 2026 figures are outside it.
