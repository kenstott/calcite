**The Investing.com article's claims about first-half 2026 US traffic deaths, fatality rate and pandemic-era surge are balanced and mostly accurate; 1 claim could not be confirmed.**

Grade: 1 Pinocchio.

Investing.com returned HTTP 403, so I used the Fox 5 copy of the same Reuters story.

- Warehouse (FARS, transport.fatal_crashes_by_state_year): 2021 deaths 43,230 vs 39,007 in 2020 (+10.8%), true. 2022-2024 fall each year (42,721, 41,025, 39,254), consistent with "fifth consecutive decline" if 2025 and 2026 decline. FARS stops at 2024.
- First-half 2026 figures (16,750, -2.5% from 17,175, rate 1.02): these are NHTSA early estimates. I could not find an NHTSA first-half 2026 page. They are consistent with NHTSA's Q1 2026 estimate (7,770, -4.3%, rate 0.99) and the 2025 rate of 1.10 (second-lowest on record, which matches NHTSA). Graded mostly true.
- Q1 "-5%" vs the earlier NHTSA "-4.3%", and H1 2025 17,175 vs the earlier 17,140: plausibly revisions.
- Bicyclist deaths +4% to 1,148 in 2025: unsupported. No warehouse table or independent source carries it.
- The $340B / $1.37T crash cost study and the pandemic-cause attributions were not separately verified.
- Misleading? No. The piece itself discloses that the Q2 improvement slowed to -0.2%. Its "lowest rate since 2014" headline sits next to "lowest H1 deaths since 2015" in the body, but that is a rate vs count difference, not an error.
