# Fact check: US traffic deaths fall in H1 2026 (Investing.com/Reuters)
Rating: 0 Pinocchios. Full detail in agent-report.html; board in agent-dashboard.png.

## Step 1: Headline figures
**Answer:** Supported. 16,750 deaths (-2.5% from 17,175); rate 1.02 per 100M VMT.
**Key Values:** NHTSA 813 833: H1 2025 = 8,120+9,055 = 17,175; 16,750/17,175 = -2.47%. H1 2025 VMT 1,630.7B; with VMT +0.8% the 2026 rate recomputes to 1.019. Derived H1 2014 rate ~1.01, H1 2015 ~1.06; H1 2015 deaths 16,193.
**Sources:** crashstats.nhtsa.dot.gov ViewPublication/813833; NSC release (VMT +0.8%).
**Confidence:** medium-high; NHTSA's H1 2026 report/press release not retrievable (403, not indexed).

## Step 2: Quarters and trend
**Answer:** Q1 -5% / Q2 -0.2% reproduce 16,750 (7,714+9,037); NHTSA July Q1 estimate was -4.3% (likely revised). Fifth straight yearly decline supported (2022 -1.2, 2023 -4.0, 2024 -4.3, 2025 -6.7%).
**Sources:** 813 833 Table 1. **Confidence:** medium.

## Step 3: 2025 and other claims
**Answer:** 2025 deaths 36,640 (lowest since 2019); rate 1.10 second-lowest ever behind 1.08 in 2014 (813 800). Bicyclists 1,103 to 1,148, +4% (813 829). 2021 +10.8% to 43,230 (813 833). Costs $340B/$30B confirmed (813 403, 2019 data published 2023); $1.37T consistent with "nearly $1.4T".
**Confidence:** high except cost total (medium).

## Not checkable here
NHTSA H1 2026 primary document; 2023 bicyclist 40-year high (secondary only); "experts" pandemic attribution; "rate rose more than other developed nations".

## Misleading-adjacent
Projections not counts; Q2 essentially flat; headline "rate since 2014" vs deaths "since 2015"; NSC reports 18,460 deaths (-2%), rate 1.12, different definition; cost study concerns 2019 crashes.
