# The Investing.com article's claims about first-half 2026 US traffic deaths, the fatality rate and bicyclist deaths are balanced and mostly accurate; 2 claims could not be confirmed.

**Grade: 1 Pinocchio.**

Investing.com returned HTTP 403, so claims were taken from the Fox 5 Atlanta copy of the same Reuters story. I did not read the Investing.com text itself.

## Verdicts
- **H1 2026 deaths (central claim): mostly true.** 16,750 estimated, down 2.5% from 17,175. The 17,175 base matches NHTSA's Q1 brief (8,120 + 9,055) and 16,750/17,175 is -2.5%. The 16,750 itself is a projection. NHTSA's six-month release was not found, so it is not primary-confirmed.
- **Rate 1.02, lowest since 2014: mostly true.** NHTSA's table puts 2014 at about 1.01, 2015 at about 1.06, 2019 at about 1.07 and H1 2025 at 1.05. 1.02 is not primary-confirmed.
- **Lowest first-half toll since 2015: true.** H1 2015 was 16,193; 2016 was 17,717; 2019 was 17,025; 2020 was 17,065.
- **Fifth consecutive annual decline: true.** FARS (warehouse) shows 43,230 (2021), 42,721, 41,025, 39,254, then the NHTSA 2025 projection of 36,640. That is four straight declines, and 2026 would be the fifth.
- **Q1 -5%, Q2 -0.2%: mostly true.** NHTSA's July Q1 brief says -4.3%. The article's split is consistent with a later revision (Q1 of about 7,713), but I did not see the revised split.
- **2025 lowest since 2019: true.** 36,640 projected against 36,355 in 2019.
- **2025 rate 1.10, second lowest in history: true.** NHTSA says this is behind only 1.08 in 2014.
- **2021: +10.8% to 43,230, highest since 2005: true.** The warehouse shows 39,007 to 43,230.
- **Bicyclists +4% to 1,148 in 2025, with 2023 the multi-decade high: mostly true.** NHTSA's fact sheet gives 1,103 for 2024 and 1,173 for 2023. 1,103 x 1.04 is about 1,147, consistent with 1,148. The "four decades" span and the 2025 figure were not confirmed from NHTSA text.
- **Crash costs of $340B, $30B to taxpayers and $1.37T: true.** NHTSA DOT HS 813 403 states all three figures. The study covers 2019 crashes, which the article does not say.
- **Experts blamed emptier roads and less ticketing: unsupported.** The experts are unnamed and no source was opened.
- **US rate rose more than in other developed nations: unsupported.** The warehouse is US-only and no international source was opened.

## Misleading-framing flags
- The headline "lowest rate since 2014" is a rate. The body's "lowest first-half toll since 2015" is a count. Both hold, but they read as inconsistent.
- Every 2025 and 2026 figure is an NHTSA statistical projection subject to revision.
- Q2 slowed to -0.2%, so the fifth annual decline depends on the second half.
- The National Safety Council's separate estimate (18,460, -2%, rate 1.12) uses a different definition and is not comparable to NHTSA's.
