# The Investing.com/Reuters article's claims about first-half 2026 US traffic deaths, fatality rate and pandemic-era context are balanced and mostly accurate.

**Rating: 1 Pinocchio (mostly accurate).**

Investing.com and the NHTSA release both returned HTTP 403. I checked the claims on Reuters' original copy (AOL, David Shepardson, Nov 10 2026), which carries the same headline, and cross-checked with web search.

| Claim | Verdict | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| H1 2026 deaths fell 2.5% to 16,750 from 17,175 (central) | True (NHTSA early estimate) | -2.47%; matches NHTSA via Reuters |
| Rate 1.02 per 100M VMT, lowest since 2014 | Mostly true | 1.02 matches; "since 2014" is Reuters' wording, not confirmed from NHTSA tables |
| Deaths lowest since 2015 | True | Warehouse FARS annual totals: 2014 32,744 and 2015 35,484 are consistent; half-year series not in warehouse |
| Fifth straight annual decline on pace | True (projection) | FARS 2021 43,230, 2022 42,721, 2023 41,025, 2024 39,254; 2025 reported lower; 2026 would be the fifth |
| 2021: +10.8% to 43,230, highest since 2005 | True | Warehouse transport.fatal_crashes: 43,230 vs 39,007 in 2020 = +10.8% |
| 2025 lowest since 2019, rate 1.10 | Mostly true | FARS 2019 36,355 is below 2020-2024; 2025 rate from NHTSA via Reuters only |
| Q1 -5%, Q2 -0.2% | Mostly true | NHTSA's Q1 release said -4.3%; the figure was revised |
| Bicyclist deaths +4% to 1,148 in 2025 | Mostly true | Not in warehouse; relayed from NHTSA via Reuters |
| Experts' pandemic causes | Mostly true | Unnamed experts; plausible, untested here |
| 2023 NHTSA cost study ($340B, $30B) | True | Matches the NHTSA cost report |

## Misleading framing
- The headline's "lowest since 2014" applies to the rate. The death count is lowest since 2015.
- The Q2 slowdown to -0.2% suggests the first-half decline is front-loaded. The headline does not convey this.
- All 2026 figures are preliminary NHTSA estimates and may be revised. The H1 2025 comparison figure was previously published as 17,140 and now stands at 17,175.
- The National Safety Council's first-half 2026 estimate (18,460 deaths, -2%) uses a different definition and is not comparable.

## Data notes
- Warehouse: transport.fatal_crashes (FARS 2008-2024; 2008 is partial). It does not cover 2025 or 2026.
- Sources: Reuters via AOL; NHTSA press release and NSC (web search only).
