# Fact-check: Justice in Aging "From DC" 11/06/2026

**Overall: 1 Pinocchio (mostly accurate; one unverified figure and omitted context).**

## Claims
1. CMS released final CY2027 PFS rule implementing H.R.1 immigrant eligibility limits - Verified (CMS-1848-F announced Nov 4, 2026; Federal Register publication Nov 9 per Mundonow). Confidence medium-high; FR page itself blocked (302), CMS/HHS pages 403.
2. Refugees, asylees, TPS holders, trafficking/DV survivors lose eligibility - Verified in substance (secondary: Medicare Rights, KFF, Mundonow).
3. Terminations begin Feb 1, 2027 - Verified by Medicare Rights/Mundonow/Goodsurance; KFF says "no later than Jan 4, 2027" - sources differ. Medium.
4. ~300,000 preliminary notices in Nov 2026 - Verified as SSA estimate (Mundonow). Not all will lose coverage. Medium.
5. SEP for those gaining status; wrongly flagged people must correct with SSA - consistent with sources; rule text not accessed. Medium-low.
6. MA insurers cutting supplemental dental for 2027 - Verified (Healthcare Dive citing Leerink: UnitedHealthcare ~70% of members, Centene 45%, CVS/Aetna 36%, Elevance 23%; Humana modestly increased dental). High.
7. 2026 also saw leaner MA supplemental benefits (Milliman link) - not opened; low-medium.
8. NFHA released 2026 Fair Housing Trends Report; for first time HUD did not provide requested data - NFHA page says HUD and DOJ "did not provide NFHA with the data requested". "First time" not independently confirmed (2025 report thanked HUD for data). Medium.
9. HUD efforts to defund nonprofits forced cuts/staff reductions/reserves/loans - NFHA reports FIP funding zeroed, 76% of respondents reported funding disruptions, grant delays up to 6 months. Largely supported; "HUD's efforts" framing and loans detail not verified. Medium.
10. Nonprofit groups handle >75% of fair housing complaints - NOT confirmed. NFHA text gives DOJ+community orgs 20,805 of complaints in 2025 vs 24,001 in 2024, no share. Unverified (2024 total 32,321 in the 2025 report; private-org share there is not shown in what I read).
11. Older adults incl. disabled protected by Fair Housing Act - true in general (FHA protects disability, not age per se; piece says "many older adults" - acceptable).
12. Webinar Nov 18; links; sign-up - not factual disputes; not checked.

## Misleading context
- "Thousands ... will lose coverage" is right (CMS Actuary ~32,000, ~0.05% of enrollees), but a reader seeing only "300,000 notices" may think far more lose coverage.
- "Major insurers" cutting dental: true for UNH, Centene, CVS, Elevance; Humana not (reports on Humana benefit cuts are generally not dental).
- The 75% figure is the one claim I could not substantiate.

## Limits
No quotes attributed to named people in the piece. Federal Register final-rule text not read. Primary NFHA PDF not read fully.
