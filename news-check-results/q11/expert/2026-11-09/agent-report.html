Summary of findings

The newsletter earns 1 Pinocchio. Its Medicare claims (final CY2027 physician fee schedule rule CMS-1848-F; about 300,000 preliminary notices in November 2026; terminations from Feb 1, 2027) match secondary reporting, and the Medicare Advantage dental-cut claim matches Healthcare Dive/Leerink. The claim that nonprofit groups handle >75% of fair housing complaints could not be confirmed in NFHA's report text.

  • The 300,000 is an SSA estimate of notices; the CMS Actuary expects about 32,000 actual losses (about 0.05% of enrollees). The piece says "thousands", which is right, but the 300,000 can mislead.
  • Termination date: Feb 1, 2027 per Medicare Rights/Mundonow; KFF says no later than Jan 4, 2027.
  • Dental: UnitedHealthcare ~70%, Centene 45%, CVS 36%, Elevance 23% of members hit; Humana increased allowances, so "major insurers" is not uniformly true.
  • NFHA: HUD and DOJ "did not provide NFHA with the data requested"; "first time" not independently confirmed.
  • Not accessed: Federal Register final text (blocked), CMS/HHS pages (403), Medicare Rights page (403). Primary-text confirmation would change confidence.
