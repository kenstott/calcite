Independent evidence
Proposed rule (CMS-1848-P, July 16, 2026) confirmed on Justice in Aging page and Federal Register link; search results report final CY2027 PFS rule (CMS-1848-F) with Feb 1, 2027 terminations; I did not read the final rule text.
