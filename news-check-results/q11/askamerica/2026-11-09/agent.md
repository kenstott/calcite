# The Justice in Aging "From DC" (Nov 6, 2026) article's claims about Medicare immigrant eligibility, Medicare Advantage dental cuts and fair housing data

**Rating: 0-1 Pinocchio (essentially accurate; 1 for unconfirmed specifics).**

Caveat: the direct fetch returned HTTP 412 and I could not obtain verbatim text. Claims come from a summary of the page, so no quotes could be extracted. No warehouse table covers these measures (search_catalog unmatched term: immigrant), so I checked independent sources.

1. CMS final rule implementing H.R. 1 immigrant limits, terminations starting Feb 1, 2027: mostly true. I read the proposed rule (CMS-1848-P) on Justice in Aging's site. Search results report the final rule (CMS-1848-F) with the Feb 1, 2027 date, but I did not read the final text. Estimates of people affected vary widely (about 32,000 to 300,000).
2. Refugees, asylees and TPS holders newly ineligible: true (Justice in Aging proposed-rule page).
3. MA insurers cutting dental benefits for 2027: true. Leerink via Healthcare Dive reports UnitedHealthcare cuts hitting about 70% of members, Centene 45%, CVS/Aetna 36% and Elevance 23%. STAT corroborates. Humana modestly raised dental allowances, and CMS calls the market stable.
4. NFHA 2026 report, HUD data missing for the first time: true. NFHA's page says "Critical federal data missing for the first time since 1996."
5. HUD funding attacks forced service cuts, nonprofits handle more than 75% of complaints: mostly true. NFHA reports 76% of reporting members had funding disruptions. The 75% figure was not found on the 2026 highlights page; an earlier NFHA figure ties about 75% to FHIP grantees specifically.

Misleading-context flags: this is an advocacy newsletter, so it is one-sided in framing. The scale of immigrant terminations is contested. The dental cuts are not uniform across insurers.

Sources: justiceinaging.org tag page and template-comments page; healthcaredive.com MA 2027 article; statnews.com MA 2027 article; nationalfairhousing.org 2026 report page.
