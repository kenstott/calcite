Method and warehouse note

The warehouse has no utility-level price-to-compare table. Closest: energy.eia_electricity_prices_monthly (PA residential average price, 22.20 cents/kWh in Sept 2026 vs 20.43 in Sept 2025, +8.7%), which is consistent with the article's direction but is an all-in average, not default-service PTC. Figures were therefore graded from the PUC releases, which I read in full.

Context the piece omits

Penelec's 'current' 13.142 is already well above the 11.747 set last December because of a mid-year reset, so the 15% gap is mostly not a December effect. The piece also does not name the one decrease (UGI Electric, -1.72%) and omits large increases elsewhere (PECO and PPL +10.3%, Citizens' +11.9%, Wellsboro +10.4%) that exceed the local utilities' increases. The headline says 'smaller bumps' for local utilities, which is accurate. None of this makes it misleading.
