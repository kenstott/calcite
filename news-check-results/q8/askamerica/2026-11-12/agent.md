Accurate (checked against primary-source-derived figures):
- 3.6% COLA announced Oct 14, 2026 (CPI-W Q3 avg 328.746 vs 317.265 = +3.62%); ties fifth-largest since 1993 (with 2011); sixth straight year at 2.5% or more.
- COLAs 3.2/2.5/2.8 vs Part B hikes 5.9/5.9/9.7; 2026 Part B $202.90 (Federal Register).
- Trustees' 2027 Part B projection $209.50 (about 3.25%); it is an estimate, and private forecasters expect $216-219.
- OASI depletion Q4 2032, $29.3T 75-year gap, 22% cut, payroll taxes 91.3% of income.
- Part A premium-free for most (Medicare.gov); Bilello tweet: 72M matches SSA, 26% is near compounding of 2022-2026 COLAs (about 25.2%).

Unsupported: about 29 million traditional Medicare retirees (CMS shows 34.3M traditional Medicare overall); Gallup 80-90% of retirees rely on Social Security "in some capacity" (Gallup's recent figure is 62% major source).

- Larger COLA worsens solvency and timeline "may now be less than six years": no estimate, and ignores offsetting payroll-tax revenue from higher wages (mostly false).
