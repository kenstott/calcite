The Motley Fool article's claims about the 2027 Social Security COLA, Medicare Part B and OASI solvency are biased and mostly accurate; 1 claim could not be confirmed.

Article under reviewhttps://www.fool.com/retirement/2026/11/07/social-security-trump-bump-led-2027-cola-is-double-edged-sword-for-tens-of-millions-retirees/

Summary

Rating: 1 Pinocchio. The headline figures are accurate: 3.6% COLA announced Oct. 14 (CPI-W Q3 average 328.746 vs 317.265, +3.62%), Part B $202.90 (+9.7%), Trustees' $209.50 Part B projection, OASI depletion Q4 2032, $29.3 trillion 75-year gap, payroll taxes 91.3% of income. The weak points are causal: calling two Trump policies 'primarily responsible' for the COLA, and asserting with no estimate that the larger COLA accelerates benefit cuts. The Part B comparison rests on a projection (private forecasters say $216-219).

Limits: SSA.gov and ycharts returned 403 or empty, so Trustees and SSA numbers come from search summaries of those pages; the warehouse has no COLA, CPI-W or Trustees tables.
