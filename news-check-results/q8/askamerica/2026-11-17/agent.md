Honesty 92 (honest). 11 of 12 claims true, 1 mostly false. Scores describe this article only.

Verified true against the FDIC failed-bank list, the FDIC API and the FDIC Nano Banc press release:
- Six failures between Nov 14, 2025 and Nov 14, 2026. The FDIC list shows none between June 27, 2025 and Jan 30, 2026, so the count does not depend on the window.
- Nano Banc ($736,172K) exceeds the other five combined ($712,100K).
- Closing dates and acquirers match the FDIC list.
- Assets, deposits and costs match for four banks in the FDIC API. Nano and Tioga figures are FDIC preliminary estimates, confirmed via the FDIC press release and Banking Dive. The exact Nano and Tioga dollar digits were not seen in a primary table.
- Credit card delinquency (FRED DRCCLACBS): 2.85% in 2026Q2, 3.08% in 2024Q4. Both match.
- FDIC $250K rules, the trust rule ($250K per beneficiary, up to five, $1.25M maximum) and the SIPC $500K/$250K cap all match the agencies' own pages.

The one error and the framing problem:
- The article says West Georgia has the "largest published" cost estimate ($97.3M). Its own table shows Nano Banc at $114M, which the FDIC published Sept 25.
- West Georgia is largest only relative to assets (about 32%, against 15.5% for Nano). Kentland is about 31%, and the others are about 8%.
- The article calls Nano's and Tioga's estimates "pending" although both were announced at closing.
- Nano's state regulator put assets at about $690M at closing, and Sunwest assumed about $605M of deposits, not $686M. The ranking is unaffected.

Not confirmed: the call-report as-of dates for the four older failures. The FDIC API gave only the figures.
