What is misleading even though facts check out

  • Largest-estimate framing. The Quick Read and body say West Georgia has the largest published estimate, yet the FDIC announced $114M for Nano on Sept. 25, and the closing section calls Nano's and Tioga's estimates "pending" though both were announced at closing. Defensible: West Georgia has the largest cost relative to assets (about 32% vs 15.5% for Nano).
  • Better framing omitted. Cost relative to assets: West Georgia ~31.8% and Kentland ~31.4%, Nano ~15.5%, others about 8% (chart). Nano still has the largest dollar cost.
  • Mixed vintages. Nano's state regulator put assets near $690M at closing and Sunwest assumed about $605M of deposits rather than $686M; ranking is unaffected.
  • The count of six is robust to the window: the FDIC list shows no failures between June 27, 2025 and Jan. 30, 2026.
