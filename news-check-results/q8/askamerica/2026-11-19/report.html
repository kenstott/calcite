The IPWatchdog article's claims about the GAO report on USPTO backlog reduction, examiner attrition and workforce strain are balanced and mostly accurate.

Summary

The article is largely accurate on GAO's core findings (attrition 7.6% vs 4.8% threshold, backlog 838,000 to 756,000, 599,000 goal, unlikely before 2031, PPAC met twice since January 2025). It misidentifies the addressees as Senate officials (they are House), cites 31 U.S.C. 117 instead of 717, and renders subject-matter training as 'subject matter eligibility'. Rating: 1 Pinocchio. Omission: GAO also found total pendency rose from 26.2 to 29.0 months and wait for a final decision rose 11% even as the backlog fell. No warehouse table carries examiner, backlog or PPAC data, so grading rests on the GAO report.
