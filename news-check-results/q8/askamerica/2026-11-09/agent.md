# The Colorado Sun article's claims about Polis's 2027-28 Medicaid budget proposal, TABOR and Medicaid enrollment are accurate.

Rating: 1 Pinocchio (mostly accurate).

| Claim | Verdict | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Medicaid covers about 1 in 5 Coloradans | True | Warehouse ACS 2024 CO population 5,862,189 (census.acs_population); HCPF Dec 2025 enrollment 1,229,956, about 21%. The warehouse Medicaid enrollment table is cataloged but would not query, so enrollment is from web evidence. |
| Costs rise by more than $900M next year absent cuts | True | Sun Sept 18 article: $918M added to 2027-28. |
| Since 1994 health budget +828%, TABOR cap +271% | Mostly true | Matches the Sun's own TABOR explainer. It is the whole HCPF budget, all funds, nominal, not strictly Medicaid. Not independently verified. |
| Successor sworn in Jan 12; election Tuesday | True | Nov 3, 2026 and Jan 12, 2027 are both Tuesdays; the Sun voter guide confirms the Nov 3 election. |
| $250M GF increase, about $300M specified cuts plus $300M+ unspecified, 4.3% cap, $488 per pupil, $200M ballot measures, quotes | Not checkable here | The governor's letter (Google Drive) returned only "Loading..."; the request folder listed files but contents were unreadable. No warehouse table carries state budget proposals. |

Context and caveats: the piece fairly presents the dispute (Polis says the budget increases health funding; the Sun says services must still be cut). It omits that enrollment is down 31% since 2023, and the 828% vs 271% comparison is stated without the all-funds nominal context. A Sun Nov 6 article reports Amendment 87 passed, after this piece ran. The article's Nov 3 correction (pregnant women coverage) is already incorporated.
