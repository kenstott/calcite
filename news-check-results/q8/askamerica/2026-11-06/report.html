Summary

Nearly every figure NPR relays matches the MPI fact sheet (about 980,000 deportations, 20% with convictions, 8,000 to 19,000 interior deportations a month, 310,000 to 1.5 million self-departures). NPR uses MPI's 980,000 figure without noting that MPI's own comparable estimate is about 480,000 for people here a year or more.
