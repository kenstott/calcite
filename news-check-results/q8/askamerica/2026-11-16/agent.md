Source: https://news.vt.edu/articles/2026/11/thanksgiving-meal-farmer-share-earnings-price-meal-groceries-expenses-agriculture-supply-chain-turkey.html (12 Nov 2026; fetched successfully).

Warehouse: search_catalog (food dollar, farm share, marketing bill, Thanksgiving dinner cost) found no table with the ERS Food Dollar farm share. Only food CPI and farm income tables exist, so claims were verified against USDA ERS primary pages.

Claim grades:
1. "$80, or $8 per person" typical 2026 dinner: unsupported. AFBF's 2026 survey publishes Nov. 18, and its 2025 expanded menu was $77.09 ($7.71 per person), so $80 is plausible but not confirmed.
2. Farmers get about 19% of the at-home retail dollar (2024): true. ERS reports 18.5 cents.
3. $15 of $80, $65 to marketing: mostly true. The arithmetic is right, but it applies the all-grocery average to a holiday basket.
4. 56% of food dollars spent dining out: mostly true. ERS puts food away from home at 58.9% in 2024 ($1.27T vs $901B). 56.3% is an inflation-adjusted 2025 figure.
5. $750 take-out, 7%, about $53: mostly true. ERS gives 7.1 cents per food-away-from-home dollar; the arithmetic is right, but that average is assumed to apply to this meal.
6. $180 Cracker Barrel heat-and-serve, about $13: mostly true. 7.1% of $180 is $12.78. Third-party prices run about $175 to $190.
7. "Mainly buying convenience or marketing services, not food": mostly true but an overreach. The marketing share is 81.5 cents at home and 92.9 cents away, but it pays for processing, transport, labor and retail, not only convenience.
8. Headline, "farmers earn less than you would think": mostly true but misleading. ERS's farm share is gross returns to farms, not earnings. Farms spent 5.1 of the 11.8 cents per food dollar on inputs, leaving 6.7 cents of value added.

Misleading framing to flag:
- Gross farm receipts are presented as farmer "earnings".
- Average shares are applied to specific meals.
- The ERS model was revised in March 2026, so older figures are not comparable.
- Davis's credentials were not independently checked. The aaec.vt.edu and hnfe.vt.edu pages failed with TLS certificate errors; the CALS page loaded.
