The ABC30/ABC News Thanksgiving travel article's claims about airline volumes, airfares and Amtrak are somewhat biased and mostly accurate; 4 claims could not be confirmed.

**Rating: 1 Pinocchio.**

Article: https://abc30.com/story/thanksgiving-travel-record-number-travelers-expected-fly-take-trains-drive-holiday/20032744/ (ABC News/GMA, Nov 11, 2026). The connector's fetch worked. The GMA original (Nov 10) is identical.

**Warehouse check.** search_catalog found no table for TSA throughput, Hopper fares, A4A or carrier forecasts, or Amtrak 2026 ridership. The nearest are transport.t100_segments (through 2025) and bts_tsi_monthly. The claims are forward-looking projections, so all were graded against outside sources.

**Claims**
- Hopper: domestic Thanksgiving roundtrip fares up 39%, international up as much as 30%. PARTIALLY FALSE. Hopper's index (Sep 25) and ABC's own Sep 26 piece say $402, up 31%. No 30% international Thanksgiving figure appears. A later Hopper update could explain 39%, but I found none.
- Hopper: 2.2% more flights scheduled. UNSUPPORTED. Hopper's index reports no flight-count change.
- Airfares highest in at least 10 years. TRUE (Hopper).
- A4A projects nearly 29 million travelers. UNSUPPORTED. I found no A4A 2026 release. A4A's newest page is its Nov 20, 2025 forecast of more than 31 million for Nov 21 to Dec 1, so 29 million would be lower than last year's forecast.
- Central claim, "Record number of travelers expected to fly, take trains." PARTIALLY FALSE or overreaching. The only industry total cited is below A4A's 2025 forecast. The airline-level evidence is American's flights up about 4% (85,000 vs 80,759 in 2025). The URL says "drive", but the text has no driving data.
- American: more than 8 million customers on 85,000 flights, Nov 19 to Dec 1. TRUE. The release itself returned HTTP 403, so this is from secondary reports of it.
- American: one flight every 13 seconds. TRUE. 85,000 flights over 13 days is 6,538 per day, or one every 13.2 seconds.
- American: 7,021 flights on Nov 29, its largest day since the pandemic. TRUE per reports of the release.
- Sunday Nov 29 is the busiest day. TRUE. Thanksgiving is Nov 26, so Nov 29 is the Sunday after, and it is American's peak day.
- United: more than 500,000 customers a day, more than 4,800 daily flights, more than 540,000 on Fri Nov 20, about 600,000 on Sun Nov 29. UNSUPPORTED. The figures appear only in ABC's report, and I found no United release. The weekdays are correct.
- Amtrak is adding capacity on the Northeast Corridor, Nov 20-30, plus extra Washington to New York round trips on Nov 24-25 and 28-29. TRUE (secondary coverage).
- Amtrak "expecting a record number of passengers." UNSUPPORTED. I found no Amtrak 2026 target. 2025 was a record, at 1.2 million or more, about 6% higher.
- High fuel prices amid the Iran war. TRUE. Jet fuel is about $196 a barrel against $99 before the war, per AP/ABC.
- The $1.3 trillion figure is a sidebar teaser to a separate story, not a claim of this article.

**Misleading framing**
- The "record" headline rests on an unverified 29 million figure that, as reported, is below last year's forecast. The airline-level figures (American, United) support growth for those carriers, not a record for all fliers.
- Fare increases appear inflated relative to Hopper's published 31%.

**Limits:** American's release returned HTTP 403, so its figures come from secondary reports. The United, A4A 2026 and Amtrak 2026 primary releases were not found.
