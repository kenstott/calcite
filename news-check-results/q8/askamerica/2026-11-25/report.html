  • Vance post (Nov 19): over $6B, 6,400 retailers, 4,600 devices - matches the post exactly.
  • USDA Sept 18 release: $5.8B, nearly 6,000 retailers, 2,000 devices, 170 NYC retailers - matches. The Epoch Times reported 5,335 disqualified and about 1,840 devices three days earlier.
  • SNAP cost: warehouse shows $93.5B (FY2024) and $95.8B (FY2025); USDA says $100B. Fair.
  • The White House Fraud Ledger defines 'fraud stopped' as dollars saved annually via administrative actions; no method is published for the $6B.
  • ProPublica reports recipient fraud is about $64M/yr (0.08% of benefits) and electronic theft from recipients ($470M in 2025) is far larger while USDA cut fraud staff.
  • Kansas City indictment: DOJ page empty, no corroboration found.

Exclusion disclosure

The warehouse query excluded rows where month IS NULL, which are the per-state fiscal-year Total rows (689 rows per year including them). Including them double counts: $187.0B (FY2024) and $191.5B (FY2025), exactly twice the monthly sums of $93.5B and $95.8B. The monthly-only figures are used.
