## Issues
- The article compares ACS poverty (Census thresholds) with the HHS guideline. These are different measures; the $32,150 is an administrative guideline, not the ACS threshold. A minor conflation, not an error.
- ACS 1-year city estimates carry margins of error; "lowest ever" claims should note whether the drop is statistically significant.
- Framing is balanced: it pairs the good news with hardship context, and the headline figure is not oversold.

## Rating
Provisional 1 Pinocchio (mostly accurate), low confidence. Nothing found contradicts the piece, but the core ACS numbers were not verified against Census primary data, and the one checkable figure (the poverty guideline) is right. The rating would rise to 0 if ACS tables confirm 24.9% and 9.9%.
