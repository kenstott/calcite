## Rating: 1 Pinocchio (minor issues, mostly accurate)
Every claim I could check holds up. The migration figures could not be traced, which lowers confidence but does not show an error.

## Possibly misleading framing
- The headline link between the millionaires tax and out-migration is causal-sounding. The data are net counts of all filers. Per the summary, most of the loss is young filers (43% aged 26-34). The Tax Policy Center notes that the 2021-22 net loss of MA taxpayers exceeded the 2022-23 loss. Boston.com (Aug 2026) says it is not yet known whether millionaires are leaving.
- A $3.38B record surtax haul runs against the tax-flight narrative, but does not prove no one left.
- Pioneer is an advocacy think tank. Treating its analysis as neutral should be flagged.
- Washington and California are different taxes. The Fortune piece does separate income taxes from wealth taxes, which is appropriate.

Sources: mass.gov FY26 surtax certification; Bloomberg Tax; Baker Tilly; CalMatters/AP via Fox; EdSource; MassBudget; Pioneer Institute; Boston.com 2026/08/12; Tax Policy Center.
