# Fact-check: Colorado Sun, "Polis November budget proposal 2027-2028, Medicaid cuts" (2026-11-02)

Method note: the article was read through WebFetch, which returned a summary rather than full text, so exact wording of quotes could not be checked. Corroboration came from other outlets' coverage (Axios Denver, Denver7, Colorado Politics, Becker's Dental, Summit Daily). I did not retrieve the primary documents (the governor's OSPB budget request, HCPF materials, Legislative Council TABOR figures).

## Misleading-framing check
- Polis says the budget increases health care funding, and the piece reports a $250M general fund increase. The piece reports this tension fairly rather than adopting either side's framing. A "cut" here is a cut from projected growth, not from current spending, and readers can be confused by that. The article appears to make the distinction.
- The unspecified $300M+ cut is a placeholder, so headline totals overstate how concrete the proposal is.
- The 828% vs 271% comparison is a long-baseline stat that is not inflation- or enrollment-adjusted. If the article presents it without that context, it is selectively framed. I could not check how it was presented.

## Overall rating
**1 Pinocchio (mostly accurate), provisional.** Every claim I could corroborate held up, and nothing was contradicted. Several figures, including the headline $600M total, the 828%/271% comparison and the schools, prison and ballot items, could not be verified, so the grade rests on a partial check.
