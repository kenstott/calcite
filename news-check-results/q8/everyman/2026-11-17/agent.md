# Fact-check: 24/7 Wall St., "Six FDIC-Insured Banks Failed in the Past Year..." (Nov 15, 2026, David Beren)

Method: WebFetch/WebSearch only. The article text was returned only as a summary (copyright), so claims below are those in that summary. No quotes from named people were reported. Search results were summarized by the tool; I did not open the FDIC press releases directly (one FDIC URL returned 404).

## Materially misleading / context
- Cost figures are FDIC estimates that change as retained assets are sold; the piece should treat them as preliminary.
- "Ranked by size" uses mixed asset dates (call report vs closing), so the ranking is right but the dollar values are not strictly comparable.
- The credit-card statistic is unrelated to bank failures and is filler; it does not mislead.
- Total failures are tiny relative to the system (about 4,400 insured banks), which the piece does not say; the headline could alarm readers.
- No quotes to verify.

## Rating
1 Pinocchio: core facts correct; small rounding/date-basis issues and one unverifiable prior-period statistic.
