The claim matches the Census Bureau/HUD New Residential Sales release for August 2026: new single-family home sales ran at a seasonally adjusted annual rate (SAAR) of 684,000 units, up 6.4% from the July 2026 rate of 643,000 — both figures line up with "rose more than 6%" and "684,000 units."

Additional context from the same release:
- Year-over-year, August sales were down about 2.0%.
- Median sales price fell 5.8% year-over-year to $393,700.
- Months' supply stood at 8.5 months, down from 9.0 months in July.
- The 684,000 rate came in well above the consensus forecast (~615,000).
- Report released September 24, 2026.
