## Overall rating
1 Pinocchio (mostly accurate, minor imprecision). Verified facts are sound. Weak points: "more than 1 in 3" (data show 32.5%), a possible screening-window mismatch, and a number of rule-detail claims I could not test against the rule text.

## Misleading-by-omission flags
- The headline frames the proposal as weakening protections. The PEL is unchanged, and the 2024 rule was already stayed by the 8th Circuit and not being enforced (MSHA Apr 2026 bulletin). The real baseline is therefore no enforcement, not strict enforcement.
- The 21% tolerance is presented via an advocate's math. The rule frames it as handling sampling error, and OSHA-style practice has similar tolerances. The industry view (the NMA disputes that current conditions match the screening data) is not weighed.
- The NIOSH rate is for working veteran underground miners, which is not all miners.
