Method note: WebFetch returned only a summary of the article, not verbatim text, so claims are taken from that summary. I did not have the article's exact wording or quotes.

## Rating
1 Pinocchio (Mostly True; "a few shading or minor issues"). Every checkable number matches the PUC. The Shapiro/PJM attribution is unverified from primary sources.

## Materially misleading or missing context
- The two utilities named for rates (Penelec +3.2%, West Penn +2.6%) are among the smallest increases. PECO (+10.3%), PPL (+10.3%), Wellsboro (+10.4%) and Citizens' (+11.9%) rise about three to four times as much, so a reader could take the typical increase to be mild. Rates for the other utilities are not covered in the summary I saw.
- Last December's increases were roughly twice as large for Penelec and West Penn, so the report's comparison is fair for them, but this year's statewide spread is wide.
- The price to compare covers default generation supply only; total bills also include distribution charges. Shopping with a competitive supplier can beat the price to compare.
- The PUC release does not attribute the rises to PJM or Shapiro; that framing comes from the station.
