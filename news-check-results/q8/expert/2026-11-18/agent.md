# Fact-check: Grist/Yahoo nitrate petition story: 1 Pinocchio

**Answer:** Mostly accurate. Figures match the Nov 16, 2026 petition and EPA record. Key issue: "nearly 120 million served dangerous levels" is 119.7M people served by 13,205 systems with at least one sample >=1 mg/L (2021-23); 1 mg/L is 10% of the 10 mg/L (as N) MCL.
**Key values:** $94.5B cost / $364.1B benefit over 50 yrs (One Water Econ, petitioner-commissioned); 12,594 cancers/yr is the upper bound of 2,300-12,594 (Temkin 2019); EPA Six-Year Review 4 (89 FR 59623, Jul 23 2024) did not revise nitrate.
**Discrepancy:** Article says reassessment started under Obama 2016; petition says IRIS reassessment began Sept 2017, suspended 2019, restarted 2023, ORD eliminated. Likely conflation of IRIS and Six-Year Review.
**Not checkable here:** 62% share, Iowa 42% (EWG says 46%), Illinois >2M, top states.
**Sources:** petition PDF (foodandwaterwatch.org), Federal Register, EWG, Iowa Public Radio, Temkin 2019.
**Confidence:** high on numbers, medium on history, low on state figures.
