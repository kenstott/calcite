Fortune migration piece: 1 Pinocchio, numbers hold, causal framing is thin

Summary of findings

Rating: 1 Pinocchio. All numeric claims matched sources: 31,384 net residents, $3.64B net AGI (2024) vs $4.18B, NH 7,818 and FL 6,032 (44.1%), FL $1.29B + NH $869M (59.3%), $200k+ $2.32B (63.7%), FY26 surtax $3.38B prelim vs $2.4B budget, Prop 40 rejected, Washington repeal rejected.

  • Fortune's "certified" $3.38B is preliminary; $2.4B is a budget benchmark.
  • Misleading context: lost income is mostly $200k+ filers, not surtax payers (above about $1.1M); migration data cannot show tax motive; the 2024 income loss fell 13%.
  • Not checkable: raw IRS SOI tables fetched directly (used Pioneer/Boston.com); Barnes and Spatt quotes; Maine/Rhode Island details.
