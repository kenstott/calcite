# Fact check: WJAC "Electric rates set to rise Dec. 1" (2026-11-25)

**Rating: 1 Pinocchio.** Every checkable number matches PA PUC releases (11/23/2026, 11/24/2025, 6/1/2026).

## Claims
- 10 of 11 utilities rise: verified (UGI -1.72% only decrease). Confidence high.
- Penelec 13.142 to 13.564 c/kWh, +3.2%: verified. Last Dec 11.004 to 11.747, +6.75%: verified. New rate ~15% above post-Dec-2025: verified (+15.5%).
- West Penn 12.075 to 12.386 (+2.6%): verified. Last Dec 10.318 to 10.947: verified. ~13% above: verified (+13.1%).
- Nine of 11 raised last Dec: verified (9 up, 2 down).
- Default-service customers only; weather raises bills; PUC urges budget billing/CAPs: verified.
- Shapiro criticized PJM, will work with governors: verified (pa.gov statement 11/18/2026).
- PUC attributes to wholesale costs: verified. "Can include PJM capacity costs": not in the PUC release read (no PJM mention); plausible, unverified.
- Two Facebook comments (Missy Ann, Alicia Sherwood): not checkable, anecdotes.

## Misleading context
- Features the two smallest increases; Citizens +11.9%, PECO/PPL +10.3%, Wellsboro +10.4% are far larger.
- "Smaller than last year" omits June 1, 2026 steps (Penelec +11.88%, West Penn +10.3%); the 15%/13% figures combine both.
