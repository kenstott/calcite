Fact check: WJAC, Electric rates set to rise Dec. 1

Accurate on every checkable number; rated 1 Pinocchio for framing

All 14 checkable figures match the PA PUC release of 11/23/2026 and prior PUC releases. Nothing is contradicted. Two Facebook comments cannot be checked. One attribution (PUC tying prices to PJM capacity costs) is not in the PUC release I read.

  • Framing: the story features the two smallest-increase territories (Penelec +3.2%, West Penn +2.6%). Citizens (+11.9%), PECO and PPL (+10.3%), Wellsboro (+10.4%) and Pike County (+8.9%, estimate) are much larger, so the statewide picture is bigger than the lead examples suggest.
  • Describing this year's increase as "smaller" omits the June 1, 2026 step (Penelec +11.88%, West Penn +10.3%). The "about 15%/13% higher" figures are right but are cumulative over two changes.
  • Would change the rating: a PUC statement explicitly naming PJM capacity costs would clear that item.
