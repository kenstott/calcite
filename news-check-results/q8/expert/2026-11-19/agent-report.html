Fact check: IPWatchdog on GAO USPTO backlog report

Mostly accurate: 1 Pinocchio

Nearly every figure in the article matches GAO-27-108491: backlog 838,000 to about 756,000 (82,000 in 19 months), attrition 2.9% to 7.6%, 4.8% benchmark, 11% longer waits, two PPAC meetings. Three slips: the 756,000 figure is attributed to Squires' Senate testimony, whose written text says below 754,000 by September; the statute is cited as 31 U.S.C. 117 where GAO cites 717(b)(1); and the 40% figure is for new examiners in their first year (FY2021-23), described loosely as junior examiners.

  • Misleading by omission, mildly: GAO sets no deadline for the 599,000 goal and projects backlog under 599,000 only with low attrition (by 2029) or high productivity; 2031 scenarios range 640,000 to 968,000. GAO says hires (1,054, Aug 2025-Jun 2026) and departures (1,209, Feb 2025-Jun 2026) periods differ.
  • Limitation: I read the article through a summarizing fetch tool, so verbatim quotes could not be compared character by character. 'Three-year low' may come from oral testimony I could not check.
