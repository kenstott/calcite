# Fact-check: Inquirer, "Camden's poverty rate is its lowest in at least 15 years" (2026-11-19)

Grade: provisional 2 Pinocchios (unverified; large unexplained gap from earlier vintages).

## Claims and checks
- City poverty 24.9% (2025), lowest since 2010. Answer: NOT verified. The Census API redirected to a missing-key page; Census Reporter has no 2025 ACS release; QuickFacts returned 403. Prior vintages found: ACS 2024 1-yr 33.9% (23,632 of 69,729), 2024 5-yr 30.2%, 2022 1-yr 31.6% (Data USA, secondary). That implies about a 9-point one-year drop. Confidence: low on the 2025 figure, high on the earlier ones.
- County 9.9%. Earlier: 11.6% (2024 1-yr), 12.4% (2024 5-yr). Plausible, not verified.
- Median household income $45,900 (2025), vs 2024 1-yr $38,735 (about +18%). The claim that income peaked at $47,200 in 2021 conflicts with Data USA's 2021 nominal $30,247; this may be an inflation-adjustment issue and could not be resolved.
- 2025 poverty line $32,150 for a family of four. Correct (HHS/ASPE, 90 FR 5917). High confidence.
- Not checkable here: 76% above the line but struggling (United Way), 10th lowest among cities of 65,000+, 8% vs 14% real income growth since 2010, 6,000 served monthly at Cathedral Kitchen, the quotes (Golderer, Flewelling, Noonan), the stipend plan, and the mayor's office not responding.

## Caveats
Poverty falling while income remains below its 2021 peak needs explanation. ACS 1-year margins of error for Camden were not retrieved. If the 2025 ACS row shows 24.9% and $45,900, the grade falls to 1 or less.
