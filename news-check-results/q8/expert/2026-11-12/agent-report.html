Fact check: Motley Fool 2027 COLA piece

Mostly accurate: 1 Pinocchio

Of 10 claims, 7 verify, 2 are framing or speculation, and 1 (payroll taxes over 91% of income) could not be checked because SSA Trustees pages returned HTTP 403. Core numbers (3.6% COLA, $209.50 projected Part B, Q4 2032 OASI depletion, 22% cut) match primary or primary-citing sources.

  • Misleading-ish: the headline's "Trump bump" attributes the COLA to policy; sources support tariffs and war-driven energy costs but nobody quantifies it.
  • The Part B figure is a June projection; CMS final premium (Nov) could differ; private forecasts run $216-219.
  • The claim that a bigger COLA brings depletion under six years is the author's inference, not Trustees output.
  • Would change grade: if payroll share is not 91%, or CMS final premium exceeds 3.6% rise.
