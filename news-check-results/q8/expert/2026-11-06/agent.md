## Overall: 1 Pinocchio (minor shading, essentially accurate)
Every checkable number matches the MPI fact sheet.

Issues, in order of importance:
1. The segment says voluntary departure "is not the same as a deportation". Yet the roughly 980,000 headline, which MPI calls "deportations", includes about 170,000 voluntary departures and returns. The measure is not named. Removals alone were about 59% of the total (about 580,000), per a secondary summary of the fact sheet that I did not check against the PDF.
2. The 1.86 million FY2000 comparison sets a fiscal-year annual figure beside an 18-month cumulative figure. The annualized comparison still supports "not higher than in the past".
3. The "3 million self-deported" is really DHS's claim of total departures. The 310,000 to 1.5 million range is a modeled estimate with a different population (at least one year of residence), so the two are not like for like.
4. The "tenfold" claim is unsupported by the primary source I read.

Confidence: high on claims 1 to 11 (primary document read in full). Medium to low on claims 13 to 15.
