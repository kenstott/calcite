# Fact check: Water Online "Toxic forever chemicals found in tap water of 197M Americans"
Grade: 2 Pinocchios.
- Figures (197M, +23M, 3,595 new sites, 13,459 total, 29 PFAS) match EWG release as reported; EWG primary page 403, so method not independently verified. Confidence medium.
- "EPA is finalizing rollback": only a proposed rule (Fed. Reg. 2026-10085, May 20 2026) found; no final rule. PFOA/PFOS 4 ppt kept, opt-in extension to 2031: confirmed.
- 197M = people served by systems with any detection (modeled), not above-limit exposure; NRDC counts 80M+ above limits. Misleading framing.
- Environment America/PIRG 83% of 1,602 sites 2022-2024: confirmed (sites, not drinking water).
- CDC 99%: ATSDR says "nearly all"; 99% is EWG phrasing.
- Quotes: Andrews confirmed verbatim; Faber only paraphrased in secondary reports.
- Not checked: ~30,000 industrial polluters, EWG filter test of 10 filters.
Sources: see agent-report.html.
