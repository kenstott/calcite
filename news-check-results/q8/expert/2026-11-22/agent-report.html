Water Online PFAS piece: mostly accurate EWG press release, 2 Pinocchios

Summary of findings

2 Pinocchios. The piece (a republished EWG release, 11/19/2026) accurately reports EWG's figures (197M, 23M more, 3,595 new sites, 13,459 total) but says EPA is "finalizing" the rollback; I found only the May 20, 2026 proposed rule, no final rule.

  • "197 million drink contaminated water" is an EWG modeled estimate of people served by systems with any detection, many at trace levels; it is not a measure of exposure above a limit. NRDC's UCMR 5 analysis counts 80M+ above current enforceable limits.
  • Rollback: proposal covers PFHxS, PFNA, GenX and PFBS mixture; PFOA/PFOS stay at 4 ppt with opt-in extension 2029 to 2031. Matches piece except "final". Anti-backsliding is EWG's legal opinion; litigation (AWWA v. EPA) is pending.
  • Environment America/PIRG: PFAS at 83% of 1,602 sites, 2022-2024 (confirmed). Sites, not people, and not drinking water.
  • CDC 99%: ATSDR says "nearly all"; 99% is EWG's phrasing. Could not retrieve EWG's own release (403), so 197M method, 30,000 polluters, and the 2020 "200 million" paper are not independently verified; quotes confirmed by secondary reports (Andrews verbatim; Faber paraphrased).
  • Andrews told WRAL the findings do not necessarily mean contamination is worsening; "matches 2020 estimate" is a modeled-to-modeled comparison.
