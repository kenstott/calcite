# Fact-check: Fortune, "Pentagon military budget in jeopardy, global defense spending hit $8 trillion" (2026-11-15)

**Verdict: 2 Pinocchios.** Most facts check out; the headline "$8 trillion" is misleading (SIPRI 2025 actual: $2.887T; $8T is a projection traced to a Dolan column citing Stavridis, Carlyle attribution unconfirmed).

Caveat: the Fortune page was read only via a fetch-tool summary.

## Claims
- $1.5T FY27 request, +44% (implied FY26 ~$1.04T). Sources: Stars and Stripes 2026-11-08. Confidence high.
- Smith: budget "in excess of $1 trillion" avoided. Stars and Stripes. High. Contractor probe quote: not found.
- G7 2.0% GDP, 3.8%, $8T: matches capital.com Dolan column; Carlyle attribution not confirmed. Medium-low.
- EU EUR 418bn 2025 (2.2% GDP), EUR 454bn 2026: Council/EDA. High. (Council also gives 2.4% GDP for 2026.)
- Hegseth Shangri-La 30 May 2026, 3.5% GDP ask: Military Times/NPR. High.
- Patterson (CFR) deficit/inflation warning: CFR article. High.
- US ~one third of world spend, >$1T FY26: SIPRI-based ~37%; Hegseth testimony. Medium.
- $83bn EU military aid to Ukraine: Kiel shows >EUR 80bn through Aug 2025; basis differs. Medium-low.
- Not checkable: Bilmes expectation, van Steenis comment.
