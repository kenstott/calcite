# Fact-check: ABC News, "Measles cases surpass 5,000 in US, infections confirmed in 45..." (Nov 6, 2026, Mary Kekatos)

Method: the ABC page was fetched through a summarizing tool, so I worked from that summary rather than the verbatim text. Exact wording of quotes could not be checked. I compared each claim to the CDC measles page, the Wisconsin DHS release, and other outlets.

## Verdict: 1 Pinocchio (mostly accurate, minor issues)

## Claims

| Claim | Result |
|---|---|
| At least 5,182 confirmed US cases in 2026 | Verified. The CDC page shows 5,182 as of Nov 5, updated Nov 6. |
| 45 states plus DC | Not matched. The CDC page as I read it lists 47 jurisdictions, said to be 46 states plus New York City and DC. ABC's "45 states" may come from an earlier count, or my read of the CDC page may be off. This is a possible minor undercount. |
| 18 cases among international visitors | Verified (CDC). |
| 8 measles-associated deaths in 2026: 6 PA, 1 NY, 1 WI | Verified as the state-reported total. ABC reports the same figures. Wisconsin DHS release (Nov 5), PBS Wisconsin, WaPo and Fox News confirm the Wisconsin infant death. Pennsylvania has reported six deaths. |
| CDC has confirmed only 4 of the 8; number "subject to change" | Verified. The CDC page lists 4 deaths and marks them subject to change. |
| NY child death, first in over 30 years | Verified. Reported Nov 3 by state and Lewis County officials, with many outlets covering it. The child's vaccination status has not been disclosed. |
| Wisconsin infant death, first since 1990 | Verified (Wisconsin DHS, Nov 5). |
| Pennsylvania death of an unvaccinated Jefferson County resident, among those "announced this week" | Partly unverified. A Jefferson County death (a 40-year-old unvaccinated woman) was confirmed by the state on Sept 15, not in the week of Nov 3-6. I could not confirm a separate new Jefferson County death that week. If ABC means this week, the timing is wrong. I am treating this as a possible timing error, because I read the article only through a summary. |
| About 96% of cases are unvaccinated or of unknown status, 2% one dose, 2% two doses | Not checkable here. The CDC page text did not load the 2026 breakdown. It is plausible given the outbreak data (Pennsylvania reports more than 99% unvaccinated). |
| At least 11% hospitalized, more than 1 in 10 under 5 | Not checkable here. The CDC page text did not load the 2026 hospitalization data. |
| Plurality of cases aged 5-19 | Not checkable here. |
| CDC recommends MMR doses at 12-15 months and 4-6 years; 93% effective with one dose, 97% with two | Matches standard CDC guidance (93%/97% are the CDC's figures). I did not re-fetch the page. |
| Kindergarten MMR coverage 92.4% in 2025-26, down from 95.2% in 2019-20 | Not checked here. Plausible as a CDC SchoolVaxView figure. |

## Context that could mislead

- The "8 deaths" headline figure is state-reported, while the CDC has confirmed only 4. ABC does disclose this.
- The Jefferson County timing issue (see the table).
- The 45-state versus 47-jurisdiction mismatch is small, but the count is the headline framing.
