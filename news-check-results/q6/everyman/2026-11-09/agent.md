## Overall grade

Provisional: 1 Pinocchio (mostly accurate). Every verifiable factual claim checked out. Most of the rest is partisan opinion and prediction, which cannot be graded. This grade rests on a summary, not the verbatim text.

## Materially misleading context

- Guests' assertions (Blanche on "threats" and on Means; Sanders on RFK Jr.) are attributed opinions, not established facts.
- The reported claim that Wiles put donor money ahead of health comes from Means alone. Reports note it could not be independently verified and the White House did not address it directly.
- Reports of Wiles's departure conflict (fired vs resigned Nov 5).
