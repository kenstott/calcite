# Fact-check: WGME, "Portland logs second-warmest start to November as drought lingers across Maine" (Nov 16, 2026)

Method limits: WebFetch returned a summary of the article, not verbatim text. No Python charting or dashboard produced. Primary sources reached: NWS Gray/Portland climate report (CLM), Drought.gov (USDM data valid 11/10/26). Daily/period Nov 1-15 Portland data (NOWData) was not reachable.

## Claims and verdicts
1. Headline: "second-warmest start to November." Body (as summarized) says Nov 1-15 average 48F is THIRD warmest, with 2022 warmest at 50.6F. Internal inconsistency; not resolvable here. FLAG.
2. Nov 1-15 average 48F vs typical 43.1F; highs about 57F vs 51F. Not checkable here (no access to Nov 1-15 station data).
3. Warmest day Nov 4 at 73F. Not checkable here.
4. 1.13 in rain so far vs typical 4.25 in monthly. Not checkable here (Nov data); typical monthly total is plausible.
5. October was very dry. VERIFIED: NWS Portland October 2026 total 1.94 in, 3.48 in below the 5.42 in normal (9 wet days vs 11.8 normal).
6. Drought: severe drought from York to Aroostook; extreme on Midcoast and parts of Aroostook. PARTLY VERIFIED: Drought.gov (valid 11/10/26) shows Maine 42.9% D2, 8.0% D3, 0% D4, 92.8% in drought. Statewide magnitude consistent; county placement not checked (county data not retrieved).
7. Downeast improved from moderate drought to abnormally dry. Not checkable here (no county-level access).
8. CPC favors warmer than average through end of November, near-average precipitation. Not checked (CPC outlook not fetched); not checkable here.
9. Polar vortex shift late Nov/early Dec might bring cooler weather, uncertain effect; active weather possible Thanksgiving week. Forecast opinion, not a checkable fact.
10. Quote: Maine needs "several days of steady, soaking rain." Attributed to the piece/meteorologist; consistent with drought conditions, not independently checkable.

## Rating
Provisional 1 Pinocchio (mostly accurate). Verifiable facts (dry October, widespread severe-to-extreme drought) hold up. Deduction is for the headline/body mismatch (second vs third warmest) and for unverified Nov 1-15 figures.

## Misleading-context flags
- Headline rank conflicts with body text.
- A 15-day partial-month ranking is not a month ranking; it can shift by month end.
- Statewide drought is much larger than "lingers" suggests: about 93% of Maine in drought, about half in severe or extreme.
