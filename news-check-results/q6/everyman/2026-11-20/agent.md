## Misleading or context issues
- The headline "rise" is a 5 bp change; Q2 fell 7 bps. The piece says nothing about that, though it is small.
- Delinquency (4.42%) is still below the Q1 2026 figure of 4.44%, which the piece does not mention.
- The piece is labeled as generated with automation and reviewed by an editor. Some sources report conflicting changes (one search summary cited 6 bps and 51 bps for the headline rate, which appear to be the serious-delinquency changes), but the HousingWire figures are internally consistent.
- MBA data count forbearance loans as delinquent, which is not explained.

Conclusion: numerically accurate where checkable, with a few unverified items. 1 Pinocchio.
