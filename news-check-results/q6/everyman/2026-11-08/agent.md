# Fact-check: GMA, kindergarten vaccine exemption rate hits record (CDC, 2025-26 school year)

## Overall grade: 1 Pinocchio (mostly accurate, with caveats)

The headline numbers match the CDC's SchoolVaxView page and other outlets' coverage of the same release. I could only read the GMA article through a summarizing fetch, not verbatim. I could not reach the CDC's state-level data table or the MMWR text. Several details are therefore unverified, and the varicella line looks internally odd.

## Flags on what may mislead
- **Record framing:** the "record" claim holds for the CDC's series, and 41 states plus DC rose. That national rate is dominated by a few states: Idaho at 17.5%, and 24 states above 5%. The 4.2% figure hides that split.
- **Missing context:** the CDC's accompanying statement reportedly did not mention the record exemption rate. The article, as I read it, does not say so. The CDC also reports about 155,000 exempt children and about 280,000 kindergartners without documentation of two-dose MMR. These figures show the coverage gap is larger than the exemption rate alone.
- **"Coverage rose" for two-dose varicella:** if the article really says this, it should be flagged. It conflicts with the CDC's statement that coverage fell for every vaccine tracked.
