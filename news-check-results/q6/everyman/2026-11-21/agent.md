# Fact-check: CNBC, "Property tax revolt: How reforms could affect your bill" (Kate Dore, 2026-11-16)

Method: WebFetch of the article (returned a summary, not verbatim text, so quotes below are checked only as paraphrases), plus web searches. I could not open most primary documents directly; I relied on search snippets and secondary reporting. Treat "verified" as "corroborated by search results" unless stated.

## Claims and verdicts
| Claim | Verdict | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Florida voters approved Amendment 3 on Nov 3 (expanded homestead exemption, lower assessment cap) | Verified | Multiple outlets: passed ~62% (needs 60%); homestead exemption $50k to $150k in 2027, $250k in 2028 |
| Amendment 3 cost to local governments: $4.9B yr 1, $8.7B yr 2, $11.8B/yr by 2031 | Partly verified, attribution doubtful | Figures match the state Revenue Estimating Conference (Florida Trend, WTXL, PRISM). Article credits the Tax Foundation. Tax Foundation's own number could not be confirmed (it says about $12B; Newsweek cites $8.4B for year 2). Likely a misattribution or a re-use of state figures |
| NC voters approved an amendment requiring the legislature to limit property tax increases | Verified | NCSL/news: levy-limit amendment passed, unofficial, not self-executing |
| Oklahoma voters rejected a property tax measure | Verified | SQ 847 (lower caps) rejected |
| Wyoming voters rejected a property tax measure | Not confirmed | Found the Wyoming Initiative 1 (50% homestead exemption) but no outcome in my sources |
| Ohio abolition effort missed the ballot, backers will try 2027 | Not confirmed | No source found |
| At least 168 state property tax measures in 40 states + DC, Jan 2020 to Jun 2026 (NACo) | Verified | NACo "State Policy Trends: Property Tax Reform 2020-2026" snippet. Note NACo says most are relief measures (exemptions, credits), not caps |
| Property taxes ~28% of local funding in 2024 (Lincoln Institute) | Not checked here | Could not access |
| Median US home price ~$300k (2020), ~$419k (2025), NAR | Not checked here | Could not access NAR data |
| Median owner-occupied property tax bill ~$3,300 (ACS) | Not checked here | Could not access ACS tables |
| Quotes: Matthew Chase (NACo), Jared Walczak (Tax Foundation), Sheriff Rick Staly (Flagler) | Not checkable here | Only a paraphrase of the article was available; Florida Sheriffs Association opposition to Amendment 3 is corroborated generally |

## Rating: 1 Pinocchio (mostly accurate, minor issues)
Core facts on election outcomes and the NACo count check out. Gaps: the cost estimates seem to be attributed to the wrong organization, and two claims (Wyoming, Ohio) plus the NAR/ACS/Lincoln figures could not be verified.

## Potentially misleading
- "Revolt" framing: NACo's 168 measures are mostly relief and exemptions, not caps or abolition.
- Amendment 3 figures are projections and the headline numbers come from a state estimating conference, not the Tax Foundation.
- Results were unofficial; NC's amendment does not itself cap anything yet.
- The headline implies effects on "your bill", but Florida changes start in 2027 and the NC and Ohio items have no current effect on bills.
