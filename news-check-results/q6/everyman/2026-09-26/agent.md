# Fact-check: CDC measles cases and MMR kindergarten vaccination coverage

## Verdict: True

Both figures in the claim match current CDC reporting.

### Measles cases
The CDC's measles data tracker reported **3,659 confirmed measles cases** in the United States for 2026, as of the **September 24, 2026** update (week-over-week, this was an increase of 188 cases, or 5.4%). Of these, 95% (3,478 of 3,659) are outbreak-associated, and 2026 has become the worst year for measles cases in the U.S. since the disease was declared eliminated in 2000.

### MMR kindergarten vaccination coverage
CDC's SchoolVaxView data show that national MMR vaccination coverage among U.S. kindergartners was **95.2% in the 2019-2020 school year** — the peak recorded — and has declined every year since. For the **2025-2026 school year**, CDC reported MMR coverage of **92.4%**, down slightly from 92.5% the year before (2024-2025). This is below the ~95% threshold generally considered necessary for herd immunity against measles, and CDC estimated roughly 280,000 kindergartners lacked documentation of a completed MMR series in 2025-2026.
