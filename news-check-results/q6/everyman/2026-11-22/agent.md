## Overall grade: 1 Pinocchio (mostly accurate)
Nearly every number matches the primary report. Minor issues are the unverified Woodcock quote and the unsupported causal link to the FCPA executive order.

## Misleading-context flags
- The record low is for public companies and their subsidiaries only, not total SEC enforcement. Total SEC actions are a different count.
- A low count is not the same as weak enforcement. The SEC says it now prioritizes quality, and the report shows a bigger share of actions against individuals and more disclosure-fraud cases. The headline implies a retreat; the data are consistent with both a retreat and a shift in focus.
- The framing "under Atkins" mixes in a transition year. FY2026 began Oct 2025, and the enforcement director changed during it (Goodwin describes FY2026 as a "transitionary" year).
- The SEC's overall results for FY2026 should be read alongside this subset.
