## Results
All 12 graded claims are true. Author claims scored honesty 100 (10 of 10 true).
- 16 actions, down 71% from 56, record low since FY2010: true.
- Less than half the prior low of 37 (FY2013); 52 fewer than the 68 in FY2022: true.
- Weakest first full year of a new administration since at least FY2013 (73 in FY2018, 51 in FY2014): true.
- Settlements: $184M total; average $12M (-18% from $15M); median $1M (-76% from $4M): true.
- 99% of settlements were civil penalties; disgorgement and interest were $1M vs $108M in FY2025: true.
- 63% of settling defendants had a monetary settlement vs a 92% average; zero admissions of guilt; FY2022-25 averaged 21: true.
- Issuer-reporting allegations in 50% of actions vs 39%; 100% of civil actions, third straight year: true.
- No FCPA actions, vs a 10% average; the Feb. 10, 2025 executive order: true.
- Civil actions were 25% of the total; 3 of 4 were resolved on a different day; all 12 administrative proceedings were resolved the same day: true.
- Cooperation noted for 53% of settling defendants vs 66%; 16% had cooperation but no penalty: true.
- Sample of 1,065 actions against 903 entities: true.
- Atkins quote (May 8, 2026): the speech reads "we no longer measure the success of our program by the quantity of enforcement actions". The article's bracketed alteration matches the report's own: true.
- Choi quote: verbatim from the report's page-1 callout: true.

## Framing caveats (not errors)
- The scope is public companies and subsidiaries only. The article omits that SEC-wide standalone actions fell only about 22% (303 to 235). The SEC reported 348 total actions and $1.095B in monetary relief for FY2026. Public-company actions were under 7% of standalone actions.
- The 25% civil share rests on just 4 civil actions.
- FY2025 partly overlapped Atkins's tenure (sworn in April 2025).
- The article gives no counterview. The D&O Diary notes private securities litigation is rising as SEC activity falls.
- The Woodcock quote (Sept. 18, 2026 speech) was verified only through the report's footnote, not the speech text.
