Warehouse note

officials.house_race_results has no 2026 rows. Its two-party margins on contested races (1982 +12.5, 2006 +8.3, 2010 -7.0, 2018 +11.2) differ from Ballotpedia all-party gaps but give the same ranking. Warehouse 1990 D seats = 270 vs Ballotpedia 267 (decisive-round vs election-night count). The NYT Upshot piece the article links loaded only as a preview but confirms 222 electoral votes in the compact and five new trifectas reaching 283.
