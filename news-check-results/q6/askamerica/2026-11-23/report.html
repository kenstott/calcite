The Texas Tribune/TPR article's claims about Texas college stopouts, student debt and the completer earnings gap are mostly accurate and cut against its own case.

Article under reviewhttps://www.tpr.org/education/2026-11-18/one-in-three-texas-students-leaves-college-without-a-degree-new-study-says

Summary

Rating: 1 Pinocchio. The article accurately relays most figures of the Philanthropy Advocates/Kirksey study (about 913,000 enrollees, 49% completed, 38% left, at least $1.09B debt, $20,800 earnings gap). Three slips: the headline says one in three where the study finds 38%; $6,250 is called the average where it is the median; and GPAs are said to hover 'just above 1.0' where the report says half of first-year leavers had 1.0 or lower. The primary PDF could not be fetched (over the 25MB cap), so report figures come from FOX 4's coverage of it; the warehouse holds no stopout data, only ACS earnings as a context check.
