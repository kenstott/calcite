# ABC News measles >5,000 article: 1 Pinocchio

The article is largely accurate, with a few unconfirmed items.

- 5,182 cases in 45 states plus DC: consistent with the warehouse. NNDSS (health.cdc_nndss_weekly) shows 4,641 indigenous cases at MMWR week 43 (Oct 24), rising about 240 a week. Other outlets reported 4,588 to 4,852 in late October. The exact figure was not reproducible because the data ends Oct 24.
- Eight deaths (6 PA, 1 NY, 1 WI), 4 CDC-confirmed: the CDC listed 3 in late October, so 4 is plausible. The state announcements were not found independently, so not checkable here.
- 96% unvaccinated or unknown, 2% one dose, 2% two doses, 11% hospitalized, 18 international visitors: cdc.gov returned HTTP 403, so not checkable here. The 93% and 97% effectiveness figures match long-standing CDC figures.
- Kindergarten MMR 92.4% in 2025-26: confirmed by secondary reports on the CDC's August 2026 release. The 2019-20 figure of 95.2% was not confirmed in search.

Nothing is materially misleading, and the article discloses the gap between reported and CDC-confirmed deaths.
