The ABC News measles-over-5,000 article is largely accurate; its death count rests on state announcements the CDC had not yet confirmed

Summary

Grade: 1 Pinocchio. The 5,182 total is consistent with NNDSS data in the warehouse (4,641 indigenous cases by MMWR week 43, ending Oct 24, rising about 240 a week). The kindergarten MMR figure (92.4%) matches reporting on the CDC's August 2026 release. State death announcements and the CDC's four confirmed deaths could not be confirmed from primary sources.

Exclusion disclosure

One exploratory query excluded the rollup rows 'Total' and 'U.S. Residents' (reporting_area NOT IN ('Total','U.S. Residents')) to sum state rows. It returned 9,269 for indigenous cases because regional rollup rows remained and double counted. That figure was discarded and not used. The headline figure, 4,641, comes from the 'Total' row without that exclusion.

Claims

  • 5,182 cases, 45 states plus DC: consistent with the warehouse trend; exact figure not reproducible.
  • Eight deaths (6 PA, 1 NY, 1 WI), 4 CDC-confirmed: plausible against the CDC's 3 in late October; not checkable here.
  • Vaccination shares, 11% hospitalized, 18 international visitors: CDC page blocked (403); not checkable here.
  • Kindergarten MMR 92.4% in 2025-26: confirmed by secondary reports. The 95.2% figure for 2019-20 was not confirmed.

No material misleading framing; the article discloses the 8 versus 4 death gap.

What This Report Does Not Answer

  • Independent sources for rest: cdc.gov returned HTTP 403; state death announcements and the 11% hospitalization share were not independently confirmed.
