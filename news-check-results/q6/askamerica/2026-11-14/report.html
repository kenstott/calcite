Claim checks

  • 4,187 (Jan-Oct 2026), 2,754 (2025), 939 (2023), 1,141 (2024), 584 (Oct 2026), +84% from 2,275: all reproduce from the CSV (4187/2275 = 1.840). True.
  • Code G06N3/0475 526 in Sept 2026, 18 to 119 a month in 2020-2022: matches CSV; code introduced in the January 2023 CPC revision and flagged incomplete pending reclassification.
  • LLM-title counts 7/112/601/780 and first month of 10+ in Aug 2024: match.
  • AI share peaks 9.99% (Apr 2026), 9.97%, 9.75%, 9.41% (Dec 2022), 5.8% (Jan 2019): match. The table of top filers (SAP, Google, IBM) is not in the CSV and could not be checked.

Misleading or fragile points

  • The Sept 2026 code spike (526, vs 263 in Aug and 347 in Oct) is the figure most exposed to retroactive reclassification, which the article discloses.
  • 'Generative' in any title is counted, which the article admits adds noise (generative design), so the total is an upper-bound proxy.
  • October 2026 'all utility applications' is 50,039, about 36% above Oct 2025 (36,827; both five-Thursday months), so the Oct share (8.58%) rests on an unusually large denominator I could not check.
  • Counts reflect filings from about 18 months earlier, which the FAQ states.
  • Commercial context: the page carries affiliate and service promotions, and the About page says AI tools help produce content.
