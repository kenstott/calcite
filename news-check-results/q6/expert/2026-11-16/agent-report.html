Mostly accurate: 1 Pinocchio, but AccuWeather's $16-20B Isaias loss is 8x reported damage

Summary of findings

Grade: 1 Pinocchio. Core season facts check out: 10 named storms, one hurricane (Isaias), first hurricane Oct 8 (ties the 1905 record), Cat 2 landfall near Destin Oct 10, Pacific 24/13 as of Nov 3 (Wikipedia later shows 25/13).

  • Misleading: Isaias $16-20B and Arthur $4-6B are AccuWeather 'damage and economic loss' estimates; reported damage is ~$2B+ and $0.7-1.3B. The figures are attributed, but not flagged as outliers.
  • Omitted: Isaias peaked as Cat 3 (120 mph), so 'Cat 2' is landfall intensity only. 'Latest in 121 years' is a tie with 1905, not a clean record.
  • Not checkable here: the exact DaSilva quote (no source found; the article text was only available as a summary), 'first single-hurricane season since 1925', AccuWeather forecast path (11-16 in March, 8-11 later partly confirmed by Newsweek/Claims Journal).
