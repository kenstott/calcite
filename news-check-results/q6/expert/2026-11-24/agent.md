# Fact-check: Pew "Democratic wave sweeps in more split Senate delegations"
**Rating: 0 Pinocchios.**

## Claim: 10 split, 23 unified D, 17 unified R in 120th
- **Answer:** Verified by independent rebuild. Splits: AK, IA, KS, MS, NC, OH, SC, TX, PA, WI.
- **Key Values:** Jan 2027; 56 D caucus (54 D + 2 I) vs 44 R.
- **Sources:** Wikipedia 2026 US Senate elections; Yahoo/NBC results.
- **Confidence:** High, from an independent recount.

## Claim: 3 splits in 119th
- **Answer:** Verified (ME, PA, WI). **Confidence:** High.

## Claim: Johnson 13th most conservative, Baldwin 6th most liberal
- **Answer:** Verified. Voteview HS119 gives Johnson 14th including VP Vance, 13th without; Baldwin 6th.
- **Confidence:** High.

## Claim: Klobuchar and Blackburn elected governors, same-party successors assumed
- **Answer:** Governor wins verified; appointments not yet made, so the assumption is unverified.
- **Confidence:** Medium-high.

## Claim: historical series (96th=27, floor of 20 for 1973-94, 113th=17), "since 2019", and bill/pair anecdotes
- **Answer:** Not checkable here: the Biographical Directory series was not re-derived. The anecdotes match my background knowledge but were not re-fetched. "Since 2019" is borderline against the 116th's 9.
- **Confidence:** Medium.

## Overall
Accurate. Caveats: independents are counted with their caucus, and the appointment assumption. The Wikipedia SC account is internally inconsistent but irrelevant to the count.
