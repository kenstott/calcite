# Fact-check: TBF 2026 Greater Boston Housing Report Card release

**Grade: 1 Pinocchio.** Mostly accurate; one overstated figure.

## Claim: new housing units fell "by more than half"
- **Answer:** Overstated. Net new units 15,241 (Jul 2025-Jul 2026) vs 29,024 prior year = -47.5% (WBUR); Governing says "nearly half".
- **Sources:** WBUR, Governing. **Confidence:** medium (secondary; prior year inflated by delayed pandemic projects).

## Claim: population +9,000 in 2025, slowest since 2011 ex-pandemic
- **Answer:** Corroborated (49,000 in 2024). **Sources:** Boston Globe, Foundation email. **Confidence:** medium.

## Claim: permits lowest in a decade
- **Answer:** Corroborated; 7,746 units in 2025 vs 12,000-15,000/yr 2015-2021. **Source:** Governing. **Confidence:** medium.

## Claim: six communities under $500K, down from 42 a decade ago
- **Answer:** Corroborated (WBUR garbles it as "down 42%"). **Confidence:** medium.

## Claim: over 48% of renters cost-burdened in 2024
- **Answer:** Corroborated by WBUR. Census ACS API is key-gated ("Missing Key"), so no primary re-derivation. **Confidence:** medium.

## Not checkable here
Herbert quote, 9th of 11 permit rank, $181,000 income, one in six renters, ~30,000 international / 32,000 domestic migration. Schuster's quoted line did not match WBUR's quotes (may be from the release).
