# Fact check: Inside Climate News, federal land sell-off (Nov 13, 2026)

**Rating: 1 Pinocchio.** 26 claims/quotes: 11 accurate, 7 imprecise, 8 not checkable here; none found false.

## Summary
- Core claim verified: plan says it will "decrease the size of the federal estate"; LWCF no longer used to expand holdings.
- Imprecise: "federal judge" was an IBLA administrative judge stay (Sept 1); Pitkin permit approved by county Sept 23, federal sign-off pending, Forest Service keeps ownership; 2,500-acre rule is notice plus 90 days with congressional disapproval, not approval; 3.2B offshore acres unsupported (~1.7B published); 17% workforce is gross (OPM net ~9% through Nov 2025); Boulder acreage 80/81/88.5; Maroon Bells 191,000 visitors, not hundreds of thousands.
- Misleading by framing: "untold acres" because plan names no acreage; sales are to state/local/tribal buyers, industry resale is critics' prediction; plan non-binding.
- Not checkable: primary plan PDF 404 (quotes via AP/Outdoor Life/E&E), Alaska 28M acres 2026 claim, Interior non-response, Groetzinger/O'Neill quotes, Burgum 'red tape' quote.
