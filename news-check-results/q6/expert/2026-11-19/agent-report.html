Grist El Nino piece: 1 Pinocchio, mostly accurate

Summary of findings: mostly accurate, with modeled figures stated more firmly than the evidence warrants

Rating: 1 Pinocchio of 4. The checkable headline claims hold: record-strength El Nino (Carbon Brief forecasts a Nino3.4 peak near 3.6-4 C against 2.75 C in 2015-16), Newsom's statewide emergency (Sep 21, 2026), and record-low Lake Mead (1,040.50 ft) and Powell (3,519.91 ft) in August 2026.

  • The 450,000 deaths figure is a modeled, not yet peer-reviewed projection (451,000, Jun 2026-Feb 2027), covering heat only; the article's phrase 'could kill' is fair but the uncertainty deserves more emphasis.
  • Regional forecasts are probabilistic. NOAA itself says the precipitation signal is less consistent than temperature; the piece does flag local uncertainty, which is to its credit.
  • Could not check: the verbatim expert quotes (the fetch tool returned only a paraphrase of the article) and exact NOAA probabilities such as Northeast >50%. I could not confirm the final Atlantic hurricane tally. Direct CPC pages were not opened; the November CPC discussion was found only on a third-party blog.
  • Would change the rating: a CPC map contradicting the stated outlooks, or the 450,000 figure being withdrawn.
