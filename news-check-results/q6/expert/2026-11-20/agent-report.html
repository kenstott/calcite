Q3 2026 delinquency article: 1 Pinocchio

Summary: nearly every figure checks out; framing is the weakness

Rating: 1 Pinocchio. The headline 4.42% (+5 bps quarter, +43 bps year) and the loan-type figures match MBA data as reported by Scotsman Guide and reconcile arithmetically to Q2 2026 (4.37%) and Q3 2025 (3.99%). The FHA 1.89% and VA 1.06% foreclosure highs, state increases and the 4.1% October unemployment rate also check.

  • Not checkable here: Walsh's remarks on wages, savings and consumer debt; the primary MBA release returned HTTP 403, so verification is via secondary coverage.
  • Misleading framing: a 5 bp rise follows a 7 bp fall in Q2; FHA delinquency actually fell 5 bps; the true story is FHA/VA foreclosures. Wyoming's +72 bps is a small-state swing. Unemployment is flat while delinquency is up 43 bps YoY, so "tracks closely" is weak.
  • Would change the verdict: the MBA release contradicting any secondary figure.
