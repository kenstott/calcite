1 Pinocchio: the Bridewell headline numbers hold, but one quarter-on-quarter figure and the framing need care

Summary of findings

The headline is accurate: Bridewell counted 2,889 leak-site victims in Q3 2026 against 1,609 in Q3 2025 (+79.6%, about 80%), and manufacturing was the top sector at 474 victims (16.4%).

  • Inconsistent figure: the article reportedly says Q3 was about 30% above Q2, but Bridewell's own report says +26% (2,299 to 2,889). GuidePoint says +21%.
  • Source inconsistency: Bridewell's Q2 manufacturing share (12.4%, 274 victims) implies 11.9% of its own Q2 total.
  • Framing: the figures are leak-site postings, not confirmed attacks; active groups grew 29% (86 to 111); ReliaQuest attributes much of manufacturing's jump to Qilin and The Gentlemen.
  • Not checkable here: Coveware ransom payment figures (average $1,025,278, median $400,000, 28% paid).
  • Limitation: industrialcyber.co returned HTTP 403, so I could not read the article; claims were taken from summaries of it and checked against Bridewell's primary report. My own ransomware.live recount gave 2,902 (Q3 2026), 2,149 (Q2) and 1,398 (Q3 2025), so the year-on-year base is unstable.
