# Fact-check: CNN SOTU 2026-11-08

Grade: 2 Pinocchios.

Mostly accurate segment, but several guest claims are overstated: Pinocchio grade 2 of 4
Of 26 claims in the CNN State of the Union transcript (Nov 8, 2026), about 17 hold up, 4 are wrong or contradicted, and 5 could not be verified. Most errors are in partisan guests' numbers, not host framing.

Contradicted: Gidley said Trump's immigration approval is above 50%; polls show 41-43%. Sanders said 19 US troops died in the Iran war; the Pentagon reported 21 (Oct 19), later 22. Sanders attributed 15 million lost coverage to the 2025 law; CBO says about 10 million for the law, about 16 million only with ACA credit expiry. Gidley's 20 million Biden-era entries is exaggerated.

Misattributed or inconsistent: Bash's $14M Schumer investment matches the GOP Senate Leadership Fund's Kansas spending for Marshall; Senate Majority PAC was reported near $5M. Hamilton gave 38 and 36 years as his pastorate.

Confirmed: crime at historic lows (murder 4.1/100k, FBI 2025), Reflecting Pool dismissal, Dec 11 funding deadline, Hamilton the first Kansas Democratic senator in 94 years, Wiles out as chief of staff with Miller replacing her, Kushner $2B Saudi investment, Medicare for All 66% (KFF, framing-sensitive).

Misleading even where true: Don Jr. wedding payments omit that Kremlev says he was repaid; Sanders' 66% drops to about 52% when private insurance elimination is stated; Means's allegations against Wiles are unproven and Wiles's exit timing is disputed.

Limits: the transcript was readable only through a summarizing fetcher (quotes paraphrased, not verbatim); no DOJ case count, Hamilton county swing, or Musk $80M figure was verified; the Iran count is as of mid-October; no vote margins located. A CNN fact-check of the piece's host is not warranted: the 'report' is a live debate, so the grade reflects claims aired, not editorial intent.
