Fortune Social Security story: 1 Pinocchio

Findings: mostly accurate reporting, missing context

1 Pinocchio. The 22% cut (OASI, 4Q 2032, 78% payable) and CRFB's $190 trillion (real, 75 years, 165% of GDP) check out. The cut is 17% for combined OASDI (3Q 2034). The MacGuineas bond-market quote is not in CRFB's release; the 2.6-point rate, 6.6% yield, $500/month, 70 million and TD Securities dates could not be checked here. Rating would rise if those prove wrong.
