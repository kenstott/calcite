# Fact-check: "One in three Texas students leaves college without a degree" (TPR / Texas Tribune, 2026-11-18)

## Summary: mostly accurate, 1 Pinocchio; the $6,250 "average" is really the median ($12,155 is the mean)
Verdict: 1 Pinocchio (minor errors, core finding right). Primary source: Philanthropy Advocates / Kirksey "Stopouts Report 1" (Nov 2026). Cohort 913,443 (article: 900,000); 38% stopped out in 6 yrs (347,061), 49% completed, 14% still enrolled; ~29% of stopouts borrowed.

## Claim-by-claim
- "About one in three... left without a degree": 38% (more than one in three, and 14% still enrolled). Confidence high.
- 900,000 students, HS class 2017-2022: report 913,443. Accurate rounding. High.
- 49% / 38% (>300,000) / 14%: exact (443,473 / 347,061 / 122,909). High.
- "$1.1 billion over the last five years": report says at least $1.09B held by 331,373 stopouts who never completed, observed 2 to 7 years by entering class (2017 class $258M ... 2022 class $60M); it is a floor, not a five-year accumulation. Misframed. High.
- "Average borrower owed $6,250": report says typical (median) borrower owes $6,250; average is $12,155. Wrong statistic, understates by about half. High.
- "Nearly 30% had debt": 29% of six-year stopouts. High.
- Most left in first year: 51% in year one, 78% by end of year two. High.
- GPA "just above 1.0": half of year-one leavers at 1.0 or below; median year-one exit GPA about 1.04 (community college). Roughly right but "averages" imprecise. Medium.
- $20,800 wage gap at 10 years: $60,500 vs $39,700, real 2024 dollars, for HS classes 2012-2016 (not the 2017-22 cohort); article does not say this. Statewide public HS grads. High.
- Abbott 60% goal by 2030: 60x30TX began 2015 (THECB, endorsed by Abbott) for ages 25-34; now "credential of value" and expanded to working-age Texans. Attributing it to Abbott as setter is loose; I found no dated Abbott statement with the exact wording. Medium.
- Hinojosa governor-elect, has not said whether she keeps the goal: she beat Abbott on 2026-11-03 (AP call; about 52.8% to 45.7% per Wikipedia). Her silence not verifiable. Election high; silence not checkable.
- Pollock (Texas State Building and Construction Trades Council) on her team: Tribune press-conference piece says he "will also serve on her team"; "appointed" is the article's paraphrase. Medium.
- Grantham quote ("were truly set up for success"): attributed spoken quote; not checkable here (no transcript; could not confirm it appears in the report). 

## Misleading by context
- "Left college" counts only stopouts after a year gone; the 14% still enrolled are excluded, so the 38% is not a final rate.
- Wage gap is correlational and compositional (completers differ from non-completers); presented as an outcome of leaving.
- Debt total is a floor and rises with time.
