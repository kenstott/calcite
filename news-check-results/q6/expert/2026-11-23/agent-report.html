Fact-check: "One in three Texas students leaves college without a degree"

Mostly accurate, 1 Pinocchio: the $6,250 "average" is actually the median

The piece's core finding is right: 38% of 913,443 Texas college entrants (HS classes 2017-2022) stopped out within six years, 49% completed, 14% were still enrolled. Two errors: the article calls $6,250 the average debt, but the report says it is the median (mean $12,155); and "$1.1 billion over the last five years" is really at least $1.09B held by 331,373 stopouts across classes observed two to seven years, a floor that will grow.

  • The $20,800 wage gap ($60,500 vs $39,700) comes from HS classes 2012-2016, not the studied cohort, and is correlational.
  • Abbott "set" the 60% goal loosely: 60x30TX dates to 2015 (THECB, Abbott-endorsed), originally for ages 25-34.
  • Not checkable here: the Grantham quote and Hinojosa's silence on the goal. I could not find the quote in the report.
  • Would change the rating: a primary statement showing Abbott formally set the goal, or a published correction of the median/mean wording.
