# Fact Check: 2026 Measles Cases & Kindergarten MMR Coverage — Step Record

Checked: 2026-09-26

## Step 1: Measles case count (CDC, 2026)

**Answer:** Confirmed — CDC reports 3,659 confirmed measles cases in the U.S. in 2026, as of September 24, 2026 (page updated September 25, 2026). Matches the claim exactly.

**Key Values:**
- Confirmed cases (2026, as of 9/24/26): 3,659
- Outbreak-associated: 3,478 (95%)
- Jurisdictions with cases: 47
- Prior post-elimination-era full-year record (2019): 1,274 confirmed cases
- CDC explicitly excludes probable cases from this count; no evidence of a mid-2026 change to the confirmed/probable case definition or to outbreak-vs-sporadic classification. CDC flags the 2025–2026 counts as "preliminary and subject to change" (routine revision, not a definitional shift).

**Sources:**
- CDC, Measles Cases and Outbreaks (data-research page): https://www.cdc.gov/measles/data-research/index.html (primary, fetched directly)
- Contagion Live, Measles Update Sept 25, 2026: https://www.contagionlive.com/view/measles-update-september-25-2026 (secondary corroboration)

**Confidence:** High. Verified directly on CDC's own page (primary source fetch), and the same 3,659/9-24-26 figure is independently corroborated by two unrelated news outlets citing CDC.

## Step 2: Kindergarten MMR coverage — 2019–20 baseline

**Answer:** Confirmed — CDC's MMWR report for the 2019–20 school year states 95.2% national MMR coverage among kindergartners.

**Key Values:**
- 2019–20 national MMR coverage: 95.2%
- State range: 86.6% (Alabama) to 99.1% (Mississippi)
- ~3.68 million kindergartners assessed; 48 states reported coverage data
- Caveats CDC attaches to this year specifically: COVID-19 disruption to the 2019–20 assessment itself; variable state assessment dates (school-year start through April 30); differing state requirements/data-collection methods across years.

**Sources:**
- CDC MMWR, Kindergarten Vaccination Coverage 2019–20: https://www.cdc.gov/mmwr/volumes/70/wr/mm7003a2.htm (primary)

**Confidence:** High. Primary CDC report fetched directly; figure also echoed consistently across multiple secondary sources (Forbes, KFF).

## Step 3: Kindergarten MMR coverage — 2025–26 current value and full trend

**Answer:** Confirmed — CDC reports 92.4% MMR coverage among kindergartners for the 2025–26 school year, down from 95.2% in 2019–20. The decline is monotonic year over year, not a two-point comparison hiding a rebound.

**Key Values (national MMR coverage by school year):**
- 2019–20: 95.2%
- 2020–21: 93.9%
- 2021–22: 93.5%
- 2022–23: 93.1%
- 2023–24: 92.7%
- 2024–25: 92.5%
- 2025–26: 92.4%
- 2025–26 exemption rate: 4.2% (up from 3.6% the prior year); ~280,000 kindergartners without MMR documentation
- State range 2025–26: 98.9% (West Virginia) to 75.2% (Idaho)

**Sources:**
- CDC SchoolVaxView data page: https://www.cdc.gov/schoolvaxview/data/index.html (primary)
- CDC MMWR 2022–23 report (93.1%): https://www.cdc.gov/mmwr/volumes/72/wr/mm7245a2.htm (primary)
- CDC MMWR 2023–24 report (92.7%): https://www.cdc.gov/mmwr/volumes/73/wr/mm7341a3.htm (primary)
- Axios, "Kindergarten vaccination rates fell again in 2025-2026, CDC says": https://www.axios.com/2026/08/17/kindergarten-vaccine-rates-fall (secondary)
- CNBC, "U.S. kindergarten vaccine coverage falls, CDC says": https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/17/us-kindergarten-vaccine-falls-cdc.html (secondary)
- KFF, "Kindergarten Routine Vaccination Rates Continue to Decline": https://www.kff.org/medicaid/kindergarten-routine-vaccination-rates-continue-to-decline/ (secondary)

**Confidence:** High for the 2019–20 and 2025–26 endpoints (both primary CDC, cross-checked with 2+ independent secondary sources). Medium-high for the intervening-year values (2020–21 through 2024–25) — these were sourced from secondary aggregation of CDC MMWR reports rather than re-fetching each individual annual MMWR page, though the 2022–23 and 2023–24 points were confirmed directly against their MMWR primary pages.

---

## Overall Verdict

**TRUE.** Both headline figures in the claim — 3,659 confirmed measles cases as of September 24, 2026, and kindergarten MMR coverage falling from 95.2% (2019–20) to 92.4% (2025–26) — match CDC's own published data exactly, with no evidence of a methodology change (confirmed-vs-probable case definition, or MMR survey definition) that would invalidate the comparison. Standard CDC-documented caveats about cross-year comparability of the kindergarten survey (state variation in requirements/timing, COVID-era disruption to the 2019–20 baseline year) apply but do not change the reported national topline figures, which are what the claim cites.
