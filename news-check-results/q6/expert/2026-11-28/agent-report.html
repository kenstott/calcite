The core numbers hold: 1 Pinocchio

Summary of findings

The piece's headline numbers are accurate; I grade it 1 Pinocchio (mostly true, minor unconfirmed detail). NOAA's Los Angeles gauge recorded a preliminary peak of 8.194 ft MLLW on Nov 25, exactly tying the 8.194 ft of Oct 27 (the article says 8.19 ft, tying a record from the previous month). La Jolla reached 8.114 ft on Nov 25 versus 7.963 ft on Oct 27, a new record, as stated.

  • Naples sandbag/plywood/aging-seawall details confirmed by Long Beach Post and CBS LA.
  • The $125M retrofit figure is reported elsewhere as the cost of the remaining four phases with funding unresolved; "resumed" was not confirmed.
  • Possibly misleading: waves hitting La Jolla houses and Pacifica pier damage match mid-November swell coverage (Seal Beach homes flooded, pier closures Nov 14-16) better than Thanksgiving-week tides; I could not tie them to Thanksgiving week.
  • Not checkable here: Newport Beach 70 sea walls and staff 20 to 60, San Diego boardwalk closures, and all four named quotes. Dec forecast is supported by NOAA (above-normal tides Dec 22-26).
  • Caveat: NOAA data are preliminary; the tie depends on 0.001-ft precision and could change on verification. Title says "Southern California" though piece spans to San Francisco.
