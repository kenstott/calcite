Mostly accurate: 1 Pinocchio

Summary of findings

The piece (Hechinger/AP, 2026-11-18) is largely accurate: 1 Pinocchio. Its core national figures (enrollment loss ~1.4M, 1 in 4 schools down 15%+, teachers +1%, $190B relief, 21+ takeovers) match the Stanford paper (EdWorkingPaper 26-1624) and Chalkbeat. Not verified: Seattle and San Francisco local figures and the SF deficit projections. One quote, that the nation has never had more teachers per student, may overreach the paper's 15-year comparison. The Stanford release says 'over a million' while the 1.4M comes via coverage. I could not open the primary paper or the Stanford release (403), so confidence is medium. Framing caveat: the headline stresses delay; the enrollment fall is partly offset by rising disability costs the article does mention.
