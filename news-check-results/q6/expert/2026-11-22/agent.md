# Fact-check: InvestmentNews "SEC enforcement against public companies hits record low under Atkins" (Steve Randall, 2026-11-12)

## Summary
Rating: 0 Pinocchios on facts (one contextual flag, so at most 1). Every figure I could locate matches the Cornerstone/NYU Pollack SEED report.

Verified (NYU Stern summary, D&O Diary, CLS Blue Sky):
- 16 actions in FY2026, down 71% from 56: confirmed.
- $184M settlements, lowest in SEED, more than $600M below FY2025: confirmed.
- 99% civil penalties, $1M disgorgement plus interest: confirmed.
- No admissions, first since FY2021: confirmed.
- No FCPA actions, first year in SEED: confirmed.
- Issuer reporting at 50% vs 39%: confirmed.
- Civil actions 25% (4 of 16), highest since FY2013: confirmed.
- Individuals named in a larger share than any of prior 10 years: confirmed.
- Average $12M, median $1M: confirmed by D&O Diary. The 18% and 76% drops are not independently confirmed (prior-year values not found).
- Atkins quote, May 8, 2026 conference: confirmed (CLS Blue Sky, Harvard CorpGov).

Not checkable here: cooperation noted 53% (lowest since FY2014) and 16% without monetary settlement. The full SEED report was not retrievable.

Misleading context:
- The scope is only public companies and subsidiaries. SEC-wide actions fell about 24% (American Banker, unverified here).
- The headline "under Atkins" implies causation. The data show timing, not cause.
- Atkins says counts are "not the only or most telling" measure.
- Mean $12M vs median $1M shows the totals are skewed by a few large cases.

Confidence: high on the figures, medium on the framing.
