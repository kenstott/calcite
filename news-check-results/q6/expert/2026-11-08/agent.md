# Fact-check: GMA, kindergarten vaccine exemption rate hits record (CDC, 2025-26 school year)

**Overall grade: 1 Pinocchio (mostly accurate, minor gaps)**

Caveats on method: The GMA page fetched, but only as a model-written summary, not verbatim text. I checked its claims against the CDC SchoolVaxView page (dated Aug 17, 2026), Fox10 Phoenix, MLO-Online (a CDC summary), AHA and Ideastream. I could not open the CDC state-level data table or the MMWR. State rankings and prior-year per-vaccine figures are therefore unverified. I wrote no files.

**Potentially misleading or missing context**
- The headline "record" 4.2% is accurate. But overall coverage fell only about 0.1 point, and most vaccines sit at 92.0 to 94%, so "exemptions are surging" would overstate the effect on coverage. Exemptions rose in 41 states and DC, and 24 states exceeded 5%.
- Comparing 92.4% with a "95% pre-pandemic" level mixes years. The decline is gradual from 2019-20 onward, so a reader could infer a sudden drop that did not happen.
- The count of about 280,000 kindergartners without documented MMR completion (Fox10) is a key figure the summary did not mention. Exemptions are only one part of the gap, since missing documentation and non-compliance also contribute.
- State extremes are ecological. Rates reflect state exemption laws and reporting rules (for example, differences in how exemptions are documented), not just parental attitudes.
- The 2020-21 baseline year was affected by pandemic data disruptions, which could exaggerate the long-run rise.

**Confidence:** Headline national figures are high. State rankings, the 2020-21 baseline and the quote are low to medium because I could not reach the primary data table.
