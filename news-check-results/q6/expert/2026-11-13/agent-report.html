CFR piece is largely accurate: 1 Pinocchio

Summary of findings: mostly accurate, stale in places, a few claims unverified

Of the roughly two dozen checkable claims in Lindsay's CFR article, every one I could test is consistent with later reporting. No claim was contradicted. Overall grade: 1 Pinocchio, held there only because several claims are unverified, not because any is wrong.

  • Seat tallies were accurate when published and are now behind: AP Nov 12 shows 255 D / 169 R versus CFR's 254 / 165 with 16 undecided.
  • Senate: CFR says Alaska and Mississippi uncalled; Wikipedia (Nov 12) lists Mississippi as a Democratic flip, so only Alaska remains.
  • Turnout (123.5M, 49.1%, second-highest midterm since 1914) matches the UF Election Lab and Brennan Center; another tally gives 124.33M and 49.42%. All preliminary.
  • Not checkable here: GOP outspending, average age of new senators, 12-of-14 control changes, the 283 electoral-vote compact scenario, the New England delegation claim, 118 new House members.
  • Redistricting "2 of 15": direction confirmed (Texas GOP won 1 of 5 targeted, Democrats flipped two GOP seats, California Democrats flipped 5 of 5), exact count not confirmed.
  • Potentially misleading: the 13-point popular-vote margin and "second-largest since 1942" ranking are not certified; sources differ (1974 vs 1976). Forecast-style projections (259 D) are NBC's, not final results. A later source could change any figure; counts are still moving.
