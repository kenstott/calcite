# Fact check: CNBC property tax revolt (2026-11-16)
**Rating: 0.5 Pinocchios (0-1).** No claim contradicted; 8 of 12 claim groups verified; 2 unconfirmed; quotes not checkable.

- **Claim:** Median SF home price ~$300k (2020) to ~$419k (2025), NAR
  - **Answer/Key Values:** Verified (2025: $419,300 SF; $414,300 all types). 2020 figure not retrieved
  - **Confidence:** medium
- **Claim:** Florida Amendment 3 approved; expands homestead exemption for non-school levies, lowers cap
  - **Answer/Key Values:** Verified: passed ~62% (60% needed); $150k in 2027, $250k in 2028; non-homestead cap 10% to 5%
  - **Confidence:** high
- **Claim:** North Carolina amendment requires legislature to limit increases
  - **Answer/Key Values:** Verified, but not self-executing: directs future legislation
  - **Confidence:** high
- **Claim:** Oklahoma and Wyoming rejected
  - **Answer/Key Values:** Verified (OK SQ 847; WY Initiative 1 ~43% yes). Results unofficial until canvass
  - **Confidence:** high
- **Claim:** Ohio abolition lacked signatures; retry 2027
  - **Answer/Key Values:** Verified (announced June 5, 2026; 413,488 valid needed; group targets 2027)
  - **Confidence:** high
- **Claim:** 168 state measures, 40 states + DC, Jan 2020-Jun 2026 (NACo)
  - **Answer/Key Values:** Verified on NACo report
  - **Confidence:** high
- **Claim:** Property tax ~28% of local funding in 2024 (Lincoln)
  - **Answer/Key Values:** Verified: 28.4% average
  - **Confidence:** medium
- **Claim:** Florida cost $4.9B / $8.7B / $11.8B (Tax Foundation)
  - **Answer/Key Values:** Verified on Tax Foundation page; same path in FL Revenue Estimating Conference
  - **Confidence:** high
- **Claim:** Median owner bill ~$3,300 (ACS)
  - **Answer/Key Values:** Not confirmed: 2024 ACS 1-yr median found at $3,211 via secondary sites; article says 'last year'
  - **Confidence:** low
- **Claim:** Bills under $250 to over $10,000 (Tax Foundation 2026)
  - **Answer/Key Values:** Not located; NYC median above $10,000 and Birmingham $1,156 in ACS 5-yr (LendingTree)
  - **Confidence:** low
- **Claim:** Quotes: Chase (NACo), Walczak (Tax Foundation), Staly (Flagler Sheriff); NY rock salt
  - **Answer/Key Values:** Not checkable here (interview quotes, no transcripts)
  - **Confidence:** n/a
- **Claim:** 1 mill = $1 per $1,000; bills can rise as millage falls
  - **Answer/Key Values:** Definitional, correct
  - **Confidence:** high

Misleading by omission: Florida cost figures are static estimates; NC amendment is not self-executing; national medians hide state variation.

Sources: CNBC article; Tax Foundation FL Amendment 3; NACo 2020-2026 report; NCSL; Ballotpedia; WOSU; NAR; Lincoln Institute; Census B25103 (secondary).
