# Fact-check: ABC News, "Measles cases surpass 5,000 in the US with infections confirmed in 45 states: CDC" (Mary Kekatos, 2026-11-06)

Overall: 1 Pinocchio (essentially accurate; one framing issue on geography). Article fetched directly (ABC page via WebFetch).

| Claim | Grade | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| At least 5,182 confirmed 2026 cases | True | CDC measles page, as of Nov 5 2026: 5,182 (up 330 from prior week per Contagion Live Nov 6; US News headline matches). High confidence. |
| 18 cases among international visitors | True | CDC page: 18. |
| Cases in "45 states and D.C." | Mostly true / imprecise | CDC lists 47 jurisdictions, with New York City and New York State separate. Removing NYC leaves 46 = 45 states + DC. I counted the CDC list: it matches the article's number only if NYC is excluded and DC counted separately. Also 18 visitor cases are not assigned to a state (5,164 jurisdiction cases). Consistent with the article. |
| Eight measles deaths in 2026: 6 PA, 1 NY, 1 WI | True as state-reported; CDC counts only 4 | Contagion Live Nov 6: state health departments report 8; PA death this week was the state's sixth; NY child (Lewis County outbreak); WI infant. CDC page shows 4 "subject to change". Article states this CDC/state gap, so it is not misleading. |
| CDC confirmed four of the eight | True | CDC page "4*". |
| NY first death in 30+ years; WI first since 1990 | Partly checked | Search results: WI first in "more than 35 years" (consistent with 1990); NY "first in 30 years" reported. No primary state release read. |
| PA death: unvaccinated Jefferson County resident | Partly checked | Earlier PA death (40-yr-old woman, Jefferson Twp, Jefferson County coroner) was reported in Sept; Contagion says the latest PA death was a 21-year-old unvaccinated woman. Which county the newest death occurred in was not verified. |
| ~96% unvaccinated/unknown; 2% one dose; 2% two doses | True (secondary) | Contagion Oct 23 and Star-Advertiser Oct 30 report 96/2/2 from CDC. CDC page charts did not render for me; not read at primary directly. |
| At least 11% hospitalized | True (secondary) | Star-Advertiser Oct 30: 511 hospitalized, 11%. "More than 1 in 10 under 5": not verified (CDC chart did not render). |
| Plurality of cases ages 5-19 | Not checkable here | CDC age chart not rendered; only a snippet that under-20s are 58% of cases. |
| MMR 93% (1 dose) / 97% (2 doses) effective; schedule 12-15 mo and 4-6 yr | True | CDC page states 93%/97%. |
| Kindergarten MMR 92.4% in 2025-26, vs 95.2% in 2019-20 | 92.4% true; 95.2% not checked | News relays of CDC release (Aug 2026, Healio, HealthDay) confirm 92.4%. I did not retrieve CDC SchoolVaxView for 2019-20 (commonly reported as 95%). Note one relay said prior year was 92.5%, so the decline is mostly pre-2024, which the article's framing (vs 2019-20) correctly conveys. |

Context sanity checks: 2025 full year was 2,289 cases (CDC), so 2026 is more than double. Outbreak-associated share 96% (4,993/5,182).

Misleading-ness: none material. Reader could mistake "45 states" as the full jurisdiction count (CDC says 47 incl. NYC), and the 8-vs-4 death gap is disclosed. Hospitalization and age figures were confirmed only through secondary outlets.

Limits: CNN (HTTP 451) and the CDC demographic charts were inaccessible; no state health department primary releases read.
