# Fact check: WGME "Portland logs third-warmest start to November as drought lingers across Maine" (Nov 16, 2026)

Overall: 1 Pinocchio (mostly accurate; rank soft, a few overstatements). Full report: agent-report.html; board: agent-dashboard.png.

## Step 1: Temperature
**Answer:** Supported, with a rank caveat. Avg temp Nov 1-15 = 48.00 F, tied exactly with 1969. That is tied 3rd/4th since 1941 and tied 4th/5th on the 1871-2026 record (1938 = 50.17 F is second). URL slug and HTML title tag say "second-warmest" (wrong); displayed headline and body say "third".
**Key Values:** 2026 avg high 56.7 F vs NWS normal 51.2 F (article: 57 vs ~51, ok). Nov 4 = 73 F (ok). 2022 = 50.60 F (ok). Article's "typical" 43.1 F matches the 2006-2025 mean (43.2), not the NWS 1991-2020 normal (42.6). 11 of 15 days above normal ("nearly every day" overstated). n=15 days; 152 Novembers.
**Sources:** NOAA RCC-ACIS StnData PWMthr (Portland Area threaded record); ACIS 1991-2020 normals.
**Confidence:** High on values; medium on rank (exact tie, record choice).

## Step 2: Precipitation
**Answer:** Totals supported; "six days" not reproduced; framing misleading.
**Key Values:** 1.13 in Nov 1-15 (ok). 5 days with >=0.01 in, plus 2 trace days. Normal to date 2.02-2.07 in, so ~55% of normal, versus the whole-month 4.25 in the article cites (ok as monthly mean, n=30). Oct 2026 = 1.67 in vs 5.25 normal, 23rd driest of 155 ("unusually dry" ok).
**Sources:** NOAA RCC-ACIS PWMthr.
**Confidence:** High.

## Step 3: Drought
**Answer:** Supported. Latest USDM was valid Nov 10 (released Nov 12).
**Key Values (own overlay, % of Maine area):** D1+ 96.4 to 89.4 (Oct 27 to Nov 10); D2+ 53.4 to 49.1; D3 3.5 to 7.7. Washington D1+ 93 to 43%, Hancock 79 to 46% (Downeast easing real, about half still D1). D3 in Lincoln 68%, Sagadahoc 58%, Knox 49%, Aroostook 19%.
**Sources:** USDM shapefiles 20261027/1103/1110; Census cb_2023_us_county_20m. USDM stats API returned empty arrays.
**Confidence:** Medium-high (percentages approximate, +/-2-3 pts at coasts).

## Step 4: Outlooks
**Answer:** Temperature supported; precipitation "near-average" fair but loose.
**Key Values:** CPC 8-14 day (Nov 24-30, issued Nov 16): temp Above 50%; precip Above at the 33% tier.
**Sources:** CPC GIS 814temp_20261116, 814prcp_20261116.
**Confidence:** Medium.

## Not checkable here
Polar vortex shift, Thanksgiving-week activity, "Northeast just came out of an active stretch" (hedged forecasts / undefined).

## Misleading flags
Pairing 1.13 in with the full-month 4.25 in; undisclosed base period for 43.1 F; "second-warmest" slug/title.
