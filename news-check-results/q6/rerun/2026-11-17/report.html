The WGME article's claims about Portland's warm start to November and Maine drought are biased and mostly accurate; 1 claim could not be confirmed.

Article under reviewhttps://wgme.com/news/local/portland-logs-second-warmest-start-to-november-as-drought-lingers-across-maine

Summary

Rating: 1 Pinocchio. Observations (48.0 F average, 73 F peak, 57 F average high, 1.13 in, 2022 warmest at 50.6 F) match NOAA/ACIS data, and the county drought picture is broadly right (USDM Nov 10: extreme drought in Lincoln, Knox, Sagadahoc and part of Aroostook). But the rank is off: the URL says second-warmest, the title says third, and on the full Portland record (ACIS PWMthr) 2026 is tied fourth (2022, 1938 and 1975 warmer; 1969 tied at 48.00), third only post-1941. The 1.13 in is set beside a full-month 4.25 in normal; the Nov 1-15 normal is about 2.0 in.

Misleading by omission: the warmth was front-loaded; Nov 12-15 ran at or below normal. The CPC outlook claim could not be read from its image map.

Data caveat: a first drought query joined weather.drought_monitor_weekly to geo.counties and fanned out (row_fanout warning on geo.counties); I discarded it and used the single-table query, so county figures here are not affected.
