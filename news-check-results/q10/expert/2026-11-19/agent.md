# Fact-check: Insurance Journal, "FEMA Won't Help Texas After July 2026 Floods" (Nov 9, 2026)

**Verdict: 1 Pinocchio.** Every checkable claim matches the original Texas Tribune article (Oct 21, 2026). The piece is a republication and is materially stale.

## Summary
- Magazine copy dated Nov 9 reprints the Oct 21 Tribune story (Insurance Journal news version 2026/10/22). It says Abbott/TDEM are reviewing the appeal process. Texas Tribune Oct 28 reports Nim Kidd formally appealed on Oct 27 for 20 of 30 counties, including Kerr and Uvalde, plus the hazard mitigation denial, and Trump pledged more aid. Misleading by omission.
- Verified (Tribune original): FEMA denial of individual assistance; $37M of $1.9B (~2%) Texas share, Kidd; Hamilton "must have the appropriate financial reserves" (NEMA conference, via Politico); Brim-Estelle quote (Rebuild field director, shortened); Community Foundation $1.3M, 947 households, 58 housing; Mahaleris quote (Oct 15 tax request, July 22 SNAP); Abbott September FEMA local-government announcement; July 2025 Kerr floods killed more than 100; FEMA's five review factors.
- Hamilton was Senate-confirmed Aug 7, 2026 (51-47), so the title is correct.
- Context caveat: the 2% is the state's share of an emergency-response budget, not of disaster recovery costs.
- Not checkable: none graded so; the Politico original of Hamilton's remarks not fetched. I did not independently access FEMA's denial letter.
- Limits: article fetched via summarizing tool, not verbatim; confidence medium-high.
