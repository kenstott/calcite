# Fact check: CNBC "Unions helped Democrats win big in 2026"

**Rating: 1 Pinocchio (bordering on Geppetto checkmark).** 19 of 31 claim groups verified (2 with caveats), 0 contradicted, 12 not checkable here.

## Summary
- Election results (OH, MI, NV, FL, Congress) all verified. El-Sayed primary win was 48.5-47.5; general ~52.5-45.5; Benson ~54-44 (partial); Moody 50.88-47.98.
- AFL-CIO figures match its release: 14.9M reached, 639 members elected, 83% very motivated (+10), seven-figure media.
- NEP union-household margins: 2020 D+16, 2022 D+15, 2024 D+8 (CNN/NEP 53-45; other tallies D+10 to D+13).
- Misleading element: "D+22, highest of four cycles" splices CNBC's own poll with NEP exit polls; poll not accessible. AFL-CIO cites exit-poll D+21 and 15% union share vs CNBC's 16%.
- Causal headline not isolated by data; wave-year context.
- Not checkable: CNBC Voter Poll numbers, Fain "largest in lifetime", UAW door/call counts, Shuler quotes verbatim, interviews.
- Would change rating: if CNBC's union-household definition differs from NEP, 2 Pinocchios.
