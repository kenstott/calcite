# Fact check: Fox Business, "Trump demands probe into 'fake' pre-election GDP data after Q3 growth revised up to 4.3%" (Nov 26, 2026)

**Rating of the piece: 0 Pinocchios (accurate). Trump's underlying claim: 4 Pinocchios (unsupported; BEA published schedule and routine revision).**

- Q3 2026 advance 3.4% (BEA, Oct 29): VERIFIED (bea.gov advance release). Confidence high.
- Second estimate 4.3% on Nov 25, +0.9 pt: VERIFIED (BEA GDP page). High.
- Drivers (investment, consumer, government up; exports down): VERIFIED on BEA page. The article's attribution of the revision to more complete source data: consistent with BEA practice; BEA revision page not reachable. Medium.
- Three estimates per quarter; final Dec 23: VERIFIED (BEA schedule). High.
- Midterms Nov 3: true. Trump Thursday (Thanksgiving) Truth Social post, "FAKE", "immediate investigation": corroborated by Washington Times, Bloomberg, HuffPost; Truth Social post itself not directly fetched. Medium.
- No evidence offered: consistent with all coverage. 
- Not checkable here: GOP losses magnitude; original post text.
- Misleading context: Trump says the numbers were not corrected before the election, but the advance estimate came Oct 29 on BEA's pre-announced schedule; the second estimate was always scheduled Nov 25. A 0.9-pt advance-to-second change is larger than the ~0.5 pt reported average but not unprecedented (figure from secondary coverage, unverified). Note Q3 2025 was also reported as 4.3%; do not confuse.
- Piece itself gives the "without evidence" caveat and BEA schedule, so little is misleading.
