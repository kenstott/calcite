# Fact-check: KFF Health News, "What's behind the spike in early Social Security claims?" (Paula Span, 2026-11-19)
Rating: 1 Pinocchio. Facts largely check out; causal story (DOGE turmoil) is Urban Institute's hypothesis presented with limited weight to alternatives (SSA "peak 65", Social Security Fairness Act, high earners).
Limits: WebFetch returned only a summary of the article, not verbatim text. SSA trustees page returned 403; trustees figures come from search snippets quoting it. Urban primary report not accessed.

- Claims Oct 2024-Apr 2025 +267,000 (13%): matches KFF; other outlets give 276k, 15% annualised, ~320k through May. Medium; secondary only.
- Oct 2025-Apr 2026 -235,000 (~10%), "almost exact reversal": only seen via KFF page. Not independently verified. Note net is +32k.
- OASI depletion late 2032 (Q4 2032, 3 months earlier than 2025): confirmed (Axios quoting 2026 Trustees). Up to 22% cut (78% payable): confirmed by Trustees text via search/CRR. High.
- 2026 taxable max $184,500: confirmed. High.
- Warren/Moreno preparing bipartisan legislation: June 23 2026 NYT op-ed confirmed; no bill introduced as of Oct 2026 per Newsweek. Consistent.
- 2021 Boston College study (fear headlines -> earlier claiming): exists (Quinby & Wettstein CRR WP 2021-10); "about a year earlier" size not confirmed. Medium.
- CRR early claiming at 62 falling 2019-2023: not checked directly. Wettstein "overwhelming" quote, SSA non-response: not checkable here.
- Misleading risk: causal attribution to DOGE is correlation/timing; reversal is also consistent with pull-forward or other drivers.
