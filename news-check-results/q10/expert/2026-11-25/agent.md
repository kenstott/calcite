# Fact-check: CIDRAP, hepatitis B birth-dose vaccination falling
**Rating: 0-1 Pinocchios (accurate; context caveat).**

**Answer:** Study figures match Truveta/NEJM Evidence (EVIDpha2600326): 80.0% (2020-22) to 65.6% (Jun 2026); 1,338,961 infants; 90.5% tested; 0.18% HBsAg+; untested 15.6% to 7.6%. Policy context confirmed (ACIP Dec 2025 8-3 vote; Jan 2026 schedule cut; judge's block 16 Mar 2026).
**Key values:** see above. Truveta says 80.0% is an average, article says median. Schedule count 17 vs 11 vaccines (ABC7) or 18 vs 11 diseases (NBC).
**Not checkable:** 75.4-76.8%, 360,000, 640/yr (NEJM 403, CIDRAP PDF 404; Truveta page lacks them). 96.9% consistent with Truveta's 93.9% for 2026.
**Misleading risk:** EHR sample, not national; decline began 2023, before the ACIP vote; 640 is modeled.
**Sources:** evidence.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/EVIDpha2600326; truveta.com/blog/research/newborn-hepatitis-b-vaccination-decline; hhs.gov ACIP press release; cidrap.umn.edu and pbs.org judge-ruling stories; bostonglobe.com 2026/03/16.
**Confidence:** medium-high verified items (secondary summary of primary); low on unchecked.
