Summary: accurate report, about 1 Pinocchio of context risk (rating 0 to 1)

The CIDRAP piece reports the study's numbers correctly. Birth-dose coverage fell from 80.0% (2020-22) to 65.6% (June 2026); 1,338,961 infants and 1,075,767 mothers; 90.5% tested; 0.18% HBsAg-positive; untested mothers 15.6% (2018) to 7.6% (2026). Policy context (ACIP vote Dec 2025, schedule cut Jan 2026, judge's block March 2026) is confirmed by CIDRAP, PBS, Boston Globe and HHS.

  • Not checkable here: NEJM Evidence returned 403 and the CIDRAP PDF 404, so 75.4-76.8% (other groups), 360,000 and 640 per year could not be read in the primary report. Truveta's page lacks them.
  • Minor: Truveta calls 80.0% an average, the article says median. The schedule cut is 17 vs 11 vaccines per some outlets, 18 vs 11 diseases per NBC.
  • Misleading risk: the data are Truveta EHR, not national births, and the authors say the decline began in 2023, before the ACIP vote, so the policy context should not be read as cause. The 640 figure is a modeled estimate.
  • Would change the rating: the primary PDF contradicting the unverified figures.
