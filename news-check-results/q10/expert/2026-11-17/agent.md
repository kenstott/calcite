# Fact-check: Iowa Farm Bureau, "November Crop Production Report: USDA Cuts Corn Yield, Markets Rally" (11/11/2026)

## Summary: 0 Pinocchios (borderline 1); every checkable number matches USDA, two precision flags
Source of truth: USDA NASS Crop Production, released 2026-11-10 (https://esmis.nal.usda.gov/sites/default/release-files/796144/crop1126.txt), compared with October (crop1026.txt). Futures: Brownfield close 2026-11-10.
Caveat: I could only read an automated summary of the article (full verbatim text refused), so wording-level flags are provisional. The piece has no attributed quotes.

Misleading context: "good for Iowa" is fair for yields, but Iowa harvested corn area fell (13.15M to 12.8M acres) and US corn is still the second-largest crop ever; the rally follows a prior low (Jan soy hit $11.84 the week before), so it is a bounce, not a reversal.
Confidence: high on numbers (primary source), medium on wording flags.
