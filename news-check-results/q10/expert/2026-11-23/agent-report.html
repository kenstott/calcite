Al Jazeera Anthropic IPO piece: 2 Pinocchios

Summary: the $81.3bn figure checks out, but the record claim is framed against SpaceX's pre-greenshoe $75bn

Anthropic's IPO raised $81.3bn (980M shares at $83, above the $70-78 range), plus a $26bn private placement (total $107.3bn), at about $1.5tn valuation, Nasdaq ticker ANT. All confirmed by multiple outlets. However SpaceX's June IPO rose to $85.7bn after underwriters exercised the greenshoe (SpaceX IR), so "biggest ever" holds only if Anthropic's own greenshoe is excluded or exceeds it. Also $81.3bn includes secondary sales; about $79.4bn goes to Anthropic.

  • Huang stepped down at Nvidia (Nov 12): confirmed, but reports describe an affair with a woman US officials suspect of spying; the article's "unproven claims linked to Chinese intelligence" is fair.
  • Nvidia down more than 30% in a month and Nasdaq down 7.6%: not confirmed exactly; sources show Nvidia 23-35% below Oct 6 peak and Nasdaq over 10% below its peak. Window-dependent.
  • Quotes from Dan Ives and Jay Ritter: not checkable here (article not fetched verbatim, no primary source found).
  • Caveat: the article text came via a summarizing fetch tool, so wording is unverified.
