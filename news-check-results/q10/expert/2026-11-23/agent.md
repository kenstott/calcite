Step 1: Fact-check of Al Jazeera "Anthropic raises $81.3bn in biggest IPO of all time"

**Answer**: Overall 2 Pinocchios. Core numbers are accurate; the record framing and market-move figures are weaker.

**Key Values**
- $81.3bn raise, 980M shares at $83 (range $70-78), ~$1.5tn valuation, Nasdaq "ANT", $26bn private placement (total $107.3bn): confirmed by NPR/KUOW, CTV, Axios. Of the $81.3bn, ~$79.4bn goes to Anthropic (rest is secondary sales).
- "Surpasses SpaceX $75bn": SpaceX base was $75bn but final was $85.7bn after greenshoe (SpaceX IR, June 15 2026), above Anthropic's $81.3bn. "Biggest ever" holds only base-vs-base: misleading.
- Huang stepped down at Nvidia (Nov 12): confirmed; claims tied to alleged Chinese-spy associate, Beijing denies.
- Nvidia down >30% in a month, Nasdaq down 7.6%: not exactly confirmed; sources show Nvidia 23-35% below Oct 6 peak, Nasdaq >10% below peak. Window-dependent.
- Quotes (Dan Ives, Jay Ritter): not checkable here; article text obtained only via a summarizing fetch, no verbatim.
