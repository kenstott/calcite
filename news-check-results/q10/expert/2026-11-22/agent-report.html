Summary of findings: numbers match CDC, state tally is the weak point

The Nov 19, 2026 article's national figures match CDC FluView week 44 (ending Nov 7): 11.3% lab positivity, 3.2% ILI, 2.6M illnesses, 36,000 hospitalizations, 2,100 deaths, 5 pediatric deaths. Rating: 1 Pinocchio (minor imprecision).

  • CDC's table shows 1 very high plus 6 high jurisdictions (7); the article names Louisiana plus five high (6 total) and headlines "6 states". Jurisdictions may include non-states; state names were not verifiable here because the map is not in the page text.
  • The ">3% of doctor visits for flu" is ILI, which captures any respiratory pathogen, not confirmed flu.
  • Now stale: CDC week 45 (Nov 14) shows 3.8% ILI, 2 very high + 9 high jurisdictions, 3.7M illnesses, 50,000 hospitalizations, 2,800 deaths, 7 pediatric deaths. Cumulative hospitalization rate 12.0 per 100,000 is the highest for week 45 since 2010-11.
  • Not checkable here: Durbin's vaccine quote verbatim from a primary transcript (a trade-press report carries a related quote).
