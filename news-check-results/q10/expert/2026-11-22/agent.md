**Answer:** National figures match CDC FluView wk 44 (ending Nov 7, 2026): 11.3% positive, 3.2% ILI, 2.6M illnesses/36K hosp/2,100 deaths, 5 pediatric deaths. Durbin's claim verified (Oct 29 briefing: highest mid-October rate since 2010-11).
