Barge-fleet article: accurate, with 1 Pinocchio for framing

Summary of findings

The piece is accurate on every figure I could check; I rate it 1 Pinocchio (0-4 scale) because it relays a trade-group projection as a headline outcome and describes the WRDA as merely proposed. Fleet of 19,668 barges (2026), 17.6-year average age, 16,240 projected for 2035 (-17.4%) all match CoBank's Nov 10, 2026 report.

  • The 2035 figure is a CoBank projection; "could strain" in the headline is appropriately hedged, but the farm-margin impact is not quantified by the piece or by CoBank as far as I saw.
  • WRDA 2026 (H.R. 9497) passed the House 415-9 on Sept 16, so "proposed" is stale; the $6B+ is an authorization, not appropriation.
  • Could not verify: USDA 9% against the baseline table, and the AgWeb/World Grain pages (403). The article's quote "structural tightening" was confirmed only through Maritime Executive.
  • Minor source variation: Farm Progress gives 19,660 barges and RFD-TV about 19,500; rounding.
  • Conclusion would change if CoBank's inputs (Current Data LLC counts, retirement rate 500-1,000) proved wrong; retirements range is wide.
