Mostly accurate numbers, selective framing: 2 Pinocchios

The checkable claims hold: EIA weekly retail gasoline fell to $3.564 (week of 11/23/26), the lowest since the week of 3/9/26 (3.459), i.e. about eight months; CNN reported a 50.5-cent three-week drop, the largest in dollars since 2008. But the release omits that prices are about $0.53 (18%) above a year ago ($3.032), 20% above the pre-war $2.982, and that Thanksgiving 2026 is on track to be the costliest on record (AAA prior record $3.58 in 2022; GasBuddy forecast $3.62). The drop reverses a spike from the Iran war (began late Feb 2026; above $4 April to early November).

  • Bloomberg item and individual local figures (Birmingham 11.8c, Missouri 19.3c, Wyoming ~18c, Albuquerque 18.6%, Dallas/OKC under $3, Philadelphia under $4, Lynchburg $3.21, Arizona 1.7M travelers): not individually verified; could not fetch the primary pages.
  • The "biggest since 2008" holds in cents, but CNN says percentage-wise it is the biggest since 2020.
  • "As Trump predicted": Trump made several predictions (e.g. $3.70, under $3 by year end/early 2027); only the direction is borne out; causal credit to policy is unverifiable, and oil fell on ceasefire/deal news.

Caveat: I read the release via a summarizing fetch tool, not verbatim, so quote-level checks are limited.
