Misleading-by-omission flags

  • CDC's preliminary severity assessment is low for all ages; the piece omits it.
  • The state map uses ILINet (any fever-plus-cough illness), not lab-confirmed flu.
  • Durbin's statement concerns the early-season mid-October week. The warehouse shows the weekly rate peaking at 2.7 (Nov 7) and 2.3 for Nov 14; at the Nov 5-7 week, 2022-23 (2.5) was nearly as high.
