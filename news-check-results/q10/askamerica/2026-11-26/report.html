The White House release "Gas Prices Plunge Ahead of Thanksgiving" (Nov. 24, 2026): claims about the national average, the three-week drop and Trump's prediction are very biased and mostly accurate.

EIA weekly retail regular gasoline (energy.eia_retail_gasoline_prices, through 2026-11-23); AAA/GasBuddy via CNN, Bloomberg and local outlets

Article under reviewhttps://www.whitehouse.gov/releases/2026/11/gas-prices-plunge-ahead-of-thanksgiving-just-as-president-trump-predicted/

Summary

Every checkable number holds: the national average is at an eight-month low and the three-week drop is the largest since 2008. The release is misleading by omission. It frames a war-driven spike unwinding as a Trump-delivered win ('real relief'). It leaves out that gas is still about 19% above last year and that Thanksgiving could still be the priciest on record. Trump's own prediction was only that oil prices would fall 'right after the election'.

Misleading framing

  • Omitted: still above the year-ago level (CNN $3.72 vs $3.09); Thanksgiving could still be the costliest on record. Local stories show prices 40-83 cents above last year.
  • Omitted: CNN and Bloomberg attribute the drop to seasonal demand, winter blend, Hormuz crude flows, no war re-escalation and refinery recovery.
  • The cited prediction was a Sept. 9 remark about oil prices after the election, not pump prices.
  • Minor: Cincinnati 'overnight' is a 6-cent one-day drop; Lynchburg $3.21 is the lowest station price, not the city average (AAA metro $3.47).
