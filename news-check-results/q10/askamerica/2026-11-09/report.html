The TheGrio article's claims about Birmingham homicide totals, the mayor's statements and violence-intervention funding are partly false.

Checked 2026-11-09 against WVTM13, AL.com/NNPA, ABC 33/40 and the City of Birmingham ARPA report. AskAmerica catalog has no Birmingham city-level homicide table.

Article under reviewhttps://thegrio.com/2026/10/15/birmingham-homicides-mayor-randall-woodfin-violence-prevention/

Summary

Rating: 2 Pinocchios. Birmingham's 2026 homicide decline is real (34 through Q3 vs 61 and 129 at the same point in 2025 and 2024). But TheGrio presents those year-to-date counts as full-year totals (63 for 2025, 130 for 2024, attributed to a nonexistent 'Birmingham state legislature'); AL.com reports 88 and 152. The causal credit to violence-intervention programs is also stronger than the sources support: police credit enforcement teams and ATF partnerships, and domestic violence is up 18.3%.

Misleading framing

The article omits that violent crime is down only 8.6%, domestic violence is up 18.3% (WVTM13), and ABC 33/40 documented a June cluster of shootings and transparency complaints. The 2025 national murder decline is also a confound. The 2025 ARPA report is internally inconsistent on funding totals ($5.51M, $5.41M, $2M). No Birmingham-level homicide table exists in AskAmerica (term 'birmingham' unmatched), so independent sources were used. The mayor's Facebook post was not fetched.
