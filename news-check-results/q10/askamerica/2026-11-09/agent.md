# The TheGrio article's claims about Birmingham homicide totals, the mayor's statements and violence-intervention funding are partly false.

Article: https://thegrio.com/2026/10/15/birmingham-homicides-mayor-randall-woodfin-violence-prevention/
Rating: 2 Pinocchios. These scores describe this article only.

## Summary
Birmingham's 2026 homicide decline is real: 34 through Q3, versus 61 at the same point in 2025 and 129 in 2024 (police, via WVTM13). TheGrio presents those year-to-date counts as full-year totals. It says 2025 had 63 homicides and 2024 had 130, "according to the Birmingham state legislature", which does not exist. AL.com reports 88 for 2025 and 152 for 2024.

## Claims
- 2025 = 63 vs 2024 = 130 (author, central): mostly false. These are YTD counts; full-year figures are 88 and 152 (AL.com via NNPA).
- 34 homicides, 31 closed (author): true (WVTM13: 34, 91% solved).
- Down 46% vs last year, 74% vs 2024 (Woodfin): mostly true as a same-date comparison (police counts give -44% and -74%).
- Fewest homicides in 75 years (Woodfin): not checkable here. 2015 was the last sub-100 year before 2025 (92), so it is plausible, but no source documents the 1951 benchmark.
- Pickett quote: true (WVTM13).
- $5,512,378 for CVI: mostly true. The city ARPA report is internally inconsistent: it gives $5,512,378 once, $5,412,379 twice, and $2M elsewhere.
- One Hood, GVRS and street-outreach program descriptions: true per the ARPA report. The 10% annual-reduction goal was not found in a fetched primary page.
- Black mayors "drove down" violence nationally (author, causal): not checkable here. The 2025 national murder decline is a confound.

## Misleading framing
The article omits that violent crime is down only 8.6%, domestic violence is up 18.3%, and police credit enforcement teams, ATF partnerships and drones (WVTM13). It also omits ABC 33/40's report of a May to June cluster of shootings with transparency complaints. Causal credit to violence intervention is stronger than the sources support.
