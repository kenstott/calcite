The CIDRAP article's claims about newborn hepatitis B vaccination rates, the ACIP vote and the Truveta study are somewhat biased and mostly accurate.

Article under reviewhttps://www.cidrap.umn.edu/public-health-alerts/hepatitis-b-vaccination-rates-us-newborns-falling-public-health-alerts-report

Summary

Rating: 1 Pinocchio. Nearly every number matches the NEJM Evidence paper (read in full). Flaws: HBsAg called an antibody (it is an antigen); the lede implies the decline followed the ACIP downgrade though the paper says it began in mid-2023 and predated the vote; a single EHR sample (Truveta) is presented as the national rate; 'median' where the paper says 'mean'. Warehouse births (about 3.6M per year) confirm the study's denominator. The NEJM Evidence editorial and index pages returned HTTP 403; the Vaccine Integrity Project report (Dec 2, 2025) loaded and only supports that it exists and is CIDRAP-led.
