## Not checkable here
- "Largest since" / "lowest since" / "record" claims (corn since June 23, 2026; soy since Aug 11, 2026; live cattle since Apr 30, 2024; hogs largest short since June 2006): would need the full CFTC history; not checked. The hog "record" framing is the article's own series calculation.
- Weekly price moves, WASDE-day timing, Nov 16 mixed session.
- Dec-Mar corn carry (10 1/4 to 11 1/4 cents), Iowa soybean basis 71 under January, hog index-to-December gap.
- Commercial net long of 9,832 in SRW wheat (not looked up).
- Calendar: Nov 18 EIA ethanol, Trump-Xi meeting, Nov 19 Export Sales/Slaughter, Nov 20 Cattle on Feed and COT, Nov 30 first notice day and Nogales reopening: not verified.

## Misleading-context flags
- Headline "Funds Sold Into the WASDE Rally" is partly interpretive: positions are a Tuesday snapshot and the article itself notes cattle gains came after it. Fine, disclosed.
- "Record Short" for hogs depends on the article's own series; the headline presents it as fact. Futures-only numbers (short 139,282 vs long 77,046, net -62,236) would give a different magnitude, so the "record" is specific to the combined series.
- Positioning is a week old by publication; the article is explicitly educational, not a trading recommendation.

## Rating
1 Pinocchio: all verifiable figures match the primary source; residual uncertainty comes from unchecked historical-rank and farm-level claims.
