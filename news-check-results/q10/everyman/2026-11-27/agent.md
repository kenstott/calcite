# Fact-check: Fox Business, "Trump demands probe of pre-election GDP data revision" (Bradford Betz, Nov 26, 2026)

Method: WebFetch of the article (returned only a summary, not verbatim text, so quote wording is as relayed), BEA news release pages, web search. No charts produced.

## Claims
| # | Claim | Verdict | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | BEA revised Q3 2026 GDP up to 4.3% | Verified | BEA second estimate, Nov 25, 2026: "increased at an annual rate of 4.3 percent" |
| 2 | Advance estimate was 3.4%, published Oct 29 | Verified | BEA advance release 3rd qtr 2026, Oct 29 (3.4%) |
| 3 | Revision was 0.9 point | Verified | BEA: "revised up 0.9 percentage point from the advance estimate" |
| 4 | Advance estimate came 5 days before the Nov 3 midterms | Verified | Oct 29 to Nov 3 is 5 days; midterms are Nov 3, 2026 |

| 7 | Trump demanded an immediate investigation, called earlier figures "FAKE", said revision came "yesterday", and quoted "This is not a small error, this is a massive, and purposeful, error." | Not checkable here | Could not locate the Truth Social post; searches returned only older, unrelated episodes. Fox is the only source. "Yesterday" fits Nov 25 |
| 8 | Trump's claim of deliberate understatement is "without evidence" | Supported | BEA documents a routine, source-data-driven revision; no evidence of intent found |

## Pinocchio rating
Fox's reporting: 0 Pinocchios (accurate and sourced on every checkable fact). The article reports Trump's claim and labels it unevidenced.

Trump's underlying claim (deliberate, purposeful error): in the Fact Checker's scale, 4 Pinocchios if scored on its own. BEA attributes the change to late source data, and the revision raised the figure, so it is not an obvious fit for an election-timing motive.

## Materially misleading / context flags
- Not stated in the summary I could access: how large a 0.9-point advance-to-second revision is relative to typical BEA revisions. I did not verify historical revision sizes, so I make no claim.
- The 4.3% figure is itself still subject to the Dec 23 third estimate.
- Caveat: the Trump post and exact article wording rest on a paraphrase of the page, not a verbatim read.
