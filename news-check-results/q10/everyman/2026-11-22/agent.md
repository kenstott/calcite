| Claim | Verdict | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Week ending Nov 7 | Verified | CDC Week 44 |

## Rating: 1 Pinocchio (minor issues)
Nearly all numbers match CDC.

## Misleading or missing context
- State count: CDC reports 7 jurisdictions at high or very high; the piece frames it as 6 (and the headline says 6 "high").
- "At least" dropped from burden estimates; figures are preliminary and CDC says severity may change.
- Counts are small versus past seasons; deaths and hospitalizations are early-season, and CDC says only that activity is "higher than typically observed at this time of year."
- Data is nearly two weeks old (Week 44) at publication; Week 45 was due Nov 20.
- "High" refers to outpatient ILI activity levels, not severity.
