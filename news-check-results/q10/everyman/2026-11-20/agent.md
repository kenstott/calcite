## Overall grade: 1 Pinocchio (largely accurate)
Nearly every number traces to the CoBank report. Weaknesses are small.

## Context and misleading-risk flags
- The headline "could strain" is forecast-based. A 17% fleet cut is not a 17% capacity cut: barge capacity also depends on towing, lock performance and utilization, none of which the figures address.
- The article is an aggregator of secondary coverage of one CoBank report. All numbers share one source.
- WRDA $6B is an authorization, not money appropriated; the Senate bill is a different size and scope. A reader could take $6B as committed funding.
- The CoBank report shows Cairo-Memphis barge rates at $20.04/ton vs. an $18.37 three-year average, about 9% above average. That is a modest premium and arguably relevant to "strain," so the margin impact is not yet large.
- Retirement range 500-1,000 per year against 410 built implies a net loss of 90-590 a year, while the 17% projection implies about 380 a year. The numbers are consistent only toward the middle of that range.
