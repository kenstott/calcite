# Fact-check: CIDRAP, "Hepatitis B vaccination rates in US newborns falling" (Public Health Alerts)

Limits: I read the CIDRAP piece only through WebFetch's summarizer, not verbatim. The linked source PDF was unreadable (raw binary). The NEJM Evidence paper was not retrievable. So the study's own numbers could not be checked against the primary paper.

## Claims and verdicts

| # | Claim | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Birth-dose coverage fell from a median 80.0% (2020-22) to 65.6% in June 2026 | Not checkable here. The primary paper was unavailable. Independent JAMA data (via NEJM clinician summary and CIDRAP) show a decline from a peak of about 84% (Feb 2023) to about 73% (Aug 2025). That is a different dataset and period, but the direction is consistent and a further fall to 65.6% is plausible. |
| 2 | Truveta data: 1.3M infants, 1.1M mothers, 2018-June 2026; 90.5% of pregnancies had a recorded HBV test | Not checkable here |
| 3 | Untested mothers fell from 15.6% (2018) to 7.6% (2026) | Not checkable here. Note this is an improvement in screening, the opposite direction from the headline. |
| 4 | About 360,000 infants a year born to mothers without a recorded test; over 640 a year to untested HBV-infected mothers (0.18% HBsAg positivity) | Arithmetic is internally consistent (360,000 x 0.18% = 648). The inputs could not be verified. These are model estimates, not observed cases. |
| 5 | Coverage by maternal status: 96.9% HBV-positive; 75.9% negative; 76.8% unknown; 75.4% untested; HBV-positive 93.9% in Jan-Jun 2026 | Not checkable here |
| 6 | ACIP dropped the universal birth-dose recommendation in late 2025 | Supported. CIDRAP's own report: 8-3 vote, birth dose only for infants of HBsAg-positive mothers, others advised to delay to at least 2 months. The CDC moved to "individual-based decision-making" in Dec 2025. |
| 7 | HHS January 2026 schedule overhaul cut routine vaccines from 17 to 11 | Supported by CIDRAP's linked report |
| 8 | A federal judge blocked the changes in March | Mostly supported. CIDRAP reports Judge Brian Murphy (Boston) issued a preliminary injunction against the schedule changes and the ACIP reconstitution. I could not confirm the month from the page. |
| 9 | Birth dose is a key safety net against perinatal HBV | Consistent with the literature. The birth dose is associated with a roughly 99% drop in perinatal cases. |

No attributed quotes were found beyond paraphrase of the study authors.

## Rating: 1 Pinocchio (mostly accurate; the one checkable item set is sound, but the central numbers are unverified)

## Potentially misleading
- The headline implies a broad vaccination collapse. The decline is real in the data cited, but the highest-risk group (HBV-positive mothers) still shows 93.9-96.9% coverage.
- The 640-infants figure is a modeled upper-bound risk, not confirmed infections.
- Screening of mothers improved, which is not a headline finding.
- The data come from one electronic-health-record network (Truveta), which may not represent the whole US.
- Coverage is described as falling "after" the ACIP change. That timing is correlational. The earlier decline (2023 to 2025) began before the ACIP vote.
