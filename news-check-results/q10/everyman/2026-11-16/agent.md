## Rating: 1 Pinocchio (mostly accurate, minor gaps)
The headline numbers match CBO and Treasury, and the arithmetic holds together.

## Misleading or missing context
- The summary I read shows receipts falling from "tax cuts" and customs duties falling, but no explanation of the Supreme Court IEEPA ruling and tariff refunds. That ruling accounts for much of the customs drop and is not tax policy. Receipts also came in $193bn below CBO's February baseline, mostly from smaller-than-expected customs duties. The summary presents the $1,853bn baseline next to the actual $1,992bn without saying the miss was about $139bn.
- The 6.2% versus 5.8% comparison mixes actual and projected GDP bases. This could be a minor apples-to-oranges comparison.
- Calendar timing shifts (veterans benefits payments moved from Sept 1 into August) affect monthly but not full-year totals.
