# Fact-check: Al Jazeera, "Anthropic raises $81.3bn in biggest IPO of all time" (2026-11-19)

Method: WebFetch of the article (returned a summary, not verbatim text), plus two web searches. I did not open primary filings (SEC S-1/424B), so every verdict rests on secondary reporting.

## Claims
| Claim | Verdict | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| IPO raised about $81.3bn | Supported (secondary) | CTV, Axios, NPR/KUOW, SCMP and others report $81.3-81.35bn |
| Largest IPO ever, ahead of SpaceX's $75bn in June | Mostly supported | Multiple outlets agree. One noted SpaceX's final total rose after the underwriters' option was exercised, so "largest" holds for the initial offering. |
| 980m shares at $83, above planned $70-78 range | Supported | Reports say 980m at $83, against a plan of 825m at $70-78 |
| "Company and its seven co-founders sold" shares | Partly unverified | Reports say about 23m shares came from "certain stockholders", and about $79.4bn went to Anthropic. I found nothing confirming the co-founders sold. |
| Extra $26bn private placement, $107.3bn total | Supported | Filings as reported |
| Valuation about $1.5tn | Supported, with a caveat | The figure is fully diluted. |
| Trades on Nasdaq Thursday as "ANT" | Supported | Reports say it opened at $111.50, up 34% |
| Jensen Huang stepped down after unproven espionage claims | Supported | Reported around Nov 11-12. Colette Kress is interim CEO. The espionage link is reported as unproven. |
| Nvidia down more than 30% | Not confirmed | I found drops of 12% to 19% (more than $855bn of market cap). A 30% drop may be a longer window. |
| Nasdaq Composite down 7.6% over the past month | Not confirmed | I found no source |
| Asian markets mixed | Not checked | |
| Jay Ritter (University of Florida) comment on profitability | Not checkable here | Quote attribution not located |
| Dan Ives (described as "Yorkville Ives") calls the debut a near-term win | Not checkable here | The firm is normally Wedbush. The "Yorkville Ives" label looks wrong, or came from the summary tool. |

## Overall rating
1 Pinocchio (mostly accurate). The core facts check out. The Nasdaq and Nvidia figures, the co-founder detail and the analyst attributions are unconfirmed.

## Misleading or missing context
- "Biggest ever" depends on the initial offering. The $107.3bn headline includes a private placement, which is not an IPO.
- Some of the $81.3bn went to selling shareholders, not to Anthropic.
- The $1.5tn valuation is fully diluted.
- The fetch tool returned a paraphrase, so the quote wording was not verified.
