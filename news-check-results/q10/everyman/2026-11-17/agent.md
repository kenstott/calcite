## Rating: 1 Pinocchio (mostly accurate) at most; no false claims found.

## Context the piece may underplay
- The headline says "Markets Rally", but the one-day rise followed earlier sharp declines. Farm Futures and farmdoc note unusually large October and November yield revisions.
- The yield cut was below the trade estimate of 180.3, which explains the rally (AgWeb called it a surprise).
- USDA's projected season-average price is $4.60, above the $4.49 December futures settle quoted. This is not a contradiction, but the two should not be confused.
- Iowa is unchanged at a record while the national yield falls. Readers could wrongly infer a broad cut.
