# Fact-check: "FEMA Won't Help Texas After July 2026 Floods" (Insurance Journal, Nov 9, 2026)

Piece is a Texas Tribune article (Emily Foxhall), originally published Oct 21, 2026, reprinted in Insurance Journal's Nov 9 magazine feature. Limits: I fetched the article through a summarizing tool, so quotes are as relayed, and I checked claims by web search only (no primary documents such as the FEMA letter, hearing video or council report).

## Claims and verdicts
| Claim | Verdict | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| FEMA denied Texas's individual assistance (IA) request for the July 2026 floods (Uvalde, Kerr) | Supported | FEMA letter dated Sept 28 (CBS Austin, Fox 7, News 4 San Antonio, Texas Tribune) |
| Abbott/TDEM reviewing the decision and appeal process | Outdated | TDEM (Kidd) formally appealed about Oct 28 for 20 of 30 counties (News 4 San Antonio). Trump promised more aid (KSAT/Click2Houston, Oct 28). The Nov 9 reprint does not say so. |
| Kidd and Abbott served on the FEMA Review Council; report released in May says states should lead | Supported | NBC DFW, States Newsroom, Texas Tribune |
| Kidd told legislators the state pays $37M of $1.9B 2027 emergency response budget (2%) | Partly checked | Reported by States Newsroom. I found no hearing transcript or video. Not to be confused with TDEM's $37.7M spent on this disaster. |
| Kerr was flooded in July 2025 (100+ dead) and again in 2026 | Supported, not re-verified in detail | Widely reported |
| Community Foundation of the Texas Hill Country: about $1.3M to 947 households, 58 housing | Not checkable here | Foundation figures not located |
| Abbott Oct 15 tax-deadline request; July 22 SNAP replacement window | Not checkable here | No primary source found |
| Hamilton quote on state reserves (via Politico) | Not checkable here | Not located |
| Brim-Estelle and Dickson quotes | Not checkable here | Not located; relayed from the Tribune |
| Sept announcement of FEMA help for local governments (public assistance) | Plausible | FEMA public assistance was approved for some; 8 counties were denied additional public assistance |

## Rating: 1 Pinocchio (mostly accurate, with a timeliness problem)
Core facts hold up. No contradicted claim found.

## Materially misleading
- Staleness: the "last week", "last month" and "still reviewing" framing dates from Oct 21. By the Nov 9 publication date the state had appealed and Trump had pledged more aid. A reader would think no appeal was filed.
- Headline "FEMA Won't Help Texas" overstates. Public assistance continues for some local governments, and the IA decision is under appeal.
