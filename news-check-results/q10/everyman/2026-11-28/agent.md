# Fact-check: Medical Daily, Black-White mortality gap 1993-2023

Verdict: 0 Pinocchios (accurate). Caveat: WebFetch gave me a summary of the article, not its verbatim text, so I checked the claims as summarized. Medical Daily's exact wording was not read.

None were left as not checkable. The one gap is that I could not open the journal full text or the PubMed page (cookie wall). I relied on the abstract text quoted in search results and the ACS press release.

## Misleading-risk flags (facts right, framing could mislead)

1. "Excess deaths fell 56%/68%" is a relative gap measure. It does not mean Black mortality fell by that share. The piece says so correctly, but headline readers may misread it.
2. The gap is still large. In 2023 Black men died at a mean age of 62.3 years versus 72.6 for white men, and Black women at 69.7 versus 78.0. The article does say the gap is large.
3. The 1993 baseline is a high point for the gap. The study itself notes that other comparison periods could change cause-specific results.
4. The homicide, overdose and maternal-death disparities are persisting or worsening. The headline's "narrowed sharply" does not cover them, though the piece does include them.

Overall: 0 Pinocchios. Every checkable claim matches the study and the ACS release.
