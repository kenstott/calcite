# Is it true? Census Bureau announced Sept 15, 2026 that real median household income was $87,460 in 2025

**Verdict: True.**

The U.S. Census Bureau published its annual "Income, Poverty and Health Insurance Coverage in the United States" report on September 15, 2026, reporting that real median household income was $87,460 in 2025 — the highest on record dating back to 1967. This was a 2.6% increase from the 2024 estimate of $85,210. The same release also reported the official poverty rate fell 0.5 percentage points to 10.2% in 2025.

The figures come from the Current Population Survey Annual Social and Economic Supplement (CPS ASEC), fielded in February, March, and April 2026, which provides the Bureau's annual national income and poverty estimates.

Additional detail found in coverage of the release: income gains were uneven across groups (e.g., +3.0% for White households, +2.9% for non-Hispanic White households, +4.8% for Black households), and some outlets noted lower earners lost ground even as the median hit a record.
