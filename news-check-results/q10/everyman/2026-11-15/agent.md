## Overall rating

**1 Pinocchio (mostly accurate), provisional.**

Every checkable factual claim (election outcomes) held up. The rating is provisional because most numeric claims are union self-reports or CNBC's own poll, and I could not verify them.

## Potentially misleading framing

- The headline credits unions with helping Democrats win. Much of the 2026 result was a broad Democratic wave across many demographic groups and states, including states Trump won easily in 2024. Union households backing Democrats is correlation, not proof that union mobilization drove the outcomes.
- The AFL-CIO's mobilization figures (15 million voters engaged, 600+ members elected) are self-reported and should not be read as measured impact.
- The union-household margin is surveyed data with sampling error. A move from 8 to 22 points may not be a precise comparison if the 2024 and 2026 polls used different methods.
