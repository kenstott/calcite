# Fact-check: WFTV data center electricity piece - 1 Pinocchio

**Answer:** Mostly accurate (about 26 of 31 claims verified). Rating: 1 Pinocchio. Key problems: Dallas Fed 2-6% is the superseded March 2026 draft (Oct revision: 6.9% nationally by 2025, 15.0% N. Virginia, 12.3% by 2028); Georgia $20/month staff warning omitted its condition; MISO annual vs summer prices unlabeled; Henry Hub record date disputed (NGI Jan 26 $30.565; EIA/AGA $30.72 Jan 23).

**Not confirmed:** Oregon 15% of IOU load; North Dakota +40%/-3 c/kWh; PJM gas 44%; Data Center Coalition criticism.

**Key Values:** PJM 28/29 $325/MW-day, 6,831 MW short, 525 MW new; IMM $29.4B of $63.6B (46%); ERCOT 474 GW; PGE +29.7%/-1.3%/-2.1%; LBNL 4.4% (2023), 6.7-12% (2028); Grid Strategies 24 to 166 GW.

**Sources:** see agent-report.html (PJM, Monitoring Analytics, Dallas Fed WP 2606 orig/r1, MISO, EIA, DOE, Georgia PSC/Power, OPB, LBNL, NERC, Grid Strategies).

**Confidence:** high on auction/tariff/agency numbers; medium on secondary; low on the unconfirmed four.
