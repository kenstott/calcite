Materially misleading context
- The piece is sponsored retail-supplier content, so its framing leans toward price-risk urgency.
- The 46% attribution is a market-monitor model that others contest. The Dallas Fed 2-6% is a modeled estimate, not an observed effect.
- The 90 GW utility forecast likely double-counts projects. The article partly says so, noting the ~65 GW analyst view.
- The Georgia $20/month caution omits a later stipulation under which Georgia Power says the increase will not materialize.
- The $950M Georgia savings figure is a company projection stated without a caveat.
- The "record price" error for the PJM 2028/29 auction overstates the price trend.
