What NIFC's own live page shows (fetched directly, per this corpus's research-first practice)

Per this warehouse's own guidance to fetch a primary source directly rather than settle for a caveated gap, NIFC's live 'Wildfires and Acres' page (nifc.gov/fire-information/statistics/wildfires) was fetched.
