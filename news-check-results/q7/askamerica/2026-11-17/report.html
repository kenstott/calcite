Flags

  • Customs framing. 'Declined since May' is loose: net customs were about $22B a month in March-April, about zero in May, negative in June (-$25.6B) and July (-$8.5B) as refunds exceeded collections, then recovered to $19.2B in October. The October drop is a comparison to a strong October 2025, not a continuing slide, and the piece does not quantify refunds versus replacement tariffs. Treasury does not split IEEPA refunds from other customs refunds. The Feb 20, 2026 Supreme Court ruling (6-3) and May refund onset are confirmed by outside sources.
  • Causal explanations (enrollment, per-enrollee costs, OBBBA reforms, military personnel/R&D) are the author's attributions; the data confirm direction and size, not cause.
  • Context omitted: CBO had estimated $409B; Treasury confirmed $404B. One month is a noisy base; a 42% jump in a single month is not a trend.
  • 'The National Debt Is Unsustainable' is opinion; projections are not graded.
