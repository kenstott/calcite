The Fox Business article's claims about Edmunds new-vehicle prices, EV declines and auto-loan rates are accurate.

Rating: 0 Pinocchios.

Checked: Fox Business piece (fetched; all linked pages fetched). Edmunds' own page returned 403, so Edmunds figures were checked against a syndicated copy (Yahoo) and Edmunds Q3 coverage (KPRC).

Findings
- $50,087 October average transaction price, up about $500 m/m: matches the Edmunds copy. True.
- MSRP $52,493 less $2,406 discount = $50,087: arithmetic reconciles. True.
- Segment prices (hybrid $44,855, PHEV $66,355, ICE $50,751, EV $56,809): match the copy. True.
- EV -11.8%, PHEV -5%, ICE +2%, hybrid +2.8%, overall +1.8% y/y: match the copy. Mostly true; Edmunds' Oct 2025 ATP was reported at $49,105, which implies +2.0%, so the base may have been revised.
- LA APR 7.3% and about $800 payment: match the copy. True, but local; Edmunds' national Q3 2026 APR was 7.0%, unchanged from a year earlier.
- Drury "squeezed from both sides": attributed to a FOX interview and not independently verifiable. Edmunds Q3 supports the substance: record $787 payment and 25.5% of loans at 84 months or longer.
- "First time ever" above $50,000: true for Edmunds' series only. Kelley Blue Book reported a record $50,614 in Dec 2025 and $50,562 in Sept 2026.

Warehouse check: no Edmunds ATP or APR table exists (catalog search: "edmunds" unmatched). BLS CPI new vehicles (econ.new_vehicles_cpi, CUUR0000SETA01) rose only +0.74% y/y in October 2026 (180.039 vs 178.723). That is a quality-adjusted index, so the gap with Edmunds' ATP is not a contradiction, but it shows milder price growth than the headline suggests.

Misleading-framing flags (not errors of fact)
- "Record" is nominal and specific to one vendor's series.
- "Higher interest rates" rests on an LA-only figure; the national APR was flat y/y.
