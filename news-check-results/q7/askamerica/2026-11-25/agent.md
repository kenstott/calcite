## Materially misleading framing

- The Oregon cost headline is the board's most optimistic scenario, which assumes 3% administrative cost. Milliman's best estimate puts the plan only about $0.6B below status quo. The article omits that.
- The "bargain" comparison sets a federal ten-year add-on (2027-2036) against all-payer national spending (2025-2034). The article does note that the national total includes military, IHS and elective services.
- There is a typo: "Single-player ballot measures."

The warehouse covered only the national spending figures (health.nhe_summary). Everything about Oregon, the CEA and the ballot measures was graded from primary documents or searches, not warehouse data.
