Method and sourcing

search_catalog found no HIPAA or OCR breach table in the warehouse, so claims were checked against independent sources. The HHS OCR portal itself did not render (JavaScript), so totals were recomputed from the Paubox-linked sheet (a derivative copy of HHS data), prior Paubox reports, and HIPAA Journal. The iRhythm figure rests on search summaries because SecurityWeek returned 403. The article's other links (Paubox email guide and inbound-security product page) were fetched; they are promotional and make no claim tested here.

Materially misleading context (not counted against the factual rating)

  • Two breaches drive everything. iRhythm (8,179,772) is 80.9% of the total; with COPC the pair is 94%. Without iRhythm, network server people affected would be about 1.93M, well below October 2024's 5.13M. The article names both firms but never says the 'highest in five years' result is a single-incident story.
  • Partial-month data. All 34 submission dates fall 10/01-10/16/2026, yet the data is compared with full-month prior years and labeled 'October 2026'.
  • Date discrepancy. The sheet dates iRhythm at 10/03/2026; one search summary says the portal listed it Oct 29. Unresolved.
  • Reporting month is not breach month. iRhythm's intrusion was June 2026 and COPC's August 2026; counts reflect HHS posting dates, not when attacks occurred.
  • Loose terms. 'Most common' is used for rankings by people affected; categories overlap (multi-location breaches counted in each), and 'attack vector' is really breach location.
  • Commercial context. The publisher sells inbound email security and closes with a pitch, while the data shows email is a minor vector this month (4 incidents, 7,649 people).
