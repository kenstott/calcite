The OPB/NPR article's claims about the 2026 House vote margin, flipped seats and majority size are balanced and accurate; 3 claims could not be confirmed.

Article: https://www.opb.org/article/2026/11/19/democrats-house-win-was-the-largest-in-50-years/ (NPR, Nov 19, 2026). It loaded fine.

Claims and grades:
1. Central: "biggest win for either party in 50 years, since after Watergate" is mostly true. The article's 56% to 42% is a 14-point margin. The warehouse (officials.house_election_results, contested races) gives 1976 as 56.1 to 41.9, a 14.3-point margin. Brookings Table 2-2 gives 56.2 to 42.1 (14.1), and 16.6 for 1974. So 1976 is equal to or slightly above 2026 within rounding. A Scripps/WFTV story says "since 1976", which fits. The article's comparison list omits 1974 and 1976.
2. 56% to 42% popular vote is unsupported. The warehouse has no 2026 rows. AP, CBS and the NPR state pages carry no national vote shares.
3. 260 seats projected, "most since the 102nd Congress", is true. House Clerk: 102nd had 267 Democrats, 101st 260, 103rd 258. AP and CBS on Nov 22 report 258 seats won, with Minnesota and Tennessee still outstanding. 258 plus those two equals 260.
4. Control of the House changed five times in 20 years (2007, 2011, 2019, 2023, 2027) is true per the House History pages.
5. 2008 margin "almost 11 points" is true. The warehouse gives 10.7 and Brookings 10.6. The September corrected Brookings files do not contain Table 2-2.
6. "46 seats flipped" is unsupported. AP and CBS report 44 net gained, and no page I read gives a gross flip count. The figures are not contradictory.
7. 2010 "66 flipped, netting 64" is unsupported. I fetched no primary table. The ACA-backlash framing is interpretive.

Other checks: the 6 other claims rest on the same fetched sources. The 2018, 2006 and 2010 margins are under 10 points in Brookings. My contested-only recomputation gave 10.9 for 2018, an artifact of excluding uncontested seats.

Misleading framing: "since after Watergate" implies 2026 beat everything after 1974. In fact it is a tie with 1976 within rounding, and the article does not say so.
