1 Pinocchio (mostly accurate, with several core figures unverified). Data caveats

ag.nass_livestock_inventory mixes rollup rows (class_desc ALL CLASSES) with leaf classes, which double counts if summed; cattle figures use INCL CALVES January 1 survey values only. County hog Census rows are fragmented across sub-rows, so the 2022 hog figure (about 82,000) is a rough sum, and the county hog survey series ends in 2005. ATTAINS use_name mixes a Total Body Contact rollup with parts; only the rollup was used. The NRCS CEAP PDF exceeded the fetch size cap.
