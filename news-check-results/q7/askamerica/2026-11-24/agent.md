## Verified or checked against data
- Gratiot cattle more than doubled: TRUE. ag.nass_livestock_inventory, cattle incl. calves, Jan 1: 24,500 (2000) to 60,000 (2025), 2.4x.
- Hogs "tripled" since 2000: PARTIALLY FALSE. NASS shows 33,500 (Dec 2000) and about 82,000 in the 2022 Census (2.4x, rough sum of fragmented rows), after a 2012 peak near 139,000. Tripling holds only against 1990s levels (about 20,000). The county hog survey series ends in 2005.
- About 3/4 of Michigan rivers fail standards: TRUE / consistent. EPA ATTAINS 2026, Michigan rivers, Total Body Contact Recreation: 28,104 miles Not Supporting vs 4,714 Fully Supporting (86% of assessed miles, 39% of all 71,283 miles). That is all causes, not waste only.
- CAFO thresholds: MOSTLY TRUE. EPA's large-CAFO threshold for dry-manure laying hens is 82,000 (30,000 with liquid manure), not 80,000.
- 3,450-head CAFO, up to 34 million gallons: TRUE. The New Lede says KB Dairy forecasts 16 to 34 million gallons a year, so "up to" omits the lower bound.
- Farm Bureau "economically useless wilderness": TRUE. It appears in the Farm Bureau complaint in Michigan Supreme Court appendix No. 165166.
- Gratiot has 24 CAFOs: MOSTLY TRUE. The article's own photo caption says 25, so it contradicts itself.

## Unsupported (not false)
No warehouse table covers these (search_catalog returned nothing for CAFO permits, manure volumes or E. coli sampling), and no primary source was retrievable:
- 272 CAFOs, 1.1 million tons and 3.93 billion gallons, and "more waste than the human population".
- 420 million gallons a year in Gratiot.
- The 10x E. coli figure from Borrello's MDPI paper (the page returned no text).
- The state E. coli standards (300, 130 and 1,000). They appear in Bridge's own companion story, which is not independent of the article.
- The 2024 Michigan Supreme Court ruling and the June 2026 appeal.
- Hauling cost of "$300 an hour".
- Borrello's central causal claim that CAFOs "dwarf" other inputs.

## Misleading or one-sided framing
- Causation rests mainly on one researcher and advocates. Bridge's companion stories cite up to 330,000 failing septic systems and sewer overflows as other sources, and the three-quarters figure covers all sources.
- ATTAINS impaired river miles rose from 12,320 (2018) to 28,104 (2026), but assessed miles also grew, so much of that rise is more monitoring.
- Internal inconsistencies: 24 vs 25 Gratiot CAFOs, and the heading "Eleven years and counting" beside a quote saying EGLE has been handcuffed for "six years".
- Industry declined to comment, and the article discloses this.

## Data caveats
- ag.nass_livestock_inventory mixes rollup rows (class_desc ALL CLASSES) with leaf classes, which double counts if summed. The cattle figures use the INCL CALVES Jan 1 survey only.
- The county hog Census rows are fragmented, so the 2022 hog figure is a rough sum.
- The ATTAINS use_name column mixes a Total Body Contact rollup with its parts. I used only the rollup.
- web_search was unavailable in this session, so no independent copies of EGLE records or court opinions were found.
- The NRCS PDF exceeded the fetch size cap. The Reuters link returned HTTP 401. Both concern other states or context, not Michigan figures.
