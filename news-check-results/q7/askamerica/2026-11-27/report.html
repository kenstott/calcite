Materially misleading framing

  • The headline says the rules 'threaten survival'; the letter attributes most of the decline to the 1994 Riegle-Neal Act and decades of growth. The proposal's marginal effect is not quantified, and about 4,000 community banks remain.
  • The release says 15% last year and 'over 70'; the group's own letter says 13% and 72% for this year, and the warehouse shows 14.4% and 69.9% at end-2025. Thresholds are the group's own, inflation-adjusted back in time, which the release does not say.
  • The lending-to-Main-Street comparison rests on the group's own analysis, and the release is an advocacy statement. The letter's 1,300 FDIC staff cut (about 20%) matches Bloomberg Law reporting, not an official FDIC figure.

What This Report Does Not Answer

  • 1992 and early-1990s shares (45%, under 20%): banking.financials starts 2010; no 1992 series loaded. Not independently verified; only Better Markets' own cited FDIC BankFind analysis found.
