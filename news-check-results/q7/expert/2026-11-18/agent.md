# Fact-check: CSU CEO op-ed, Gazette 2026-11-17

**Grade: 2 Pinocchios.** Drought facts largely verified; "total water use reduced by nearly half since 2000" is overstated (Colorado Sun: deliveries -29% 2000-2025, per-capita -44%, population +42%; Maven's Notebook ~-20%).

Verified: warmest winter/water year (Colorado Climate Center); snowpack 22% of median Apr 1 (NRCS; peak was 51%); yield 40-41% (CSU); Water Shortage Preparation Mar 18; 3.0 years storage at start of 2026; 42% growth.
Caveat: 70% Colorado River counts reuse (~50% raw). Omits storage fell to 2.3 years by Oct (lowest since 2012-14).
Not checkable here: 58% Pikes Peak precip YTD (trackers give 56% to 88%), modest irrigation-season use rise, one-day native grass watering, state climatology forecast.
Confidence: high on drought facts, medium on conservation claim.
