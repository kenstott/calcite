# Fact check: KFF Health News single-payer cost story (Nov. 25, 2026)
Grade: 1 Pinocchio. Board: agent-dashboard.png; report: agent-report.html.

## National spending ~$5.3T (2024)
**Answer** True. **Key Values** $5,278.6B 2024; ~$8.97T by 2034. **Sources** CMS/Health Affairs NHE projections 2025-34. **Confidence** high.

## CEA $47T / $4.7T per year
**Answer** CEA: $49.4T gross single-payer cost and $47.4T net federal deficit, 2027-2036. Article's "$47T federal spending" is mislabeled; $4.7T/yr is the derived average deficit. **Sources** WH CEA report ch. 4. **Confidence** high.

## Oregon $69.7B / $76.3B, 3 in 5 pay less
**Answer** Not checkable here. Oct. 15, 2026 draft: UHP $66.2B with savings (Milliman $85.5B pre-savings) vs status quo $72.8-78.3B; most households pay less in nearly every scenario. **Sources** UHPGB draft report. **Confidence** low (final report not accessible).

## Ballot measures under a quarter
**Answer** False for California (Prop 186, 1994: 26.58% yes). Oregon (~20%, loose) and Massachusetts (non-binding district questions, mostly passed) unverified. **Confidence** high for CA, low others.

## Vermont 2014
**Answer** Not re-verified this session. **Confidence** medium.

## Misleading context
CEA is an advocacy document with productivity assumptions; independent estimates $25-36T/10yr (CRFB). Article read only via summarizing tool; quotes not verified verbatim.
