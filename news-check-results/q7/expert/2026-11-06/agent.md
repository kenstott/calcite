## Misleading / context issues
- Headline "most in the nation" rests on 12 vs 6 in one quarter and 32 YTD; small counts swing widely. Arkansas's farm count is also not per-farm normalized; the DEBT project's 2021-mid-2026 totals put California first (95), Arkansas 2nd (83), Georgia and Iowa 82, so Arkansas's lead is recent, not long-run.
- Chapter 12 only counts a subset; NALC's own Pittman says it understates distress.
- Causal attribution (Iran war fuel spike, tariffs) is plausible by timing (diesel rose from March) but Arkansas's 2025 surge (33 filings) preceded the March 2026 shock, so fuel cannot explain the 2025 lead; Arkansas Farm Bureau (Oxner) says it is still working out why. Rice acreage collapse and milling problems are plausible local drivers. National filings were already rising in 2025 (+46%) before the shock.
- The 19% national rise is mostly Chapter 12; Chapter 11 figure is all industries.

## Grade: 1 Pinocchio (mostly accurate)
All checkable numbers match primary sources; the weaknesses are unproven causal framing, small-n ranking, and the mixed Chapter 11 all-filer statistic. Unchecked: RMI 28%, 2025 rice acreage, tariff and Iran claims, direct quote.
