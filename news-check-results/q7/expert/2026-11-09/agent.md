# Fact-check: PSO rate increase approved for December 2026 (Radio Oklahoma News)

Grade: 1 Pinocchio. Core facts check out; the framing omits that 3.55% is not additional to the ~$11/month interim charge residents already pay, and that OCC staff still must verify the figure.

Limitation: WebFetch returned only a summary of the article (not verbatim), so quotes could not be checked word for word. No direct attributed quotes were in the summary.

## Claims
- OCC voted 2-1 on Nov 5, 2026: TRUE (Hiett, Bingman yes; Chair Kim David no). Confidence high (Tulsa Flyer, OKC Fox).
- Permanent 3.55% residential increase: TRUE as reported; David said it is not on top of interim rates; OKC Fox says OCC staff must verify 3.55%. Confidence medium-high.
- Appears on December 2026 bills: TRUE (OKC Fox: December billing cycle after revised tariffs approved). High.
- Interim charge about $11/month since July 1: TRUE ("nearly $11" per Tulsa Flyer). High.
- No residential refunds; industrial credits within 90 days: TRUE (Tulsa Flyer, OKC Fox; PSO's position, OCC has not confirmed residential eligibility). Medium-high.
- PSO six days to file revised tariffs/calcs; dollar impact unreleased: TRUE. High.
- Large-load tariff, >75 MW class: TRUE; all commissioners agreed, regulation details unsettled. High.
- Linked to Data Center Customer Ratepayer Protection Act: TRUE, HB 2992 (2026), OCC page. Medium-high (signing date sources differ).

## Misleading context
Headline "increase approved" implies a new jump; for residents it is a small step above the interim rate. PSO's backed settlement would have been about 1%. A commercial ~3% increase was reported elsewhere.
