Figures check out; framing is shaded (1 Pinocchio)

Every headline number matches the HHS data Paubox links (34 breaches, 10,116,362 people; network server 27 breaches / 10,106,558 people; EMR 64,728; Other 34,828; Email 4; iRhythm 8,179,772; COPC 1,351,650). I recomputed all from the Google Sheet copy; the HHS OCR portal itself is a JavaScript app I could not query directly, so the sheet is a secondary copy of it. Rating: 1 Pinocchio.

  • Misleading: the category counts (27+5+4, plus 3 EMR) exceed the 34 breaches because one breach can list several locations; people totals by category sum above 10,116,362. The piece never says categories overlap.
  • Misleading: "network server" is a location of breached information, not an "attack vector". iRhythm was reportedly social engineering of third-party hosted apps; COPC was voice phishing.
  • Misleading: EMR is called "second most common" in the people-affected section; by count EMR had 3 breaches, fewer than Other (5) and Email (4). Second most people is correct.
  • Headline driven by one breach: without iRhythm, network server is 1,926,786 people; without COPC as well, 575,136. The "highest in five years" claim holds against 2023-2025 (1.15M, 5.13M, 1.42M) but I could not verify 2022. "Accessed" for all 10.1M is stronger than HHS data, which counts individuals affected.
  • Prior-year comparisons verified: 2025 network server 1,419,860 / 22 breaches; EMR 20,081; Email 6; 2024 email 15. Confidence high for current data, medium for the five-year claim.
