# Fact-check: Cyprus Mail/Reuters 9 Nov 2026, "2026 set to be one of four hottest years"
**Answer:** 0 Pinocchios. All checkable claims match WMO's COP31 State of the Climate Update.
**Key values:** Jan-Sep 2026 about 1.47 C above 1850-1900; 2026 among four warmest; 2015-2026 warmest 12 years; record sea level; tenth straight record ocean heat year (prelim. through July); El Nino intensifying. Full-year 2025 1.43 C, 2023 1.46 C.
**Not checkable here:** Kennedy quote "Every indicator here is telling the same story", Copernicus "all but certain to exceed 1.5 C", "nine data sets", exact COP31 dates (location Antalya confirmed).
**Misleading risk:** 1.47 C is a nine-month figure below 1.5 C; Paris threshold is a long-term measure. Article read only via fetch summary.
**Sources:** https://wmo.int/node/39185 ; https://wmo.int/node/39187 ; https://wmo.int/ar/node/39210
**Confidence:** high on WMO facts, low on the three unverified claims.
