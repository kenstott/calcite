# Fact-check: Fox News "One industry increased DC lobbying 337% under Trump" (Nov 14, 2026)

**Grade: 2 Pinocchios (half true, misleading framing).** 

## Claims
- **337% rise, Q3 2026 vs Q3 2024**: Answer: reported by AAF (Pence-founded, anti-tariff). Measures lobbying revenue on contracts that list tariffs among issues, so includes non-tariff work. Headline says "one industry"; the figure is not by industry. No dollar base given. Sources: political.org 2026/11/14 republication; AAF original and LDA filings not retrieved. Confidence: low-medium.
- **239% rise in disclosures mentioning tariffs; 14 orgs >$1M vs 2; 105 >$100k vs 26**: AAF figures, same sourcing, not reproduced from lda.senate.gov. Not checkable here beyond the republication.
- **Greszler quote "Affordability is top of mind for many Americans"**: appears in political.org attribution. Confidence medium.
- **NY Fed: tariffs added 2.9 pts to consumer goods prices**: Verified (Amiti, Heise, Weinstein, Oct 2026; through Feb 2026, 67-item non-oil goods basket; effect eased to ~2% by Aug after court ruling). Fox omits date and scope. Confidence high.
- **Canada trade fell $50B (6.5%) before Section 338 tariffs**: not found. $50B appears only as a GSA procurement removal figure; Section 338 50% tariffs took effect Aug 2026 on ~$20B imports. Not verified; confidence low.
- **AAF: 75,000 fewer manufacturing jobs, up to 1M fewer jobs**: AAF estimate, modeled, not verified.
- **White House says tariffs strengthen manufacturing**: standard stated position; not tested.

## Misleading
Advocacy source, no dollar totals, correlation presented as causation (tariff exemption lobbying), multi-issue filings counted, "industry" headline unsupported.
