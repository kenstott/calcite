# Fact check: Fox Business, "New vehicle affordability squeezed..." (Nov 18, 2026)

Rating: 1 Pinocchio (mostly accurate). See agent-report.html and agent-dashboard.png.

## Summary
Edmunds October 2026 ATP $50,087 (first above $50k in Edmunds' series); MSRP $52,493; discount $2,406 (arithmetic consistent). Powertrain prices and YoY changes match a second outlet (Fox4). Edmunds.com returned 403, so corroboration is secondary.

## Per claim
- ATP $50,087, first above $50k. Answer: true for Edmunds only; KBB reported $50,562 (Sep) and $51,109 (Oct). Confidence medium-high. Sources: Fox4, KUOW/NPR.
- +1.8% YoY. Answer: my calc from reported Oct 2025 $49,105 gives +2.0%; minor. Medium.
- Powertrain: hybrid $44,855, PHEV $66,355, ICE $50,751, EV $56,809; YoY EV -11.8% (calc -12.6% from $65,021), PHEV -5%, ICE +2%, hybrid +2.8%. Medium.
- LA APR 7.3%, ~$800 payment: matches Fox4; single metro, not national. Medium.
- Drury quotes: only the "converging pressures" quote found; the other two not checkable here. Low.
- "Negative equity at record rates": share 29.1% Q3 2026 vs 30.9% Q1 2026 peak and 31.9% Q1 2021; average owed $7,107 vs $7,214 record. Total interest $9,938 is a record. Partly unsupported. Medium.

## Misleading flags
Headline framing omits EV prices down ~12%; LA APR stands in for national rates; "record negative equity" not supported by Edmunds data.
