Fact check: mostly accurate (1 Pinocchio) - Edmunds figures hold, two framing issues

Findings: figures check out; "record rates" for negative equity and unverified quotes are the weak spots

The Fox Business piece (Eric Revell, Nov 18 2026) reports Edmunds' October 2026 average new-vehicle transaction price of $50,087, the first time above $50,000. Every number I could trace matches a second outlet repeating the same Edmunds data (Fox4). I could not reach Edmunds directly (HTTP 403), so confidence is medium-high, not high.

  • MSRP $52,493, discount $2,406: arithmetic consistent (52,493-50,087=2,406).
  • Powertrain prices (hybrid $44,855, PHEV $66,355, ICE $50,751, EV $56,809) and YoY (EV -11.8%, PHEV -5%, ICE +2%, hybrid +2.8%, all +1.8%) match Fox4. Check: Edmunds' Oct 2025 ATP was reported as $49,105, giving +2.0%, not 1.8%; Oct 2025 EV ATP $65,021 gives -12.6%, not -11.8%. Likely revised prior-year base; minor.
  • "First time above $50,000" is true only for Edmunds' series. Kelley Blue Book/Cox reported $50,562 for September and $51,109 for October 2026, so other trackers crossed earlier. Context, not falsehood.
  • "Record prices": supported (KBB record $51,109).
  • Drury "rolling over negative equity at record rates": Edmunds data show negative-equity share of trade-ins at 29.1% in Q3 2026, below the 30.9% Q1 2026 peak and 31.9% in Q1 2021; dollar amount owed (about $7,107) is also below the Q4 2025 record of $7,214. Total interest paid ($9,938, Q3 2026) is a record. So "record" fits interest cost, not clearly negative equity. This is a quote, so the error is the source's, but Fox did not caveat it.
  • Los Angeles APR 7.3% and ~$800 payment: matches Fox4; a single metro, so it should not be read as national (national March 2026 average about 7%, $770, per Edmunds via CBT News).
  • Verbatim Drury quotes: the first appears in search summaries of the Fox piece itself; the "squeezed from both sides" and "stretching out loan terms" quotes could not be found elsewhere. Not checkable here.

What would change this: access to the Edmunds release showing different prior-year bases or the full Drury quotes. Misleading-by-omission: headline implies broad buyer squeeze while EV prices fell nearly 12% and the LA-only APR stands in for national rates.
