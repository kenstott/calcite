Fact-check: Better Markets on FDIC merger rules

Result: 1 Pinocchio - broadly accurate advocacy, one overstated characterization

The piece (Better Markets, Nov 23 2026, Phillip Basil) is largely accurate on the proposal's direction but overstates that it triggers "automatic merger approvals". Only de minimis transactions are deemed approved (in as few as 5 business days, unless the Attorney General objects), and applications are deemed substantially complete if the FDIC does not identify missing information within 21 days. Standard deals keep 90/150-day clocks, extendable to 180/270.

  • Not verified: community banks 45% to 15% of assets and large banks under 20% to over 70%. Related FDIC figures: community banks 14% of assets in 2011; banks over $10B went 27% (1984) to 80% (2011). Plausible direction, exact figures and the "last year" value not confirmed.
  • "Rubber-stamp", "absurd provisions", "upside-down banking rules" are opinion, not checkable. Claim that the biggest banks lend least to Main Street: not checked.
  • Misleading by omission: the piece does not state that the automatic approval applies only to de minimis deals, and the proposal does not mention community banks specifically (Skadden); it discusses rural competition.
  • What would change this: the proposal text itself (Federal Register) was not read; I relied on law-firm and trade summaries.
