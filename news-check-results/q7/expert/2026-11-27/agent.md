# Fact-check: Better Markets, "FDIC merger rules threaten survival of community banks" (2026-11-23)

Rating: 1 Pinocchio. Broadly accurate advocacy; overstated "automatic approvals" framing.

## Step 1: Proposal exists and timing
- Answer: True. FDIC announced a merger-review proposal 2026-09-17; 60-day comment period, deadline 2026-11-23.
- Sources: Skadden (skadden.com/insights/publications/2026/09/fdic-issues-proposal-on-bank-merger-transactions); HLC summary.
- Confidence: High.

## Step 2: "Timelines that trigger automatic merger approvals and application acceptance"
- Answer: Partly supported. De minimis deals deemed approved in as few as 5 business days unless AG objects; application deemed substantially complete if FDIC gives no deficiency notice in 21 days. Standard deals: 90/150 days (extendable 180/270). Not general automatic approval.
- Confidence: Medium (law-firm summaries, not Federal Register text).

## Step 3: Asset shares (community 45% to 15%; large <20% to >70%)
- Answer: Not confirmed. FDIC: community banks 14% of assets in 2011; banks over $10B 27% (1984) to 80% (2011). Direction right, exact figures and "last year" value not verified.
- Sources: FDIC 2012 Community Banking Study; KC Fed 2003.
- Confidence: Low.

## Step 4: Opinions/quotes ("rubber-stamp", "absurd provisions", "upside-down banking rules", biggest banks lend least to Main Street)
- Answer: Opinion, or not checkable here (lending claim not checked). Quotes are attributed to Basil on the page itself.

## Misleading flag
Omits that automatic approval applies only to de minimis deals; proposal does not specifically address community banks.
