## Assessment
- **Correct verdict: 0 Pinocchios.** Every figure in the article matches Challenger's release, including the rank claims, and the only caveats are context the article omits, not errors.
  - **Cuts: true.** 43,994 in October, up 2% from 43,281 in September, down 71% from 153,074 in October 2025. It is the lowest October since 2023 (36,836).
  - **Year to date: true.** 617,189, down 44%, the lowest January-October since 2022 (243,338). The release states this itself.
  - **Sectors: true.** Food led with 9,677, its highest month of the year. Tech had 5,576, its lowest month of the year, and leads the year with 171,501 (+21%). Services had 5,458 (35,542 YTD).
  - **Reasons: true.** Market/economic conditions were 16,407 in October (37%) and 130,531 YTD, which overtook AI (121,401 YTD; 1,265 in October).
  - **Hiring: true.** 444,692 October hiring plans, the highest October since at least 2009. Seasonal plans are 470,400 (+26%), with Amazon at 300,000 and UPS at 100,000. Seasonal roles are 72% of 2026 plans. Challenger counts announced plans, not completed hires.
  - **Microsoft: true.** The 60,000 voluntary exit offers are excluded because Challenger counts voluntary programs only when employees accept or a company states a reduction target.
  - **Quotes: true.** "Low-fire environment" is accurate.
  - **Context omitted:** the 71% drop is against an inflated October 2025 base. The release ties 2025 totals to federal workforce cuts and does not give a fuller reason. Amazon is the bulk of seasonal hiring.
