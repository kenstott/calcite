# Fact-check: Fox News, "One industry increased DC lobbying 337% under Trump; here's what's driving the push" (Amanda Macias, Nov 14, 2026)

Method note: WebFetch returned only a summary of the article, not verbatim text. Claims below come from that summary. I did one round of searching and did not reach the AAF report, the Senate LDA filings or the NY Fed paper directly. Most verdicts rest on secondary coverage.

## Claims

| # | Claim | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | AAF (founded by Mike Pence) report: lobbying disclosures mentioning tariffs rose 239% in Q3 2026 vs Q3 2024 | Reported consistently by secondary coverage (political.org, Nov 14, 2026). Primary AAF report and LDA filings not opened. Partly verified. |
| 2 | Lobbying spending rose 337% (the headline figure) | Same secondary source. Fox itself hedges that this "may include issues beyond tariffs". Partly verified. |
| 3 | 14 organizations spent over $1M in Q3 2026, vs 2 in Q3 2024; 105 spent over $100K, vs 26 | Not checkable here. These counts need LDA database analysis. |
| 4 | Greszler: companies pay lobbyists for tariff exemptions instead of investing; small businesses may shrink or close | Attributed opinion. Consistent with her published AAF argument. The "small businesses close" part is speculation. |
| 5 | Greszler quote: "Affordability is top of mind for many Americans." | Not checked. |
| 6 | NY Fed estimate: tariffs added 2.9 points to consumer goods prices | Verified in substance. Amiti, Heise and Weinstein, published Oct 6, 2026. The figure is goods inflation through Feb 2026 for a 67-category basket, and it eased to about 2% by August after the Supreme Court ruling. A reader could take it as all consumer prices. |
| 7 | AAF: about 75,000 fewer manufacturing jobs in the first year, up to 1M fewer jobs nationwide | Not checked. This is an AAF model estimate (an advocacy-group projection). |
| 8 | US-Canada trade down $50B (6.5%) before the Section 338 tariffs | Not checked. |
| 9 | Trump says tariffs strengthen manufacturing; White House says foreign exporters bear the cost | Attributed position. The NY Fed work contradicts the cost-incidence claim, since retail prices rise. |

## Misleading context
- The headline says "one industry" drove 337%. The summary shows no single industry is isolated. The figure is total reported lobbying revenue, which Fox concedes may include non-tariff issues.
- The source is an advocacy group, founded by a prominent Trump critic, that opposes the tariffs. The piece leans on AAF for most of its figures.
- Earlier AAF memos gave very different percentages (for example 599%, 655% and 690%) for different periods and bases. The baseline, period and metric change from report to report.
- Lobbying growth is not shown to be caused by tariffs. Disclosures that mention a topic are not the same as money spent on it.

## Rating
**2 Pinocchios (mixed).** The cited numbers appear to be accurately reported from the AAF source, and the NY Fed figure is real. The headline overstates what the data isolate. It treats an advocacy group's unaudited tabulation, which includes non-tariff lobbying, as a finding about one industry. Several statistics could not be verified here.

Sources: political.org (Nov 14, 2026); Axios (Oct 7, 2026); InvestmentNews; Bloomberg Government; The Hill; Cato blog.
