Limits: I read the page through WebFetch (a summarizing fetch, not verbatim text) and ran a few web searches. I did not reach primary sources such as FAA statements, Mississippi certified results, or the NYT report itself. The checks below are shallow and rely on secondary coverage.

Note: the transcript records what guests said. A claim being false would be the guest's error, not NBC's. NBC's own claims are the host's framing (for example, "more than 7,000 canceled flights").

## Overall grade

Provisional: **not gradable on the 0 to 4 Pinocchio scale**. The verifiable core (Colom's win, the Huang scandal) checks out. The key factual claims about the FAA outage could not be confirmed.

## Potentially misleading framing

- The Huang scandal rests on anonymous intelligence sourcing. No public evidence shows Huang knew or passed sensitive information. Lee's phrasing, that China "gained access through" the affair, states a disputed intelligence conclusion as settled fact.
- Duffy's explanation of the outage (an old router, no cyber cause) is self-serving and uncorroborated in the transcript.
- Duffy attributes the funding shortfall to Congress while the department holds $12.5B in appropriated funds. That deserves scrutiny.
