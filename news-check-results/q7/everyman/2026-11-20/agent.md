## Rating
1 Pinocchio (mostly accurate). Headline numbers are consistent with the independent KBB record-price finding; no contradicted fact found.

## Misleading or context issues
- The headline says "record prices," and the piece's main number (first above $50K) is Edmunds-specific; KBB's record is $51,109, so "first time over $50K" is not a universal claim. Readers may conflate the two series.
- Mixing a national price with a single-market (Los Angeles) APR and payment invites reading it as a national interest-rate figure; national APR was about 7.0%.
- Price growth of about 2% YoY is modest; "squeezed" framing relies on levels and loan terms, not a sharp rise. EV prices actually fell.
- The fetched page title differed from the URL slug (apparent headline revision), so the version checked may not match what was originally published.
