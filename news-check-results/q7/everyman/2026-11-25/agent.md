# Fact-check: KFF Health News on single-payer costs and Oregon (midterm election)

Limits: WebFetch returned only a summary of the article, not verbatim text, so I could not see exact wording or every claim. Only three searches were run. Claims below are those the summary exposed.

## Claims

| # | Claim | Verdict |
|---|-------|---------|
| 1 | Oregon panel estimates the plan costs about $69.7B/yr starting 2032 | Not confirmed as stated. Oregon UHPGB/Milliman materials show 2032 figures of $68.4B to $86.5B depending on scenario: $69.4B low, $77.7B best and $86.5B high (Step 2); $68.4B low and $75.2B best (Step 3). A Sept 2026 draft cites $85.5B and news coverage cites $75.2B. $69.7B is in the range but I did not find it as a published figure. Plausibly a low or later-version figure. |
| 2 | Board says plan would save money overall (admin costs, drug prices); up to 3 in 5 people would pay less | Not checked against primary documents (not located in the time available). |
| 3 | Sen. Bruce Starr says it would raise the state budget 43% | Quote not checked. Attributed opinion from a partisan source. |
| 4 | White House estimate: Medicare for All costs $4.7T/yr, or $47T over 10 years | Could not find the White House source. A comparable figure exists elsewhere ($47.4T net over 2027-2036, Holmes & Edwards via American Action Forum). Other estimates are lower (Mercatus $32.6T, Urban about $34T). $4.7T/yr x 10 = $47T is internally consistent. Status: source not found. |
| 5 | US health spending was about $5.3T in 2024 | Confirmed. CMS actuaries: $5,278.6B. |
| 6 | Projected to exceed $72T over 2025-2034 | Plausible and consistent. CMS projects $5.7T in 2025 rising to nearly $9.0T in 2034. I did not sum the series. |
| 7 | Vermont abandoned single-payer in 2014 under Shumlin | Consistent with the historical record (from background knowledge; not re-verified this session). |
| 8 | Ballot measures in CA, MA and OR failed to reach a quarter of voters | Not verified in this session. |
| 9 | Sanders expected to chair Senate HELP; El-Sayed among new supporters; Trump opposed | Not verified. |
| 10 | Quotes from Cox (KFF), McDonough (Harvard), Woolhandler (CUNY), Richter (Vermont Health Care for All) | Not checkable here: I could not see the verbatim text. Attributions are plausible for these experts. |

## Misleading-context flags

- Juxtaposing $47T (a 10-year federal figure) against total US spending of $72T (all payers) is fair framing as the article presents it, but gross cost is not net new spending. Estimates vary widely ($32T to $47T), and a single White House figure is one side's number.
- Oregon's 2032 cost is a range, with the board's savings claims contested by a Finance and Revenue minority report. A single point estimate understates that uncertainty.

## Grade

Provisional: 1 Pinocchio (a few shading issues, mostly sound). The one verified numeric claim is accurate. The Oregon figure is within the published range but I did not find it as stated, and the White House figure is unsourced here. Confidence is low because verification was limited.

Sources: oregon.gov/uhpgb (Milliman final report; July 2026 board presentation); States Newsroom (newsfromthestates.com/node/421205); Health Affairs NHE Projections 2025-34; American Action Forum M4A analysis; Mercatus.
