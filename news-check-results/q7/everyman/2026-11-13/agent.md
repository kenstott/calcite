Verdict: 0 Pinocchios (The Truth). Every checkable claim matches Challenger, Gray & Christmas's October 2026 report.

Primary source: https://www.challengergray.com/blog/october-layoffs-at-three-year-low-seasonal-hiring-plans-up-26/
Corroboration: CPA Practice Advisor (cpapracticeadvisor.com/?p=193395), TheStreet Pro, Trading Economics.

## Misleading-context flags (minor)

- Amazon accounts for nearly two-thirds of seasonal hiring and only five companies announced plans (11 in 2025). The headline-level "hiring plans up 26%" is driven by one employer; the article names Amazon but does not give the concentration.
- Challenger counts announced plans, not realized layoffs or hires, and is not a government labor statistic. Low announcements coexist with weak permanent hiring ("not cutting deeply, but not committing to permanent headcount").
- Tech remains the largest sector YTD at 171,501; the low October tech figure should not be read as a trend reversal.
- The 71% drop is against an unusually high base (Oct 2025 was the highest October since 2003).

## Rating

0 Pinocchios. Accurate figures and a faithfully quoted attribution. The caveats above are context, not errors.
