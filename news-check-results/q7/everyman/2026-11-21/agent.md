# Fact-check: CNBC, "Trump bought Nvidia, Palantir, SpaceX amid federal contracts" (Nov 19, 2026, Luke Fountain)

Method note: WebFetch returned only a summary of the CNBC piece, not verbatim text. I did not reach the OGE filing, USAspending or SAM.gov. Verdicts rest on secondary reporting plus the SEC filing.

## Claims

## Misleading framing
- The headline and structure imply cause and effect ("bought... amid federal contracts"). The filing shows only date-ranged trades in discretionary accounts. It shows no knowledge or direction by Trump, and CNBC says so near the end.
- Value ranges are wide bands. The Nvidia and SpaceX buys were small (tens of thousands of dollars) against a $946M contract, so the totals say little about any single position.
- The SpaceX share purchase is flagged as undated relative to the Musk dinner. That is fair, but it makes the dinner a hint rather than a finding.
- Most trades in the filing are routine, such as ETFs and sales. Contract timing coincidences are expected for large, widely held companies.

## Rating
**1 to 2 Pinocchios (leaning 1).** Core facts check out: the filing, the NASA award, Huang's resignation, the White House response. The Palantir contract amounts, the CEO-meeting timing and the 500 first-term figure are unverified here. The insinuating framing is the main problem, not any individual number.

Sources: ibtimes.com (Trump disclosure Nvidia/SpaceX/Tesla), space.com and govconwire.com (NASA $946M), sec.gov Nvidia 8-K (Nov 2026), thenextweb.com and phoronix.com (Huang), goodmorningamerica.com (21,000 trades).
