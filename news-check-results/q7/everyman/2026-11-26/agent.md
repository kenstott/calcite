## Rating: 1 Pinocchio (mostly accurate)
All hard USDA and DTN numbers match primary sources. The deviation is that the cited farmdoc breakeven ranges are wider than the published budget figures (upper ends of $5.20 corn and $11.60 soy not found), and the futures-spread claims could not be confirmed.

## Potentially misleading context
- The USDA corn yield of 179.6 was a cut from October, and a surprise one; the article's "yields are strong" framing omits that corn production fell month over month.
- The breakevens are farmdoc's 2027 budgets (trend yield 245 bu corn, 77 bu soy; $321 cash rent), but they are compared with 2026-crop prices and national yields. This mixes crop years and Illinois-only costs with national price data.
- Fertilizer figures are national retail averages, and the nitrogen-saving and fuel numbers are generic, not from a cited study.
