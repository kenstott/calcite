# The Spokesman-Review article's claims about Medicaid cuts, Apple Health coverage and a state lawsuit are very biased and partly false.

Article: "Democratic lawmakers in Washington state say healthcare funding should be priority for Congress" (Nov 9, 2026). The fetch succeeded.

**Rating: 2 Pinocchios.**

Warehouse check: search_catalog returned ACA marketplace tables and census.acs_health_insurance, but no table with Apple Health/Medicaid enrollment or state Medicaid spending. The one WA ACS row returned (2024 public_coverage 106,888 of 7.70M) is a single-cell variable, not total public coverage, so I did not use it. Claims were checked against web search results only; I did not fetch those pages.

| Claim | Verdict |
|---|---|
| About 10,000 immigrants lost Apple Health last month | Mostly true. Reports cite 10,000-11,000. |
| WA loses about $3.2B over two years; at least 250,000 lose coverage | Mostly true on the 250,000 (governor's office); other reports say 200,000 by end of 2027. The $3.2B was not confirmed, and the article does not state its source or timeframe. |
| AG filed a lawsuit "Thursday" to block the cuts | Unsupported. I found no November 2026 filing; the latest was a June 2026 multistate suit over the work-requirements rule. |
| AG said at least 20% of residents could lose coverage | Unsupported, and inconsistent with the 250,000 figure (about 3% of residents) in the same article. |
| The federal cuts cause the coverage losses | Partially false. Only projections were found. |
| 15% of adults and 36% of children on Apple Health; 1.9M enrolled; $21B total/$13B federal; up to $1T national cuts | Not checked here. No matching table, and not confirmed in the sources read. |
| OBBBA passed July 2025; ACA subsidies expired last year | True, public record. |

**Materially misleading:** the 20% and 250,000 figures sit side by side with a gap of roughly 7x. The article does not reconcile them. Murray's linking of Medicaid cuts to rising healthcare costs is opinion. Ferguson's "draconian" is advocacy.

Sources: Spokesman article (fetched); KIRO7, Washington Examiner, atg.wa.gov (search results only).
