**Claims and verdicts**
1. "The 2026 elections were a blue blowout" (author, central): TRUE. Early AP counts show Democrats won both chambers, about 54-44 in the Senate with two races pending and about 228 House seats.
2. "Congress will have a massive Democrat majority in both chambers": TRUE. The margins are large, but they are early counts, not certified.
3. "Some 35 percent of 2024 Trump voters stayed home": MOSTLY TRUE. The figure is a J.L. Partners survey estimate (35% of Trump voters vs 22% of Harris voters), presented as settled fact without attribution.
4. "In 2026, there was a double-digit shift" (white working-class vote): UNSUPPORTED. The warehouse has no 2026 exit-poll data and I found no source. One exit-poll analysis says non-college whites stayed with Republicans, by smaller margins.
5. "Democrats blocked most issues that addressed cost-of-living matters": UNSUPPORTED. No tally or definition is offered. Republicans held both chambers in 2025-26, so "blocked" can only mean Senate filibuster votes.
6. Moreno: the $7,500 EV credit was eliminated: TRUE.
7. Moreno: "A year later, electric vehicle prices are down 3 percent" (Moreno's central claim): MOSTLY TRUE. KBB reports the October 2026 EV average transaction price at $56,220, down 2.7% year over year; Cox says down 2.9%. The warehouse (econ.new_vehicles_cpi, BLS CUUR0000SETA01) shows all new-vehicle CPI UP 0.74% over the same year (180.039 vs 178.723).
8. Moreno: "subsidies raise costs": MOSTLY FALSE. A 3% fall in an average transaction price does not isolate a subsidy effect. Mix shift, manufacturer incentives (11.3% of the average price) and a collapse in demand after the credit expired are alternative explanations. Overall new-vehicle prices rose.
9. Moreno: Democrats "are closing refineries": PARTIALLY FALSE. The real closures (Phillips 66 Los Angeles, Valero Benicia) were company decisions that cited costs and California regulation. I found no national Democratic closure program. This was checked by web search only; I did not query EIA refinery capacity tables.
10. Steve Guest's quoted line ("biggest lie in the history of worldwide politics"): opinion, not checkable. It was not scored.
