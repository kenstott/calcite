The WCAX/AP Thanksgiving dinner cost article's claims about Farm Bureau prices, diesel and grocery inflation are mostly accurate.

**Rating: 1 Pinocchio.**

Article: https://www.wcax.com/2026/11/18/heres-how-much-more-americans-are-expected-pay-thanksgiving-dinners-this-year/ (AP story). The connector's own fetch of the article worked. The six linked AP pages returned HTTP 403, so the claims that rest on them were checked against BLS, EIA and USDA directly.

## Claim checks
- **Dinner for 10 up 6% to $58.63, first rise since the 2022 record of $64.05:** True. The Farm Bureau release and analysis (fetched) say the same.
- **16-lb turkey $23.68 vs $21.50:** True (+$2.38, 10.1%).
- **Veggie tray 5 cents cheaper, peas 4 cents cheaper, pie crusts cheaper, pumpkin filling dearer:** True, consistent with -3.7% on $1.31, -2% on $1.99, -2.7%, and +1.9% for pumpkin mix.
- **Diesel record $6.53 in September:** True. Warehouse table energy.eia_retail_gasoline_prices (EIA weekly) peaks at $6.529 on 2026-09-21. AAA's $6.53 is from search coverage, not read directly.
- **Diesel $5.96 on Wednesday, up 58% from a year ago (AAA):** Mostly true, not directly confirmed. EIA weekly shows $5.937 on Nov 16 against $3.868 a year earlier, which is +53%. AAA's Nov 16-17 readings were about $5.99-6.00. The samples differ.
- **Food at home up 22% from Oct 2021 to Oct 2026:** True. Warehouse table econ.food_cpi (CUUR0000SAF11) goes from 265.478 to 322.876, which is +21.6%.
- **USDA forecasts food-at-home +2.3% for 2026 against a 2.6% average:** True. The USDA ERS Food Price Outlook says 2.3% for 2026 and a 2.6% average for 2006-2025.
- **USDA wholesale frozen hen $1.76 per pound, up 29%:** True as an estimate. The ERS October outlook has $1.76 against $1.36. The Nov 17 outlook raised it to $1.77. The "record" label was not independently tested.
- **Aldi $39, Walmart $35:** True per press coverage of the retailers' announcements. The Aldi page I fetched did not carry the figure.
- **Mark Jordan (Leap Market Analytics) says turkey production rose substantially and bird flu cases are waning:** True on production. ERS has 2026 production up 7%. HPAI detections were 9 in October 2026, but the "waning" trend was not separately tested. His diesel quote is an attributed opinion.

## Materially misleading
The lede says "Record turkey prices... could make Thanksgiving dinner more expensive." The retail turkey in the Farm Bureau survey is $1.48 per pound ($23.68). In 2022 it was $28.96 ($1.81 per pound), so retail turkey is far from a record. Only USDA's wholesale hen price is a record. The body of the article keeps the two apart, but the opening sentence does not. The lede also pairs "record turkey prices" with fuel costs, while the article's own expert says diesel likely did not affect this year's turkey prices.
