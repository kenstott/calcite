Summary

Rating: 1 Pinocchio. Nearly every figure matches the Farm Bureau release, EIA diesel, BLS food-at-home CPI and USDA ERS (2.3% forecast, 2.6% average, turkey +7% production, wholesale hens $1.76-1.77).
