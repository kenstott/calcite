Summary

Rating: 1 Pinocchio. The checkable numbers hold up: EIA data in the warehouse puts the Connecticut household bill at about $206 a month in 2025, second only to Hawaii, and the Eversource $33 and $20 figures match NBC Connecticut. The framing is one-sided. The piece says the renewable mandate keeps ratcheting up, when Class I fell from 30% to 25% in 2026. It also relays incumbents' savings claims without noting critics dispute them. Several items (Demicco and Allie-Brennan figures, bill hearing status, OCC deadline extension) could not be verified. Not verified at all: Cabrera, Fazzino, Khan, Kiner and McGinnis platform text, the new-nuclear rule, and the Jan. 6 session date (consistent with the state constitution).
