Exclusions disclosed

My county queries applied the predicates a.c IS NOT NULL and b.c IS NOT NULL: they excluded every county whose count is suppressed (1-9 deaths) in either window. That excluded 1,970 of 3,148 counties, leaving 1,178 for the up/down tally (233 up, 593 down, 352 equal). The Ohio tally excluded the suppressed Ohio counties through the same rule, leaving 38. The March 2026 suppression count used provisional_drug_overdose IS NULL, which counted rather than dropped the 1,629 of 3,144 suppressed counties. The Rio Arriba rank excluded suppressed counties and counties under 1,000 residents. Including suppressed counties gives no trend to compute, so no alternative headline exists.

Misleading framing

The headline presents 430 rising counties as established though the figure could not be reproduced and covers only counties with reportable counts. The article does caveat small numbers and provisional data. The 12-month windows end in March, so the 'past year' is not calendar 2025.

Limits

Axios pages, the CDC press release and the CDC dashboard returned HTTP 403. NPR (via APR) and the article's other linked MedicalDaily pages were read; none contradicts the checked figures. The warehouse has no coroner data.
