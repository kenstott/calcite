Summary

Accurate. Every figure in the Granite State Report piece matches the NHFPI issue brief, press release and NHPR, which I read directly. Central claim: CCSNH 12-month enrollment up 6.1% (23,325 to 24,741), with 9,364 dual-enrolled high schoolers = 37.8%; GSR's own subtraction (15,377) is correct. Warehouse IPEDS fall headcount for the seven community colleges (14,645 in 2014 to 9,204 in 2023, -37%) corroborates the direction of the fall decline. Only GSR's Promise-eligibility paraphrase adds two qualifiers the brief does not state. The 12-month IPEDS data are not in the warehouse, so figures are verified to the brief, not recomputed.

Framing and data-quality notes

  • The headline pairs 6% growth with a one-third high school share; no 2014-15 dual-enrollment count exists, which the article discloses, so growth cannot be attributed to high schoolers.
  • Source-side: the brief's -17.6% tuition change differs from its two dollar figures' ratio (-16.9%); the press release says eight of ten fast-growing occupations need a credential while the brief and NHPR say six of ten need a four-year degree.
  • The warehouse join to edu.ipeds_institutions raised a row_fanout warning; each college appears once per year, so the sums 14,645 and 9,204 are not multiplied. Fall data end 2023.
