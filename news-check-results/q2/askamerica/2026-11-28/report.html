Summary

The headline numbers hold. After-tax profits (FRED CP, identical to warehouse NIPA line 45) rose from $3,590B (Q3 2025) to $4,419B (Q3 2026), +23.1%; GDP rose 6.0% to $33,056B; the ratio was 13.37% versus 13.14% in Q2. Two problems: the ratio did not reach 10% in the dot-com run-up (about 5-7%; 10% came in 2005-06), and the 'consensus' of a further 25% profit rise is not what published forecasts say (about 15% for 2027).
