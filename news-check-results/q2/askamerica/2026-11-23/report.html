What checked out

Oregon FBI estimates match the warehouse: state violent rate -7.3% (FBI 7.9%), homicide -24.3% (24.4%), property -11.4% (12.1%), burglary -20.0% (20.4%). Portland PD vehicle thefts fell 5,492 to 4,004, matching 'nearly 1,500'.

What did not

  • Misattribution: the 2,600 and 1,500 figures are Portland-only per the FBI release as reported by KATU. Statewide, property crime fell roughly 12,000 offenses.
  • Framing: the headline's 'violent crime' is not what the data shows. Simple assault and intimidation make up 57% of the category and the growth; aggravated assault is +14 offenses; homicide is down.
  • Mixed bases: state figures use UCR definitions over 2025 and Jan-Aug 2026; city figures use PPB's broader NIBRS person category year to date through October, so 'despite state trends' compares different measures.
  • Unverifiable: all PPB tracker figures (6%, 11%, 412, 92, 57%, 28%, 14%) and the spokesperson paraphrase could not be tested.

Method notes

Tables: crime.cde_offenses (state rates summed over 12 months) and crime.cde_reta (Portland PD, ORI OR0260200; aggravated assault = total assault minus simple). The FBI release was read through KATU's report, not the FBI page. Return A is subject to revision and differs from PPB definitions.

What This Report Does Not Answer

  • Portland Police Bureau tracker figures (6%, 11%, 412, 92, 57%, 28%, 14%): The PPB tracker is an interactive dashboard not in the warehouse and not readable via fetch; those figures are graded unsupported, with FBI Return A for Portland PD (2025 vs 2024 only) as a partial cross-check. No 2026 city data exists in the warehouse.
