Summary

The article relays the governor's office figures faithfully and FBI data support the headline trends: Michigan murder rate fell 41% 2021 to 2025 (7.71 to 4.54 per 100k), robbery fell about 50% 2019 to 2025 (54.4 to 27.2), and 2025 violent crime (427) was the lowest since 2015. The causal credit to state spending is untested; national homicide fell 38% and robbery 39% over comparable windows, and Bridge Michigan's reporting shows experts tie the earlier spike mainly to COVID. The article's 'grants, social services' and 'drugs and weapons' wording differs slightly from the release (revenue-sharing and violence-intervention grants; drugs and ammunition).
