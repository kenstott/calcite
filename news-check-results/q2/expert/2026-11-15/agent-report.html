Hechinger free-tuition story: 1 Pinocchio

Summary: mostly accurate, one unexplained $3,000 price discrepancy

The article's checkable facts hold: Middlebury's $175,000 cap, Sallie Mae's 79%, MIT and Harvard at $200,000, and the Strada and Gallup/Lumina survey findings are consistent. The $97,386 Middlebury sticker matches the college's own chart but conflicts with its $94,386 comprehensive fee. Not checkable here: Hechinger's 445-college analysis, the 35 to 50 beneficiaries, the quotes, and Princeton/Chicago/Swarthmore thresholds. The article was read via a summarizing tool, so quote wording is unverified.

Claim-by-claim

  • Middlebury $175,000 free tuition: verified (Fall 2027 entrants; need and asset tests apply). High confidence.
  • $97,386 sticker, $24,462 other costs: partly; components sum to $94,386 (72,924 + 20,920 + 542). Medium.
  • Sallie Mae ~80%: verified 79%. High.
  • Strada ~70% confused: consistent (over two-thirds very confusing or mixed). Medium.
  • Gallup/Lumina three-quarters off by more than $5,000: consistent. Medium.
  • MIT, Harvard $200,000: verified. Princeton, Chicago, Swarthmore: not confirmed.
  • 445 colleges, 35-50 students, quotes, list-price cutters: not checkable here.
