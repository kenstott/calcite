# Fact-check: Hechinger Report, "With free-tuition promises, some colleges try to untangle confusion over what they cost" (Jon Marcus, 2026-11-13)

## Verdict: 1 Pinocchio (mostly accurate; one figure and several claims unconfirmed)

Note on method: the article was read through a summarizing fetch tool, not verbatim, so quotes and exact wording could not be checked against the page text. Only WebSearch/WebFetch were used.

### Claim results
- Middlebury free tuition up to $175,000 family income. **Verified.** Middlebury announcement (Sept 2026), FAQ. Applies to entrants Fall 2027 ("next year" fits). Requires need, full-time enrollment, asset test. High confidence.
- Middlebury sticker $97,386; non-tuition costs $24,462. **Partly.** Middlebury's tuition page chart shows $97,386, but the comprehensive fee is $94,386 (tuition $72,924 + room/board $20,920 + activity $542); 97,386 - 72,924 = 24,462 so the article is internally consistent with the page's chart. The $3,000 gap is unexplained (the $2,000 books estimate does not close it). Medium confidence; a likely college-side inconsistency, article repeats it.
- Sallie Mae: nearly 80% of families dropped a school on price. **Verified** (79%, How America Pays 2025 and 2026). High.
- Strada: nearly 70% find aid process partly/completely confusing. **Consistent**: Strada May 2026 reports over two-thirds rated cost communication very confusing or mixed; only about one third call aid straightforward. Exact "nearly 70%" wording not matched. Medium.
- Gallup/Lumina: over three-quarters of adults without degrees cannot estimate public four-year price within $5,000. **Consistent** with "less than one quarter within $5,000" (Lumina-Gallup, via CBS). Whether measure is net or sticker unclear. Medium.
- Thresholds: MIT $200,000, Harvard $200,000 **verified**. Princeton/Chicago $250,000 and Swarthmore $200,000: **not confirmed** in my searches. 
- Not checkable here: Hechinger's "445 colleges where no student pays sticker price" (analysis of federal data, not reproduced), 35-50 Middlebury beneficiaries of ~630 class, Levine and Baucom quotes, Prescott/Tulsa/Concordia price cuts, Penn calculator, Earlham/Wake Forest/Marist programs.

### Misleading-context flags
- Free tuition is not free cost of attendance; the article says so (caveat present). Middlebury aid also depends on assets and need.
- Article's own point (few students benefit) is supported by Levine quote, but not independently checked.

### Sources
middlebury.edu announcement and Tuition-Free Program FAQ; middlebury.edu tuition and fees page; Middlebury Campus (Feb 2026); Sallie Mae How America Pays 2025 fact sheet; Strada (strada.org, May 2026) via HR Dive/Washington Monthly; Lumina-Gallup via CBS News; Bloomberg/CBS (Harvard, MIT, Yale).
