1 Pinocchio: mostly accurate, a few loose numbers

Summary of findings

The story's core claims check out: Democrats won Congress, HR1 cuts began Oct 1 2026, a 105-day session follows in January. Loose points: ~10,000 immigrants losing coverage (state says ~14,000), $3.2B is a replacement cost per biennium rather than a loss, the 250,000 figure has no source I could find, and the AG lawsuits target work-requirement rules and SNAP, not the immigrant Medicaid cut. Quotes could not be verified verbatim because I only had a summary of the article. A Democratic Congress does not guarantee reversal given likely veto.
