# Fact-check: Spokesman-Review, 2026-11-09, Democratic lawmakers in WA on Medicaid cuts

**Rating: 1 Pinocchio (mostly accurate, with imprecision).** Caveat: I could only read a summary of the article (fetch tool refused verbatim text), so attributed quotes were not checked word for word.

## Claims
1. Democrats set to regain control of Congress in January: TRUE. AP/NPR (OPB 2026-11-04) report Democrats won House and Senate (a few Senate races uncalled early). High.
2. OBBBA (2025) Medicaid cuts; some already in effect: TRUE. HR1 provisions took effect Oct 1 2026. High.
3. About 10,000 immigrants (refugees, asylees) lost Medicaid last month: ROUGHLY. HCA/OPB/KUOW say about 14,000 lose coverage Oct 1; another report says over 11,000 lost health and food benefits. Article's figure is on the low side. Medium.
4. WA could lose about $3.2B over two years: PARTLY. Governor's office frames $3.2B per biennium as the cost to replace the federal cuts; KFF estimates $31-51B federal loss over 10 years. Consistent in magnitude; wording "lose" vs "replace" is loose. Medium.
5. At least 250,000 residents without coverage: NOT FOUND. Only a Washington Examiner headline "250K may lose" seen; earlier estimates were 194,000 (House bill, 2025). Low. Gov office says 600,000+ face work requirements from Jan 1 2027.
6. AG's office filed a lawsuit to block the cuts: PARTLY. AG Brown joined a June 29 2026 24-AG suit over the Medicaid work-requirement rule and a SNAP immigrant-eligibility suit; I found no suit against the immigrant Medicaid eligibility cut itself. Medium.
7. 105-day session in January: TRUE (odd-year session length per WA constitution/statute practice). High.
8. Quotes (Jeffries, Ferguson, Murray, Riccelli on Cantwell, Strickland, Schrier): NOT CHECKABLE here verbatim; I found no transcripts or the full text. Positions are consistent with the public record but unverified.

## Misleading context
- "Reversing" cuts: Trump has said he would block Democratic legislation; a veto is likely, which the article partly notes via Murray. Democrats holding Congress does not mean reversal.
- Cuts to immigrants' coverage are scheduled differently from the larger 2027 work-requirement changes; headline numbers mix timelines.
