# Fact-check: Fortune defense-budget piece (2026-11-25)

Overall: **1 Pinocchio**. 18 claims: 8 verified, 4 verified with shading, 2 discrepant, 4 not checkable.

| # | Claim | Verdict | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Defense fell from 49% to 13% of the federal budget since 1962 | Verified | Riedl (Brookings, 2026-11-23): 'approximately 49% to 13%'; Cancian (Brookings, Nov 2026): 62% in 1955 to 13% in 2026. |
| 2 | 'little headroom in the budget to increase defense spending' | Verified | Riedl, same wording. |
| 3 | 'something will have to give' | Verified | Riedl; context is deficits heading to 14% of GDP, not defense alone. |
| 4 | 'newly elected Congress ... faces difficult fiscal choices' | Verified | Riedl. |
| 5 | America at a 'dangerous moment' for defense spending | Verified, shaded | Riedl: dangerous moment for defense to fall to its lowest level as a share of the economy since the 1930s. |
| 6 | Defense at its lowest share of the federal budget since WWII | Plausible, not stated by source | Brookings gives 13% in 2026 but not a postwar-low claim. |
| 7 | Defense 2.8% of GDP in 2026 | Not checkable | Brookings says about 3% (Riedl, Cancian); no 2.8% located. |
| 8 | Lowest share of GDP since WWII; near 3% through 2030 | Shaded | Riedl says since the 1930s. Cancian: FY2027 request would lift it to 3.6%; Fortune omits this. |
| 9 | $917 billion defense spending FY2026 | Not checkable | CBO site blocked (403); no secondary source gave the level. |
| 10 | Defense up $48B (5.8%) | Verified | AAF summary of CBO Monthly Budget Review, FY2026 (+$48B, 5%). |
| 11 | Major mandatory programs nearly $3.5T, +$217B | Verified | CBO via AAF: Social Security +86, Medicare +77, Medicaid +55 = +218 (rounding). |
| 12 | Deficit $1.9 trillion | Discrepant | CBO and Treasury: about $2.0T (CRFB, AAF); $1.9T was the February baseline. |
| 13 | Net interest up $114B (11.1%) | Verified | CBO: +$115B, 11%. The $1.14T level was not confirmed. |
| 14 | National debt $40.7 trillion | Not checkable | Only debt held by the public ($32.4T, 100% of GDP) was found. |
| 15 | RAND warning 'three years ago': China 'largely negated' US advantage | Discrepant | Riedl attributes the line to a 2024 bipartisan commission; RAND/2023 not confirmed. |
| 16 | JEC report: $23T interest in a decade; Schweikert quotes | Not checkable | No JEC October 2026 report located. |
| 17 | CRFB: Social Security seven years six months from cuts | Partly verified | 2026 Trustees: combined OASDI 2034 (17% cut), OASI 2032 (22%). CRFB live clock not readable. |
| 18 | US-Iran conflict | Verified | Cancian paper discusses the Iran war. |

## Summary
- Core 49% to 13% share claim and all Brookings quotes match Riedl (2026-11-23) and Cancian (Nov 2026).
- Deficit: Fortune $1.9T vs CBO/Treasury about $2.0T.
- "Since WWII" vs Brookings "since the 1930s"; 2.8% of GDP not found (Brookings ~3%).
- RAND/2023 attribution vs Riedl's 2024 commission.
- Omitted: defense dollars rose 5.8%; FY2027 request would reach 3.6% of GDP.
- Not checkable: $917B, $40.7T, JEC $23T.
- Confidence: medium; CBO site blocked (403), figures via AAF/CRFB; Fortune read via summarizing fetch.

## Sources
Fortune; Riedl (Brookings); Cancian (Brookings PDF); AAF; CRFB; CBO (403).
