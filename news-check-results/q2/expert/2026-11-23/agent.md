# Fact-check: WW "Violent crime rose in Portland over past two years" (2026-11-19)

**Answer:** 1 Pinocchio. The figures check out against what I could reach. The headline says "violent crime", but the data is PPB "person crime", which is driven by simple assault and intimidation.

**Key values**
- Person crime +11% vs 2024 and +6% YTD through Oct 2026 vs 2025. Simple assault and intimidation is 57% of person offenses (+412 offenses). Aggravated assault is up ~1% and homicide is down.
- FBI Oregon: violent crime -7.9% and murder -24.4% (2024 to 2025); property crime -12.1%, burglary -20.4%; about -16% YTD through Aug 2026.
- Portland property crime: about -4% in 2025, +1 point in 2026 (not independently checked).

**Sources:** WW article; KATU report of the FBI Oregon release (https://katu.com/news/local/oregon-violent-crime-vehicle-thefts-statistics-fbi); PPB open data page (could not access the tracker).

**Confidence:** medium. PPB figures are verified only secondhand through the WW article, and the article was read only through a summarizing fetch tool. The FBI figures are confirmed via KATU, not the FBI release itself.

**Not checkable here:** the PPB tracker numbers directly, and the exact wording of quotes.
