# Fact-check: IBTimes UK NYC murders record low

**Rating: 0 Pinocchios.** Core numbers verified against NYPD figures (via NY1, amNY, QNS).

## Claims
- **Answer:** 230 murders Jan-Oct 2026, record (prior 244 in 2017), -15.7% vs 273. **Confidence:** high (three outlets agree; NYPD release itself not retrieved).
- **Answer:** Shootings 536 vs 598 (-10.4%); victims 649 vs 746 (-13.0%); major crime -6.5%; Oct -8.6%; transit +2.2% YTD; Oct shootings +4.4%, victims +5.8%. All true. **Confidence:** high.
- **Answer:** 2025 305 murders, -20.2% vs 382: true (NYPD Jan 6 2026 release). 2024 "377" unconfirmed (NYPD uses 382); 2023 391 in some tallies, 386 initially. **Confidence:** medium.
- **Answer:** "26 gang takedowns", "4,500+ guns": not checkable here (25 and 3,259 as of Aug 11). **Confidence:** low.
- **Misleading flags:** omits October transit +12.7%; record applies to Jan-Oct window only; headline invites causal reading but body disclaims causation.

Sources: ny1.com 2026/11/09 NYPD October stats; amny.com/news/october-nypd-shootings-report-2026; qns.com/2026/11; nyc.gov NYPD PR001 and PR010; ibtimes.co.uk article.
