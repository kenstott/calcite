# Fact check: WCAX/AP Thanksgiving dinner costs (2026-11-18)

Verdict: 0 Pinocchios (accurate, minor caveats).

- **AFBF feast for 10 = $58.63, up 6%** -> Confirmed. AFBF release: $58.63, up >6% (2025 $55.18)
- **First increase since 2022; 2022 record $64.05** -> Confirmed. AFBF: $5.42 (8.5%) below 2022 record
- **16-lb turkey $23.68 vs $21.50** -> Confirmed (+10.1%, $1.48/lb). AFBF survey
- **Veggie tray -5c, peas -4c, pie crust cheaper, pumpkin filling up** -> Directionally confirmed. AFBF: veggie tray -3.7%, peas -2%, crusts -2.7%; pumpkin pie mix +1.9%. Cent amounts not checked
- **Diesel $5.96, up 58%** -> Mostly confirmed. AAA $5.9989 (Nov 16), $5.9848 (Nov 17); $5.96 plausible for Nov 18; year-ago about $3.78 consistent with ~58%
- **USDA: wholesale turkey hen record $1.76/lb, +29%** -> Figure confirmed; 'record' unconfirmed. USDA ERS Oct outlook $1.76, +30%; Nov WASDE reportedly $1.77. No source found calling it a record
- **Bird flu hurt flocks since 2022; production rose, demand grew** -> Not checked here (partly). Context claim; not verified to a primary table
- **Quote: Mark Jordan, Leap Market Analytics** -> Quote present in AP text; not independently verifiable. Primary is the AP interview
- **Aldi $39, Walmart $35 for 10** -> Confirmed. Retailer promos (NBC, Grocery Dive: Aldi $38.74). Different baskets from AFBF
- **Food at home +22% Oct 2021-Oct 2026** -> Confirmed (21.7%). FRED/BLS index 265.084 to 322.535. BLS published no Oct 2025 value (funding lapse), so no official 12-month figure
- **USDA predicts 2.3% food-at-home in 2026 vs 2.6% average** -> Confirmed. ERS Oct 2026 outlook: 2.3% (1.9-2.7); 2006-2025 avg 2.6%

Caveats: $1.76/lb 'record' unconfirmed; Aldi/Walmart baskets not like-for-like with AFBF; BLS lacks Oct 2025 data (22% computed from index levels, 21.7%).

Confidence: high for AFBF/CPI/ERS, medium for diesel and turkey record.
