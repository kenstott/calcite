Fact check: WCAX/AP Thanksgiving dinner cost story

Accurate: 0 Pinocchios, with minor caveats

Every core number in the AP piece (WCAX, 2026-11-18) matches primary sources: the AFBF 10-person classic feast at $58.63 (+6%, first rise since the 2022 record of $64.05), turkey at $23.68, food-at-home CPI up 21.7% over Oct 2021 to Oct 2026, and the USDA 2.3% forecast against a 2.6% average.

  • Not confirmed: that $1.76/lb is a record wholesale price (USDA figure is real, about +30%; no source labels it a record; the November update reportedly reads $1.77).
  • Not checked: bird flu/production narrative and the cents-level item changes; diesel $5.96 sits within about 4 cents of AAA readings on Nov 16-17.
  • Context a reader needs: the Aldi ($39) and Walmart ($35) totals are store promotional baskets, not the AFBF menu, so they are not a like-for-like saving. The AFBF figure is a volunteer shopper survey, not a CPI measure. BLS has no Oct 2025 price data, so the 22% is computed from index levels and no official 12-month rate exists for Oct 2026.
  • Would change the grade: evidence the $1.76 is not a record, or a different AAA diesel reading for Nov 18.
