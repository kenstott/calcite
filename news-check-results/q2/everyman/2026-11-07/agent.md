# Fact-check: Breitbart, "Jobless Claims Fall To 57-Year Low of 189,000" (Nov 5, 2026, John Carney)

## Overall grade: 1 Pinocchio (a few shading issues, headline numbers accurate)

**Limits on this check.**
- The Breitbart page loaded, but the fetch tool returned only a paraphrased summary of it, not verbatim text. I could not check exact wording or any quotes.
- The summary showed no attributed quotes, so I checked none.
- The DOL release PDF (dol.gov/ui/data.pdf) fetched as unreadable binary, and other DOL pages returned 403. I therefore verified against FRED's ICSA series, which is DOL data. I also used secondary reports (FXStreet, InvestingLive, FXTribune) that cite the DOL release.

| Claim | Verdict | Source and confidence |
|---|---|---|
| Initial claims fell 6,000 to 189,000 in the week ended Oct 31 | True | FRED shows 189K for 10-31 against 195K for 10-24; secondary reports agree. High. |
| 189,000 is the lowest since 1969 and matches the July 18 reading | Mostly true, but the headline overstates it | FRED shows 189K for 7-18, so this week ties the low rather than setting a new one. "Falls to a 57-year low" implies a fresh record. A search hit also mentioned a 189K reading for the week of Apr 25, 2026; I did not verify it. Medium to high. |
| Four-week average fell to 194,750 | True | The FRED weeks 200K, 195K, 195K and 189K sum to 779K, which gives 194,750. Also reported as 194,750 by secondary outlets. High. |
| Four-week average is about 13.6% below a year earlier (225,500) | Not checked here | The arithmetic holds: 194,750 / 225,500 is about 0.864. I did not retrieve the year-ago average. Low confidence. |
| 2019 claims averaged 217,712 | Not checked here | Plausible but unverified. Low confidence. |
| Year-to-date average is 208,068, the lowest since 1969 | Not checked here | I did not compute it or compare it with earlier years. Low confidence. |
| Continuing claims were 1.718 million for the week ended Oct 24, up 23,000 | True | Secondary reports citing DOL, which also say the prior week was revised to 1.695 million. Medium to high. |
| Continuing claims were 244,000 below a year earlier | Not checked here | I did not find the year-ago figure. |
| Share of covered workers collecting benefits "fell from 1.3% to 1.1%" | Likely wrong | The secondary reports say the insured unemployment rate held at 1.1%. This claim comes from a paraphrase, so check the original wording. Medium to low. |

**Materially misleading points.**
- "Falls to 57-year low" is a tie with July's 189,000, not a new low.
- The article compares the recent monthly average with 2019's best single week. Comparing an average with a single week is a framing choice.
- The advance figure is seasonally adjusted and subject to revision. The prior week was revised from an earlier figure to 195,000.
- Claims this low also reflect low layoffs alongside weak hiring. Initial claims alone do not show labor-market strength.
