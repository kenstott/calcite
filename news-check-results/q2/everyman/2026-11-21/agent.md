## Misleading context
- The Farm Bureau's 6% rise follows three straight declines. The 2026 total is still about 8% below the 2022 record.
- A rival estimate of $78.93 (down 0.4%) circulates. It uses a different menu and is not comparable.
- The Aldi and Walmart figures use different menus (Walmart's differs from the AFBF's), so they cannot be compared with the AFBF total directly.
- The 58% diesel jump and the 22% five-year food price rise are presented as drivers. Jordan says diesel likely did not affect this year's turkey prices. The headline cost story is therefore mostly a turkey story.

## Rating
**1 Pinocchio.** The core numbers match the primary source. Several secondary stats could not be checked. The framing around diesel is slightly strained.
