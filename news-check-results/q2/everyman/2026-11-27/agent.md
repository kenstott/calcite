## Rating
Provisional 2 Pinocchios ("some shading"). Most figures are consistent with CBO and Brookings in direction. The deficit figure is stale ($1.9T vs the $2.0T actual), and the "lowest since WWII" and 1962 comparisons are not confirmed.

## Potentially misleading
- The piece compares defense with entitlement and interest growth. That is a share-of-budget argument. It does not measure the China military investment gap in the headline, which is a separate question about capability.
- Share of budget falls partly because other spending grows. Defense dollars still rose (reportedly 5.8%).
- Rating would likely move to 1 Pinocchio if the CBO figures were confirmed.
