## Misleading or context issues
- Headline stat mixes two bases: the study's partial sample (403 facilities) versus total state sales. The article says this itself and notes Dominion's higher 28%, so the framing is fair.
- Oregon is 18.6% (study) versus ~23% (ECOnorthwest). The article presents both with their sources.
- The CO2 figure differs between the preprint and the published version, and is not flagged.
- Several quotes could not be confirmed verbatim.

Overall: core figures verified; unverified items are paywall or time limits, not contradictions. 1 Pinocchio.
