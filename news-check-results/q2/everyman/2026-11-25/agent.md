# Fact-check: HPPR/KERA, "Texas public school districts suffer historic enrollment decline" (2026-11-19)

Method: WebFetch returned only a summary of the article, not verbatim text. Quotes could not be checked verbatim. Corroboration came from web search and a Fox 4 report. I did not retrieve TEA or State Board of Education primary documents.

## Claims
| Claim | Verdict |
|---|---|
| Enrollment fell by more than 135,000, the largest one-year drop on record | Supported. Texas Tribune, Fox, and CBS Austin all report Morath's preliminary figure of about 135,000, presented to the SBOE on Nov. 18. |
| Prior record was about 122,000 in 2020-21 (COVID) | Supported by Fox 4. |
| Nearly 212,000 lost over two years | Supported. Texas Tribune reports 76,000 plus 135,000, or about 212,000. |
| Morath blamed low birthrates | Supported. He also cited reduced migration and non-public options. |
| 66% of districts lost enrollment | Supported. Morath said 66% of districts declined. |
| Record high of more than 5.5 million in 2024-25 | Partly checkable. One source says 2025-26 enrollment fell "below 5.5 million" and another puts this year at 5.33 million, which is consistent. I did not see an explicit "record" statement. |
| Share of school-age children in public school is 85%, down from 89% | Not confirmed. Fox says about 85%, down from about 90% a decade ago. The 89% and "pre-COVID" framing differ. |
| About 28,000 public students moved to private schools in the first year of vouchers | Not confirmed. No source found. Fox says the ESA program counts are not in this data. |
| Prosper and Princeton ISD grew by as much as 14% | Not checkable here. No TEA district data was found. |
| Most districts lost millions because funding follows students | Plausible. Funding is tied to attendance, but the specific dollar claim was not verified. |
| Morath is expected to be replaced by Governor-elect Gina Hinojosa when his term ends in January | Governor-elect part supported. Hinojosa beat Abbott on Nov. 3, 2026. The "term ends" and "expected to be replaced" claims were not found anywhere. This is speculation presented as fact. |

## Materially misleading
- The voucher claim conflicts with the reporting I found. Texas's ESA program starts in 2026-27, and Fox notes the enrollment data does not include students leaving for it. Morath did not cite vouchers. Saying 28,000 students already moved to private schools in "the first year" risks overstating the program's role. The 2025-26 decline is more consistent with birthrates, migration and homeschooling.
- The data is preliminary. The official snapshot comes in February, so the headline "historic" figure may change.
- Baselines vary across sources: 5.33 million versus a drop from 5.5 million.

## Rating: 2 Pinocchios
The core headline figures are accurate. The voucher figure and the personnel prediction are unsupported, and the voucher framing could mislead on causation.
