## Rating

Insufficient evidence for a Pinocchio rating. The material is mostly interview statements by officials, so the facts that can be checked are the attributed ones. Tentatively 2 Pinocchios for the Duffy segment, because the cause claim and the $50 billion ask are unsupported and the funding framing is one-sided. Duffy's earlier statements, such as blaming an Amtrak crew for the Sept 21 outage, were disputed.

## Misleading flags

- Duffy's cause attribution for the outage was unchallenged on air, per the summary.
- The $12.5 billion "not enough" framing omits that the FAA itself put the gap at about $20 billion. The $50 billion is a larger figure than the one the FAA gave Congress.
- The Van Hollen leadership challenge may be overstated relative to public reporting.
