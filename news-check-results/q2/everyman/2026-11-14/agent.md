# Fact-check: Fortune, "CBO October budget / Treasury deficit" (2026-11-10)

Limits: the CBO PDF and publication pages returned HTTP 403, so I could not read CBO directly. I used CBO figures as reported by CRFB and the American Action Forum. WebFetch also returned only a summary of the Fortune article, not verbatim text, so quotes and wording are secondhand.

|---|---|---|---|

| 2 | $125B larger than October 2025 | Verified (secondary) | CRFB. |

## Misleading framing
- The headline slug mentions "$100 billion weekly". I could not verify any such figure.
- The timing-shift explanation, if presented as the cause of the increase, overstates how much is calendar noise.
- The piece mixes CBO's rolling and fiscal-year measures and attributes the deficit increase to outlays.
- The buyback strategy changes debt maturity, not the deficit. Linking it to the deficit story is loose.

## Rating: 2 Pinocchios
The October monthly numbers match CBO. The FY2026 headline deficit is understated ($1.9T versus $2.0T), the outlays/deficit increase is conflated, and the "not formally confirmed" claim about Treasury looks wrong. Several claims, including the Sullivan quote, could not be checked.
