Limits: WebFetch returned only a summary of the article, not verbatim text. Python was not available in this harness. I ran 3 searches. Many claims were not checked.

## Pinocchio rating

Provisional rating: 1 Pinocchio. The verified claims are accurate, and the one soft spot is the Strada paraphrase. Roughly half the claims were unchecked, so this is provisional rather than a full grade.

## Potentially misleading framing

- The headline framing, that free-tuition pledges mostly change perception, is supported by Middlebury's own president. He said the college was already effectively free up to about $135-140K, and the new threshold adds an expected 35 to 50 students.
- "Free tuition" can mislead. The pledges cover tuition only, not room and board, which the piece notes. Marist's cap is a net cost, not a price.
- The start date differs between sources (Class of 2031 versus Fall 2027).
