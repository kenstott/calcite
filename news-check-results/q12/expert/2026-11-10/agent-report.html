Fact-check: gov.ca.gov "California is booming" (Nov 10, 2026)

Mostly accurate numbers, but a misleading deficit claim: 2 Pinocchios

Most headline figures check out: +129,300 jobs, $17.40 minimum wage, $64.7B farm receipts, $188B exports, $366B venture capital. The exception is "ZERO projected deficit for two years": the LAO (Nov 5, 2026) puts the 2026-27 structural deficit at $18.5B and out-year deficits at $8-10B a year. I rate that claim 3 Pinocchios.

  • Omitted context: 5.1% unemployment is tied for the highest of the 50 states (US 4.2%); the 75% venture share is driven by OpenAI and Anthropic.
  • Could not verify: the exact $7.7B revenue figure, the 496,000 national jobs figure, the 4.3M small businesses, the bill-signing details, and Prop 2's election result. I did not read the BEA table behind $4.5T.
  • What would change the grade: the LAO fiscal outlook due later in November, or a Department of Finance document that supports the "zero deficit" wording on a different definition.
