# Fact-check: California is booming (2026-11-10)

Verdict: 2 Pinocchios overall; 'ZERO projected deficit for two years' = 3 (LAO: $18.5B structural deficit 2026-27, $8-10B out-year).

## CA added 129,300 jobs Sep 2025-Sep 2026
- **Answer**: True
- **Key Values/Sources**: BLS state release 10/20/2026; EDD (+0.72%).
- **Confidence**: medium (primary sources where noted; others secondary)

## US net payroll jobs ~496,000 over same period
- **Answer**: Not independently confirmed
- **Key Values/Sources**: BLS state release gives no national yoy; Sep national gain was only 29,000 (~45k/month trailing). Plausible, not verified.
- **Confidence**: medium (primary sources where noted; others secondary)

## Unemployment 5.1%
- **Answer**: True, context omitted
- **Key Values/Sources**: BLS: tied with Connecticut for highest among states; US 4.2%. Down from 5.5% a year earlier.
- **Confidence**: medium (primary sources where noted; others secondary)

## Minimum wage $12 to $17.40 next year
- **Answer**: True
- **Key Values/Sources**: DIR/Dept of Finance certified $17.40 for 1/1/2027 (now $16.90); $12 dates to 2019 for large employers.
- **Confidence**: medium (primary sources where noted; others secondary)

## Economy $4.5T, up 50%+ since Newsom took office
- **Answer**: Plausibly true, unverified
- **Key Values/Sources**: Governor's 10/5 release cites BEA but not table/quarter; BEA 2025 annual ~$4.25T, Q1 2026 annualized ~$4.4T (trade press). Nominal, not inflation-adjusted.
- **Confidence**: medium (primary sources where noted; others secondary)

## $366B VC through August 2026; ~75% of US startup funding
- **Answer**: True, context omitted
- **Key Values/Sources**: PitchBook (to Aug 20) $366B; Crunchbase says $385B and >75% by late Oct. Over half of CA total is OpenAI and Anthropic rounds; 2025 share was 62%.
- **Confidence**: medium (primary sources where noted; others secondary)

## Top three revenue sources $7.7B above projections since April
- **Answer**: Not verified
- **Key Values/Sources**: LAO May 2026 had $25B upgrade vs Governor's Budget; Controller showed ~$7.4-7.6B ahead of estimates through Feb/Mar. Exact $7.7B-since-April not found.
- **Confidence**: medium (primary sources where noted; others secondary)

## ZERO projected deficit for two years
- **Answer**: Misleading
- **Key Values/Sources**: LAO (11/5/2026): $18.5B structural deficit in 2026-27, budget 'precariously balanced', $8-10B annual out-year deficits; 2027-28 Fiscal Outlook due later in November. A balanced enacted budget is not zero projected deficit.
- **Confidence**: medium (primary sources where noted; others secondary)

## Prop 2 'Save for California's Future Act' lets state save more
- **Answer**: True if it passed; result not found
- **Key Values/Sources**: ACA 20 on Nov 2026 ballot: raises rainy-day cap 10% to 20%. I found no certified result; release wording presumes passage.
- **Confidence**: medium (primary sources where noted; others secondary)

## Manufacturing output $391B (2025)

- **Confidence**: medium (primary sources where noted; others secondary)
