# The California Governor's "California is Booming" release's claims about GDP growth, unemployment and jobs are biased and mostly accurate.

Source: https://www.gov.ca.gov/2026/11/10/california-is-booming/ (checked 2026-11-11). **Rating: 2 Pinocchios.**

## Verdicts
- GDP about $4.5T: TRUE (BEA SQGDP2 2026Q2 annualized $4,530,521M, nominal).
- "Up more than 50% / $1.5T since Jan 2019": PARTIALLY FALSE framing. Nominal 2019Q1 to 2026Q2 is +51.7% (+$1.54T); versus annual 2019 it is +47.6%; real (chained 2017$) growth 2019 to 2025 is only +15.5%, the same as the US (+15.6%). This is the central claim.
- Manufacturing $391B, 18% larger than Texas: TRUE (CA $390.5B vs TX $332.1B = +17.6%, BEA SAGDP2 2025).
- Unemployment 5.1%, lowest since Feb 2024: MOSTLY TRUE, but omits that CA is tied with CT for second-highest among states (DC 5.6%).
- Wages about 20% above national: TRUE (actually +23.2%: $97,593 vs $79,216).
- Under 12% of population: TRUE (11.5%).
- 26% of national job growth (129.3k of 496k): PARTIALLY FALSE. Arithmetic fine, but the denominator does not match current PAYEMS (Sept-to-Sept +580k gives about 22%); the Governor's July release said 17% and a year-to-date figure was 14.6%.
- "$4 trillion private-sector output": roughly true (2025 private-industry GDP $3.84T).
- Minimum wage $17.40 in 2027: TRUE (DIR news 2026-66; Finance certification July 31, 2026).
- Exports $188B, $162B manufactured: TRUE (USTR/Commerce secondary summary: $188.4B, $161.9B).
- Farm receipts $64.7B, +4.5%: TRUE (CDFA).
- CalCompetes $1.8B, 271 businesses, $37.6B, 75,000 jobs: MOSTLY TRUE as the office's own figures; these are company commitments, not realized outcomes.
- E15 / fusion bills signed: TRUE (Governor's Sept 19 and Sept 30 releases).
- Venture capital leadership: plausible (Governor's Aug 24 release $366B; Crunchbase about $385B YTD, over 75% of US) but more than half comes from OpenAI and Anthropic.
- NOT VERIFIED: patents, Nobel claims, R&D, unicorns, 603% growth, 4.2% productivity, $7.7B revenue beat, zero-deficit claim, 55,000 exporters, 94%, 99.8%, $5M, 17,600 businesses assisted (not on the cited CalOSBA page), 10%.
- NOT CHECKABLE: attacks on Trump policies and MAGA radio (opinion).

## Materially misleading
1. Nominal growth presented as real strength.
2. Unemployment rate omits its national rank.
3. Jobs-share denominator and window shift across the Governor's own releases.
4. Small-business counts inconsistent across the office's releases (7.6M versus 4.3M businesses).
5. Self-reported program outcomes treated as results.

## Exclusions
Rows with a null measure and rollup rows (United States, All industry total) were excluded; no headline figure changes.
