The California Governor's "California is Booming" release's claims about GDP growth, unemployment and jobs are biased and mostly accurate.

Governor's press release 2026-11-10; BEA SAGDP1/SAGDP2/SQGDP2, BLS LAUS, FRED PAYEMS; checked 2026-11-11

Article under reviewhttps://www.gov.ca.gov/2026/11/10/california-is-booming/

Summary

The release's hard numbers largely check out (GDP near $4.5T, manufacturing $391B, unemployment 5.1%, wages, minimum wage, exports). The framing is selective: the 50% growth is nominal; real growth is about 15.5%, same as the US. The release omits that 5.1% unemployment is tied second-highest among states (only DC higher), and the jobs-share figure varies by release (17%, 26%, 14.6%). Rating: 2 Pinocchios.
