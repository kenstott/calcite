# Fact-check: "California Is Booming" (gov.ca.gov, 2026-11-10)

Source type: Governor's office press release (advocacy, not news). Note: WebFetch returned a summary of the page rather than verbatim text, so claims below are those reported in that summary. There are no attributed quotes from third parties; the only quoted line is the release's own tagline, "The numbers keep betting on us."

## Claims

| # | Claim | Verdict | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 129,300 of the nation's 496,000 net payroll jobs added Sept 2025 to Sept 2026 | True, but misleading framing (see below) | EDD September 2026 release: +129,300 (0.72%) vs US +496,000 (0.31%) |
| 2 | Unemployment 5.1% | True | EDD: 5.1% in September, unchanged 3 months, down from 5.5% a year earlier. The release omits that this is still among the highest state rates and that CA lost 9,300 jobs in September alone |
| 3 | Minimum wage rising to $17.40 next year, from $12 when Newsom took office | True | Dept. of Finance certification July 31, 2026: $17.40 effective Jan 1 2027 (from $16.90). The $12 baseline (2019) was not separately checked |
| 4 | $366B venture capital to California companies as of Aug 2026 | True with caveat | PitchBook figure as of Aug 20, 2026. Crunchbase later put it at about $385B |
| 5 | About 75% of US startup funding this year | Roughly true, source-dependent | Crunchbase says over 75%. PitchBook trailing-12-month share is about 70%. Over half of California's total is OpenAI and Anthropic alone, so this reflects a few megadeals, not broad-based startup activity |
| 6 | 2025 goods exports record $188B | True | USTR/Commerce: $188.4B, up about 2.4% from $183.9B |
| 7 | $64.7B farm sales in 2025 | True | CDFA: $64.7B cash receipts, up 4.5% |
| 8 | $391B manufacturing output (2025) | Not checkable here | No source found in my searches |
| 9 | Over 50,000 US patents to California residents, FY2025 | Not checkable here | Search returned nothing |
| 10 | Top three revenue sources $7.7B above projections since April | Not checkable here | Not found. The LAO said in May 2026 that revenue was about $25B above the January budget proposal, a different baseline |
| 11 | Signed budget projects zero deficit for two years | Not verified | Governor's own claim. Independent analysts (LAO) have repeatedly projected structural deficits in out-years, so this deserves scrutiny |
| 12 | Prop 2 raises rainy-day deposit cap from 10% to 20% | Not verified in this run | Checkable against the ballot measure text |
| 13 | $7.5B research bond proposed; recently signed laws (antitrust, 120-day pop-up permit, E15, fusion strategy, Competes credit extension) | Not verified individually | Linked to the governor's own releases of Sept 19 and Sept 30 |

## Misleading context

- The jobs comparison cherry-picks the 12-month window. California's September month was a loss of 9,300 jobs, and August was revised down by 16,300. The 129,300 gain is 0.72%, which is faster than the US 0.31%, but the state's unemployment rate is still above the national rate.
- A 5.1% unemployment rate sits oddly beside the title "booming".
- The VC boom is concentrated in AI megadeals and is a measure of capital raised, not jobs or wages. Different sources give 70% to 75%.
- Press-release lacks any mention of the downsides (housing costs, deficits, out-migration).
- Several headline numbers are the governor's own claims with no independent source.

## Rating

**2 Pinocchios** (some shading of the facts). Nearly every number I could check is accurate, but the framing is selective (one favorable window on jobs, a single-source 75% share driven by two companies, a "zero deficit" claim not independently supported) and about four figures could not be verified. Not a case of false statements; a case of promotional selection.

Sources: edd.ca.gov September 2026 release; hrwatchdog.calchamber.com and dir.ca.gov (minimum wage); Crunchbase/PitchBook coverage via aiweekly.co and others; ustr.gov California data; cdfa.ca.gov statistics.
