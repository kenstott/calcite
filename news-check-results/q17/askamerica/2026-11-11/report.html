Summary

Largely accurate (about 1 Pinocchio). Core survey figures match the Commonwealth Fund as relayed by other outlets, and the marketplace enrollment drop matches CMS data in the warehouse (Feb 2025 21.8M to Feb 2026 19.2M). Two projection figures (Medicaid 11.5M, CBO 7.5M), the $750-$4,035 premium range and a few subgroup percentages could not be verified because the Commonwealth Fund site returned 403.
