Fact-check: AJMC "Uninsured rate rises to 11.2%"

Mostly accurate: 1 Pinocchio, with two unverified claims

The core figures (11.2% uninsured among adults 19-64, up from 8.9%; 46% inadequately insured; marketplace 8.5% to 6.4%; KFF enrollment 22.1M to 19.2M) match the Commonwealth Fund survey and KFF. The primary Commonwealth Fund page returned HTTP 403, so verification relied on secondary coverage that quotes it.

  • Misleading framing: the 11.2% is working-age adults only; the 73% opinion figure covers adults 19+. The authors caution this survey has historically produced lower uninsured estimates than federal surveys (NHIS 2025 all ages: 8.3%). The decline is survey-based and the causal attribution (subsidy expiry, unwinding) is the authors' interpretation.
  • Possible mischaracterization: the CBO "7.5 million marketplace reduction": CBO's 7.5M figure I found is added uninsured from Medicaid changes (2034).
  • Not checkable here: $750-$4,035 premium range, SEP under 150% FPL, verbatim quotes.
