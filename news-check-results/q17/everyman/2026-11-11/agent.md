# Fact-check: AJMC, "Uninsured Rate Rises to 11.2% as Coverage Erodes Before Medicaid Cuts" (Oct 22, 2026)

Limits: the Commonwealth Fund report page returned HTTP 403, so I could not read the primary report directly. I checked the AJMC summary against secondary coverage of the report (Becker's, Fierce, HealthExec, Healthcare Dive, Stateline snippets) and KFF (read directly).

## Context the piece omits or underplays
- Survey-based estimate; the authors themselves note it runs lower than federal surveys on uninsurance. CDC NHIS put 2025 uninsured at 8.3% for all ages, a different population and method, so the figures are not directly comparable.
- Causation: coverage losses are attributed to the end of continuous Medicaid enrollment and enhanced credits, but the survey shows association, and the administration's fraud-reduction explanation is disputed rather than refuted.
- Medicaid decline spans 2022 to 2026, a longer window than the 2024 to 2026 headline.
- The headline's "11.2%" is working-age adults only, not the whole population.

## Overall rating: 1 Pinocchio (mostly accurate)
The headline figures match the report as covered elsewhere, and the KFF figures are exact. Deductions: several numbers (Medicaid, premiums, CBO figures, field dates) could not be confirmed, the 73% wording is looser than reported elsewhere, and the piece is a summary of an advocacy-adjacent survey without the federal-survey comparison. Nothing found is false.
