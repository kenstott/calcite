# Fact-check: Breitbart, "States with Highest Unemployment Rates Kept Democrats in Power" (2026-11-26, Matt Mercer)

**Rating: 2 Pinocchios.** Nearly every individual fact checks out. The framing is selective and implies a causal story the data does not support.

## Claims
| Claim | Verdict | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Five highest-unemployment states: CT 5.1, CA 5.0, MI 5.0, AZ 4.9, OR 4.9 | True (BLS Oct 2026 prelim; AZ/OR tie 46th; DC 5.4 is higher but not a state; NM 4.8 next) | BLS LAUS laumstrk, released 2026-11-20 |
| Hobbs won another term (AZ) | True, 55.7-42.5 over Biggs | AP via azfamily; Wikipedia |
| Dems flipped AZ legislature | True (both chambers, first trifecta since 1966; counts partial). "Statewide offices" not verified as flipped | KJZZ, azfamily 2026-11-06 |
| Kotek reelected by 10 points (OR) | True: 53.4-43.7 (9.7) in KATU count; certification Dec 10 | KATU, OPB |
| Becerra elected to succeed Newsom (article spells "Beccera") | True; typo | AP via azfamily |
| CA voters rejected "billionaires' tax" | True, Prop 40 rejected, AP call Nov 10 | ABC7, KTVU |
| Benson elected to succeed Whitmer (MI) | True, about 53.7-44.6 early count | Michigan Advance, Fox |
| Lamont won third term; Dems expanded legislature (CT) | True (e.g. Senate 29-7 reported; one recount pending) | CT Mirror, WFSB |
| ALEC's Jonathan Williams praised the Prop 40 result | Not checkable here: could not locate the quote. ALEC and Williams do oppose wealth taxes | alec.org |
| Five lowest: SD, ND, NH, VT, NE; led by Republicans | Rates true, but Hawaii (2.8) ties Nebraska for 5th and is omitted; Hawaii has a Democratic governor (Green, from general knowledge, not rechecked). Republican governorship of the others not rechecked this session | BLS |

## Misleading
- "Kept in power" implies unemployment did not hurt incumbents. Democrats also flipped governorships in low-unemployment states: Georgia 3.3, Iowa 3.3, Ohio 3.4, Alaska 4.3, Texas 4.4, Nevada 4.7 (MultiState; AP). The result tracks a national Democratic year, not state jobless rates.
- Top and bottom five selected; the tie at the bottom is dropped. Arizona and Oregon are not statistically different from the US rate of 4.1 per BLS.
- Five states is n=5; state-level pattern is ecological and cannot show why voters voted. Rates are post-election-adjacent (Oct, preliminary).

## Confidence
High on rates and the main election results; medium on legislative counts (partial) and lowest-five partisanship; low on the Williams quote.
