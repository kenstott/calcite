Mostly accurate numbers, misleading frame: 2 Pinocchios

The article's unemployment rates and election results are almost all correct: CT 5.1, CA 5.0, MI 5.0, AZ 4.9, OR 4.9 (BLS, Oct 2026 preliminary; US 4.1). But it omits Hawaii (2.8, tied with Nebraska), and Democrats also flipped governorships in low-unemployment states (GA 3.3, IA 3.3, OH 3.4), so the "unemployment did not matter" implication is cherry-picked.

  • One quote (ALEC's Jonathan Williams) was not checkable here.
  • Legislative counts were partial; Hawaii governor and lowest-five partisanship not rechecked.
  • Five-state, state-level comparison is ecological and not causal.
