# Fact-check: White House, "President Trump Was Right: Tariffs and Tax Cuts Are Reshoring Manufacturing, Creating Jobs" (Nov 6, 2026)

## Overall grade: 3 Pinocchios

The headline jobs numbers are accurate and match BLS. The causal story built on them is not. Several manufacturing claims are false or misleading when checked against the BLS series, and the "tariffs and tax cuts are reshoring manufacturing" framing is not supported by the data cited.

**Method.** The whitehouse.gov page was fetched. The tool returned a paraphrase rather than verbatim text, so I found no attributed quotes beyond the release's own "crushed expectations" language. I checked claims against BLS, BEA/Census and BEA GDP pages, plus news aggregators where primary pages failed. Two checks hit access limits: the ISM report is behind a login, and the DOL weekly claims PDF could not be parsed. Both were checked through secondary sources only.

### What is materially misleading even where facts check out

- **Causal attribution.** The data cannot show that tariffs and tax cuts caused the gains. The release uses a single month and a partial-window baseline (since the December 2025 low). Over the full period since January 2025, manufacturing employment is essentially flat (+8k).
- **Baseline selection.** It compares a post-trough rebound against the 2023-24 decline and omits the 2021-23 gain of more than 700k, which came after the pandemic recovery.
- **Mislabeled statistics.** A year-to-date trade change is presented as a monthly one, and "strongest since 2023" is contradicted by two 2026 months.
- **Counter-evidence omitted.** Record capital goods imports and a headline manufacturing level (12,681k) still below January 2025 (12,673k) plus 2025's decline are not mentioned.

### Not checked or not checkable here
Claims 8, 12, 15 to 17, 19, and the wage and claims specifics. These are unverified, not false. I did not read the original reports the release cites for them.
