# Fact-check: ABC News, "With premiums on the rise, what to know as ACA coverage open enrollment begins" (Nov 2, 2026, Mary Kekatos)

## Verdict: 1 Pinocchio (mostly accurate, minor imprecision)

The ABC13 page was fetchable, but only as a summary. WebFetch declined to give verbatim text, so quotes are checked as paraphrased. The piece is by Mary Kekatos, dated Nov 2, 2026, and is syndicated across ABC stations. Secondary sources came from search snippets. The KFF and CMS primary pages were not opened directly (a guessed KFF URL returned 404), so confidence is medium-high rather than high.

## Context the piece may leave out (not errors)
- 13% is an average across plans. State-run marketplaces average about 16% and HealthCare.gov states about 11%.
- Insurers' proposed median rise was about 15%, higher than the 13% finalized.
- Subsidized enrollees' net cost depends on the credit formula, not the benchmark rise alone, so the headline premium number overstates what many people will pay.
- Effectuated enrollment is not comparable to plan selections. The CMS 2026 plan-selection total was 23.1M, down about 5%.

## Overall
The figures I could check match primary or near-primary sources. The weaknesses are the loosely labeled 19.2M enrollment figure, the rounded "3 million" drop, and the unchecked CBO 2035 horizon and state deadlines. Nothing is materially misleading.
