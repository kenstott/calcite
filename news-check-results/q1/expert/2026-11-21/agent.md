# Fact-check: OPB, Oregon school attendance (2026-11-19). Rating: 1 Pinocchio

**Answer:** Substantially accurate. 2024-25 baselines verified (OPB 2025-11-20: 9th grade on track 86.6%, chronic absence 33.5%). 2025-26 claims (~32% chronic, 69% regular, 86.8% on track, 36,512 to 36,200, 473 fewer 9th graders) are consistent but seen only in secondary coverage. The ODE At-A-Glance data was not accessed.
**Key values:** national ~21% vs published estimates 21.8% (RAND), 22.6% (AEI), 23% (39 states+DC, K-12 Dive); there is no official national figure, and the years and bases differ. 69% regular implies 31% non-regular against the stated 32% chronic, which is minor. Other sources give 64.5% and 70.6% for 2025-26 regular attendance, showing definitional variation.
**SB 817 (2021):** verified. It removed Class C citations and fines on parents. Wells's argument for reinstating consequences is opinion; Malheur County's reported results are anecdotal.
**Not checkable here:** the "more than 80% ten years ago" figure, the 11th and 12th grade rates, the 9th grade ~33%, and the Williams quote.

**Confidence:** medium.
