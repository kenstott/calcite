# Fact check: Q3 2026 AI lobbying records (aiweekly.co)

Rating: 1 Pinocchio. All figures verified at lda.gov: Nvidia $2.15M, Anthropic $2.11M, OpenAI $1.53M, Meta $5.88M (Q3 2026, in-house LD-2), each AI firm at a record vs 2025-26 quarters (Anthropic H1 $3.53M and OpenAI H1 $2.22M also reconcile).

Problems: "specifically backed" the CATS Act overstates (S.5105 is one of many bills listed; forms show no position); Semafor author is Ashley Gold (Oct 21), not Alexis Dufresne; headline "antitrust talks begin" overstated.

Not checkable here: Semafor claims on administration criticism and Lehane; Alphabet's Q3 figure not located. Outside-firm spend excluded from the totals.

Sources: lda.gov API; Semafor 10/21/2026; aiweekly.co pages. Confidence: high on figures, medium on the "backed" judgment.
