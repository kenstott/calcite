# Fact check: CNBC, "Homebuilder sentiment plunges to 14-year low..." (Nov 17, 2026)

## Verdict: 0 Pinocchios (accurate)
Every checkable claim matches NAHB's Nov 17, 2026 release. Caveat: CNBC text was read through a summarizing fetch tool, not verbatim.

## Claims
- HMI 25, lowest since April 2012: verified (NAHB; down 4 from 29). Confidence high.
- Year-ago HMI 38: verified (NAHB Nov 2025 = 38). High.
- Current sales 28 (-6), expectations 30 (-2), traffic 16 (-2, lowest in 15 yrs excl. 2020): verified. High.
- 46% cut prices, highest post-Covid (Nov 2025: 41%): verified. High.
- 68% used incentives, post-Covid high; 20th straight month at least 60%: verified. High.
- Regions (3-mo avg) NE 34, MW 35, S 27, W 23 with declines 4/5/3/3: verified. High.
- 30-yr 7.51% Monday per MND: verified (MND 11/16 = 7.51%; 11/17 = 7.48%). High.
- 6.38% a year earlier: plausible; secondary sources show MND/Mortgage Research 6.38% around 11/17/2025; MND archive page for that date not retrievable (404). Medium.
- Owens (immigration enforcement) and Dietz quote ("Mortgage rates above 7%...") : verified against NAHB. High. Fuel-price attribution appears in NAHB blog. Medium-high.

## Misleading flags
- Not misleading overall. Omitted: average price cut steady at 6%. MND rate is a daily lender series; Freddie Mac weekly was 7.11% on Nov 12, so "rates above 7%" is series-dependent.
- Not checkable here: none beyond exact year-ago MND daily value.

Sources: nahb.org press release 2026/11; eyeonhousing.org 2026/11 and 2025/11; mortgagenewsdaily.com/newsletter/n/20261116, 20261117; mortgageresearch.com Nov 17 2025.
