Weather claims

Fall-to-date mean (Sep 1 to Nov 15), MCI threaded record: 2026 = 65.99F. Warmer: 1963 (68.88), 1931 (67.14), 1956 (66.18), 1897 (66.15), so 5th on the article's date. Through Nov 24: 63.92F, behind only 1963 (66.96) and 1931 (65.51): 3rd. No year is within 0.1F. Record length 139 seasons matches the article. First 32F low was Nov 12; the 1931 record date (Nov 24, low 28F, Nov 23 low 36F) matches. Nov 12 is 12 days before the record, so 'not very far off' is generous. The 7 to 8 degree anomaly was not recomputed against normals.

Utility claims and framing

BPU and United Way homepages, plus the linked KCUR pages, contain none of the cited figures. $94.96 to $117.21 is +23.4%, matching the 23% usage rise, which implies no rate change. The article itself says November figures are preliminary (month half over), skips 2025 in the bill comparison, and BPU serves Wyandotte County, Kansas, while weather data is for the Missouri-side airport. The headline cause is not demonstrated: assistance requests fell (gas about 20%, electric 5.5%), and late-November cold would raise heating use.
