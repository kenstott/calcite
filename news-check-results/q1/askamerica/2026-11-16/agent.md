The CBS Face the Nation Nov. 15, 2026 transcript's claims about FAA funding, ICE deportations, measles and Social Security are balanced and mostly accurate; 2 claims could not be confirmed.

**Rating: 1 Pinocchio.**

Source: https://www.cbsnews.com/news/face-the-nation-full-transcript-11-15-2026/ (fetched successfully, along with the per-guest transcript pages). The report page is saved at news-check-results/q1/askamerica/2026-11-16/report.html.

**Verified**
- **ICE FY2026 deportations (central claim):** the transcript says "just over 483,000" and calls it a record. This is true. ICE's figure is 483,200, reported by several outlets. The warehouse table crime.dhs_repatriations_by_year peaks at 432,330 removals in FY2013 and stops at FY2023, so the record is consistent with it. Caveat: TRAC estimates only about 316,000 formal removals, so the counting basis is disputed.
- **GAO quote:** "the FAA is buying delivery speed at the risk of cost uncertainty and potential disruptions" is verbatim from GAO-26-107992 (Sept. 14, 2026).
- **"Insolvent in six years":** mostly true. The 2026 Trustees Report puts OASI depletion in 2032 and OASDI in 2034. This comes from the warehouse table's catalog description; my direct query on that table failed. I did not check the Medicare date.
- **19,000 ICE arrests per week:** mostly true. VisaVerge reports the verbal instruction, and DHS calls these goals, not quotas.
- **FAA ground stop:** a CBS politics-page headline corroborates it ("Federal officials received years of warnings before FAA ground stop"). I found no other coverage.

**Problems**
- **Rep. Himes: "$50 billion sent in the Biden administration" (mostly false).** In the transcript, $50 billion is Secretary Duffy's new request. The host cited $25 billion under Biden and $12.5 billion under Trump.
- **Himes: "1,300 employees of the FAA to resign or to retire" (unsupported).** Search summaries show about 1,200 DOT deferred resignations and 300-400 probationary firings, not 1,300 FAA departures. The Flying Magazine page was paywalled.
- **Brennan: "$25 billion in 2021 under Biden" (unsupported).** I found no source.
- **Measles elimination status "now official" lost (unsupported).** KFF reported in January 2026 that the loss was pending. I found no November confirmation, and the warehouse has no measure of PAHO status. NNDSS shows 3,297 measles cases year to date through week 37 of 2026, which fits a continuing outbreak.
- **Moolenaar labeled "(D-MI)" on the aggregate page:** a transcription error. The per-guest page correctly says Republican.
