The MS NOW (MaddowBlog) article's claims about October 2026 CPI, Trump's 'inflation is solved' remark and real wages are accurate.

Rating: 0 Pinocchios.

Checked claims:
- Trump said "The inflation is solved" in a Nov. 3 Fox phone interview: true (Roll Call transcript).
- Oct CPI +0.4%, worse than the 0.3% expected: true. Warehouse econ.fred_indicators CPIAUCSL 336.298 to 337.476 is +0.35%, which rounds to 0.4%. CNBC confirms the forecast.
- Core +0.3% vs 0.2% expected: true. CPILFESL 338.896 to 339.900 is +0.30%.
- No year-over-year figure because of the 2025 shutdown: mostly true. BLS published no all-items annual rate, but NYT computed about 4% itself (up from 3.7% in September).
- Trump repeatedly said "Prices are coming down" while prices rose: true. Roll Call has the Oct 12 gaggle; the CPI index rose every month Jun-Oct.
- Real average weekly earnings unchanged in October: mostly true. The quoted NYT sentence was not found verbatim in the fetched live-blog text (both NYT URLs fetched). The substance matches BLS real earnings, found via search only because bls.gov returned 403.
- Fed pressure to hike again: true (Waller, Logan, market pricing, per CNBC).

Caveats and misleading check: bls.gov returned HTTP 403, so the BLS release was not read directly. Nothing is materially misleading. The piece omits that NYT's own estimate puts annual inflation near 4%, which would strengthen its point. The framing is editorial but the facts hold.
