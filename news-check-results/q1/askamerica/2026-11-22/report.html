The Urban Milwaukee article's claims about Wisconsin utility rate hikes, ownership and residential electricity costs are mostly accurate.

EIA-861 via energy.eia_utility_annual (loaded 2012-2023); PSC order via WPR. Overall: 1 Pinocchio

Article under reviewhttps://urbanmilwaukee.com/2026/11/11/wisconsins-for-profit-electric-utilities-hike-rates-more-than-public-utilities/

Summary

1 Pinocchio. The article's numbers reproduce. In EIA-861 data (warehouse, 2012-2023), investor-owned utilities raised residential revenue per kWh 20.3% from 2020 to 2023 versus 6.7% for cooperatives and 10.9% for municipals; the article's 2020 base levels and customer shares match. 2024-2025 endpoints are not in the warehouse and were not verified. The framing is the weakness: it implies ownership drives the gap while scale, density, taxes and generation ownership are uncontrolled, and it omits that the PSC trimmed requested hikes (We Energies 3.9% and 3.5%, return cut 9.8% to 9.5%).

WITH w AS (SELECT * FROM energy.eia_utility_annual WHERE state_abbr='WI' AND report_year BETWEEN 2012 AND 2023 AND customers_residential>0 AND sales_residential_mwh>0 AND service_type='Bundled'), full_ AS (SELECT utility_id FROM w WHERE report_year IN (2020,2023) GROUP BY utility_id HAVING count(*)=2) SELECT report_year, entity_type, count(*) n, sum(customers_residential) cust, sum(revenue_residential_thousand)/sum(sales_residential_mwh) AS cents_per_kwh_x10 FROM w WHERE utility_id IN (SELECT utility_id FROM full_) AND report_year IN (2020,2021,2022,2023) GROUP BY report_year, entity_type ORDER BY entity_type, report_year
query — 14 rows — 651 ms
SELECT utility_name, entity_type, report_year, customers_residential, round(revenue_residential_thousand/sales_residential_mwh,4) AS usd_kwh, round(sales_residential_mwh*1000/customers_residential) kwh_per_cust FROM energy.eia_utility_annual WHERE state_abbr='WI' AND report_year IN (2020,2023) AND service_type='Bundled' AND (customers_residential>100000 OR utility_name ILIKE '%Cedarburg%' OR utility_name ILIKE '%Grafton%') ORDER BY utility_name, report_year
