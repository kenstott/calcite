The Breitbart article's claims about state unemployment rates and 2026 Democratic election wins are biased and mostly accurate.

BLS LAUS October 2026 (preliminary), warehouse econ.regional_employment, checked 2026-11-28; election results from news reports

Article under reviewhttps://www.breitbart.com/politics/2026/11/26/states-with-highest-unemployment-rates-kept-democrats-in-power/

Summary

The unemployment figures are accurate: Connecticut 5.1, California and Michigan 5.0, Arizona and Oregon 4.9 (BLS and the warehouse agree), and the Democratic wins are real. But the piece has one clear factual error (Becerra was never a U.S. Senator), drops Democratic-led Hawaii from the lowest-unemployment list where it ties Nebraska for fifth, and implies a causal story that five hand-picked states in one midterm cannot show. Findings

  • Highest five non-DC states are CT, CA, MI, AZ, OR: confirmed. DC (5.4) is higher but not a state. New Mexico (4.8, Democratic-led) is only 0.1 behind, on preliminary data.
  • Lowest five: SD 2.0, ND 2.2, NH 2.5, VT 2.6, then Hawaii and Nebraska tied at 2.8. The article omits Hawaii (Democratic governor).
  • Election outcomes confirmed by news reports; Arizona 'statewide offices' flip not verified.
  • False: Becerra described as 'former Senator' (he served in the U.S. House, as California AG and as HHS Secretary); name misspelled.
  • Whitmer/Newsom as 'far-left' is opinion. The Newsom clemency video quote was not found verbatim; 548 commutations were confirmed.

Misleading framing

The headline implies voters rewarded Democrats despite weak labor markets. Two of five races were open seats, the national midterm environment (which Lamont tied to President Trump) is a confound, and 0.1 to 0.2 point differences are within preliminary-estimate noise. Omitting Hawaii made the contrast with Republican-led low-unemployment states cleaner than the data.
