**The Breitbart article's claims about state unemployment rates and 2026 Democratic election wins are biased and mostly accurate.**

**Rating: 1 Pinocchio.**

Article: https://www.breitbart.com/politics/2026/11/26/states-with-highest-unemployment-rates-kept-democrats-in-power/ (fetched in full).

## Claim-by-claim
| Claim | Verdict | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Five highest-unemployment states are AZ, OR, CA, MI, CT (central) | True | Warehouse econ.regional_employment, Oct 2026 (prelim): CT 5.1, CA 5.0, MI 5.0, AZ 4.9, OR 4.9. Matches BLS LAUS page (fetched). |
| CT "highest" at 5.1 | Mostly true | DC is 5.4 but is not a state. |
| AZ and OR 4.9; CA and MI 5.0 | True | Warehouse and BLS. |
| Lowest five (SD, ND, NH, VT, NE) are Republican-led | Partially false | Hawaii (Democratic governor) ties Nebraska at 2.8 for fifth and is omitted. SD 2.0, ND 2.2, NH 2.5, VT 2.6. |
| Becerra is "former Senator" | Mostly false | He won, but he served in the U.S. House, as California attorney general and as HHS Secretary, never in the Senate. Name misspelled "Beccera". |
| Kotek reelected by 10 points | True | About 53.4% to 43.7% (OPB). |
| Hobbs reelected; Democrats flipped the Arizona legislature and statewide offices | True on governor and legislature | Hobbs about 55.7% to 42.5%. Democrats took both chambers (KTAR). The "statewide offices" flip was not verified. |
| Benson won Michigan | True | Benson beat John James. |
| Lamont third term; Democrats expanded the Connecticut legislature | True | WFSB. |
| California voters rejected the "billionaires' tax" | True | Prop 40 failed. |
| ALEC's Williams quote | True | Verbatim on alec.org (fetched). |
| Newsom clemency for 548 (video caption quote from Trump) | Not found verbatim | The 548 commutations were confirmed; the exact Trump quote was not located. |
| Whitmer and Newsom as "far-left" | Opinion | Not testable. |
| Headline: high unemployment states "kept Democrats in power" (causal) | Mostly false | Five hand-picked states in one election show no relationship. Two of the five races were open seats, and Lamont credited voter reaction to President Trump. Hawaii and New Mexico (4.8, Democratic-led) are also at the extremes or close to them. |

## Misleading framing
The headline implies voters rewarded Democrats despite weak labor markets. That is not shown. Differences of 0.1 to 0.2 points are within preliminary-estimate noise. Omitting Hawaii also made the contrast with Republican-led low-unemployment states look cleaner than the data.

## Limits
Election results come from news reports, not certified counts. No warehouse table covers elections, so the unemployment figures are the only warehouse-checked claims. The warehouse and BLS agree on every rate.
