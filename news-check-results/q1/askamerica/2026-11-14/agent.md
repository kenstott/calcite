The AI Weekly alert's claims about Q3 2026 lobbying spending by Nvidia, Anthropic, OpenAI and Meta are accurate.

Rating: 0 Pinocchios (engine-scored).

The alert is a short rewrite of a Semafor scoop (Oct 21, 2026), which I read in full. I checked each claim against law.lobbying_filings and law.lobbying_activities (LDA.gov LD-2 filings).

| Claim | Warehouse | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| Nvidia $2.2M, Anthropic $2.1M, OpenAI $1.5M in Q3 2026 | Nvidia $2.15M, Anthropic $2.11M, OpenAI $1.53M (in-house expenses) | True |
| Record highs for each | Prior quarterly highs since 2024: Nvidia $1.95M, Anthropic $1.97M, OpenAI $1.20M. I could not see pre-2024 quarters for Nvidia. | True |
| Meta led Big Tech at $5.9M | Meta $5.88M, Amazon $4.49M, Google $3.23M, Apple $3.09M | True |
| OpenAI listed AI-security information-sharing with antitrust protections | The OpenAI filing text reads "AI-security collaboration and information-sharing, including proposed antitrust protections" | True |
| Anthropic specifically backed the Collaboration on Adversarial Threats and Security Risks Act | The Anthropic filing lists S.5105 among the issues it lobbied on | Mostly true |

Caveat on the last claim: LD-2 forms list issues and bills but do not say whether the filer supports or opposes them. Semafor says so itself. The alert's "backed" overstates what the filing shows. It is a small wording problem and does not change the grade.

Other notes:
- The "$2.2M" for Nvidia is $2.15M rounded up.
- The alert's headline phrase "as Antitrust Talks Begin" is the aggregator's framing. The filings only show antitrust-related topics being lobbied on.
- Semafor's claim that OpenAI and Anthropic roughly doubled and tripled their spend in the first half could not be checked cleanly. The warehouse is missing some 2025 quarters for those clients. It does show Anthropic rising from $0.84M in Q4 2025 to $1.97M in Q2 2026.
- The "$7" and "2,000" figures in the page are newsletter promotion, not claims.
