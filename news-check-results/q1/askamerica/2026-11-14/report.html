Summary

The alert is a short rewrite of a Semafor scoop. Its figures match LDA.gov filings in the AskAmerica warehouse (Nvidia $2.15M, Anthropic $2.11M, OpenAI $1.53M, Meta $5.88M in Q3 2026 in-house expenses) and each is a company record. One flaw: it says Anthropic 'backed' the CATS Act, but the filing only lists the bill; Semafor itself says forms do not show support or opposition. Rating: 1 Pinocchio.
