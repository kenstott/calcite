Summary

The core facts hold: Argus jet fuel peaked at $4.88 in April and fell to $2.70 in June; fuel is roughly double last year at the autumn peak; Hopper's holiday fares are up 31% (Thanksgiving) and 23% (Christmas); BLS airline fares ran +22% to +23% y/y. Weaknesses: the late-October $4.90 figure is not independently confirmed (AP reported $4.30 on Sept 26 and $4.53 on Sept 17), 'highest in a decade' comes from Hopper alone, and the present-tense 'prices today are more than double' is stale because the Argus index was $3.47 on Nov 23. The 15% to 30% fuel share is below IATA's 2026 estimate of nearly one-third.
