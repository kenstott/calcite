Summary

The article's core USDA ERS figures for the September 2026 farm income forecast check out against this corpus's own ag.ers_farm_income table, which carries the identical vintage ('Data as of September 3, 2026'). 2026 net farm income of $158.4B, the 2.6% nominal and 5.5% real decline from 2025, the roughly 70% jump in government payments, and the 4.5% rise in production expenses to $492.8B all match the ERS data to within rounding. Why the February-forecast comparison is a genuine gap, not a red flag

ag.ers_farm_income is a snapshot table: for the 2026 data year it holds a single publication_date value, 'Data as of September 3, 2026,' meaning this corpus's ETL captured the latest ERS release rather than an archive of every successive forecast revision ERS publishes across the year (typically February, August/September, and November). Confirming the article's stated $5 billion upward revision from February would require either a stored prior-vintage snapshot or a fetch of USDA ERS's archived February 2026 release, neither of which is available through this connector. This is disclosed as an explicit gap rather than assumed to be true or false.
