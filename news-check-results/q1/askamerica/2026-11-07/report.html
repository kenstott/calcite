The ABC13/ABC News ACA open-enrollment explainer is accurate on premiums, insurer exits and enrollment, with one dating oddity

Checked 2026-11-07. Warehouse has no ACA marketplace premium or plan-selection table; claims checked against KFF primary pages fetched live.

Summary

Rating: 1 Pinocchio (mostly accurate). The core figures (13% benchmark silver increase, 42% over 2025-2027, 11 insurer exits, Cigna 11 states and 350,000+, 19.2 million enrolled, down about 3 million) match KFF and the HHS/ASPE figure KFF cites. Issues: the story says KFF published 'Friday' but is stamped Mon Nov 2 while KFF's page is dated Nov 6; the framing omits that these are benchmark premiums (not net costs) and that 7 insurers are entering.

Method and warehouse coverage

search_catalog found no table for marketplace premiums, plan selections or subsidies. Closest tables are census.sahie_insurance (2006-2023; national uninsured 25.5 million in 2023, 27.6 million in 2021, queried) and acs_health_insurance, which pre-date 2026 and cannot test these claims. Claims were verified against KFF pages (Nov 6 9-things brief, Oct 28 insurer tracker, Jul 28 enrollment brief) and healthinsurance.org. NILC returned HTTP 403, so the immigrant-eligibility list and the CBO 1 million figure were not independently opened.

Claim table

  • 13% average increase in finalized benchmark silver premiums; second straight double-digit year: TRUE (26% in 2025-26).
  • 42% rise 2025-2027: TRUE (1.26 x 1.13).
  • Most enrollees subsidized, not those above 400% FPL: TRUE.
  • 19.2 million enrolled early 2026, down about 3 million: TRUE. KFF reports 19.2M down from 21.8M (12%) and cites ASPE's 'nearly three million'; plan selections fell only about 1M, a different measure.
  • 11 carriers exiting at least one state as of Oct 28: TRUE; omits 7 entering.
  • Cigna exits all 11 states, 350,000+ people: TRUE.
  • Deadlines (Jan 15 most states; Idaho Dec 15; MA Jan 23; VA Jan 29): TRUE per search-confirmed sources; Connecticut dates not confirmed.
  • Reconciliation law immigrant eligibility; CBO 1 million more uninsured by 2035: not independently verified (NILC 403); attributed to KFF/CBO.
  • KFF analysis 'published Friday' in a piece stamped Nov 2: DISCREPANCY; KFF page is dated Nov 6.

Misleading-by-omission flags

Headline 'premiums on the rise' refers to benchmark silver premiums, not what subsidized enrollees pay; KFF notes subsidies cushion this, which the article mentions briefly.

What This Report Does Not Answer
