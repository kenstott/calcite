# Fact-check: ABC13/ABC News "With premiums on the rise, what to know as ACA coverage open enrollment begins" (Mary Kekatos, stamped Nov 2, 2026)

**Rating: 1 Pinocchio (mostly accurate).**

Warehouse: no table carries ACA marketplace premiums, plan selections or subsidies. Only census.sahie_insurance (2006-2023; uninsured 27.6M in 2021, 25.7M in 2022, 25.5M in 2023) and acs_health_insurance exist, which pre-date 2026. Claims were verified against KFF primary pages fetched live, plus healthinsurance.org.

| Claim | Verdict |
|---|---|
| Finalized benchmark silver premiums up 13% on average, second straight double-digit year | True (KFF: 13%; 26% in 2025-26) |
| Up 42% 2025 to 2027 | True (1.26 x 1.13) |
| Most enrollees subsidized, but not those above 400% FPL | True |
| 19.2M enrolled early 2026, down about 3M | True (KFF: 19.2M vs 21.8M, -12%; cites ASPE "nearly three million"; plan selections fell only ~1M, a different measure) |
| 11 carriers exiting at least one state as of Oct 28 | True, but omits that 7 are entering new states |
| Cigna exits all 11 states, 350,000+ people | True (350,000 is Q1 on-exchange enrollment) |
| Deadlines: Jan 15 most states; Idaho Dec 15; MA Jan 23; VA Jan 29 | True per healthinsurance.org-type sources; Connecticut dates unconfirmed |
| Reconciliation law restricts immigrant eligibility; CBO ~1M more uninsured by 2035 | Not independently verified (NILC fetch returned HTTP 403; CBO PDF not opened) |
| KFF analysis "published Friday" | Discrepancy: article stamped Mon Nov 2, KFF page dated Nov 6 (Friday). Likely updated after the stamp, or mis-dated. |

Misleading-by-omission: "premiums on the rise" refers to benchmark silver premiums, not net cost to subsidized enrollees (KFF: net payments rose 58% for 2026 sign-ups, a separate figure). Exits are shown without the seven entrants.
