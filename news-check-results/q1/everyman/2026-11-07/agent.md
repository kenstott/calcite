# Fact-check: ABC News, "With premiums on the rise, what to know as ACA coverage open enrollment begins" (Nov 2, 2026, Mary Kekatos)

**Overall grade: 1 Pinocchio out of 4 ("mostly accurate"). Possibly 0.** Every checkable number traces to KFF's pre-open-enrollment analysis or to CMS data that KFF cites. I found no outright errors. The only weakness is framing and omissions.

**Method.** The abc13 page fetched, but only as a summary. I could not get verbatim text, so I can't verify exact quote wording. The piece is an ABC News story by Mary Kekatos, syndicated across ABC stations under the same story ID. It appears to be a data-driven explainer with no attributed quotes beyond naming KFF, CMS and CBO. I checked it against KFF's "9 Things to Watch for the 2027 ACA Open Enrollment Period", other KFF pages, CMS and CBPP.

| Claim | Verdict | Source and confidence |
|---|---|---|
| Open enrollment starts Nov 1, 2026 in most states and ends Jan 15, 2027 | Accurate | KFF confirms the Nov 1 start in most states. The Jan 15 end date is the standard HealthCare.gov deadline, which I did not check separately. Medium-high. |
| State deadlines: CT, ID and MA started in October; ID closes Dec 15; MA Jan 23; VA Jan 29; CA, DC, NY and RI Jan 31 | Not checked here | I did not check state exchange sites. These are plausible and consistent with past years. Low. |
| KFF: benchmark silver premiums up an average 13% in 2027, the second straight double-digit rise | Accurate | KFF: 13% finalized (HealthCare.gov states 11%, state-based marketplaces 16%), after 26% in 2026. High. |
| About a 42% rise between 2025 and 2027 | Accurate | KFF: 26% in 2026 and 13% in 2027 give about 42% cumulative (1.26 x 1.13 = 1.42). High. |
| Enhanced premium tax credits expired this year | Accurate | They lapsed at the end of 2025. KFF names this as the main driver of the enrollment drop. High. |
| About 19.2 million enrolled in early 2026 | Accurate, with a wording caveat | KFF and CBPP both cite 19.2 million. This is effectuated enrollment (people who paid) as of February 2026. It is not plan selections, which were about 23 million. The piece's "enrolled" is fine, but readers may confuse the two measures. High. |
| About 3 million fewer than a year earlier | Roughly accurate | KFF: down 12%, or 2.6 million, from 21.8 million. CBPP: 22.1 million to 19.2 million, about 2.9 million. The baseline differs by source, so "roughly 3 million" is a slight round-up under KFF's number. Medium-high. |
| As of Oct 28, about 11 carriers will leave the marketplace in at least one state in 2027 | Accurate | KFF: 11 carriers as of Oct 29. Becker's counts 12. The one-day date difference and the 11-versus-12 spread reflect different counters. Medium-high. |
| Cigna exits all 11 states where it sells ACA plans, affecting more than 350,000 people | Accurate | KFF confirms the 11 states. Press reports give 369,000 lives. High. |
| Under the 2025 reconciliation law, only lawful permanent residents, Cuban and Haitian entrants and COFA residents remain eligible for tax credits; refugees, asylum applicants and others lose them | Accurate, with a nuance | KFF confirms the list of eligible groups. Eligibility to buy coverage is unchanged, so these groups lose subsidies, not access. The piece's "lose eligibility" is correct only if read as eligibility for tax credits. Medium-high. |
| CBO: about 1 million more uninsured by 2035 from this change | Accurate | KFF cites the CBO estimate. High. |

**Potentially misleading framing, none of it false:**
- **Sticker price versus net price.** Benchmark premium percentages are list prices. What enrollees pay depends on subsidies, and losing the enhanced credits raised net costs far more than the 13% suggests. Readers could take 13% as their expected increase.
- **Enrollment measure.** The 19.2 million figure is effectuated enrollment, while the usual headline numbers are plan selections of about 23 million. The piece does not spell out the difference.
- **Baseline for the 3 million drop.** The 2025 baseline varies by source, from 21.8 to 22.1 million.
- **Forward-looking omission.** CBO projects marketplace enrollment falling from a 2025 peak of 22.4 million to 13.1 million in 2027. I did not confirm whether the piece mentions this, and it would give useful context.

**Not checkable here:** exact quote wording, which would need the full text; the state-by-state deadlines; and CMS's own primary effectuated-enrollment report, which I only reached through KFF and CBPP.
