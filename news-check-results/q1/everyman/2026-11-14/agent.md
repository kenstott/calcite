# Fact-check: AI Weekly, "Nvidia, Anthropic and OpenAI Set Q3 Lobbying Records at $2.2M, $2.1M and $1.5M as Antitrust Talks Begin"

Piece: aiweekly.co/alerts/nvidia-anthropic-and-openai-set-q3-lobbying-records-at-22m-21m-and-15m-as (Alexis Dufresne, dated Oct 22, 2026; credits Semafor, 10/21/2026).

Limits of this check: I could not reach the Senate LDA database (HTTP 503) or the actual LD-2 forms, and fourweekmba.com returned 403. Everything below rests on secondary reporting of the filings (Semafor, The Verge via Digg, TheNextWeb, a fourweekmba snippet) plus Senate bill pages. I did not see the aiweekly page's full text, only a summary of it from the fetch tool. I have no independent primary confirmation of any dollar figure.

## Materially misleading or sloppy points
- The URL slug says 22m/21m/15m while the headline says $2.2M/$2.1M/$1.5M. Slug is a ten-fold error; the headline is right.
- "Backed" the bill: the filing only shows Anthropic lobbied on it.
- Sourcing: it credits Semafor, but Semafor gave no OpenAI or Anthropic dollar totals; those come from The Verge. Minor attribution gap.
- Publication date (Oct 22) differs from Semafor's 10/21; harmless.
- Other outlets disagree on the combined total (Gadget Review $7.5M vs $5.8M, which is the sum of the three). I could not reconcile Gadget Review.

## Rating
Mostly accurate on numbers; the stance claim and headline framing are overstated. **1 Pinocchio** (some shading, facts basically right). Would drop to 0 if "backed" were "lobbied on" and the antitrust-talks framing removed.
