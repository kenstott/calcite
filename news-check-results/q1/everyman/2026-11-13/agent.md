## Grade: 0 Pinocchios (The Geppetto Checkmark)

Every checkable factual claim matches primary data (BLS), and the piece explains why the annual rate is missing.

## Possibly misleading context

- The piece leans on monthly data because the BLS annual figure is missing. The last published annual rate was reportedly about 3.7%, up from about 3% at the start of Trump's term. Per the summary, the piece does not give this, which would be the strongest rebuttal of "solved". The omission makes it weaker, not wrong.
- "Worse than expected" is a market-consensus judgment. The misses were small (0.4 vs a slightly lower forecast; core 0.3 vs 0.2).
- Real weekly earnings "unchanged" depends on rounding. One source gave about +0.02% for the month, and the previous month was revised.
- Monthly figures do not on their own show that inflation is not "solved". The piece's real rebuttal is the level and trend.
