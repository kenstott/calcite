# Fact-check: Star-Advertiser, "State jobless rate holds at 2-year high of 2.8%" (2026-11-23)

## Limitation
The article itself returned HTTP 403 to WebFetch, and no cached copy was found. I could not read its text, so I cannot extract its actual claims or quotes. Every claim below is inferred from the headline and from a secondary source. Per-claim grading of the article's text is NOT CHECKABLE HERE.

## What I could verify (secondary source: Maui Now, 2026-11-19, citing Hawaii DBEDT)
- Hawaii seasonally adjusted unemployment rate for October 2026 was 2.8%, unchanged from September. Supports the headline word "holds".
- Counts: 677,800 employed, 19,650 unemployed, labor force 697,450.
- US rate fell from 4.2% to 4.1%.
- Counties (not seasonally adjusted): Honolulu 2.6%, Hawaii County 3.0% (unchanged), Kauai 4.5%, Maui County 2.8% (down from 3.1%).
- Nonfarm payrolls +1,000 from September; year-over-year unchanged. October 2025 comparison data was unavailable because of the federal shutdown.

## "2-year high"
- A Star-Advertiser piece of 2026-10-23 on September data said 2.8% was the highest since September 2024. Since October is also 2.8%, "holds at a 2-year high" is consistent. Not checked against the BLS LAUS or DBEDT primary releases, which I did not reach. Secondary evidence only.

## Not verified
- Any attributed quotes (e.g., economist Tian's forecast that the rate will keep rising), and any other figures in the article. I have not seen the text.
- Primary sources (BLS LAUS, DBEDT release) were not fetched directly.

## Possible misleading context (unconfirmed without the text)
- The year-over-year comparison is missing for October 2025 because of the shutdown. An article claiming trends against last year should say so.
- The seasonally adjusted and unadjusted rates differ (unadjusted rate cited as 2.8% vs 3.2% in September), so rate-based claims should specify which one.
