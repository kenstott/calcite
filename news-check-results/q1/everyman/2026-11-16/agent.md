## Quotes
Himes: "obligated is a fancy D.C. word for spent"; Cassidy on children dying of measles; RFK Jr.: "The Senate no longer has any leverage over me." These are attributed in the transcript itself. I did not verify them independently.

## Overall rating: 2 Pinocchios (provisional, low confidence)
The piece is a transcript, so most statements are guests' opinions or CBS reporter framing. Findings: one party mislabel, one figure (Megaspeed $12B) that conflicts with available reporting, and one record deportation figure that could not be confirmed. Roughly half of the claims could not be checked.

## Materially misleading flags
- The Megaspeed chip volume of $12B is far above the figures reported elsewhere.
- Mixing "removals" and "deportations" with different counting methods can overstate the record.
- Himes and Moolenaar present chip-smuggling suspicions as open questions. Both are careful, and Alice Huang's alleged spy role is unproven.

## Limitations
The WebFetch tool summarized the page rather than giving the full text, so claims may be missed. Several claims remain not checkable here only because of time and tool limits, not because the data is inaccessible.
