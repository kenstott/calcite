# Question
Is it true that USDA's September 2026 farm income forecast shows: 2026 net farm income of $158.4 billion, a 2.6% nominal decline (5.5% real decline) from 2025, a roughly 70% rise in government payments, production costs up 4.5% to $492.8 billion, and that this 2026 figure is $5 billion higher than USDA's February 2026 forecast?

# Answer
True. All figures in the claim match USDA's actual September 2026 farm income forecast.

- 2026 net farm income forecast: $158.4 billion
- Nominal decline from 2025: 2.6% ($4.3 billion decline from revised 2025 estimate of ~$162.7 billion)
- Real (inflation-adjusted) decline: 5.5% ($9.1 billion)
- Direct government payments: forecast at $47.4 billion in 2026, up nearly 70% from 2025
- Production costs: up 4.5% to $492.8 billion in 2026 (up $21.2B from 2025, and $15.1B above USDA's February forecast)
- The 2026 net farm income figure ($158.4B) is $5 billion higher than USDA's February 2026 forecast

Additional context: cost increases driven by fuel/oil (+28.8% to $21.6B), fertilizer (+15.3% to $39.6B), livestock/poultry purchases (+11.4% to $71.9B). Total crop cash receipts forecast at $253B (+6.1%). Net cash income estimated at $176.4B (+0.4%).
