## Overall rating

1 Pinocchio equivalent is too harsh; this is a transcript of interviews. The anchor's factual framing checks out. Rating: **0 Pinocchios (accurate)** for the checked material. Nothing found contradicts the transcript.

## Possible misleading context

- Trump's 31-32% approval is at the low end of polls; averages are around 42-43%. A guest cited it without that context.
- Many items are guests' opinions presented live and are not fact-checked in the transcript itself (for example that the vote was a mandate for affordability over impeachment).
- House total was still unsettled (19 races uncalled), so "up to 259" is an estimate.
