# Fact-check: Breitbart, "States with Highest Unemployment Rates Kept Democrats in Power" (Matt Mercer, 2026-11-26)

Method: WebFetch returned only a summary of the article, not verbatim text. Claims below come from that summary. Checked against the BLS state ranking page (October 2026, preliminary) and news coverage of the November 3, 2026 election. No charts or dashboard were produced.

## Misleading framing

- **Headline implies cause.** It says high unemployment "kept Democrats in power." Voters in these states are not shown to have ignored the economy. Re-election results are also driven by national mood, candidates and incumbency, and 2026 was a broad Democratic midterm. AP reports Democrats also won governorships in Texas and other Republican-held states.
- **Cherry-picked comparison.** The omission of Hawaii (claim 11) removes a Democratic state from the low-unemployment group.
- **Small gaps.** The top five run from 4.9% to 5.1%, and New Mexico is at 4.8%. Preliminary monthly rates have margins of error larger than these gaps. The U.S. rate was 4.2% in September.
- **Ignores partisan mix elsewhere.** Nevada and Washington (4.7%), Minnesota, Delaware and Kentucky (4.6%) are also high. Alaska and Florida (4.3%), Texas (4.4%), Kansas and Louisiana (3.9%) are Republican-led, so the pattern is weaker than the article implies. Many of these governors were not on the ballot, so I did not check their parties against sources.
- **Wording.** "Kept in power" is accurate for AZ, OR, CA, MI and CT governors. Arizona's legislature was flipped, not kept.

## Rating: 2 Pinocchios (Washington Post scale)

The numbers are right, but the piece leaves out Hawaii, gets Becerra's résumé wrong, and implies a causal story that its own data does not support.
