## Rating: 1 Pinocchio (provisional)
The verifiable context is accurate, and the numbers are internally coherent. The core data claim is unverified by me.

## Materially misleading or missing context
- Correlation presented as ownership effect. The comparison is raw averages and does not control for size, density, generation mix, transmission, or fuel costs. The article's "hike more" headline implies the cause is for-profit ownership.
- Percent versus dollar framing. Percent increases starting from lower municipal bases understate how large the gap is in dollars. Dollar increases (e.g. $391 vs $128) are the fairer comparison.
- Sample is 65 utilities that reported all 25 years, which is a survivorship filter. Customers are 86.6% IOU, so averages skew toward the big IOUs. The author says that including small non-reporters does not change conclusions, and I could not check that.
- Municipal and co-op utilities differ in taxes (no corporate income tax) and wholesale power purchasing. Neither is mentioned in the piece's headline framing.
- It is labelled "Analysis", so it is opinion-adjacent, but the headline reads as a plain factual finding.
