# Fact-check: Texas Tribune, "Parents of students with disabilities fear losing Texas' 'life-changing' school vouchers" (Nov. 13, 2026)

Limits: WebFetch returned only a summarized version of the article, not verbatim text, so attributed quotes could not be checked word for word. Verification used search snippets, not full primary documents.

## Claim ratings

| Claim (as summarized) | Verdict | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Hinojosa is the incoming governor | Supported | KUT (Nov. 3, 2026) and Fox 4 report Hinojosa defeated Abbott. |

## Overall rating: 1 Pinocchio (leaning accurate, with unresolved items)

The political framing and program amounts are broadly supported. Two specifics need correction or sourcing: the 18,800 count, which conflicts with the 20,700 figure in other reporting, and the audit attribution to Eckhardt.

## Potentially misleading framing

- The headline and framing center on risk to disability families. Disability-account students are a minority of the roughly 85,000 to 100,000 funded accounts, and the Senate is Republican-controlled. Repeal is therefore far from certain.
- The $227 million loss figure is a critics' estimate and should be read as such.
- If the audit was credited to Eckhardt, the existing Huffines-announced audit should be disclosed.
