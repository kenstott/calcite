## Caveats a fair report should carry
- Monthly payroll figures are noisy and get revised. The 12-month average of 48,000 is much weaker than this month's 165,000, and September's revised +60,000 is also modest. A report that presents one strong month as a trend would be misleading.
- The unemployment rate dropped one tenth of a point. That is within the survey's margin of error.

No error found in any figure I could verify, and I found no evidence of a misleading framing. Because I could not read the article, I cannot grade its own wording, quotes or framing.
