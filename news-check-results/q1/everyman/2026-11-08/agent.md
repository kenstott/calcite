# Fact-check: White House, "President Trump Was Right: Tariffs and Tax Cuts Are Reshoring Manufacturing, Creating Jobs" (Nov 6, 2026)

## Overall grade: 3 Pinocchios (Mostly False framing; the headline numbers are mostly accurate)

The release's main economic figures check out against primary sources. They are the October payrolls, the BLS manufacturing gain, the revisions, the ISM streak, Q3 GDP and the trade-deficit change. The misleading part is the causal story. It presents one month's data, and a 101,000 manufacturing rebound measured from a low, as proof that tariffs and tax cuts are reshoring manufacturing. I did not find data supporting that causal claim.

Limits on what I checked:
- WebFetch returned a condensed paraphrase of the White House page, not verbatim text. The release's quotes, if any, were not checkable here.
- I did not open the Reshoring Initiative survey, the Reuters piece or the FRED series.
- I could not retrieve a "since January 2025" manufacturing figure.
- I could not check the claim of 1.25 million private jobs since January 2025.

## Materially misleading even where the facts check out
1. **Causation.** The October jobs report, ISM, GDP and trade data cannot show that tariffs and tax cuts caused reshoring. The piece cites no counterfactual.
2. **Baseline.** Measuring manufacturing from the December 2025 low, and calling it "since returning to office," hides earlier 2025 losses.
3. **Trade deficit.** A year-to-date decline is presented as a September drop. The monthly deficit barely moved, and record imports are read as success.
4. **Record imports.** A record capital goods import share is presented as reshoring evidence. It could equally reflect imported inputs, which tariffs raise the cost of.
5. **Single data points.** The piece leans on one month's jobs report, a sampled survey and an unconfirmed jobless-claims "low."
6. **Source mix.** Several citations are secondary or partisan (Breitbart, the White House's own releases) rather than agency data.
