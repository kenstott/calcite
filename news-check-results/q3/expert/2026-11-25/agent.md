# Fact check: Newsweek, adults living with parents (2 Pinocchios)

**Answer:** Mostly accurate, but "record share" is wrong. 25.2M (33%) of ages 18-34 lived with parents in 2025; Realtor.com's peak share was 33.6% in 2020. Only the count is a record.
**Key values:** NAR median first-time buyer age 40 (Jul 2024-Jun 2025): confirmed. Freddie Mac 30-yr 7.28% (Nov 19, 2026): "above 7%" confirmed.
**Not checkable here:** metro shares, metro/regional changes, home-price rises, 16-year down payment, expert quotes (Chan, Wachter, Jones).
**Sources:** Newsweek URL; MBA NewsLink summary of Realtor.com; NAR press release; Freddie Mac PMMS.
**Confidence:** medium on confirmed items (secondary reporting), low on the rest.
