Fact-check: ICN COP31 warnings piece

Mostly accurate: 1 Pinocchio

The headline numbers match primary sources: Climate Action Tracker 2.7C (about 4.9F) under current policies and 1.9C (3.4F) optimistic by 2100; Copernicus's 1.5C trend date near 2029. Caveats: the article was read only via a summarizing tool, so quotes could not be compared verbatim; the Thunberg "life and death" wording was not found in coverage; the Copernicus 2029 date is a trend extrapolation, not a forecast, and its page gives inconsistent dates (Jan vs May 2029; 2042 vs 2045 for 2015). Turkey hosts while Australia presides over negotiations. Höhne and Hare quotes are not checkable here.

Claims

  • CAT 5F / 3.4F: verified.
  • Copernicus 2029 vs 2045: verified with caveats.
  • GHG main driver: verified.
  • Thunberg first COP since 2021: verified; quote wording unverified.
  • Heck "fifth consecutive year": consistent with CAT.
  • von der Leyen omission: supported by Dawn/AFP.
  • Hare, Hohne: not checkable here.
