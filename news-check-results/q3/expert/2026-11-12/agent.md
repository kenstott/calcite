# Fact-check: "Interest on the debt cost $1.4 trillion last year, audit finds"

## Summary: Mostly accurate, 1 Pinocchio
**Answer:** The $1.4T headline is true as a gross figure: Treasury FY2026 interest expense = $1,360.4B (public issues 1,068.5B + intragovernmental 291.9B); GAO-27-108795 (2026-11-09) reports $1.4T. Framing issue: ~$292B is interest the government pays itself; Treasury MTS net interest is $1,051.8B. The piece discloses the split.
**Key Values:** Debt $40,171.8B at 2026-09-30 (+$2,534B vs 37,637.6B; 2.05x the 19,573.4B of 2016). Public-held interest FY2021 389.0B (GAO ~392B). CBO deficit $2.0T. CBO Feb baseline $24.4T over 2027-36 (~$2.44T/yr). P.L. 119-103 CR through Dec 11. Limit $41.1T (OBBBA, July 2025). Eight suspensions since 2013.
**Unverified:** CBO net interest $1.14T (CBO PDF unreadable; only +$115B/11% and rank behind Social Security confirmed). Dickerson's Feb 2027 / Nov 2027 dates conflict with NTU's Aug 2026 piece (May 2027; X-date Feb 2028). Dickerson/Ditch quotes and Treasury non-response: not checkable here.
**Sources:** Treasury Fiscal Data API (debt_to_penny, interest_expense, MTS tables 5/9); GAO-27-108795; GAO-26-107908; CBO MBR FY2026; AAF; CRFB; NTU; CRS R49353, IN11829.
**Confidence:** High on Treasury/GAO figures; medium on CBO claims; low on debt-limit dates and quotes.
