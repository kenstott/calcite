# Fact-check: Blaze gas prices 'myth' piece

**Rating: 1 Pinocchio.**

- Gas fell after election (GasBuddy $4.24 Oct 26, $4.12, $3.96, $3.74) -> **Supported**. EIA weekly 4.269/4.153/3.995/3.805 (Nov 16); GasBuddy runs 3-6 cents lower, consistent
- AAA $4.29 a month earlier to $3.72 Thursday -> **Supported**. AAA site: $4.2926 month ago; $3.6366 on Nov 23; $3.91 Nov 12. Thursday Nov 19 value not retrieved
- Prices still >70 cents above pre-strike -> **Supported**. EIA 3.015 week of Mar 2; 3.72-3.02 = 0.70
- NBC exit poll: 87% concerned about gas/diesel -> **Supported (secondary copies)**. Original NBC page not opened
- 59% very concerned; 67% of them voted Democratic -> **Unverified/possibly misattributed**. Search tied 59% to ABC News Voter Poll; AP found very-concerned voters favored Democrats overwhelmingly
- Trump: oil 'dropped like a rock' day after election -> **Not confirmed verbatim**. Exact phrase not found; WTI -8.3% to $88.12 on Wednesday per Latin Times
- De Haan: 'a myth'; fell 30 days pre-election every year since 2006 except 2010 -> **Quote supported, stat not independently rechecked**. Same quote appears in earlier GasBuddy statements
- ~20 million b/d through Hormuz in 2025 -> **Supported**. IEA ~20 mb/d 2025; EIA 20.9 in H1 2025
- Hormuz.now 8.3 mb/d last week -> **Not checkable here**. Source not accessed
- Winter blend switch; falling in all states; 12 states >25 cents -> **Plausible; state counts not checked**. Winter blend is standard seasonal practice
- White House blamed Iran -> **Plausible**. Vance publicly blamed Iran attacks on shipping; Nov statement not found

**Answer:** price figures are accurate (EIA/AAA); seasonal explanation sound; 'myth' framing is too absolute since WTI fell 8.3% the day after the election with analysts citing Iran policy. 59%/67% poll figures and Trump quote unverified.

**Key Values:** EIA 4.478 (9/21) -> 3.805 (11/16); AAA 4.2926 -> 3.6366 (Nov 23).
