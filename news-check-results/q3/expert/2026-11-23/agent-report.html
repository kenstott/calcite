Fact-check: The Blaze, "That's a myth" (Nov 19 2026)

Mostly accurate numbers, but "myth" overstates: 1 Pinocchio

The retail figures check out against EIA and AAA: regular gas fell from $4.478 (EIA, week of Sep 21) to $3.805 (Nov 16), and AAA shows $4.29 a month ago and $3.64 on Nov 23. The seasonal explanation is sound because the decline began six weeks before the vote.

  • Misleading framing: crude oil fell about 8.3% (WTI to $88.12) the day after the midterms, and the WSJ and others tied it to Trump downplaying more Iran strikes and diplomacy hopes after the result. So the election plausibly had an indirect effect on crude, and a flat "myth" is too strong. Retail effect lags crude.
  • Unverified: 59%/67% exit-poll figures (59% appears to be ABC's poll), the Trump "dropped like a rock" wording, Hormuz.now 8.3 mb/d (not checkable here), 12-states stat, De Haan's 2006-2010 record.
  • Would change the grade: a primary NBC poll showing the 59/67 numbers, or evidence the Trump quote is real, moves it toward 0-1.
