Newsweek mortgage-free map piece: mostly accurate, 1 Pinocchio

Findings: numbers hold, but the headline figures could not be tied to a source I could open

1 Pinocchio (some shading). Every figure I could pull from Census, Freddie Mac and NAR matches or sits within about 1-2 points of the article. The national outright share is 39.4% for 2020-24 (ACS 5-yr, n=86.6M owner-occupied units) and 40.3% in ACS 2024 1-yr; 34.4% in 2010-14 is confirmed. Freddie Mac's 7.11%, 7.18% and 6.24% are exact.

  • The state percentages (WV 56.6, MS 52.7, NM 50; DC 26.5, MD 30.6, CO 31.6) come from the 2025 ACS 1-year via Realtor.com. I could not retrieve it (the Census API now demands a key; I did not use one). Older vintages give WV 55.1 (1-yr 2024) and 53.9 (5-yr), same ranking, so the claim is plausible but ungraded.
  • The 3.75M home-sales figure is real (NAR, October 2026, lowest since August 2010) but is credited to Realtor.com.
  • Listing prices, the WV age-cohort decline and the Xu quote were not checkable here.
  • Misleading context: the headline promises a map absent from the text; the claim that mortgage-free owners "deepen the housing freeze" is conjecture; state rankings are sampled survey estimates (a gap of 1 point is within error) and ecological, not about individuals; the 5-yr and 1-yr vintages are mixed in one narrative.

What would change the verdict: the 2025 ACS 1-year tables showing state values materially off the article's, or Realtor.com data contradicting the prices.
