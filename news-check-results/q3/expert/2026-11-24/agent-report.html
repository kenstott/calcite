Mostly accurate narrative, but the headline rank claims do not match official unadjusted figures: 2 Pinocchios

Alabama's 2024 grade 4 NAEP reading score was 213 vs 214 nationally (1 point, not significantly different); the article quotes a 2-point gap and a 13th rank, while the state's own figure is 34th (49th in 2019). The 13th and 18th ranks are probably poverty-adjusted or another metric; unconfirmed.

  • Verified: 213/214, flat vs 2022 (213) (NCES snapshot).
  • Verified: ARI funding $58M (2013) to $40.7M (2018); exemptions for English learners and IEP students.
  • Not verified: $152M, $85,000 per school, ~1,000 literacy staff, 2015 rank 45th, 2019 rank 18th, fewer than 150 districts vs ~400 in Massachusetts.
  • Misleading: no gain in 2022-2024 scores; unadjusted rank gains are much smaller than 13th.
  • Limitation: article read through a summarizing fetch tool; Fordham report not read; quotes (Loewus) not checked against the report.
