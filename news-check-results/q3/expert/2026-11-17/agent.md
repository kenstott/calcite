# Fact check: Texas maternal death rate article (SA Current / Texas Tribune)

**Rating: 1 Pinocchio.** 38 claims: 26 verified, 7 accurate but shaded, 2 wrong, 3 not checkable here.

Wrong: SMM "80.4 to 83.5" (report: 83.9 per 100,000 delivery hospitalizations, 2023 to 2024); "last year's report" (prior report was Sept 2024). Shaded: "slightly above 2019" (2019 MMR 17.2, 2024 20.5 = +19%); ban "in 2021" (near-total ban 2022); Sept 1 deadline (2025 law allows Dec 1); delay motive stated as fact; Republicans "in the minority" (Senate remains R); "nearly doubled"; "new" recommendations. Misleading by omission: mental health "leading cause" is 12 of 59 sampled deaths vs 9 hemorrhage; 30 of 33 2021 infection deaths were COVID-19; contributing-factor "doubling" partly reflects data capture (nicotine info recorded for 22/60 in 2021 vs 57/59 in 2024).

## Claim table
1. [Verified] Report released Friday (Nov 13, 2026) -- DSHS PDF created/modified 13 Nov 2026 (Friday); server Last-Modified 13 Nov 2026 19:04 GMT.
2. [Accurate but shaded] Report was "supposed to" be released Sept. 1 -- Sept 1 is the historic date under Health & Safety Code 34.015, but a 2025 law (HB 4666) lets health agencies file by Dec 1; DSHS said Dec 1 (Tribune 10 Nov, 25 Sep). The report came out ahead of the Dec 1 date.
3. [Accurate but shaded] Release "delayed until after the midterm elections" -- Timing true (released 13 Nov, election 3 Nov). Motive is disputed; the article does report the agency denial and the 2025 law, but states the delay as fact in the lede and deck.
4. [Accurate but shaded] First comprehensive look since the state "banned nearly all abortions in 2021" -- SB 8 (Sept 2021) was a roughly six-week ban; the near-total ban took effect in 2022. The Tribune's own 10 Nov story says 2022.
5. [Verified] Study covers 2021 and 2024 deaths, skipping 2022-2023 case reviews -- Report pp. 5-6: 2021 and 2024 cohorts reviewed; 2022-2023 ratios computed but not case-reviewed.
6. [Verified] 2024 maternal mortality ratio 20.5 per 100,000 births -- Report p. 8: 20.5 per 100,000 live births (enhanced MMR, deaths to 42 days postpartum).
7. [Verified] 2021 ratio 23.5 excluding COVID-19 deaths -- Report p. 8: 23.5 excluding COVID (39.1 including; the 2024 report had 23.0 / 37.7, so 2021 was revised upward).
8. [Accurate but shaded] 2024 rate is "slightly above" 2019 and "pre-pandemic levels" -- 2019 MMR was 17.2 (2024 report; 2026 report gives 2018-2019 as 17.0-17.2 within a 17.0-20.7 range). 20.5 is +19%, though inside the 2013-2017 range. The report itself says "similar".
