# Fact check: Fox 9 Twin Cities Sep-Oct 2026

Rating: 0 Pinocchios (accurate).

## Claims
- 2026 second-warmest Sep-Oct at 62.4 F: Verified. Answer 62.39 F (IEM/NWS threaded MSP record, mean of daily (high+low)/2, 61 days). Confidence high.
- 2024 warmest at 63.8 F: Verified, 63.80 F. High.
- Five of past six years top 10, incl. 2021, 2023, 2025: Verified. Ranks 2021=7, 2022=24, 2023=9, 2024=1, 2025=5, 2026=2. High.
- Records since 1872: not verified to 1872; complete years in source begin 1873. Medium.
- Matz: warming at least two decades: supported (2001-2025 mean 57.6 F vs 1976-2000 54.7 F; about +2 F/decade since 2001, n=26). Medium-high.
- Matz: warm falls may feel normal: opinion, not checkable. Paraphrase only, no direct quotes.

## Misleading?
No. Single-station, two-month ranking; no causal claim. NWS monthly-mean method may differ in second decimal.
