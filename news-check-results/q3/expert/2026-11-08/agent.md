# Fact-check: AAA, "National Average Falls 12 Cents in One Week" (Nov 5, 2026)

**Overall grade: 1 Pinocchio (minor imprecision), close to 0.** I checked every figure I could reach and none was wrong. I could not verify the state-by-state rankings, the EV charging figures, or the "about 4% below the five-year average" claim for crude stocks.

The article is AAA's weekly gas-price release, https://gasprices.aaa.com/national-average-falls-12-cents-in-one-week/. The fetch worked. It contains no attributed quotes, so nothing was graded on quotes. The fetch tool returned only a summary of the page, not the verbatim text, so I could not read the exact wording of any sentence. That includes the "dipped back into the high $80 to low $90 range" sentence below.

**Misleading or confusing points**
- **Date mismatch.** The article body is priced as of 11/5 ($4.10). The page header carries the later 11/8 average ($4.0203). A reader can see two different "current" numbers on one page. This is a presentation flaw, not an error.
- **Timing of the EIA data.** The EIA figures are for the week ending 10/30, but the oil settle is from 11/4. The two sets cover different dates, though the article labels each one correctly.
- **No causal claim.** The release ties the price decline to falling crude but gives no analysis. The "high $80 to low $90" description is loose, because crude fell about 8% in a single day.
- **Everything but the EIA and oil figures is AAA's own data.** Those figures have no outside check, which is normal for this series.
