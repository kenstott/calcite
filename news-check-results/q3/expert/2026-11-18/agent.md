# Nashville crime-down-17% fact check: 1 Pinocchio

- **Crime down 17% this year, all precincts** — Partly verified. MNPD H1 Part I -19.2% (14,686 vs 18,186); mayor's office reported -16% in Sept. YTD Nov figure and per-precinct data not independently accessible.
- **North Nashville crime down 13%** — Not checkable here. No precinct-level 2025 baseline published in accessible sources.
- **Auto thefts down 30%** — Inconsistent. Mayor's office: -37% H1; WSMV Sept: -34%; MNPD Q1: -39.9%. 30% is lower than all other figures, so direction holds.
- **Sept: crime -16%, homicides -44%** — Verified as mayor's-office claim. Nashville.gov three-year page; Fox17 H1 report. Homicide counts not independently pulled.
- **Survey: 70% safe, up from 67%; crime-increasing 58% to 49%; n=1,000, 12 neighborhoods, Sept 8-15** — Partly verified. WSMV confirms survey, n=1,000, nearly half think crime rising. Percentages found only in Fox17. No MOE or questionnaire located.
- **North Nashville/Bordeaux safe 60% to 76%** — Not checkable here. Subgroup figure only from mayor citing Alliance; subsample size unknown.

MNPD H1 2026 Part I offenses 14,686 vs 18,186 (-19.2%). Confidence: medium. Survey +3 pts with no published MOE. Full report: agent-report.html
