Nashville 'crime down 17%': mostly supported, but sourced to the mayor's office (1 Pinocchio)

Summary of findings

Rating: 1 Pinocchio. The direction and rough size hold: MNPD's own Jan-Jun 2026 Part I offenses fell 19.2% (14,686 vs 18,186), and homicides, robberies and vehicle thefts fell in the mayor's office tallies. The 17% year-to-date figure and the precinct breakdowns could not be verified from primary data.

  • Auto theft figure (30%) differs from other 2026 figures (34-40%).
  • Misleading context: the 70% vs 67% safety rise is 3 points on n=1,000 with no published margin of error, so it is probably within noise. The survey was commissioned by an advocacy nonprofit, and the same survey shows nearly half still think crime is rising. MNPD calls its crime-analysis reports preliminary, not official totals.
  • The article presents the mayor's numbers as fact without independent data.
