# Fact-check: Fox Business, Amtrak record ridership (2026-11-26)

**Grade: 1 Pinocchio.** All checkable figures hold; the framing implies gas prices caused the record without evidence beyond the CEO's belief.

## Verified
- **Answer:** 37.1M trips (37,067,685), +7.4% vs 34,528,695; third straight record. Revenue $4.1B +4.9%. Adjusted operating earnings -$492.7M, +18.9% (unaudited). Sources: Amtrak Media FY26 page, CNBC, american-rails.com. Confidence: high.
- **Quotes:** Herrmann quotes match CNBC/Reuters. Berg/Hopper statements match Fox Business Nov 20. Confidence: high.
- Acela replacement began Aug 2025; $22B IIJA over FY22-26 correct. Confidence: high.

## Misleading / imprecise
- Causation rests on Herrmann's quote; Amtrak also cites infrastructure, new trainsets, World Cup, 250th; growth led by state-supported routes (+9.5%). Iran war began late Feb 2026, so high prices covered ~7 of 12 months. Gas now falling: AAA $4.10 (Nov 5) to $3.53 (Nov 28), vs $3.08 a year ago.
- "Adjusted net loss" is Amtrak's "adjusted operating earnings". Trains.com cites $494.7M operating loss (single source).

## Not fully verified
- "$2.4B a year": confirmed FY24/FY25 only. Regional trainsets replaced by 2028: Airo service begins late 2027; completion unconfirmed. Acela 2.5-hour goal: Herrmann's stated aspiration only. Confidence: medium.
- trains.com and railwayage.com returned 403.
