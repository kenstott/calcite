Fact-check: IBTimes on HUD's 2026 homelessness count

Mostly accurate numbers, unsupported causal framing: 1 Pinocchio

The headline figures match HUD and NAEH: 698,425 people on one January 2026 night, -6.3% vs the January 2025 count (implied 745,652; arithmetic gives -6.33%), NAEH estimate -6.7%, chronic homelessness +1%. The piece mirrors HUD's release, including its causal credit to border and public-safety policy, which no data supports, and HUD's "largest on record" claim, for which no series is published. What would change this: HUD's AHAR (not yet released) could revise the baseline. Not checkable here: the NYT "nearly half" claim; HUD's AHAR PDF could not be parsed.

Misleading context

  • Causal attribution to policy from a one-night count taken at the start of the term.
  • Different baselines: -9.5% vs 2024, -6.3% vs 2025; HUD says homelessness is still near historic highs.
  • NAEH figures are modelled estimates.
