**Rating: 2 Pinocchios.** Statewide figures verified; framing selective; city figures unverifiable.

- **Answer:** True. 2025 homicide rate 3.5/100k (lowest since 1966); homicides 1,666 to 1,374 (-18.6%); robbery -19.9%, property -14.3%, MVT -25.8%, violent -10.2% (2025 vs 2024).
- **Sources:** gov.ca.gov 2026-07-01; CalMatters; NBC Bay Area (DOJ data).
- **Confidence:** high.
