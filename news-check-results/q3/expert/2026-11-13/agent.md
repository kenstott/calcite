# Fact-check: "Homicides drop 15.2% across major U.S. cities" (yourNEWS, 2026-11-12)

## Summary: 2 Pinocchios. Direction right, but the piece used a superseded MCCA release and reversed NYC.
- Article figures match the MCCA Nov 9 release exactly (homicides 3,394 vs 4,000, -15.2%, 66 of 67 agencies, Jan-Sep 2026 vs 2025).
- MCCA reissued Nov 12 after correcting the NYC PD row: homicides 3,347 vs 4,047 = -17.3%; robbery -13.3% (article -9.1); rape -3.8 (-3.5); agg assault -4.0 (-3.4); combined -6.3%, 17,940 fewer (article -4.9%, 13,822).
- FALSE: NYC homicides "rose 192 to 239". Corrected: 2026=192, 2025=239 (-19.7%). Original PDF had columns swapped (47-case gap explains the whole homicide difference). NYPD: 230 murders Jan-Oct 2026, -15.7% (independent).
- TRUE: Memphis 156 to 99; Las Vegas 64 to 79; declines Indianapolis/Philadelphia/LA/Detroit/Chicago; Houston missing; preliminary data; Canada -21.3% (9 agencies).
- Crime Index (547 agencies confirmed on crimeindex.org) murder -16.6% through Aug; the figure comes from a secondary post (James Tuttle, Nov 9).
- Not checked: White House credit claim; pre-2025 start of decline (no primary fetched). Only a summary of the article was readable, not full text.
- Misleading framing: "major cities" = 66 MCCA agencies (incl. sheriffs), preliminary, Houston missing.
