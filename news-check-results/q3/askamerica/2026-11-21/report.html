Misleading framing

'Southwest' means EIA's supply region (AZ, NM, small parts of NV/CA), and hydropower is only about 4% of its generation mix, which the piece omits. The 2023 global emissions statistic sits beside a regional story and implies a larger regional emissions effect than the data support. The 'nearly seven TWh' figure appears to be the regional total attributed to two plants.
