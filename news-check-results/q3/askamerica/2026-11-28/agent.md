## Caveats and framing to flag

Warehouse (energy.eia_retail_gasoline_prices): Amtrak fiscal-year average regular gas was $3.61 in FY26 vs $3.12 in FY25 and $3.38 in FY24, but the spike began March 2026 (monthly $2.81 Jan, $3.64 Mar, $4.48 May), five months into the fiscal year. FY25 ridership grew 5.1% while gas fell. Amtrak's release credits new trains, infrastructure, NextGen Acela, World Cup and America250, and never mentions fuel or fares. The mechanism is plausible (airfare CPI rose sharply) but not demonstrated.
- Omitted: gross ticket revenue rose only 1% against +7.4% ridership, so revenue per rider fell.
