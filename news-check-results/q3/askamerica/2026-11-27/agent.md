## What checked out (warehouse: fedregister.fr_documents_significance, through 2026-11-23)
- Final rules: 2024 = 3,248 (exact). 2025 = 2,442 vs CEI 2,441. 2026 year to date = 2,639 (exact). Lowest count in the warehouse span, and lowest on record per CEI's PDF.
- Significant rules 2026 year to date: 190 (exact).
- The roughly 2,946 projection is straight-line arithmetic (2,639 x 365/327), an estimate. Only 2019 and 2025 fall under 3,000 since 2010.
- Through 9/30/2026: 2,238 final rules, 166 significant (exact). The 86/34/46 split is CEI's subjective classification.
- 106,109-page 2024 Federal Register confirmed by the CEI PDF. West Virginia turkeys and the Nov 24 ceremony confirmed by KATV.
- Proposed-rule and notice rows match the warehouse within one or two documents (exceptions: 2026 notices 18,666 vs 18,745; 2021 final rules 3,257 vs 3,255).

## Problems and misleading framing
1. The table's significant-rule cells for 2019 (69) and 2020 (79) conflict with the warehouse (262, 438) and with CEI's own PDF (first-term average 280 a year). Graded mostly false; it makes 2025-26 look high versus the first term.
2. Significant counts are revised figures (2025: 155 vs warehouse 189; 2024: 342 vs 345).
3. "The trend has continued into 2026" is partly misleading: 190 significant rules year to date already exceed 2025's full-year 155 in CEI's table.
4. Omitted context: 243 of 2025's 2,441 rules were Biden's January rules (net 2,198 per the PDF).
5. Unsupported: the Q3 27-to-6 split (CEI only); Miller's Reagan comparison; the adjusted page counts (warehouse holds none; federalregister.gov statistics page blocked automated access with a CAPTCHA; PDF gives unadjusted figures).
6. Not reconciled: presidential-document rows (240/471/307) vs warehouse PRESDOC (129/176/148), different universe; small-business rows not in the warehouse.
7. The "second turkey" argument (tariffs, subsidies, equity stakes) is opinion and untestable.
