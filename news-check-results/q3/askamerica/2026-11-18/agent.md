The FOX 17 article's claims about Nashville crime declines and resident safety perceptions are accurate; 1 claim could not be confirmed.

Claim checks
- Crime down 17%, declines in every precinct (mayor): mostly true. FBI Return A for Metro Nashville PD (crime.cde_reta, ORI TN01901, Jan-Sep 2025 vs 2026) shows all crimes 37,747 to 33,073 (-12.4%). MNPD H1 Part I offenses fell 18,186 to 14,686 (-19.2%) per FOX 17. The exact 17% is city-reported and not reproducible. Both sources are preliminary.
- Auto thefts down 30%: true. FBI Return A shows 3,299 to 2,213 (-32.9%). WSMV reports -34%.
- Homicides down 44%: true. WSMV (Sept 18) quotes the chief at 44%. FBI murder counts went 45 to 27 (-40%).
- Crime in North Nashville down 13%: not verified. No precinct data in the warehouse and no source found.
- September update showing crime down 16%: unsupported. No primary release found.
- Survey details (1,000 residents, 12 largest neighborhoods, Sept 8-15, 70% vs 67% safe, 49% vs 58% say crime is rising): true per the Alliance page and slide deck.
- North Nashville/Bordeaux feel-safe 60% to 76%: matches the slides, but the slides give a neighborhood margin of error of +/-10.4 points, which the article omits.

Misleading context
- The +3 point change in feeling safe is about the same size as the +/-3.1 point margin of error. "More residents report feeling safe" is weakly supported.
- The survey was commissioned by a police-support alliance.
- The same slides show Nashville still ranks fourth-highest among ten peer cities on 2025 crime rate. The article omits this.
- MNPD says its crime-analysis figures are preliminary and not official totals.

Not verified
- The mayor's 13% and 16% figures could not be checked against a primary source.
