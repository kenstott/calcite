The 51st article's claims about D.C. evictions, ERAP cuts and cost of living are balanced and mostly accurate.

Article under reviewhttps://51st.news/dc-record-evictions-housing-crisis

Summary

Rating: 1 Pinocchio. Nearly every number traced to a source matched: CPI, court filings, ERAP funding and demand, CLCPP service data. Small slips: 93% should be 92% for landlord representation, and 82% Black should be 81% in the Jan-Jun 2026 report. One framing error: the 77% to 63% tenant-possession decline is described as applying to tenants with full representation, but the report gives it for all closed cases with known outcomes. D.C. unemployment of 5.9% differs from 5.7% in current BLS data. OTA eviction counts and the EPI budget could not be verified. The causal story (ERAP cuts drive evictions) is attributed to advocates and contested by landlords in the piece itself.
