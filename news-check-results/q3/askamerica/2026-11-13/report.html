Article under reviewhttps://yournews.com/2026/11/12/7263898/homicides-drop-15-2-across-major-u-s-cities-as-violent-crime

Summary

2 Pinocchios. The article faithfully copied the MCCA's Nov. 9 report, but MCCA reissued it on Nov. 12 (the article's own date) after correcting New York City's row, which had 2026 and 2025 swapped. In the updated report homicides fell 17.3% (3,347 vs 4,047), not 15.2%; robbery fell 13.3% (not 9.1%); combined violent crime fell about 6.3% (not 4.9%). The claim that NYC homicides rose 24.5% and that NYC robberies and assaults rose is false: NYC homicides fell from 239 to 192 and robberies and assaults also fell. The direction of the national trend is right; the 547-agency statistic could not be found.
