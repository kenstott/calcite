The RedState article's claims about VA claims backlog, new clinics and veteran homelessness are very biased and mostly accurate.

**Rating: 1 Pinocchio.**

Article: Ward Clark, RedState, Nov 11 2026. The connector fetched it successfully. The AskAmerica warehouse has no VA benefits-claims, clinic-opening or homeless-housing tables (only Census ACS veteran counts), so every claim was graded from VA primary releases and independent coverage.

**Claims and verdicts**
- "47 new healthcare clinics": mostly true. VA's Nov 9 release confirms 47, but the 47th (West Burlington, IA) is a relocation and expansion of a 2021 clinic. The Oct 15 release said 43.
- "Backlog down nearly 70% since Jan 20, 2025" (central claim): mostly true. "Backlog" means only claims pending more than 125 days. Total pending was 551,895 in Jan 2026, of which 100,115 were backlogged. VA said 74% in July 2026, so "nearly 70%" in November implies slight regression.
- "Processing time cut 46%": true. It matches VA's release and independent reporting (about 140 to about 75 days).
- "3.11M claims, record, +23.5% vs FY2024": true. 3,111,399 vs 2,517,519 is +23.6%, and the prior record was 3,001,734 in FY2025.
- "Granted over 60%, accuracy nearly 94% (highest in three years), about 3% appealed": true. The Oct 29 VA release gives 60.1%, 93.88% and 3.2%. The Oct 15 release said "two years", so VA's own wording is inconsistent.
- Collins quote ("over $1B allocated; last year housed over 50,000; close again this year"): mostly true. FY2025 was 51,936 and FY2026 is 48,968 (VA, Nov 12). The grants total $1.17B and were announced Sept 14, 2026, so "just allocated" is loose.
- Author's claim that Collins "announced a major plan" on Veterans Day: mostly false. The money was announced in September; Collins only referenced it on Nov 11.
- Reporter's fidelity in relaying the VA release: true. The quoted block matches the Nov 11 release verbatim.
- Sidebar headline about a $290K raccoon windfall: true, per the linked RedState page, which cites NY Post and Washington Post reports.

**Materially misleading even where the facts check out**
1. The piece is a near-verbatim relay of agency self-reporting, with no independent or critical sourcing. The VA's Oct 29 release says claims volume keeps rising and VA is hiring 2,000+ more staff.
2. The headline's "backlog 70%" uses a narrow definition (over 125 days) and a favorable base date (Jan 20, 2025).
3. The homelessness "plan" framing is wrong on timing, and similar $1B-scale grants were also announced in 2023.
4. The 47-clinic count includes relocations and expansions.

**Not independently verified:** the 2.5M FY2024 figure came from coverage of VA's own release, not the VA table itself. The VA weekly workload spreadsheet (MMWR 11/07/2026) returned formulas, not computed values, so the current backlog count was not read directly.
