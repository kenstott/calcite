FEC.gov's 2026 independent-expenditure cycle totals are directionally confirmed within ~15%; the four-week Talarico window cannot be verified in this corpus

fec.independent_expenditures, election_year=2026, checked against FEC.gov's Sept 21, 2026 "Latest updates" figures

Summary

The FEC.gov claim is partially confirmed. This corpus's copy of the FEC's own bulk independent-expenditure file, filtered to election_year=2026, lands within about 15% of the article's cycle-to-date totals in both dollars and candidate count once one confirmed junk filing is removed -- a close match for two independently vintaged snapshots, though not exact. The claim that Talarico's Texas Senate race is the single largest 2026 independent-expenditure target is independently confirmed at the cycle-to-date level (it is #1, ahead of Paxton and El-Sayed, with heavy opposition spending). However, the article's four-week-window figures (Aug 24-Sep 14) -- the $328.3M total and Talarico's specific $59.6M/17-committee/$2.09M-vs-$57.5M breakdown -- cannot be verified or refuted at all, because this table's transaction_date column is NULL for every single row loaded (100% of 681,504 rows, not just 2026). There is no other per-transaction date field in the table, so no sub-cycle window can be isolated here.

What was excluded, and why

The raw, unfiltered sum of fec.independent_expenditures for election_year=2026 (19,119 rows) is $40.16 billion -- roughly 27 times the article's figure. This is driven entirely by 29 rows attributed to one candidate, Bettis, Shawn (a minor Florida House candidate, candidate_id H6FL11274), carrying fabricated line items of $9,000,000,000 and $8,000,000,000 from spender names 'TANKING' and 'Warren Buffet', plus further $1B rows from 'APPLE INC. DJIA/SP/DOW USA' and similar. No House or Senate race in American history has attracted billions of dollars in independent expenditures; these are almost certainly spam/prank FEC filings that the agency's public bulk files accept without vetting (support_oppose values, amendment_indicator='N', and otherwise well-formed rows all suggest an actual accepted filing, not a parsing artifact). Every cycle total and candidate ranking in this report excludes only this one candidate's 29 rows (candidate_name <> 'Bettis, Shawn'). Without that exclusion the 'total' is not a usable number at all ($40.16B, plainly impossible); with it, the total drops to the $1.55B-$1.66B range reported above. All other rows, including every other candidate and every named Super PAC (SLF PAC, WinSenate, Texas PAC, Americans for Prosperity Action, United Democracy Project, Club for Growth (CLF), VoteVets, LCV Victory Fund, MAGA Inc., etc.), were retained as plausible, real-world-sized filings.

Method and caveats

  • Table used: fec.independent_expenditures -- FEC bulk file independent_expenditure_2026.csv, one row per IE transaction with candidate_id/name, spender_id/name, support_oppose, amount, and amendment_indicator. 19,119 rows loaded for election_year=2026 (of 681,504 total rows across 2010-2026).
  • Cycle total: computed two ways -- all non-junk filings ($1.66B) and non-amended filings only ($1.55B) -- because 1,822 rows carry amendment_indicator A1/A2/A3 totaling $109.4M, and it is unclear from this table alone whether FEC's own published aggregate nets these against their originals via prev_file_num or not. Neither treatment produces an exact match to the article's $1.453B; both land within 7-14% above it.
  • Candidate count: COUNT(DISTINCT candidate_id) understates the true count (662) because 1,748 of 17,371 non-junk rows carry a NULL candidate_id; backfilling with candidate_name brings the count to 841, within 4.5% of the article's 881.
  • Date window: transaction_date is NULL for every row in this table (100% null share, confirmed via data_coverage and a direct re-check against all 209 Talarico rows). This is a structural gap in this corpus's copy of the data -- the FEC's live site clearly has date-stamped filings, but this snapshot does not carry that field usably. As a result, the article's Aug 24-Sep 14 four-week total and the window-specific Talarico breakdown are not checkable from this table under any query.
  • Talarico race: filtered on candidate_id='S6TX00479' (TALARICO, JAMES, TX Senate), election_year=2026 -- all 209 rows are cycle-to-date, not window-limited. Total $72,348,678, 28 distinct committees (spender_id), $6,633,153 support (support_oppose='S') and $65,715,525 oppose (support_oppose='O').
