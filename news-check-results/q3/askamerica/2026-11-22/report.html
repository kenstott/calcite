Claims

  • 2026 second-warmest at 62.4 F: true (62.39); nearest rival 1897 at 61.44.
  • 2024 warmest at 63.8 F: true; warehouse ghcnd_daily MSP gives 63.80.
  • Five of past six years in top 10: true.
  • 2021, 2023, 2025 in top 10: true (ranks 7, 9, 5).
  • Two decades of warming: mostly true; 1991-2005 mean about 55.7 F vs about 58.2 F for 2006-2026, but year-to-year noise is large. FOX 9's linked piece makes a similar claim for October.
  • Feeling that warm falls are normal: opinion.

Caveats

The warehouse holds MSP 2010-2025 and only Sept 1-22 of 2026, so 2026 and the long ranks rest on ACIS. October alone was only tied for third (57.8 F per FOX 9's linked story), so the 2026 rank leans on a very warm September. Not misleading.
