The FOX 9 article's claims about the Twin Cities' second-warmest September-October, the top-10 ranking, and the fall warming trend are accurate.

Rating: 0 Pinocchios.

Article: https://www.fox9.com/weather/twin-cities-records-2nd-warmest-september-october-2026 (fetched successfully; both linked FOX 9 stories were also fetched).

Method: the warehouse table weather.ghcnd_daily (MSP station USW00014922) was checked first. It matches 2024 exactly (63.80 F) but holds only 2010-2025 fully, and 2026 only through Sep 22. For 2026 and the 1872-on ranks I used ACIS StnData with the threaded station MSPthr, a 61-day mean ending Oct 31. These figures come from the ACIS data itself, not a secondary site.

Claims:
- True: 2026 was second-warmest at 62.4 F (ACIS 62.39). The nearest other year is 1897 at 61.44, so there are no ties within 0.1.
- True: 2024 was warmest at 63.8 F. The warehouse and ACIS agree.
- True: five of the past six years are in the top 10 (2021, 2023, 2024, 2025, 2026). 2022 (58.76) is not.
- True: 2021, 2023 and 2025 rank 7th, 9th and 5th.
- Mostly true: Sept-Oct has been warming for at least two decades. The 1991-2005 mean is about 55.7 F versus about 58.2 F for 2006-2026, but year-to-year noise is large (2020 was 52.3 F).
- Not checkable: "warmer falls may feel normal" is a perception opinion.

Framing: not misleading. Two caveats a reader should know. The record starts in 1872, but Sept 1872 is missing in ACIS. October alone was only tied for third (57.8 F, per FOX 9's own linked story), so the two-month rank leans on a very warm September.
