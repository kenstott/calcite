The Scott Peters press release's claims about 2026 wildfire statistics, wildfire costs and the Fix Our Forests Act are accurate.

Rating: 0 Pinocchios. Article: https://scottpeters.house.gov/press-releases/peters-whitesides-crank-urge-inclusion-of-forestry-bill-in-annual-ndaa-package (fetched successfully; a congressman's advocacy release, Oct 23, 2026).

Claims checked
1. Central: roughly 66,000 fires, more than 9M acres, 132% and 141% of the 10-year average. TRUE. NIFC IMSR of 10/23/2026: 65,796 fires, 9,048,486 acres, 132% and 141% of 2016-2025 averages. Warehouse (disasters.wildfire_occurrence_by_state_year, WFIGS layer) shows 9.04M acres but only 39,423 incidents, because it counts a narrower set; NIFC is the right comparator.
2. Over 1,600 firefighters and support personnel assigned. TRUE: 1,614 on IMSR 10/23 (down to 1,230 by 10/26).
3. Wildfires cost $400B-$900B annually. TRUE as a figure: matches the Joint Economic Committee's October 2023 report ($394B-$893B). Framing flag: a high-end, 2023, Democratic-staff estimate for climate-exacerbated wildfires, given without source; a Trump-era report put it at $87-428B.
4. Prevention saves $3.75 per $1. MOSTLY TRUE. The UC Davis Science study (May 2026) found $3.75 per $1 for forest fuel treatments across ~300 western fires; fuel treatments limit spread and do not prevent ignitions.
5. House passed FOFA in January 2025. TRUE (Jan 23, 2025, 279-141). Warehouse law.bills shows the Senate version S.1462 on the Senate calendar, not passed.
6. House passed Save Our Sequoias Act in March 2026. TRUE (voice vote, March 16-17).
7. One-fifth of sequoias destroyed since 2015. TRUE as consistent: NPS estimates 13-19% of large sequoias killed in the 2020-21 fires; no source found tied to a 2015 start.
8. Three leads plus 28 members; addressees. TRUE per the letter PDF.
9. FOFA is the only bipartisan, bicameral package with committee process in both chambers (letter signers). TRUE on its components (H.R. 471 passed House; S.1462 cleared Senate Agriculture 18-5); "only" is an unverifiable superlative.
10. Wildfire is a hazard to military readiness. Opinion, not checkable here; the letter's supporting facts (Washington and Arkansas fires, Guard man-hours) were not independently tested.

Misleading-framing flags: advocacy source; the cost range's age and provenance are omitted; the $3.75 ratio is for fuel treatments, not prevention generally. congress.gov returned HTTP 403, so bill status came from the warehouse's GovInfo-based law.bills.
