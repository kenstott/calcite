Rating: 0 Pinocchios on the core claims, with one loose sentence (arguably 1 Pinocchio for that sentence alone).

Verified (EIA pages read via web_fetch; warehouse queried first):
- Down 12 cents to $4.10: AAA's own figures give -12.55c ($4.2258 to $4.1003). EIA weekly regular fell 11.6c (4.269 to 4.153). True.
- More than $1 above a year ago: $4.1003 - $3.0790 = $1.02. BLS Nov 2025 average was $3.228 (different survey). True.

- Gasoline demand 8.35 to 8.80 mb/d: EIA product supplied 8,358 to 8,808 kb/d. True.
- Gasoline stocks 209.2M to 209.0M bbl: EIA 209,180 to 208,958 kbbl. True.

Weak point:
