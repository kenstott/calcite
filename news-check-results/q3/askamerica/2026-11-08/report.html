The AAA "National Average Falls 12 Cents in One Week" release's claims about gas prices, EIA inventories and crude oil are mostly accurate.

Article under reviewhttps://gasprices.aaa.com/national-average-falls-12-cents-in-one-week/

Summary

Verdict: 0 Pinocchios (accurate), with one loose spot. The 12-cent weekly drop is right ($4.2258 to $4.1003); EIA weekly retail regular fell 11.6 cents (4.269 to 4.153); EIA demand (8,358 to 8,808 kb/d), gasoline stocks (209,180 to 208,958 kbbl) and crude stocks (410,625 to 409,760 kbbl) match. The 'high $80s to low $90s' crude description is not corroborated: warehouse WTI spot was $99.64 on Nov 3 and Nov 4 is not loaded. AAA's EV charging and state rankings are proprietary and not checkable. The cataloged table energy.eia_retail_gasoline_prices was not queryable, so EIA's page was read directly.
