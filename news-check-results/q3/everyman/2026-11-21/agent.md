# Fact-check: MIT Technology Review, "Hydropower drought Colorado River" (Casey Crownhart, 2026-11-17)

Method note: WebFetch returned only a summary of the article, not verbatim text. Claims below are those the summary exposed. I found no attributed quotes in that summary, and I could not confirm there are none. Python was not used.

|---|---|---|---|

## Overall rating
1 Pinocchio (mostly accurate). The headline numbers match EIA and IEA data.

## Misleading or imprecise
- "Within 1% of the minimum" uses elevation percentages, which make the margin sound smaller than the real storage buffer. It also applies to a minimum power pool, which is an engineering limit, not a dead pool.
- The 2027 "further decline" is about 0.5%, which is flat.
- Late-September figures sit beside mid-November recovery. The reader should note that levels have moved since.
- The 85% snowmelt claim, the 7 TWh claim, the 27-year drought claim and the 2022 study were not checked here.
