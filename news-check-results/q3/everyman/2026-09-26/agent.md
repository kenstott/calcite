# Fact-check: 2026 independent expenditure totals / Texas Senate (Talarico)

**Verdict: Not confirmed — likely false / fabricated precision.** None of the three specific figures could be verified against any real source, and the numbers that could be checked (Talarico support/opposition spending) contradict independently reported figures from multiple outlets.

## What I checked

1. **"$1,452,997,175 across 881 candidates" for the full 2026 cycle.** No FEC page, FEC Record post, OpenSecrets/Statista data, or news report contains this figure. FEC.gov's independent-expenditures browse page returns no aggregate total without running a manual query (it's a raw itemized database, not a running total page). One search-engine AI summary "confirmed" this exact number and the 881-candidate count — but it did so only after I included that exact number in my query text, with no underlying source page containing it in the returned links. That is a strong sign of the summarizer echoing back my own query rather than citing real data, not independent confirmation. For calibration: full-cycle *outside spending* (a broader category than IE alone) in the completed 2024 cycle was about $4.46 billion per OpenSecrets/Statista; a $1.45B IE-only figure this far ahead of a midterm general election is not implausible on its face, but I found no primary source stating it.

2. **"$328,333,233 in a four-week window (Aug 24–Sep 14, 2026)."** No source — FEC, news, or aggregator — reports this figure. I found no evidence for this window total at all.

3. **Texas Senate (Talarico) as the single largest four-week target at $59,564,521 across 17 committees ($2,086,549 support / $57,477,973 opposition).** This is contradicted by actual reporting:
   - Texas Tribune (July 20, 2026): outside spending supporting Talarico was about **$4.1 million** (mostly Lone Star Rising PAC), while opposition spending against him was only about **$763,000** — i.e., support far exceeded opposition, the opposite of the ratio claimed in the question.
   - Texas Tribune (Sep 15 and Sep 22, 2026) and NPR (Sep 18, 2026): a September Republican ad surge did ramp up spending in the race sharply — Senate Leadership Fund's "Texas PAC" bought roughly $51M in ads, MAGA Inc. put in $10M (split, with about $5M framed as anti-Talarico attack ads), and the NRSC-aligned ad buy for Paxton was later increased to $100M. This shows real, large, and accelerating spending in this race in the same window the question cites, and Talarico is plausibly one of the most heavily targeted Senate candidates nationally.
   - However, none of these sources give a consolidated $59,564,521 total, a $2,086,549 support figure, a $57,477,973 opposition figure, or a count of "17 committees." Support also did not track anywhere near that low relative to opposition in the reporting I found (Lone Star Rising PAC's support alone was already ~$3-4M in September).

## Bottom line

- The broad narrative — a very expensive 2026 cycle, a late-August-to-mid-September spending surge, and Texas's Talarico-Paxton Senate race being one of the most heavily targeted contests in the country — is directionally consistent with real reporting.
- The specific dollar figures and the "881 candidates" / "17 committees" counts in the question do not appear in any FEC, OpenSecrets, or news source I could find, and where comparable numbers exist (Talarico support vs. opposition), they conflict with the question's figures, including which side (support vs. opposition) is larger.
- I could not verify these claims and would treat all three specific numbers as unsubstantiated/likely fabricated pending an actual pull from FEC.gov's independent-expenditures dataset for the stated date range and candidate.
