# Fact-check: Newsweek, "Map shows states where homeowners are most likely to be mortgage free"

Method: WebFetch returned a summary of the article, not verbatim text, so claims below are as summarized. Checked against the Census Bureau story (Jan 2026), Freddie Mac PMMS and search results. Limited depth: about 4 lookups.

## Claims and verdicts
1. About 41% of homeowners own outright (Realtor.com analysis of Census data). Partly supported. Realtor.com reports 41% for 2025. Census reports 39.4% (ACS 5-year 2020-2024) and "nearly 40%" for 2024. The figures differ by vintage and dataset.
2. Mortgage-free share rose from 34.4% (2010-14) to 39.4% (2020-24). TRUE. Matches the Census story exactly.
3. West Virginia highest at 56.6%. Supported as Realtor.com's 2025 figure. Census 2024 gives 53.9%. The piece mixes vintages.
4. Mississippi 52.7% and New Mexico 50%. Rank order (WV, MS, NM) is consistent with Census 2024. I could not find the exact percentages. Not checkable here.
5. Lowest are DC 26.5%, Maryland 30.6%, Colorado 31.6% (2025). Not checked against the Realtor.com table. Census 2024 has Maryland 29.0% and DC 24.3%, so the direction is consistent.
6. 59% have a mortgage. Arithmetic consistent with 41%. The West is highest at 63.8% and the South lowest at 56.8%. Not checkable here.
7. Freddie Mac 30-year at 7.11% for the week ending Nov 12, 7.18% the prior week and 6.24% a year earlier. Matches the Freddie Mac PMMS page as fetched. The page does not show dates for the prior-week and year-ago values.
8. WV median asking price $259,999 vs $415,000 nationally. Not checkable here.
9. Jiayi Xu quote: "Areas where owners rely more heavily on mortgages are more sensitive to rate changes." Not verified against the original Realtor.com release.
10. Existing home sales at a 16-year low of 3.75 million. Not verified. NAR is the usual source, and the piece attributes it to Realtor.com.
11. Experts attribute WV's rate to low prices and older residents. Plausible, and the Census story offers similar explanations (rates and prices discourage moving).

## Rating: 1 Pinocchio (minor issues)
The core numbers hold up. Where they diverge from Census, the cause is a different data vintage or source, not an error.

## Misleading or context flags
- Mixed vintages. Realtor.com's 2025 figures sit beside Census 2020-24 and 2024 figures with little distinction. A reader would see 41% and 39.4% as conflicting.
- The "mortgage-free" share is of owner-occupied homes. It is not the same as owners who have fully paid off a loan, and it includes people who bought with cash.
- The mortgage-rate and home-sales material is only loosely tied to the map. The suggestion that mortgage-free owners deepen the housing freeze is speculative.
