# Fact-check: TheBlaze, "'That's a myth': Why are gas prices plummeting now that the election is over?"

Author Joseph MacKinnon, published Nov 19, 2026. Method: WebFetch returned only a summary of the article, not verbatim text. Quote wording below is therefore paraphrase-level. Two WebSearch lookups were run.

## Claims and verdicts

1. Gas prices fell in the weeks after the midterms. SUPPORTED. AAA: $4.3556 (about Oct 12), $4.1003 (Nov 5), $3.9081 (Nov 12). The Nov 12 figure was the first reading below $4 since July.
2. National average, per GasBuddy: $4.24 (Oct 26), $4.12 (Nov 2), $3.96 (Nov 9), $3.74 (Monday before publication). NOT CHECKABLE HERE. GasBuddy's series was not retrievable. AAA's figures are directionally consistent and in the same range ($4.10 on Nov 5, $3.91 on Nov 12).
3. AAA reported $3.72 on Thursday (Nov 19). NOT CHECKABLE HERE. I only retrieved AAA through Nov 12. The trend is consistent with the claim.
4. De Haan (GasBuddy) called the election explanation a "myth", citing refinery maintenance ending and cooler-weather demand. PARTLY VERIFIED. A Nov 16 report (KTTC) has him attributing the decline to lower fall driving, the switch to winter-blend gasoline, and refineries finishing maintenance. The exact "myth" quote was not found.
5. The switch to cheaper winter blend (September) is a factor. SUPPORTED by De Haan's Nov 16 comments.
6. Trump said oil "dropped like a rock" the day after the election. NOT VERIFIED. No source found. I could not check oil prices on Nov 4.
7. The White House blamed Iran for high prices before the midterms. PLAUSIBLE but not directly verified. The 2026 Iran conflict and Hormuz disruptions are well documented.
8. 87% of exit-poll respondents were at least somewhat concerned about gas and diesel costs. NOT CHECKABLE HERE. The exit poll was not found.
9. Hormuz flow has increased but is still below pre-conflict levels; Trump is reluctant to resume major operations; US crude output is strong; inventories are rising. PARTLY SUPPORTED. The Hormuz and Iran context is documented. I did not verify the inventory and output figures against EIA.
10. Gas fell in every state in the past week, and prices may stay below $4. NOT CHECKABLE HERE. This is a forecast, and I found no state-level data.

## Overall rating: 1 Pinocchio (mostly accurate)

The core point is well supported: seasonal factors explain the drop, and the timing of the decline matches the election only by coincidence. The numbers I could check are consistent with AAA data.

## Potentially misleading framing

- The headline implies one cause ("myth") for a multi-factor decline. Hormuz and Iran developments could plausibly also have political timing, and the piece quotes De Haan against the election link without independent support.
- Trump's "dropped like a rock" claim is relayed with no check on actual oil prices that day. If crude did not fall sharply on Nov 4, that claim is unchallenged.
- The piece mixes GasBuddy and AAA figures, which use different methods and so are not directly comparable.

## Limits

I could not read the full article text, and I did not reach EIA, GasBuddy or the exit-poll primary sources. Items marked not checkable are open questions, not errors.
