# Fact-check: Fox News, "Democrats plot massive spending spree after midterm rout, and Trump could help them pull it off" (Robert Schmad, 2026-11-16)

Method and limits: WebFetch returned only a summary of the article, not verbatim text. Verification used three quick web searches. Most claims were NOT checked against primary sources (CBO, Treasury, Penn Wharton, Tax Foundation). Ratings below reflect that.

## Claims and verdicts

| Claim | Verdict |
|---|---|

AP (Oct 14, 2026) confirms a 100-day agenda with ACA credit restoration and Medicaid reversal. The Nov. 4 date and SNAP item were not confirmed. Framing as post-election is the article's own. |

## Overall rating: provisional, 2 Pinocchios (cannot confirm higher confidence)

Reasons: the core facts I could check (Jeffries agenda, Hinojosa plan, veto limit) hold up. The Medicaid cut figure and enrollment number appear inflated versus CBO-based figures I found. Not checked: most cost estimates.

## Misleading framing to flag
- "Spending spree" and "massive" are editorial; many items (ACA credits, Medicaid reversal) restore existing policy rather than new spending. Sanders, Pritzker and Hinojosa proposals are lumped in with the House agenda even though they are different actors and levels of government (a state governor's rainy-day-fund check is not federal spending).
- Presenting wish-lists from individual members and a 200-item Pritzker document as "Democrats' plan" overstates party consensus.
- Citing a costly universal health care figure ($40-70T) that no cited Democratic leader proposes in this agenda inflates the cost impression.
- Headline "Trump could help" rests on openness to ACA verification deals and high-earner taxes, which the article itself shows is conditional.
