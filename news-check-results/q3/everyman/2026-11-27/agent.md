Limits: WebFetch returned only a summary of the piece, not verbatim text, so I could not extract every quote. Most checks rely on search-result summaries, not the primary Federal Register or OIRA pages.

## Rating: 2 Pinocchios (mostly accurate numbers, with unverified and one-sided elements)

The headline rule counts are accurately taken from CEI's own annual series. Other figures could not be confirmed, and one attribution is unsupported.

## Materially misleading or missing context

- Final-rule counts are a weak measure of regulatory burden. Page counts, economic significance and the content of rules matter more. The piece makes this point itself about hidden intervention.
- The "lowest on record" claim rests on CEI's own tabulation, and the page-count series is inconsistent across CEI publications.
- The Miller quote may be misattributed. Treat it as unverified.
- It does not mention the 2025 shutdown, which depressed Federal Register output.
- It is an opinion blog from a free-market think tank. It is not neutral reporting, though it is mixed in tone because it does not simply praise the administration.
