## Misleading framing

- The headline says interest "cost" $1.4T. The $1.4T is GAO's total interest, which includes about $0.3T credited to federal trust funds. That is an intragovernmental bookkeeping transfer, not a cash cost to taxpayers. The comparable cost to the public is about $1.1T, or CBO's net interest of about $1.1T. The article does explain the split in its body, but the headline and lead overstate the burden by about 25-30%.
- The article mixes three different measures: GAO gross interest ($1.4T), GAO interest on debt held by the public ($1.1T), and CBO net interest on the public debt. A reader can easily take them as competing numbers.
- Debt-limit timing is stated as one advocate's expectation. Other projections run to mid-2027, so the "February 2027" date is less certain than the framing suggests.

## Overall rating: One Pinocchio

The core numbers are accurate and sourced to the audit and CBO. The headline relies on the gross figure that includes intragovernmental interest. Two named quotes and the $1.14T CBO level could not be verified here.
